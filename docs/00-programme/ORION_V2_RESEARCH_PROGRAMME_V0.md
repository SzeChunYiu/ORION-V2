# ORION-V2 Research Programme V0

**Status:** prospective research programme; `BLOCKED_BY_V1_FREEZE` for implementation and outcome-generating V2 experiments.

## 1. Programme identity

ORION-V2 is a clean successor research space. Existing ORION papers and V1 evidence remain owned by the ORION repository. This repository studies only the successor questions and publications that survive V1 and external donor reduction.

The programme starts from three commitments:

1. every useful V1 capability must be preserved, replaced by a demonstrably stronger parent, or explicitly deprecated after protected non-inferiority;
2. no V1 atom, paper boundary, workflow or data structure is inherited by default;
3. every surviving V2 theory must be quantitative or formal enough for machine execution, counterexample generation and protected evaluation.

## 2. Research cell

Every major finding is reviewed through at least these lenses:

- **formal epistemology and verification:** typed state, equivalence, obstruction, proof, preservation and authority;
- **statistics, causal inference and measurement:** identifiability, experiment comparison, invariance, uncertainty and dependence;
- **systems engineering, operations and management:** requirements, decomposition, workflow, scheduling, cost, risk and organizational control;
- **cognitive science, design and scientific discovery:** hypothesis/experiment search, insight, analogy, representation change and problem finding;
- **social science, law and institutions:** aggregation, incentives, strategic response, authority, precedent and governance;
- **science of science and evaluation integrity:** frontier breadth, novelty contraction, leakage, reward hacking and independent replay;
- **rotating native-domain expert:** protects each donor from being flattened into ORION vocabulary.

The adversarial rule is simple: assign every attractive V2 claim first to the expert most likely to reduce it to prior work.

## 3. Research derivation

The programme arose from a broad concern that ORION had learned deeply from AI, formal methods and scientific reasoning but had not defensibly traversed all organized human knowledge. Initial examples included quantitative politics and linguistics. Repository audit then corrected the naive omission story: ORION already contained parent-domain seeds, measurement science, computational linguistics, donor-envelope logic and external-domain programmes. The real gap was not “no social science”; it was **no independently grounded coverage and no machine theory for remote structural neighbours**.

Changed-vocabulary study then found recurring structures across remote fields:

- political ideal-point linking, psychometric equating, metrological traceability and diachronic semantics all pressure cross-generation comparability;
- contextuality, sheaf/local-to-global reasoning, database consistency and plural scientific portraits pressure local validity versus global gluing;
- structural/regular equivalence, bisimulation, interface equivalence and predictive causal states formalize “same position under a context”;
- causal Markov equivalence, inverse-problem equifinality and rough indiscernibility show probe-relative underdetermination;
- gauge redundancy supplies the scientifically opposite case: multiple descriptions of one state;
- Blackwell/Le Cam experiment comparison suggests directional decision-relative dominance and deficiency rather than one embedding metric;
- viability and learning-space theory pressure justified reachability;
- stemmatology and evolution pressure tree-only provenance;
- mechanism design and the Lucas critique pressure passive-evaluator assumptions;
- multiple-fault diagnosis and metabolic control pressure universal single-stage responsibility;
- TRIZ, structure mapping, design-by-analogy, morphological analysis and C-K/FBS theory reduce the novelty of cross-domain structural search itself.

These findings produced the present V2 object: a donor-faithful, context-relative, machine-replayable process for re-deriving scientific problem solving and frontier evolution.

## 4. Core workstreams

### W1 — all-domain structural donor atlas

Traverse independent field taxonomies. Reconstruct canonical native problems, quantitative objects, operations, invariants, probes, failures and decision rules. Search remote neighbours by structure, not merely words or citations.

### W2 — context-relative structural knowledge space

Define plural equivalence, dominance, abstraction, obstruction and incomparability relations parameterized by a declared context/probe/decision object.

### W3 — V1 capability and atom re-derivation

Bind the exact V1 capability census, then give every capability and paper-level object a disposition. No silent loss and no automatic inheritance.

### W4 — complete end-to-end scientific solver

Derive a problem-solving lifecycle from obligations, admissible actions, uncertainty, cost, authority and protected terminals rather than a fixed script.

### W5 — witnessed frontier Jump

Reconstruct Jump as minimum-responsible-level escalation across action, repair, model, representation, objective, method/tool, workflow, framework and constitution levels.

### W6 — machine donor-reduction engine

Represent native donors, structural signatures, mappings, strongest donor products, distinguishing probes and reduction receipts in deterministic machine objects.

### W7 — protected evaluation and bounded saturation

Measure coverage, donor fidelity, structural relation validity, false analogy, novelty contraction, solver value, Jump necessity, V1 parity and fresh-domain transfer.

### W8 — V2-only publication programme

Develop new candidate papers while preserving existing ORION publication ownership. Paper identity is earned only after parent reduction and protocol freeze.

## 5. Phases

### Phase A — pre-freeze research

Allowed:

- literature and repo collision audits;
- donor cards and independent reconstructions;
- theory and schema design;
- issue and paper planning;
- known-answer/hostile benchmark specification;
- V1 parity-census design.

Blocked:

- V2 runtime or framework implementation;
- V2 outcome-generating experiments;
- mutation of V1;
- scientific superiority or novelty terminals.

### Phase B — exact V1 handoff

Bind V1 commit/digest, component/capability graph, P1–P15 results and negative history, execution identities and non-retroactivity rule.

### Phase C — coverage and theory freeze

Complete native and changed-vocabulary passes, freeze structural relations, schemas, baselines, evaluation and authority custody.

### Phase D — minimal reference implementation

Implement only the objects that survive parent reduction. Run V1 parity and simple controls before frontier experiments.

### Phase E — protected scientific evaluation

Run structural-neighbour, solver, Jump, cross-domain transfer and shadow open-research suites under matched resources and independent replay.

### Phase F — derive V2 core

The final V2 architecture is derived from surviving obligations and results. It may have different atoms, paper boundaries and workflow shape from any planning artifact.

## 6. Programme terminal

The planning programme does not complete when many files exist. It completes only when:

- V1 is frozen and bound;
- the declared all-domain search is boundedly saturated;
- every V1 capability and candidate V2 object has a disposition;
- strongest donor products are tested;
- protected quantitative evidence exists;
- the V2 core and publication programme are derived without retroactively rewriting V1.
