# Formal Parent Literature Anchors V1

**Purpose:** prevent ORION-V2 from relabelling mature formal machinery as a new Machine Epistemics contribution. These are parent-owner anchors for the Conceptual Development / Transfer Discovery formal programme.

## Structure mapping and relational analogy

Gentner, D. (1983). *Structure-Mapping: A Theoretical Framework for Analogy*. Cognitive Science 7(2), 155–170. DOI: 10.1207/s15516709cog0702_3.

Parent-owned content: analogy should preferentially map relational systems rather than surface object attributes, with systematic higher-order relations constraining mappings. ORION may compare against or build on structure mapping; it cannot claim relational analogy itself.

## Anti-unification / least-general generalization

Cerna, D. M. & Kutsia, T. (2023). *Anti-unification and Generalization: A Survey*. IJCAI 2023, 6563–6573. DOI: 10.24963/ijcai.2023/736.

Parent-owned content: anti-unification is a mature family of generalization computations, dual to unification, with multiple theories and applications. An ORION abstraction result must compare against the strongest applicable anti-unification/generalization parent rather than treating generalization as novel.

## Formal Concept Analysis

Ganter, B. & Wille, R. (2024). *Formal Concept Analysis: Mathematical Foundations*, 2nd ed. Springer. DOI: 10.1007/978-3-031-63422-2.

Parent-owned content: formal contexts, Galois connections, extent/intent closure and concept lattices supply a rigorous mathematical account of one class of concept formation and contextual concept logic. ORION `ConceptState` is not a replacement; FCA is the native parent whenever the problem genuinely reduces to a formal context.

## Minimum Description Length

Grünwald, P. (2000). *Model Selection Based on Minimum Description Length*. Journal of Mathematical Psychology 44, 133–152. DOI: 10.1006/jmps.1999.1280.

Parent-owned content: compression/description length as a principle for inductive model selection. An MDL score can rank abstraction candidates but cannot by itself establish native fidelity, authority, causality or empirical truth.

## Category theory

Mac Lane, S. (1971; later editions). *Categories for the Working Mathematician*. Springer, GTM 5.

Parent-owned content: categories, functors, natural transformations, universal constructions, adjunctions and related categorical machinery. ORION may use functoriality and commuting diagrams as exact tests only when native objects and morphisms genuinely form the required structures. It must not assert that all cross-domain scientific transfer is categorical.

## Graph/relational homomorphism family

Graph and relational-structure homomorphisms are established mathematical structure-preserving maps with a large combinatorial/model-theoretic literature. The ORION partial-homomorphism interface is therefore reference semantics and a benchmark oracle, not a novel mathematical concept.

## Abstract interpretation and sound abstraction

Cousot, P. & Cousot, R. (1977). *Abstract interpretation: a unified lattice model for static analysis of programs by construction or approximation of fixpoints*. POPL 1977, 238–252.

Parent-owned content: sound abstraction with explicit relation between concrete and abstract semantics. ORION's parent-recovery requirement should be compared to mature abstraction/refinement methods rather than presented as unprecedented.

## Claim boundary

The candidate ORION residual is **not** any single formal object above. It is the empirically/formally testable proposition that a scientific AI can:

1. discover which parent representation or abstraction is useful;
2. recover the donor-native judgments;
3. construct a bounded cross-domain mapping without a fixed lesson table;
4. search for obstructions/negative transfer;
5. preserve old-valid cases and authority/measurement boundaries;
6. improve a prospective target decision or establish a new formally checked consequence;
7. deactivate when a mature parent is sufficient.

If the strongest parent federation reproduces the protected decisions at lower cost, the ORION transfer mechanism contracts.
