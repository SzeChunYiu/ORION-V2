# Wave-03 Expert Panel and Decision Log

## Panel

1. **Institution/formal-semantics lead** — satisfaction, theory morphisms, conservative consequence.
2. **Abstract-interpretation/refinement lead** — approximation order, simulation, transformer soundness.
3. **Bidirectional-transformation lead** — round trips, partiality, complement preservation.
4. **Measurement/transport lead** — calibration, uncertainty, invariance, epoch.
5. **Operations/engineering lead** — resources, units, capacity, target realization.
6. **Native-domain rotating lead** — protects source and target native judgments.
7. **Scientific-governance lead** — authority, prospective freeze, adoption separation.
8. **Adversarial-methods lead** — countermodels, false analogy, parent-product ties.

## Decision D03-01 — reject one-way preservation as sufficient

**Formal-semantics finding:** a total forward map can preserve sampled rows while adding unsupported old-image behavior.

**Bidirectional finding:** without a reverse fibre or complement, coherent updates cannot be checked.

**Decision:** require native round trip and generalized-image backward reflection except for explicitly sound over-approximating transports.

## Decision D03-02 — separate exact interpretation from conservative extension

**Objection:** a theory with additional states or judgments could pass Wave-02 exact checks.

**Decision:** exact requires bijective state, action and judgment maps, exact assumptions, authority and calibrated resources. Added structure belongs to conservative extension.

## Decision D03-03 — make future-query loss executable

**Native-domain finding:** two states may be identical for the current release decision and different for a later legal, causal, or safety decision.

**Decision:** compute all judgments differing within each collapsed fibre. Unregistered loss must still be declared; later registration expires the transport.

## Decision D03-04 — counterexample reflection must have decisiveness

**Adversarial finding:** a sound set-valued abstraction containing both `False` and `True` technically contains a falsifier but can no longer refute.

**Decision:** counterexample witnesses specify whether decisive reflection is required. Decisive falsifiers cannot be hidden inside an ambiguous set.

## Decision D03-05 — type resources as calibrated intervals

**Operations finding:** upper-bound-only scalar costs cannot represent units, lower bounds or uncertainty.

**Decision:** use dimension, unit, lower and upper bounds. Unit changes require evidence-bound calibration. Generalized intervals must contain calibrated native intervals.

## Decision D03-06 — transport validity is configuration-dependent

**Measurement finding:** changed calibration or evaluator can invalidate a transport without changing state/action maps.

**Decision:** freeze and compare source epoch, target epoch, assumptions, calibration, evaluator, resource model and authority policy.

## Decision D03-07 — compose loss upward and authority downward

**Formal finding:** multi-step translation can appear exact if only terminal maps are inspected.

**Decision:** chain uncertainty and semantic loss add; authority ceiling is the minimum; missing judgment correspondence invalidates composition.

## Decision D03-08 — common envelope is not target adaptation

**Native-target finding:** a source and target may share a role while target actions, units and validation differ.

**Decision:** require a target realization that is a right inverse of the target transport on selected states/actions, plus target calibration and target validation.

## Decision D03-09 — implement the parent product as falsifiers

**Parent-reduction finding:** satisfaction, Galois connection, lens laws and conservativity are already mature.

**Decision:** implement exact finite parent-law checkers and use them to reduce ORION claims. They remain explicitly parent-owned.

## Decision D03-10 — do not open another paper by default

**Publication finding:** Wave 03 strengthens C11 but does not yet separate it from C01/C02/C03/C06.

**Decision:** C11 remains `MERGE_UNLESS_SEPARATED`. A standalone paper requires a protected benchmark against the strongest parent product.

## Unresolved

- stuttering, weak and trace simulation;
- proof-producing institution morphisms;
- automated discovery of complements/reverse fibres;
- uncertainty composition beyond additive conservative bounds;
- causal and strategic target adaptation;
- human expert reproducibility;
- full V1 collision audit;
- protected fresh cross-domain transfer.
