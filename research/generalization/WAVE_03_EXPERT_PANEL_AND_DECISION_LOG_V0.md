# Wave 03 Expert Panel and Decision Log V0

## Panel question

How should ORION transform domain knowledge into generalized, machine-runnable theory without either copying an industry-specific model or erasing the native distinctions that make it scientifically valid?

## Decision 1 — generalization has two axes

**Finding:** Wave 02's source-to-envelope transport is necessary but insufficient.

**Formal-semantics view:** vertical abstraction determines what can be forgotten while preserving registered decisions.

**Native-domain view:** horizontal transfer requires a new target-native interpretation; the source mapping cannot supply target calibrations or interventions.

**Governance view:** the common envelope grants no authority.

**Disposition:** adopt the two-axis model:

```text
native source -> decision envelope <- native target
                         |
                  target adaptation contract
```

## Decision 2 — compile the smallest sufficient envelope

**Finding:** hand-authored generalized ontologies tend to preserve coordinates because they sound important, not because they change a decision.

**Formal-methods challenge:** derive the coarsest finite partition preserving registered judgments and action-labelled successor blocks.

**Systems challenge:** retain transition behaviour so that two states with the same current label but different recoverability are not merged.

**Disposition:** implement deterministic partition refinement and require a decision registry. Empty or incomplete registries return `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## Decision 3 — no lossy envelope is future universal

**Finding:** every non-singleton quotient block hides a distinction a future query could ask about.

**Measurement challenge:** cross-generation comparability must name target decisions and expiration conditions.

**Scientific-control challenge:** a compiled envelope may be safe for one action-selection policy and unsafe after the problem changes.

**Disposition:** add a future-query counter-probe and the terminal `SAFE_CURRENT_FUTURE_UNSAFE`.

## Decision 4 — information order is directional and task-relative

**Finding:** lexical similarity and symmetric embedding distance obscure replacement direction.

**Statistics challenge:** use garbling/decision value where available.

**Causal challenge:** observational equality does not imply interventional equality.

**Disposition:** represent equivalence, left/right dominance, registered-task equality, incomparability and `CANNOT_CHECK` separately.

## Decision 5 — local consistency and global existence are different

**Finding:** each local context can contain valid assignments while the family has no global realization.

**Physics/local-global challenge:** preserve obstruction rather than averaging local states.

**Database/systems challenge:** a locally empty context is a different failure from a global obstruction.

**Disposition:** implement exact finite global-section search with distinct terminals.

## Decision 6 — source count is not evidence independence

**Finding:** validators sharing a model, dataset, laboratory or retrieval corpus can be numerous but informationally redundant.

**Statistics challenge:** full covariance modelling is domain-specific.

**Engineering challenge:** common-cause structure must remain explicit.

**Disposition:** add a limited disjoint equicorrelation adapter using a design-effect calculation. Overlapping or unidentified dependence returns `CANNOT_CHECK`; the adapter cannot grant truth.

## Decision 7 — provenance must be component-level

**Finding:** a child artifact may inherit representation, method, calibration and evidence from different parents.

**Provenance challenge:** W3C PROV already owns general derivation graphs.

**Scientific challenge:** non-copy edges need semantic transport, and authority cannot increase through lineage.

**Disposition:** implement component-level edges, multi-parent validation and descendant-specific invalidation. Treat standalone C08 as merge-by-default unless it improves protected decisions over general provenance.

## Decision 8 — evaluation is a dynamical system

**Finding:** static scores can rank policy A above B while the distribution induced by A makes A worse.

**Economics challenge:** response functions may depend on strategic expectations, not only retraining.

**ML challenge:** performative stability and performative optimality are not the same.

**Disposition:** formalize static optimum, performative optimum, stable policies and retraining trajectories; preserve cycles as first-class negative results.

## Decision 9 — frontier science needs portfolios

**Finding:** one scalar “interestingness” score rewards novelty laundering and hides incomparable research options.

**Management/OR challenge:** budget and risk constrain portfolios.

**Open-ended-search challenge:** diversity and stepping stones can preserve option value.

**Governance challenge:** opportunity proposal is not agenda adoption.

**Disposition:** enumerate feasible finite portfolios and retain the Pareto frontier. Require falsifiability, downstream decisions and agenda authority.

## Decision 10 — parent ownership remains large

The panel assigns principal ownership as follows:

| Wave 03 object | Strongest owner |
|---|---|
| decision envelope compilation | automata/bisimulation/abstract interpretation |
| information order | Blackwell/Le Cam/statistical decision theory |
| contextual gluing | sheaf/local-global and constraint satisfaction |
| scale consistency | causal abstraction/model reduction |
| dependence correction | statistics/survey/meta-analysis/reliability |
| reticulate lineage | W3C PROV/provenance/phylogenetic networks |
| performative dynamics | economics/performative prediction/mechanism design |
| frontier portfolios | OR/innovation management/multiobjective search |

The only candidate ORION residual is their typed integration under scientific obligations, target-native adaptation, provenance, authority, reopening and honest terminals.

## Open vetoes

1. No proof that the integrated generalization compiler adds value over the strongest parent composition.
2. The finite partition theorem is not yet mechanized.
3. The equicorrelation evidence adapter is too weak for overlapping dependence.
4. Scale maps need richer stochastic and intervention semantics.
5. Reticulate lineage must be reduced directly against PROV constraints and domain extensions.
6. Performative response remains unidentified without intervention/control.
7. Frontier opportunity value requires prospective external expert evaluation.
8. Paper C11 remains merge-by-default.
