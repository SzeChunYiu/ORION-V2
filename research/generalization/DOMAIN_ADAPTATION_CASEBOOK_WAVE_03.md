# Domain Adaptation Casebook — Wave 03

The cases below are research fixtures. A shared generalized position is always context-relative and never semantic identity.

## Case 1 — Management stage gates and engineering verification/validation

### Native management theory

Objects include portfolio items, stages, review boards, evidence packets, budgets, approval authority and kill/hold/proceed decisions.

### Native engineering theory

Objects include requirements, designs, verification methods, validation targets, configuration baselines, hazards and release authority.

### Generalized envelope

`Obligation Process Network`

Shared registered judgment:

```text
terminal obligations discharged and authorized
```

Shared action role:

```text
evaluate evidence against a declared gate
```

### Preserved

- prerequisite obligations;
- evidence requirement;
- resource consumption;
- approval authority;
- completion and recovery paths.

### Not generalized away

- commercial portfolio value;
- engineering requirement semantics;
- regulatory signatory identity;
- domain-specific validation criteria.

### Falsifier

A gate process that is procedurally complete but validates the wrong intended use must not map to engineering `VALIDATED`.

## Case 3 — Quantum resource conversion and scientific evidence conversion

### Native quantum theory

Objects include free states, free operations, convertibility and resource monotones.

### Native scientific-governance theory

Objects include evidence classes, allowed transformations, authority ceilings and non-fungible support dimensions.

### Generalized envelope

`Constrained Conversion System`

Shared role:

```text
determine whether an object may be converted under admitted transformations without increasing a protected monotone
```

### Preserved

- allowed operation class;
- directional convertibility;
- non-increasing monotones;
- certificate of transformation.

### Not generalized away

- Hilbert-space semantics;
- physical entanglement/coherence;
- scientific truth conditions;
- legal or publication authority.

### Falsifier

Three correlated secondary sources cannot be converted into three independent evidential confirmations merely by aggregation.

## Case 5 — Renormalization, MAUP, homogenization and causal abstraction

### Generalized envelope


### Preserved

- abstraction map;
- retained observables;
- intervention/action correspondence;

## Case 9 — Politics and quantum contextuality

This remains an intentionally narrow structural-neighbor case.

Shared role:

```text
locally valid contextual assignments may fail to admit one global assignment
```

What transfers:

- context index;
- local judgments;
- overlap consistency;
- global gluing question;
- obstruction witness.

What does not transfer:

- quantum physical interpretation;
- voter/legislator semantics;
- probability rules;
- empirical identification assumptions.

The valid conclusion is `LOCAL_TO_GLOBAL_OBSTRUCTION_NEIGHBOR`, never semantic identity or physical equivalence.

## Case 10 — Language evolution and recursive framework generations

### Generalized envelope

`Transmission and Semantic-Fidelity System`

Shared role:

```text
track representation transmission across generations while separating learnability, compression, expressivity and semantic fidelity
```

### Preserved

- generation index;
- correspondence;
- retained distinctions;
- mergers and splits;
- learnability/compression measure;
- decision-relevant semantic tests.

### Not generalized away

- human language acquisition;
- cultural selection mechanism;
- software implementation identity.

### Falsifier

A successor representation that is easier to learn but merges a protected scientific distinction must not be called an improvement without reopening.
