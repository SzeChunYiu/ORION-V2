# Conservative Scientific-Theory Adaptation Calculus — Wave 03

**Status:** formal research candidate with transparent finite reference semantics. It grants no scientific truth, novelty, publication authority, framework admission, or target-domain success.

## 1. Why Wave 03 exists

Wave 02 established that a domain theory cannot be imported by lexical or structural resemblance alone. It introduced source-bound theory packages, forward transports, assumption ledgers, resource and authority checks, shared envelopes, and four adapted theory families.

The Wave-03 audit found that forward preservation is necessary but not sufficient.

A transport can preserve every checked source row while still:

- adding new behavior inside the old state image;
- proving a new old-language conclusion that the native theory did not prove;
- losing the complement information needed to update the native theory;
- hiding a native counterexample inside an uninformative set-valued result;
- composing through mismatched epochs;
- accumulating uncertainty without recording it;
- becoming invalid after calibration, evaluator, resource, or authority changes;
- supplying a common envelope with no executable target-native realization;
- calling source-domain success evidence of target-domain success.

Wave 03 therefore treats domain adaptation as a family of **commuting laws and fail-closed certificates**, not one mapping score.

## 4. Formal objects

### 4.1 Native scientific theory

A finite reference theory is represented as

\[
T = (\mathcal S,\mathcal A,\rightarrow,\mathcal J,\mathcal H,
     \mathcal R,\mathcal U,\mathcal E)
\]

where:

- \(\mathcal S\) is a state set;
- \(\mathcal A\) is an action set;
- \(\rightarrow\subseteq\mathcal S\times\mathcal A\times\mathcal S\);
- \(\mathcal J\) is a family of native judgments;
- \(\mathcal H\) is the assumption set;
- \(\mathcal R\) gives typed resource intervals per action;
- \(\mathcal U\) gives authority ceilings per judgment;
- \(\mathcal E\) is the theory epoch.

The finite object is a test instrument, not a claim that all scientific theories are finite-state.

### 4.2 Generalization context

A context is

\[
C=(Q,A_C,L_C,X_C,\epsilon_R)
\]

with:

- registered judgments or queries \(Q\);
- registered actions \(A_C\);
- explicitly allowed lost native judgments \(L_C\);
- required counterexamples \(X_C\);
- resource tolerance \(\epsilon_R\).

The context determines what the transport has earned. A transport valid for \(C\) is not valid for every future question.

### 4.3 Interpretation

A native-to-generalized interpretation contains:

\[
I=(f_S,g_S,f_A,g_A,f_J,H_I,K_I,C,E_I)
\]

where:

- \(f_S\) maps native states to generalized states;
- \(g_S\) is a set-valued reverse map;
- \(f_A\) maps native actions to generalized actions;
- \(g_A\) is a set-valued reverse action map;
- \(f_J\) maps native judgments;
- \(H_I\) types every native assumption;
- \(K_I\) contains resource calibrations and counterexample witnesses;
- \(C\) is the registered context;
- \(E_I\) binds source and generalized epochs.

## 5. Five interpretation grades

### 5.1 Exact interpretation

Requires:

- bijective state and action maps;
- bijective registered judgment map;
- exact round trips;
- forward simulation and backward reflection;
- every native judgment registered;
- exact assumptions and authority;
- exact resources after declared calibration;
- decisive counterexample reflection.

### 5.2 Conservative extension

The generalized theory may add new states, actions, judgments, or language, but the native image is injective and unchanged.

For logical consequence:

\[
\operatorname{Cons}(G)\cap L_T=\operatorname{Cons}(T).
\]

For operational behavior, every generalized transition wholly inside the native image must have a native witness.

### 5.3 Decision-relative adaptation

The transport preserves a declared decision family but may collapse distinctions outside it.

If two native states map to the same generalized state, every lost native judgment must be explicitly listed. A later context that registers one of those judgments expires the transport.

### 5.4 Decision-preserving quotient

This is the structural form of decision-relative adaptation when several native states occupy one generalized state but agree on every registered judgment and behavior.

The quotient is not semantic identity. It is safe only under the registered context.

### 5.5 Sound abstraction

For set-valued outcomes:

\[
J_T(s)\subseteq J_G(f_S(s)).
\]

For deterministic transformers under an abstraction \(\alpha\):

\[
\alpha(F(c))\sqsubseteq F^\sharp(\alpha(c)).
\]

Sound abstraction supports one-sided reasoning. It does not support exact equality, exact counterexample reflection, or bidirectional update unless separately proved.

## 6. Satisfaction law

For a signature morphism \(\sigma:\Sigma\to\Sigma'\), the institution-style condition is

\[
M'\models_{\Sigma'} \operatorname{Sen}(\sigma)(\varphi)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{Mod}(\sigma)(M')\models_{\Sigma}\varphi.
\]

Wave 03 includes a finite checker. It detects a translation whose maps are total and well-typed but whose semantics drift.

This law owns notation-change coherence. ORION cannot claim it.

## 7. Round-trip laws

### 7.1 Native round trip

For every native state \(s\):

\[
s\in g_S(f_S(s)).
\]

For decision-relative maps, every state in the reverse fibre must be equivalent under \(C\).

### 7.2 Generalized-image reflection

For every generalized transition inside the mapped native image, there must be a native transition witness unless the relation is explicitly a sound over-approximation.

### 7.3 Lens-style update laws

The finite parent checker tests:

```text
GETPUT
PUTGET
PUTPUT
```

These laws expose when a generalized view erases the complement needed for coherent update.

The ORION interpretation object does not claim to replace the lens literature. It uses round-trip failure as a transport falsifier.

## 8. Assumption law

Every native assumption receives exactly one treatment:

```text
PRESERVED
CALIBRATED
RELAXED
DROPPED
NOT_APPLICABLE
```

Any non-preserved treatment requires both:

- evidence;
- explicit target revalidation obligations.

An exact or conservative interpretation cannot relax or drop a native assumption.

A complete assumption ledger is necessary but not sufficient: decision preservation must still be checked.

## 9. Resource law

Resources are typed intervals:

\[
r=(d,u,\ell,h)
\]

for dimension \(d\), unit \(u\), lower bound \(\ell\), and upper bound \(h\).

A calibration maps the native interval into generalized units. The generalized interval must conservatively contain the calibrated native interval.

For an interval \([\ell_T,h_T]\) mapped to \([\ell',h']\), validity requires

\[
\ell_G\le \ell'+\epsilon_R,
\qquad
h_G+\epsilon_R\ge h'.
\]

This rejects hidden under-costing and supports explicit unit conversion. It does not infer a lower bound from a Wave-02 upper bound; the migration helper uses \([0,h]\).

## 10. Authority law

For every mapped judgment:

\[
U_G(f_J(j))\le U_T(j).
\]

Exact interpretation additionally requires equality.

Independent target evidence may later support a higher target authority, but the adaptation mapping itself does not provide that evidence.

## 11. Counterexample reflection

A native falsifier must remain addressable.

For exact or conservative interpretation, the generalized value must reproduce the refuting value.

For sound abstraction, non-decisive reflection may retain the refuting value in a set. A decisive native counterexample cannot be hidden inside a set that also contains the accepted value.

This distinction was absent from Wave 02.

## 12. Information-loss law

For every pair of native states merged by \(f_S\), Wave 03 computes which native judgments differ.

The transport fails when:

- a registered judgment differs inside the fibre, unless the transport is a declared sound abstraction;
- an unregistered differing judgment was not explicitly declared lost.

The resulting loss ledger supports a direct expiration rule:

> adding a lost judgment to a later context reopens the transport and all dependent conclusions.

## 14. Composition

A chain

\[
T_1\xrightarrow{I_{12}}T_2\xrightarrow{I_{23}}T_3
\]

may compose only when theory identities and epochs are contiguous and each registered judgment has a complete correspondence.

The composite carries:

\[
\epsilon_{13}=\epsilon_{12}+\epsilon_{23},
\qquad
\lambda_{13}=\lambda_{12}+\lambda_{23},
\qquad
U_{13}=\min(U_{12},U_{23}).
\]

Thus uncertainty and semantic loss accumulate while authority cannot increase.

The composition grade is the weakest link.

## 15. Target-native adaptation

A common envelope does not execute a source method in the target.

The required cospan is:

```text
source native theory ----> generalized envelope <---- target native theory
                                  |
                                  v
                       target realization proposal
```

A target realization must choose target-native states and actions that map back to the intended envelope objects. It additionally requires:

- target calibration;
- target known-answer or hostile validation;
- shared registered generalized decisions;
- exact source, target, and envelope identities.

Its strongest positive terminal is:

```text
READY_FOR_PROTECTED_TARGET_EVALUATION
```

It explicitly does **not** claim target success.
