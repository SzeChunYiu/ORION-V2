# Generative Regime and Invention Addendum — Wave 06 V1

**Status:** cross-cutting interface hypothesis. This addendum does **not** create K7, reopen the frozen V1/V2 parity subject, authorize a new research wave, or claim a novel creativity algorithm. It sharpens existing K0–K6 semantics after a material omission test over sports, music, arts, design, craft and historical invention practice.

## 1. Why this addendum exists

The current ORION-V2 framework already represents concept change, problem/frame reformulation, representation restructuring, method/instrument change, surprise, serendipity, affordances, procedural/tacit competence, distributed cognition and recursive scientific-development operators.

The remaining omission is narrower:

> the framework can represent a changed concept or action, but it does not yet bind the **predecessor possibility space, the transformation that changes that space, the successor possibility space, the validation of newly reachable possibilities, and the later adoption/transmission lineage** as one auditable episode.

This matters for historical invention episodes in science, mathematics, engineering, sport, music, art, design and craft. The transferable object is not the domain content or the fame of an inventor. It is the typed transformation of what could be represented, generated, done, asked or instrumented.

## 2. Generative regime

Define an optional cross-cutting object

```text
GenerativeRegime Gamma_t = (
    representation_language,
    generative_rules,
    operator_repertoire,
    active_constraints,
    problem_portfolio,
    exploratory_value_model,
    traversal_policy,
    affordance_tool_environment
)
```

`Gamma_t` is **not** a universal ontology. A domain may expose only the coordinates required for a registered decision.

Ordinary search holds the regime fixed:

```text
candidate = Search(Gamma_t, problem)
```

A regime-transform proposal changes at least one material coordinate:

```text
mu : Gamma_t -> Gamma_t+1
```

Seed transformation families are:

```text
REPRESENT
ADD_OPERATOR
COMPOSE
EXAPT
CONSTRAINT_CHANGE
TOOL_CONSTRUCT
PROBLEM_POSE
TRAVERSAL_CHANGE
COORDINATION_CHANGE
```

The vocabulary is open-world. These labels are retrieval/evaluation aids, not an exhaustive theory of invention.

## 3. Mutation, invention and innovation are different states

A random or stylistic change is only a **mutation**.

A `CandidateInvention` requires a material non-equivalence relative to a registered decision/context:

```text
Gamma_t !=_D Gamma_t+1
```

A `ValidatedInvention` additionally requires at least one of:

1. **justified reach expansion** — a previously unreachable registered capability/decision becomes reachable;
2. **protected Pareto improvement** — scientific quality/resource performance improves without violating hard integrity/authority gates;
3. **formal necessity** — the predecessor regime cannot express or solve the registered obligation and the successor has a checked witness;
4. **new discriminator access** — a new tool/representation/operator makes previously non-identifiable alternatives distinguishable.

A useful operational reach object is:

```text
Reach_D(Gamma) = set of registered decisions/capabilities reachable
                 under admissible resources, evidence and authority.
```

A regime transform receives no scientific credit merely because it increases novelty, complexity or vocabulary.

`Innovation` is kept separate from invention:

```text
Innovation = ValidatedInvention + adoption/transmission/retention state
```

Adoption can fail for social, institutional, cost, timing or governance reasons. Adoption is therefore not a truth metric, and truth is not sufficient for adoption.

## 4. Invention episode

Candidate record:

```text
InventionEpisode = (
    predecessor_regime_id,
    trigger,
    active_constraints,
    precursor_lineage,
    transformation_operator,
    changed_coordinates,
    candidate_regime_id,
    novelty_reference_epoch,
    functional_discriminator,
    strongest_parent_alternatives,
    predecessor_retention,
    counterexamples,
    validation_receipt,
    resource_delta,
    adoption_state,
    transmission_lineage,
    authority_ceiling
)
```

`novelty_reference_epoch` is mandatory for historical work. A famous invention reconstructed with post-invention knowledge is retrospective explanation, not evidence that a system could have generated the invention prospectively.

Historical datasets should therefore freeze solver-visible knowledge at or before the target epoch and preserve failed, ignored, near-miss and independently rediscovered cases alongside famous successes.

## 5. Problem genesis

Problem reformulation and **problem invention** are related but not identical.

Candidate object:

```text
ProblemGenesisCandidate = (
    motivating_observation_or_tension,
    predecessor_problem_set,
    proposed_problem,
    representation_or_scope_change,
    why_testable,
    candidate_discriminators,
    expected_information_or_option_value,
    required_resources,
    failure_conditions,
    agenda_authority
)
```

Constitutional rule:

```text
ProblemProposal != ProblemAuthority
```

A machine may propose that a new scientific question is worth investigating. It may not silently rewrite the criterion by which its current work is judged or self-grant agenda authority.

## 6. Tool and niche construction

Instrument/tool invention changes what actions or observations are possible:

```text
ToolConstruction:
(Gamma_t, Affordance_t) -> (Gamma_t+1, Affordance_t+1)
```

Candidate value is measured by changed justified reach, such as:

- making a hidden state observable;
- making a discriminating intervention executable;
- separating previously entangled variables;
- reducing a decisive measurement/proof/experiment cost;
- enabling a previously unavailable representation or manipulation.

A new tool cannot validate itself. Calibration, sensitivity, failure modes, provenance and authority remain K3/K6 obligations.

## 7. Cumulative invention lineage

Optional K3 structure:

```text
InnovationLineageGraph = (V_I, E_I)
```

Permitted edge semantics may include:

```text
INHERITED
MODIFIED
RECOMBINED
EXAPTED
INDEPENDENTLY_REDISCOVERED
REJECTED
REINTRODUCED
TRANSMITTED
LOST
```

This separates individual invention from cumulative cultural/scientific development and prevents a framework from crediting a single actor for a transformation that is structurally inherited or recombined.

## 8. K0–K6 ownership map

### K0 — identity / contract / framing

Bind `GenerativeRegimeID`, predecessor identity and `RegimeTransformID` when a regime change affects a registered decision. A changed problem criterion still creates a new comparison identity unless explicit continuity is warranted.

### K1 — plural state

Optionally retain:

- current `GenerativeRegime`;
- alternative/live candidate regimes;
- operator/tool/representation repertoire;
- retired but recoverable predecessor regimes;
- unresolved regime-level blockers.

### K2 — relation / transport

Compare predecessor and successor regimes by registered relation type:

- preserved capabilities;
- newly reachable capabilities;
- lost capabilities;
- context-specific equivalence;
- translation/representation loss;
- correspondence conditions across regime epochs.

### K3 — evidence / provenance / dependence

Bind precursor dependence, recombination, independent rediscovery, lineage, teacher/community/institutional influence and adoption evidence. Historical popularity or later impact is not independent validation of the original scientific transformation.

### K4 — action / diagnosis / recovery

Add `TRANSFORM_REGIME` only as an umbrella action proposal. Parent-owned subactions may change representation, operator repertoire, tool/environment, traversal policy, coordination or problem portfolio.

The controller must diagnose predecessor insufficiency before treating a regime transform as warranted. False formalism/tool/problem invention remains over-escalation.

### K5 — frontier / opportunity / exploration

Permit open-ended repertoire growth and stepping-stone retention as optional frontier state. Novelty, diversity and learnability may guide proposal generation but do not establish truth, utility or agenda authority.

Candidate optional object:

```text
SteppingStoneArchive = {
    regime_or_artifact,
    behavior/structure descriptor,
    current utility,
    unresolved future-option value,
    lineage,
    retention_reason,
    expiry/review condition
}
```

### K6 — evaluation / saturation / authority

Invention claims require time-sliced or genuinely prospective evaluation, strongest-parent comparisons, negative/near-miss cases and explicit invention-versus-adoption separation.

No benchmark may define success as similarity to a famous historical answer alone. Functional validity, predecessor insufficiency and independent discrimination must be scored separately from historical recognition.

## 9. Parent ownership and strongest-null requirement

Direct parent families include:

- computational creativity and transformational conceptual-space models;
- novelty search and non-objective search;
- quality-diversity / repertoire illumination;
- open-ended learning/evolution;
- design/problem-framing and scientific-instrumentation research;
- cumulative cultural evolution, recombination and exaptation;
- domain-native motor-skill, music, art/design and craft learning.

These remain parent algorithms/theories. ORION receives credit only for a protected residual in cross-transition scientific control.

Useful parent anchors for the omission test include:

- G. A. Wiggins (2006), “A preliminary framework for description, analysis and comparison of creative systems,” *Knowledge-Based Systems* 19(7):449–458, doi:10.1016/j.knosys.2006.04.009.

- J.-B. Mouret and J. Clune (2015), “Illuminating search spaces by mapping elites,” arXiv:1504.04909.

## 10. Scientificity / kill conditions

The addendum contracts back into the existing concept/action interfaces if any of the following holds:

1. `GenerativeRegime` adds no protected decision beyond existing `ProblemFrame` + concept state + action repertoire + resources/affordances;
2. mature creativity/open-ended/QD/design parents reproduce every useful transform decision at lower complexity;
3. time-sliced invention performance disappears once post-event leakage and fame/survivorship bias are controlled;
4. synthetic gains reflect benchmark vocabulary rather than genuinely changed reach;
5. regime mutation increases novelty but not validated reach, discrimination, formal necessity or resource-adjusted quality.
