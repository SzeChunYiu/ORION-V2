# Epistemic Locality, Perspective Plurality and Diverse Adaptive Intelligence — Wave 06 V1

**Status:** cross-cutting framework hardening after the 2026-08-30 non-anthropocentric/overview omission finding. This addendum does **not** create K7, equate evolution with cognition, grant natural processes normative authority, or claim a universal definition of intelligence. It strengthens the existing K0–K6 interfaces and the machine-native programme.

## 1. Core correction

Human cognition is one realized solution in a much larger space of adaptive information-processing and problem-solving systems. Human scientific knowledge remains a uniquely rich donor corpus, hostile control and collaboration boundary, but it is not treated as a specification of globally optimal intelligence.

The corresponding constitutional rule is:

> **Never universalize a locally successful intelligence, method or epistemic practice outside the substrate, environment, task family, scale, timescale, resource regime and criterion under which its competence was established.**

This is the **Epistemic Locality Principle**.

## 2. Do not collapse distinct adaptive processes

The framework distinguishes at least:

```text
COGNITION
WITHIN_LIFETIME_LEARNING
DEVELOPMENTAL_ADAPTATION
COLLECTIVE_SOCIAL_ADAPTATION
CULTURAL_CUMULATIVE_ADAPTATION
EVOLUTIONARY_ADAPTATION
MACHINE_ARCHITECTURAL_OR_POLICY_ADAPTATION
```

These processes can share abstract mechanisms such as variation, memory/retention, selection, feedback, communication, recombination, exploration or niche construction. Shared structure does **not** establish identical mechanisms, agency, foresight, consciousness or epistemic authority.

In particular:

```text
EVOLUTIONARY_ADAPTATION != COGNITIVE_REASONING
FITNESS != TRUTH
SURVIVAL != SCIENTIFIC_VALIDITY
NATURAL_SUCCESS != NORMATIVE_AUTHORITY
```

Evolution and ecology are donors of mechanisms and counterexamples, not sources of scientific or ethical authority.

## 3. Context-relative competence

A scalar claim such as `Intelligence(System) = c` is not admitted without a declared reference distribution and criterion.

Use a contextual competence object:

```text
Competence(System | Environment, TaskFamily, Timescale,
                   Resources, Boundary, Substrate, Criterion)
```

When a scalar is scientifically justified, bind its reference distribution explicitly:

```text
I_mu,Q(System) = E_(environment,task ~ mu)[ Q(System, environment, task) ]
```

Changing `mu`, `Q`, resources, boundary or timescale may change the ranking. Such a reversal is not automatically inconsistency; it can be evidence that the original superiority claim was local.

## 4. Adaptive-system profile

Before asserting intelligence, describe the relevant adaptive organization operationally:

```text
AdaptiveSystemProfile = (
    candidate_unit,
    system_boundary,
    substrate_or_embodiment,
    environment_or_niche,
    observable_channels,
    internal_or_distributed_state,
    action_or_response_repertoire,
    memory_or_hereditary_retention,
    update_or_adaptation_mechanism,
    communication_or_coupling_topology,
    objective_viability_or_selection_pressure,
    adaptation_timescale,
    resources
)
```

This is deliberately non-mentalistic. It can describe a human, an animal, a colony, a scientific community, an evolutionary lineage or an artificial system without asserting that all are minds.

## 5. Perspective frame and the overview problem

A scientific conclusion is often conditional on an implicit point of view. Make that point of view explicit when material:

```text
PerspectiveFrame = (
    system_boundary,
    agent_or_organization_scale,
    spatial_scale,
    temporal_scale,
    environment,
    representation,
    objective_or_question,
    observer_or_intervention_position,
    evaluation_frame
)
```

A perspective transition is:

```text
Pi : PerspectiveFrame_t -> PerspectiveFrame_t+1
```

Candidate parent-owned actions include:

```text
CHANGE_BOUNDARY
CHANGE_AGENT_SCALE
CHANGE_SPATIAL_SCALE
CHANGE_TEMPORAL_SCALE
CHANGE_ENVIRONMENT_REFERENCE
CHANGE_OBSERVER_POSITION
CHANGE_EVALUATION_FRAME
```

These are not automatically improvements. They are reframing actions whose value depends on a registered obligation.

## 6. Perspective dependence

For a registered judgment `J` over episode `E`, evaluate materially different admissible perspective frames when the decision can depend on boundary or scale:

```text
Omega(E) = { J(E | P_1), ..., J(E | P_k) }
```

If two warranted frames produce different judgments,

```text
exists P_i, P_j : J(E | P_i) != J(E | P_j)
```

create:

```text
PERSPECTIVE_DEPENDENCE
```

This does not mean both judgments are globally true or that all viewpoints are equivalent. It means the claim must expose its valid frame or establish a lawful transport relation between frames.

## 7. Method ecology

Methods should not be represented as globally ordered tools. Candidate optional object:

```text
MethodEcologyCard = (
    method_identity,
    native_problem_family,
    environment_class,
    system_boundary,
    substrate_or_interface_requirements,
    timescale,
    resource_regime,
    assumptions,
    evidence_requirements,
    known_failure_modes,
    transport_conditions,
    strongest_competitors
)
```

This turns the question from:

```text
Which method is best?
```

to:

```text
Under which ecology is this method sufficient, dominant, fragile or inapplicable?
```

Method choice remains parent-owned by metareasoning, algorithm selection, ecological rationality, decision theory and domain-native practice. ORION receives credit only if explicit ecological binding changes protected scientific decisions beyond those parents.

## 8. Multi-clock adaptation

Scientific and intelligent systems can adapt on different clocks:

```text
A0 = within-episode state/action adaptation
A1 = within-agent learning
A2 = developmental adaptation
A3 = social/collective adaptation
A4 = cultural cumulative adaptation
A5 = evolutionary adaptation across generations
A6 = machine architecture/search-space/policy adaptation
```

No total ordering of these channels is assumed. A faster clock is not automatically more intelligent; a slower process is not automatically more globally valid.

Cross-clock transfer requires an explicit relation receipt because similar patterns can arise through different causal mechanisms.

## 9. Diverse donor families

The donor universe should include, when scientifically relevant:

- human cognition and scientific practice;
- non-human individual cognition and embodied adaptation;
- animal collective decision and distributed coordination;
- social/cultural cumulative learning;
- evolutionary variation/selection/recombination/exaptation;
- ecological niche construction and environment–strategy fit;
- machine-native search, memory, simulation, representation and architecture change;
- hybrid human–machine and multi-agent systems.

The framework compares **mechanisms and structural distinctions**, not species prestige or anthropomorphic similarity.

## 10. K0–K6 ownership

### K0 — identity, contract and framing

When decision-relevant, bind boundary, scale, timescale, environment/reference distribution, substrate/interface and evaluation criterion. A claim whose reference frame changes cannot silently inherit global validity.

### K1 — plural state

Permit multiple live `PerspectiveFrame`s, contextual competence claims and alternative system boundaries when they change a registered decision.

### K2 — relation and transport

Extend relation requests to cross-boundary, cross-scale, cross-timescale and cross-substrate transport. Similar behavior does not imply identical mechanism or transferable competence.

### K3 — evidence, provenance and dependence

Record whether evidence arises from individual learning, collective coordination, cultural transmission, evolutionary history, simulation or machine adaptation when the distinction changes evidential force or independence.

### K4 — action and diagnosis

Permit perspective/boundary/scale changes as parent-owned reframing actions. A local failure does not justify changing scale unless the current frame is witnessed insufficient or a discriminator exists at another scale.

### K5 — frontier and exploration

Frontier search may propose alternative units, scales, environments or adaptation channels, not only new hypotheses or methods. Overview proposals remain opportunities, not evidence.

### K6 — evaluation and bounded saturation

Expand omission testing over at least:

```text
IntelligenceOrAdaptiveForm × Substrate × AgentScale ×
AdaptationTimescale × Environment × TaskFamily × Criterion
```

The Cartesian product is not enumerated. Saturation receipts must expose sampled strata, omitted strata and reopen conditions.

## 12. Scientificity and kill conditions

This addendum contracts if:

1. explicit perspective/boundary/scale state changes no protected decision beyond existing `ProblemFrame`, context and relation interfaces;
2. ordinary context-conditioned metareasoning or algorithm selection reproduces every useful method-ecology decision at lower complexity;
3. comparative donor analysis collapses into superficial analogy once native mechanisms are restored;
4. perspective plurality only adds descriptive metadata without reducing false universalization, false transfer or incorrect intervention;
5. a claimed cross-species/substrate principle fails on held-out systems or depends on anthropomorphic labels.
