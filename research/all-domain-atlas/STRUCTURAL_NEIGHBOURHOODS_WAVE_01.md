# Cross-Domain Structural Neighbourhoods — Wave 01

**Status:** broad first synthesis; not all-domain saturation and not novelty authority.  
**Purpose:** reduce remote-domain findings into testable structural neighbourhoods that can change the V2 solver, theory, Jump policy and paper programme.

## Review cell

- formal methods and mathematics;
- statistics, causal inference, measurement and decision theory;
- engineering, operations, management and safety;
- political science, economics, sociology, law and institutions;
- linguistics, history, archaeology and humanities;
- cognitive science, design and scientific discovery;
- biology, ecology, medicine and complex systems;
- science-of-science and autonomous-science evaluation.

For each neighbourhood, the native-domain reviewer protects distinctions that a cross-domain abstraction might erase. The hostile reviewer asks whether one mature parent or a compatible donor product already subsumes the proposed V2 object.

# N01 — Cross-generation comparability

## Remote parents

- political ideal-point linking across chambers/time;
- psychometric test linking, equating and measurement invariance;
- metrological calibration and traceability;
- diachronic semantic change;
- causal transport and population shift;
- ontology/schema evolution;
- scientific software/model versioning.

## Shared structural problem

An object, score, coordinate, claim or certificate is meaningful in regime `G_t`. After transition to `G_t+1`, persistent identity alone does not prove that the old and new objects have the same interpretation, scale or decision meaning.

## Candidate relation

`Comparable(z_t, z_t+1 | anchors, invariance, context, epoch, tolerance)`

with possible terminals:

- exact comparable;
- linked with quantified uncertainty;
- partially comparable;
- non-comparable;
- `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## V1 pressure

P6/P7/P13 already own certificate lifting, regime transport and responsibility-relative reuse. V2 cannot relabel them. The open question is whether measurement/linking parents add anchor, invariance and uncertainty structure not explicit in V1.

## Machine addition candidate

`ComparabilityCertificate.v0` containing source/target regimes, anchors, correspondence, invariant tests, scale transformation, uncertainty propagation, context applicability, lost information, affected commitments and expiration conditions.

## Exact tests

- same identifier but changed construct;
- linked forms with valid anchors;
- anchor drift;
- evidence content preserved but closure meaning changed;
- valid current score mapping that fails a future responsibility.

## Current disposition

`HIGH_PRIORITY__PARTIAL_V1_OWNERSHIP__PARENT_REDUCTION_OPEN`

# N02 — Context-relative structural position

## Remote parents

- structural and regular equivalence in network science;
- bisimulation and modal equivalence;
- contextual/observational equivalence in programming semantics;
- electrical/network interface equivalence;
- computational-mechanics causal states;
- state-space realization equivalence;
- role/function/behaviour/structure design representations.

## Shared structural problem

Entities with unrelated meanings may occupy the same relational or behavioural role under a declared interface. Conversely, identical terminology may hide different transition behaviour.

## Candidate object

`Relation(K1, K2 | C)` where context `C` declares allowed probes, interventions, decisions, resources, target consequences, constitution, tolerance and epoch.

## V1 pressure

P3/P7/P9 contain representation, gluing and typed structural learning. Any V2 relation family must bind exact V1 ownership and strongest parent decision procedures.

## Machine addition candidate

Plural relation receipts rather than one embedding distance: isomorphism, role equivalence, behavioural equivalence, interface equivalence, prediction, decision dominance, safe quotient, incomparability.

## Exact tests

- different vocabulary / same role;
- same vocabulary / non-bisimilar dynamics;
- equivalent at terminal interface but different internally;
- predictive equivalence that breaks under intervention;
- context change expiring an old equivalence receipt.

## Current disposition

`FOUNDATIONAL_THEORY_CANDIDATE__NO_SINGLE_METRIC`

# N03 — Indiscernibility, non-identifiability and inverse non-uniqueness

## Remote parents

- causal Markov equivalence;
- statistical identification and experiment comparison;
- demography age-period-cohort identification;
- inverse problems in geophysics, imaging and hydrology;
- rough-set indiscernibility;
- formal learning/query identifiability;
- observational equivalence in system identification.

## Shared structural problem

Multiple latent worlds induce the same observations under the current probe family. This may be temporary missing evidence, structural non-identifiability, approximate non-uniqueness or censorship.

## Distinctions V2 must preserve

- route unavailable;
- data absent but obtainable;
- insufficient sample/precision;
- same-information-family exhausted;
- genuinely non-identifiable under registered probes;
- distinguishable through a new intervention;
- redundant representation of one state.

## V1 pressure

P1/P2/P9 already contain failure-attribution impossibility, open-world closure and interventional diagnosis. V2 should add only the parent-faithful classification and machine witness not already owned.

## Machine addition candidate

`IdentificationStatus.v0` with live equivalence classes, registered probes, observed/censored status, minimum distinguishing probes, impossibility witness and required new information type.

## Exact tests

- all current observations equal but intervention differs;
- censored route must not yield non-identifiability;
- exact rank/constraint deficiency;
- additional samples of same type cannot help;
- minimum separating probe set.

## Current disposition

`HIGH_PRIORITY_SOLVER_TERMINAL_AND_ACTION_CONTROL`

- contextuality;
- database join/consistency;
- constraint satisfaction;
- distributed configuration/invariant composition;
- model integration and plural perspectives;
- legal/institutional jurisdiction conflicts.

## Shared structural problem

Every local description or pairwise overlap may be admissible while no global object satisfies the complete compatibility diagram.

## V1 ownership

P3 and `RECONSTRUCT.GLUE` already own a bounded compatibility/obstruction treatment and an exact parity counterexample. This is known lineage, not a new V2 discovery.

## V2 extension questions

- how context-relative equivalence affects overlap maps;
- how uncertainty/approximation changes obstruction judgments;
- how authority and provenance constrain gluing;
- when multiple non-isomorphic global portraits should remain plural;
- how local solver branches are recomposed.

## Exact tests

Three binary contexts with pairwise satisfiability and globally inconsistent XOR constraints; approximate mappings with threshold-dependent outcomes; authority-compatible content that is jurisdictionally non-composable.

## Current disposition

`PRESERVE_V1_AS_SPECIAL_CASE__EXTENSION_OPEN`

# N07 — Dependence-aware evidence and validator diversity

## Remote parents

- dependent-effect meta-analysis;
- graphical models and common-cause reliability;
- epidemiological interference;
- survey design effects;
- ensemble error correlation;
- Byzantine/common-mode systems;
- sociology of diffusion and copied testimony;
- multi-instrument measurement.

## Shared structural problem

Several sources, models, reviewers or experiments may share data, training, instrumentation, incentives or lineage. Their count does not equal independent evidence.

## Machine addition candidate

`EvidenceDependenceGraph.v0` and `ValidationDiversityReceipt.v0` containing lineage, common causes, model/data/instrument overlap, intervention dependencies, effective information and unidentified dependence.

## V1 pressure

P4/P8/P15 already separate evidence, authority and execution. V2 must establish incremental calibration or laundering prevention beyond provenance and standard dependence models.

## Exact tests

- three copied sources versus three independent measurements;
- three agents using the same model/data;
- independent instruments with shared calibration failure;
- correlated reviewers unanimously wrong;
- over-conservative dependence model rejecting useful independent support.

## Current disposition

`HIGH_PRIORITY_PAPER_CANDIDATE__IDENTIFICATION_RISK`

# N08 — Reticulate provenance and multi-parent inheritance

## Remote parents

- W3C PROV and process provenance;
- version-control and build dependency graphs;
- software bills of materials;
- data/model lineage;
- stemmatological contamination;
- phylogenetic networks and horizontal transfer;
- data fusion and composite scientific artifacts.

## Shared structural problem

A result or framework revision may inherit representation, code, data, semantics, instruments, evaluator and authority from different parents. Tree/commit ancestry can be insufficient for deciding which commitments remain valid.

## Machine addition candidate

Component-level typed inheritance graph with contribution kind, correspondence, applicability, authority and affected-certificate propagation.

## Exact tests

- code from parent A, data from B, semantics from C, evaluator from D;

- contamination where no single parent explains the child.

## V1 pressure

V1 already uses source projections, dependency graphs and provenance. The paper survives only if reticulate typing changes transport/reopen decisions beyond a general provenance graph.

- Shapley/attribution methods;
- socio-technical accident analysis;

## Shared structural problem

- failure only when two individually valid components interact;

# Wave-level deductions

## D1 — One knowledge space is insufficient

The same pair of structures may be near under role, tied under observation, different under intervention, directionally ordered for a decision, safely quotiented for one target and unsafe for another. V2 requires multiple typed relations with context and expiration.

## D2 — The solver state needs new coordinates

At minimum the research schemas must expose:

- live hypothesis/model portfolio;
- probe/intervention and identifiability state;
- dependence graph;
- representation/scale/epoch stack;
- capacity and queue state;
- responsibility topology;
- validation versus authority state;
- change and correspondence obligations;
- option/diversity value;
- strategic-response state.

## D3 — Jump is a subpolicy, not a root action

Jump should be considered only after a lower-level insufficiency discriminator. It must return the minimum sufficient escalation or an honest refusal.

## D4 — Many generic mechanics are parent-owned

Optimization, workflow, process control, diagnosis, experiment design, role equivalence, abstraction, provenance and analogy all have mature owners. The V2 residual, if any, is their scientific integration and preservation/authority interface.

## D5 — Paper programme should contract aggressively

Strongest candidates after Wave 01 are:

1. context-relative structural knowledge space;
2. cross-generation comparability;
3. obligation-driven solver with V1 parity;
4. witnessed Jump/minimum-level escalation;
5. dependence-aware scientific evidence;
6. frontier opportunity discovery.

Reticulate provenance and machine donor reduction remain valuable but face high subsumption risk. Performative evaluation is strong but may belong to established mechanism-design/performative-prediction parents unless the scientific-authority integration separates.
