# Human Epistemics Framework Reduction V1

**Purpose:** reduce issue #40's philosophy, cognition, non-Western/plural-epistemology and lived-practice findings against the contracted K0–K6 architecture before adding any new component.

**Decision:** material omissions were found, but they currently change interfaces, state semantics and benchmarks rather than justify K7/K8.

## 1. The central correction

The pre-reopen architecture was strong on **custody of explicit scientific commitments**. It was weaker on how a research system:

- notices that its current problem framing may be wrong;
- estimates the reliability of its own reasoning/control process;
- turns failure into a transferable lesson rather than a log entry;
- represents competence that is not exhausted by text/propositions;
- treats cognition distributed across agents, humans, tools, instruments and artifacts;
- notices and follows scientifically useful surprises outside the requested path;
- preserves exploration when short-run exploitation is effective;
- compares knowledge practices that use different epistemic access modes and source authorities.

The correction is **not** to imitate a human mind or add consciousness. It is to make these scientific-control failures representable and testable.

## 2. No-new-kernel reduction

### K0 — Identity, contract and receipt boundary

Absorb:

- `ProblemFrame` distinct from immutable comparison/criterion identity;
- source-mode identity (paper, oral testimony, demonstration, artifact, environmental observation, incident record, etc.);
- epistemic-access mode and custodial/permission identity where material;
- explicit identity when a local checklist/procedure adapts a canonical one.

New invariant:

> Legitimate reframing may change the scientific problem; it may not silently preserve the old comparison identity when the success criterion or decision semantics changed.

### K1 — Plural scientific state

Expand state forms beyond claims/models:

- declarative/propositional;
- causal/mechanistic;
- procedural/policy;
- perceptual/state-recognition;
- tacit/skill dependency markers;
- social/testimonial;
- institutional/authority;
- negative/failure knowledge;
- current `ProblemFrame` and representation epoch;
- calibrated `InquirySelfModel` working state;
- unresolved anomalies and serendipity candidates.

Do **not** treat every possible form as a mandatory field in every episode.

New invariant:

> A representation that preserves statements but destroys a protected competence, perceptual discriminator or authority condition is not fidelity-preserving for that context.

### K2 — Relation and transport

Add optional registered dimensions:

- knowledge form being transported;
- source/acquisition mode;
- demonstrated competence range;
- environmental/tool affordances;
- translation/interpretation loss;
- standpoint/context binding;
- observation/concept/language separation where the native epistemic practice requires it.

New hostile control:

> `TEXT_EQUIVALENT` does not imply `PROCEDURALLY_EQUIVALENT`, `PERCEPTUALLY_EQUIVALENT` or `AUTHORITY_EQUIVALENT`.

### K3 — Evidence, dependence, provenance and revalidation

Absorb:

- oral/community source identities and custody;
- demonstration/teacher dependence;
- dependence induced by shared model, prompt, source tradition, training corpus or evaluator culture;
- `TacitDependency` when competence exceeds explicit representation;
- `DistributedCognitiveEpisode` topology across people/tools/artifacts;
- `CriticismReceipt` and criticism uptake;
- observer/intervention coupling when evidence collection changes the system.

New invariant:

> Self-critique generated from the same model/context is dependent evidence unless a validated independence argument says otherwise.

### K4 — Action, execution, diagnosis and recovery

Absorb:

- `InquirySelfModel` as a fallible learned predictor used for control allocation;
- `FailureLesson` as expected/observed state + attribution + discriminator + repair + transfer scope;
- explicit problem/representation-restructuring proposal action;
- attentional capture for typed unexpected observations;
- offline/incubation proposal generation, with no evidence authority until externalized and tested;
- procedural competence/recovery actions;
- teaching/demonstration acquisition routes.

Candidate failure classes for experiments, not yet universal kernel enums:

- `SELF_MODEL_MISCALIBRATION`;
- `FRAMING_LOCK_IN`;
- `INSTRUCTION_COMPETENCE_CONFUSION`;
- `PLAN_REALITY_CONFUSION`;
- `DISTRIBUTED_STATE_LOSS`;
- `TACIT_INTERFACE_LOSS`;
- `DEGENERATING_REPAIR_LOOP`;
- `METHOD_MONOCULTURE`.

New invariant:

> Introspection/reflection may select a new action; it cannot by itself discharge an evidence or authority obligation about its own correctness.

### K5 — Frontier, opportunity and escalation

Absorb:

- typed surprise vector;
- `SerendipityCandidate` = unexpected encounter + recognized cross-problem value + discriminator;
- exploration budget and learning-progress/information-gain signals;
- problem-finding/reframing opportunity sources;
- plural model/method portfolios as a defense against exploitation collapse;
- encounter buffer for off-query anomalies/failed experiments with structured residuals.

Candidate failure classes:

- `SURPRISE_SUPPRESSION`;
- `NOISE_CURIOSITY_TRAP`;
- `EXPLORATION_COLLAPSE`.

New invariant:

> Novelty, surprise and curiosity are proposal-selection signals, never scientific support or agenda authority.

### K6 — Evaluation, parity, saturation and authority

Replace discipline-only saturation intuition with a declared tensor:

`Coverage = Discipline × SourceMode × KnowledgeForm × AcquisitionMode × Context × HistoricalEpoch × EpistemicPractice`.

Not every cross-product cell must be enumerated literally. The receipt must state the sampled basis, omitted/censored regions and rationale, then run changed-vocabulary passes designed to discover material new coordinates.

Add longitudinal programme evaluation:

- prospective predictions/obligations versus post-hoc accommodation;
- exploration/exploitation balance;
- recurrence after lessons learned;
- criticism uptake;
- calibration of self-model and external-review triggers;
- method diversity where parent uncertainty remains.

New invariant:

> No finite review can establish saturation of “all human civilization.” The strongest valid claim is bounded saturation over an explicit and adversarially varied coverage universe.

## 3. Reflexivity without anthropomorphism

A scientific machine needs a model of its inquiry process; it does not need a metaphysical self.

Define a working object:

`InquirySelfModel_t = P(outcome, method_adequacy, failure_class, value_of_more_compute, value_of_external_review | episode_features, history, domain, source_mode, epoch)`.

Requirements:

1. predictions are frozen before the outcome/evaluator is observed;
2. calibration is evaluated on held-out/delayed/independent outcomes;
3. self-model uncertainty is explicit;
4. the model's training/evaluator dependencies are tracked;
5. confidence does not grant authority;
6. poor calibration can trigger external review or a different method;
7. expiry/revalidation occurs after major model/representation/domain changes.

This creates an executable answer to “does the framework think about how it thinks?”: **yes, only as a fallible, externally calibrated control model whose own claims remain subject to the same epistemic rules.**

## 4. Failure as a lesson

A raw failure receipt is historical evidence. A transferable lesson requires a second object:

`FailureLesson = (expected, observed, reproduction_identity, candidate_causes, discriminators, selected_attribution, confidence, correction, regression_check, transfer_scope, counterexample, authority)`.

A lesson stays provisional when attribution is not identifiable. Future application requires a relation check between the original context and target context.

This prevents two common pathologies:

- replaying the same mistake without retrieval/transfer;
- overgeneralizing one memorable failure into a universal rule.

## 5. Surprise and serendipity

Use a vector rather than one novelty score:

`Surprise = (predictive, semantic, causal, source, evaluator, model_class, value, state_transition)`.

A surprise event may create:

- an anomaly obligation;
- a model inadequacy hypothesis;
- a source-quality warning;
- a representation-change proposal;
- a serendipity candidate linked to another open problem;
- no action, if it is diagnosed as irrelevant/noise under bounded evidence.

Serendipity is therefore modeled as **encounter generation + recognition + follow-up**, not random novelty maximization.

## 6. Practical/tacit knowledge

Do not create a hidden “tacit truth” store. The framework can only represent operational consequences:

- explicit instructions are known to be incomplete for a task family;
- demonstrations/intermediate states carry additional predictive information;
- performance transfers over some contexts but not others;
- a handoff loses competence despite preserved text;
- a human/tool/artifact is an indispensable part of the distributed cognitive episode.

`TacitDependency` is a warning about representational sufficiency, not an excuse to avoid explanation.

## 7. Plural epistemic practices

The non-Western/plural pass should change the coverage method, not create exotic named modules. Preserve native distinctions such as:

- different epistemic access modes (`pramāṇa`-style source/access typing rather than one undifferentiated “evidence”);
- observation versus conceptual construction versus linguistic report;
- evaluator-instrument grounding regress and bounded external roots;
- perspectival/context-qualified assertions;
- premise-status auditing in demonstration;
- oral/community source identity and lineage;
- custodianship, permission and translation loss in local/Indigenous knowledge.

Create an **EpistemicPracticeAtlas** research artifact whose unit is a whole practice, not a decontextualized quote. Its purpose is adversarial coverage and collision testing, not a universal ranking of traditions.

## 8. Machine-learning consequences

Machine Epistemics should eventually alter learning itself through falsifiable hypotheses:

### ML-H1 — Calibrated self-learning

Train self-models to predict error/method inadequacy/external-review value. Evaluate task accuracy separately from metacognitive calibration and review-trigger utility.

### ML-H2 — Typed failure replay

Prioritize high-confidence causal lessons and uncertainty-aware counterexamples rather than high-loss examples alone.

### ML-H3 — Vector surprise and encounter buffers

Compare scalar novelty, prediction error, information gain, learning progress and typed surprise on discovery/false-distraction trade-offs.

### ML-H4 — Procedural/demonstration learning

Compare text-only policies with demonstrations/intermediate state cues/recovery episodes under context shifts.

### ML-H5 — Affordance-conditioned control

Condition actions on actual resources/tools/access/authority rather than abstract action labels.

### ML-H6 — Distributed-system learning

Evaluate and optimize the human–model–tool–memory system as the unit when information is distributed.

### ML-H7 — Representation restructuring actions

Let the system propose new latent variables/decompositions/objectives; accept only when protected downstream obligations improve and old decisions are preserved/reopened correctly.

### ML-H8 — Exploration preservation

Test explicit exploration budgets, plural model portfolios and encounter channels against long-run opportunity discovery and short-run cost.

### ML-H9 — Observer-aware learning

Model cases where measurement, benchmark publication or deployment changes the environment/evaluator.

None of these hypotheses currently earns a new algorithmic novelty claim.

## 9. Paper propagation

### P-A

Broaden donor search from `literature` to `source ecology`, but keep scientific novelty claims anchored to appropriate scholarly/native parents. Add structural donors in manuals, incident records and practice only where their relation is source-bound and target-validated.

### P-B

Add knowledge-form/context dimensions. Make `propositional equivalence != procedural/perceptual/authority equivalence` a primary hostile family.

### P-C

This is the largest change: self-model calibration, failure lessons, surprise capture, problem reframing, representation restructuring and exploration allocation become protected task families. Naive repeated reflection becomes a weak baseline, not the method.

### P-D

Add distributed cognition, testimony/teacher dependence, criticism uptake and observer coupling to dependence/evaluator studies.

### P-E

Reframe opportunity generation around abduction/problem-finding and serendipity mechanisms. Add encounter buffers and prospective value recognition. P-E remains contingent and merges into P-C if no independent prospective value survives.

### Flagship

Replace generic “philosophy/social epistemology” with an explicit but compressed genealogy and one conceptual section: **scientific control must govern not only claims, but also framing, self-monitoring, practical competence, surprise and distributed inquiry.** The paper must remain a 3,000–4,000-word NMI Perspective, so most donor detail stays in supplementary/research artifacts.

## 10. Proposed foundation-paper organizing device

Do not grow the flagship to a 20-axis ontology. Use four public-facing control questions:

1. **What does the system know, and in what form?** — plural state/source/access.
2. **How may it change what it knows or how it frames the problem?** — action, inference, representation, transport.
3. **How does it know when its own process is failing?** — calibrated self-model, counter-probes, criticism, failure lessons.
4. **When may it stop, escalate or open a new problem?** — exploration, serendipity, saturation, authority.

Map K0–K6 underneath these questions rather than exposing the entire internal architecture in the opening pages.

## 11. Falsifiers for this reduction

The human/practice extension should contract if:

- all new benchmark decisions are reproduced by existing K0–K6 without any interface change;
- self-models add no calibrated review/action value over simple uncertainty heuristics;
- procedural/tacit markers do not predict transfer failure beyond ordinary context features;
- surprise/serendipity machinery only increases distraction;
- distributed-cognition representation adds logging but no decision value;
- expanded source modes add no material donor or failure coordinates in repeated passes.
