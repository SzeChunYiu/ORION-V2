# Non-Western and Plural Epistemologies — Omission Pass V1

**Status:** material omission pass under issue #40. This document corrects Western-canon bias in the philosophy/cognition reading programme. No tradition below is reduced to one slogan, treated as homogeneous, or granted scientific authority merely by cultural origin.

## Why this pass is required

A programme that claims to search human knowledge cannot use Western analytic/scientific categories as the invisible universal coordinate system. Several major traditions organize knowledge around different source taxonomies, relations between knowledge and practice, concept/perception boundaries, testimony/community, and perspectival constraints.

The correct method is **native reconstruction first**. Translation into Machine Epistemics occurs only after identifying what the native tradition is actually trying to explain.

---

# 1. Classical Indian epistemology — knowledge sources are themselves objects of theory

Classical Indian epistemological traditions debate `pramāṇa`: reliable sources/instruments of cognition. Major candidates include perception, inference and testimony, with some schools also defending comparison, non-perception, presumption and other sources. Logic is developed as part of the theory of how knowledge arises rather than as an isolated symbolic calculus.

## Structural donation

Machine Epistemics should not begin with one undifferentiated `evidence` type. A source item should bind its **mode of epistemic access**:

- direct observation/perception;
- inference/derivation;
- testimony;
- comparison/analogy;
- absence/non-observation;
- presumption/abduction;
- demonstrated procedure.

These modes have different failure conditions and should not be freely converted into one another.

Candidate machine object:

`EpistemicAccessReceipt = (source_mode, object, warrant, defeaters, dependence, scope, authority)`.

This is a parent threat to any V2 claim that typed evidence sources are novel.

---

# 2. Buddhist pramāṇa traditions — perception, inference and conceptual distortion

Dignāga/Dharmakīrti traditions sharply distinguish non-conceptual perception from conceptual/inferential cognition and analyze how concepts/language relate to particulars. Later Buddhist epistemologies extensively debate reliable cognition, error and language.

## Structural donation

Do not assume that the machine's conceptual representation is identical to the world it tracks. Preserve a distinction between:

- `observation/native signal`;
- `conceptual category`;
- `linguistic description`;
- `inferred structure`.

A semantic mapping can therefore fail even when the underlying observation is sound.

This strengthens P-B's source-projection and representation-transport requirements.

## Self-model implication

Buddhist not-self traditions also provide a hostile philosophical alternative to treating a persistent `SELF` as a primitive substance. For ORION, this supports the conservative engineering choice: model a changing bundle/process of capabilities, memories, policies and self-estimates without claiming a metaphysically unitary self.

No Buddhist doctrine is translated directly into an ML theorem; the donation is the pressure against unjustified reification.

# 3. Nāgārjuna and epistemic regress — how are the instruments themselves established?

Nāgārjuna's epistemological discussions raise a reflexive problem: if objects are established by epistemic instruments, how are the instruments themselves established without regress/circularity?

## Structural donation

This is a direct parent of evaluator/verifier custody:

`claim validity <- evaluator`

cannot terminate scientifically at

`evaluator validity <- evaluator itself`.

Machine Epistemics needs an explicit chain or bounded external root for criteria/evaluators, with `CANNOT_CHECK` when that grounding is unavailable.

---

# 4. Chinese epistemologies — knowing, doing, context and practiced skill

Chinese philosophical traditions should not be re-expressed as weak versions of Western justified-belief epistemology. Contemporary scholarship emphasizes knowledge embedded in learning/practice, social-moral contexts and action. Zhuangzi in particular is rich in skill/know-how, timing and practice-adapted action; famous skill narratives treat mastery as sensitivity to structure in ongoing activity rather than constant explicit rule consultation.

## Structural donation

A machine scientific system should distinguish:

- knowing a proposition;
- knowing a procedure;
- knowing **when** to apply it;
- knowing how to adapt it to the present material/context;
- possessing demonstrated skill.

Candidate object:

`PracticalCompetence = (action_family, context_discriminators, timing, affordances, demonstrated_range, failure_boundary)`.

This pressures instruction-only and static-policy models.

---

# 5. Jain many-sided/perspectival traditions — condition the assertion on standpoint

Jain logic and epistemology include perspectival/conditional forms of predication and arguments against one-sided characterization of complex objects.

## Structural donation

Treat this as a parent threat to context-relative relation claims, not as a license for relativism.

Machine implication:

`Statement(object | standpoint/context)`

may be warranted while the unqualified statement is not.

A strong system should preserve the context that makes a predication valid and expose incompatible/partial standpoints without forcing one universal scalar representation.

Further primary reconstruction is required before stronger use.

---

# 6. Islamic philosophical traditions — demonstration, critique and integrated knowledge systems

Islamic philosophy includes long traditions of logic, medicine, natural philosophy, theology and critique. Avicenna constructed a highly integrated rational/scientific philosophical system; al-Ghazālī's critique of philosophers importantly challenges whether claimed demonstrations genuinely have demonstrative premises.

## Structural donation

A proof-like or fluent derivation should not inherit the status `demonstrative` merely from form. Bind:

- premise source/status;
- inference rule;
- modality/necessity claim;
- domain assumptions;
- whether premises are themselves established.

This aligns with atomic proof/evidence verification and argues against accepting formal-looking reasoning without premise audit.

A much deeper Arabic/Islamic philosophy reading programme remains open; Avicenna/Ghazālī are initial anchors, not representatives of an entire civilization.

---

# 7. Africana philosophies and oral/intellectual repositories

Africana philosophy is plural rather than one system. Historical and contemporary African intellectual traditions include written institutions and highly structured oral repositories such as griots, as well as communal/relational accounts of knowledge in some traditions.

## Structural donation

The machine donor universe must not equate `written document` with `knowledge-bearing source`.

Candidate source objects include:

- oral testimony with speaker/community/provenance identity;
- memorized genealogical/historical knowledge;
- performance/practice;
- community-adjudicated knowledge;
- living tradition with version/lineage and access restrictions.

This strengthens the testimony/distributed-cognition/dependence model.

Avoid treating `community` as automatic truth or collapsing all African epistemologies into Ubuntu; the correct object is plurality plus source/practice structure.

---

# 8. Indigenous and local knowledge systems — living observation, practice and custodianship

Indigenous and local knowledge systems can contain generations of careful ecological observation, language, practice, environmental management and intergenerational transmission. UNESCO's LINKS programme explicitly works to connect Indigenous/local knowledge holders with scientific and policy processes while emphasizing community participation and rights.

## Structural donation

Machine Epistemics requires both **epistemic** and **governance** metadata for knowledge that has custodial/community rights.

Candidate fields:

`KnowledgeSource = (content, holder/custodian, community/context, transmission_mode, locality, temporal_depth, access_rights, consent/governance, translation_loss, scientific_relation_status)`.

The machine may learn a structural donor without acquiring unrestricted authority to copy, publish or operationalize it.

This is a major pressure on any open-web assumption that `retrievable == freely reusable`.

## Scientific-method implication

Local/Indigenous observation also reinforces the point that sustained practical observation can generate knowledge not initially framed as an academic experiment. The system should be able to ingest such observations while preserving their native context and without laundering them into an academic authority they did not claim.

# 9. Ethnoepistemology — study the epistemologies themselves as empirical phenomena

Ethnoepistemology treats human epistemological practices—including ordinary folk, specialists and academic epistemologists—as phenomena that can themselves be compared anthropologically.

## Structural donation

This suggests a higher-order donor programme:

`EpistemicPracticeAtlas = {community/practice -> source types, validation practices, disagreement rules, apprenticeship, authority, failure correction, stopping}`.

Rather than assuming ORION's epistemology and searching for domain examples, Machine Epistemics can compare **different epistemic practices as systems**.

This is a major future parent of the all-human-knowledge atlas.

# 10. Updated saturation condition

The expanded programme cannot claim human-civilization saturation until it has deliberate coverage over multiple philosophical/cultural traditions and multiple knowledge modes.

At minimum, future no-material-change passes must include:

- classical Indian epistemology / Nyāya and opponents;
- Buddhist epistemology;
- at least one Jain primary/reconstruction route;
- Chinese/Daoist/Confucian epistemological/practice routes;
- Arabic/Islamic philosophy of knowledge/inquiry;
- Africana philosophy/epistemology;
- Indigenous/local knowledge-system literature led by knowledge-holder/community-sensitive sources;
- Western analytic/continental/pragmatist traditions;
- ordinary/practical knowledge sources.
