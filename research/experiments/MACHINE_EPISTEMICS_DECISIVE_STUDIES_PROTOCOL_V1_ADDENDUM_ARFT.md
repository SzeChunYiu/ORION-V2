# Machine Epistemics Decisive Studies V1 — ARFT / External Process-Failure Addendum

**State date:** 2026-09-01  
**Status:** prospective addendum; no protected outcomes inspected  
**Applies to:** `ME-X2` and `ME-X5`

## Trigger

Fei et al., *How Do Agents Fail on AutoResearch* (arXiv:2608.14905, 2026-08-14) introduces AutoResearchEval: 100 frontier research tasks across seven scientific domains, eight harness-model combinations, 800 trajectories, and a 45-pattern AutoResearch Failure Taxonomy (ARFT). The authors interpret the recurrent deficit as lack of a metacognitive loop and leave orchestration-level repair open.

This is a strong independent challenge to any ORION claim that its obstruction taxonomy or process-control framing is itself novel.

## ME-X2 amendment

Add the following baseline/control to the prospectively frozen comparison set:

`B3_ARFT_OR_EQUIVALENT_PROCESS_FAILURE_DIAGNOSIS`

The baseline may use ARFT or an equivalently strong external process-level failure taxonomy/diagnostic system with complete trajectory/intermediate-artifact access where licensing/data access permits.

### Mapping protocol

Before protected intervention outcomes are viewed:

1. freeze a mapping hypothesis from ORION obstruction classes to ARFT patterns;
2. explicitly allow `NO_MAPPING`, `ONE_TO_MANY`, `MANY_TO_ONE`, and `MULTI_CAUSAL`;
3. do not alter the ORION obstruction taxonomy to improve post-hoc agreement;
4. treat mapping agreement as secondary—the primary endpoint remains **intervention choice and outcome**;
5. include ARFT patterns that do not fit ORION as hostile counterexamples to universality.

### Stronger mechanistic test

An ME-X2 residual now requires more than successful failure labeling. It must show:

`process failure evidence -> obstruction diagnosis -> minimum intervention choice -> improved protected task outcome/cost`

beyond a strong external process-failure diagnosis + metareasoning/MDA/VoI composition.

If ARFT-like diagnosis plus parent control makes the same intervention decisions, Machine Epistemics gains no separate obstruction-control residual.

## ME-X5 amendment

At least one cross-domain field-residual stratum should, where feasible, be sourced from:

- independently authored end-to-end scientific-agent trajectories;
- external failure taxonomies such as ARFT;
- or native-domain failure episodes not created by ORION authors.

Synthetic ORION-authored episodes may remain known-answer/unit tests but cannot be the only evidence for cross-domain recurrence.

## Public-claim amendment

Do not state that Machine Epistemics uniquely recognizes:

- process-level scientific-agent failure;
- the need for metacognitive checking/revision;
- or the insufficiency of final-output-only evaluation.

The defensible residual remains a hypothesis about **scientific transition decisions and minimum responsible interventions beyond contemporary process-failure diagnosis and strongest parent controls**.
