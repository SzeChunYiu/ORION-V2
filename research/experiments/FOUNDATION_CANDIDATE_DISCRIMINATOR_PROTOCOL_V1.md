# Foundation Candidate Discriminator and Generativity Protocol V1

**Canonical foundation issue:** #41  
**Upward-synthesis issue:** #43  
**Status:** prospective protocol for distinguishing F0–F3. No outcomes have been observed or inserted. It grants no foundation, field, theory-superiority or publication authority.

## 1. Scientific question

Can a higher-order Machine Epistemics theory recover the strongest parent sciences while generating and executing better scientific transitions than:

- F0, the strongest mature-parent federation;
- F1, selective interfield bridge theories;
- F3, a domain-native federation with no universal transition laws?

F2 survives only through prospective, resource-matched and independently evaluated evidence.

## 2. Expert cell and custody

1. **Parent-federation architect** — builds F0 using strong native methods and realistic expert routing.
2. **Interfield theorist** — constructs F1 bridges without access to F2 outcome data.
3. **Absorptive-theory team** — instantiates F2 from the frozen proposition ledger.
4. **Domain-federation team** — constructs F3 with native semantics and minimal shared interfaces.
5. **Experimental-design/statistics reviewer** — freezes cases, estimands, resource matching and sequential rules.
6. **Independent domain adjudicators** — evaluate scientific decisions without being required to use ORION vocabulary.
7. **Systems-efficiency reviewer** — measures latency, compute, memory, human labour and implementation/failure surface.
8. **Authority/governance reviewer** — evaluates permission, consent and custody separately from evidence.
9. **Hostile editor** — seeks benchmark leakage, F0 handicapping, retrospective generativity and deceptive unification.

No candidate team is the sole final evaluator of its own semantic decisions.

## 3. Frozen arms

### F0 — strongest parent federation

Native parents, expert-configured routing and strongest practical compositions.

### F1 — interfield bridge theory

Only pre-registered bridge families; no universal programme-level law.

### F2 — absorptive supertheory candidate

Frozen candidate propositions F2-P1–P9, embeddings/recovery maps and declared components.

### F3 — domain federation

Native domain methods, boundary objects and local governance; minimal universal semantics.

### Controls

- `SIMPLE_DIRECT` — smallest sufficient method;
- `HUMAN_EXPERT` — domain expert or expert team under matched time where feasible;
- `MACHINE_NATIVE` — architecture optimized for machine substrate with the same scientific interface requirements;
- `F2_MINUS_COMPONENT` and strongest-parent replacement arms;
- negative cases in which the integrated theory should remain inactive.

## 4. Information and resource matching

Before outcome access, bind for every arm:

- source corpus and access restrictions;
- tools, software and instrument interfaces;
- model/checkpoint identities;
- compute, wall time and parallelism;
- human expert time and interventions;
- evaluator access;
- memory and persistent artifacts;
- numerical precision and implementation environment;
- authorized actions and data-use permissions.

Where exact matching is impossible, report a resource–quality Pareto curve rather than one comparison point.

## 5. Case families

### C1 — consensus on a computationally reproducible but scientifically wrong result

**Structure:** multiple agents share a numerically stable implementation of a scientifically inadequate model and agree under a replicated log.

**Parents required:** distributed consensus, numerical analysis, model adequacy, provenance/dependence, assurance.

**Protected decisions:**

- agreement versus scientific correctness;
- computation versus model validity;
- common-mode dependence;
- whether the claim may close.

**Expected discriminator:** F2 should identify the weakest material warrant link and refuse promotion; F0 can match if the parent router composes all relevant validators correctly.

### C2 — calibration iteration with preserved historical claims

**Structure:** an instrument/evaluator standard changes after a discrepancy is discovered. Some prior claims remain valid under a relation; others must reopen.

**Parents required:** metrology, measurement invariance, provenance, belief revision, selective reopening.

**Protected decisions:** comparison identity, revalidation reach, old-result interpretation and uncertainty.

### C3 — exploration before a usable hypothesis space

**Structure:** current variables and action labels cannot express the decisive regularity. Exploratory/constructive actions create a representation or measurement enabling later discrimination.

**Parents required:** exploratory experimentation, automated discovery, program search, active learning.

**Hostile control:** a matched case in which exploratory machinery only wastes resources and the simple direct method wins.

### C4 — robust decision under imprecise or conflicting uncertainty

**Structure:** evidence supports a credal set or conflicting models, not one warranted posterior. The action can be reversible, irreversible or option-preserving.

**Parents required:** imprecise probability, DMDU, minimax regret, POMDP/metareasoning.

**Protected decisions:** whether precision is fabricated, which action is authorized and when monitoring triggers adaptation.

### C5 — semantic transport with local exclusion constraints

**Structure:** two domains use the same label or ontology class, but one has a local constraint decisive for the target action.

**Parents required:** ontology matching, boundary objects, context-relative relations, domain expert adjudication.

**Protected decisions:** mapping status, prohibited inference and whether reuse is safe.

### C6 — governed source with scientific utility but restricted reuse

**Structure:** a source is technically FAIR, highly informative and relevant, but collective authority or purpose restrictions block the proposed use.

**Parents required:** FAIR, CARE, research ethics, institutional authority, evidence-to-decision.

**Protected decisions:** evidence access, use permission, agenda authority and `CANNOT_CHECK`.

### C7 — machine-native internal strategy with external witness

**Structure:** a non-linguistic or program-search system finds a high-performing scientific strategy that differs from human traces.

**Parents required:** program synthesis/search, automated reasoning, numerical validation, interpretability/accountability, strongest domain algorithm.

**Protected decisions:**

- whether the strategy is scientifically valid;
- whether more compute explains the gain;
- whether its external witness is sufficient;
- whether it transfers beyond one benchmark.

### C8 — robustness and triangulation under hidden shared assumptions

**Structure:** several methods agree but share one measurement or preprocessing assumption; one genuinely independent route disagrees.

**Parents required:** robustness analysis, triangulation, dependence models, assurance and argumentation.

**Protected decisions:** effective corroboration, discordance retention, next discriminator and selective reopening.

### C9 — parent-local sufficiency and framework drag

**Structure:** one mature parent or direct method exactly resolves the case.

**Purpose:** hard negative control against compulsory F1/F2 activation.

**Protected decisions:** correct local deference and cost.

### C10 — bounded research closure with a censored route

**Structure:** all searched routes are stable, but one material community, proprietary or inaccessible route remains censored.

**Parents required:** systematic review, formal learning/stopping, qualitative saturation, authority/custody.

**Protected decision:** bounded close versus global absence claim.

## 6. Prospective generativity sub-study

### 6.1 Freeze point

For each case family, candidates receive the same pre-outcome state. Before access to the decisive result, each candidate produces:

- candidate hidden failure or bridge relation;
- predicted discriminator;
- expected possible outcomes;
- claim consequences for each outcome;
- confidence/uncertainty form;
- requested resources;
- falsifier.

Outputs are content-bound to theory and implementation versions.

### 6.2 Generativity endpoints

- valid novel discriminator rate;
- parent-federation novelty after ancestry audit;
- prospective prediction accuracy/calibration;
- scientific value of proposed measurement/experiment;
- false novelty and irrelevant complexity;
- cost to first decisive information;
- rate of negative but informative predictions;
- cross-domain transfer.

### 6.3 Anti-leakage controls

- hidden outcome and hidden target paper where possible;
- no access to later literature or final case results;
- training/source contamination audit;
- outcome-blind expert evaluation of proposal quality;
- retrospective explanations scored separately and never counted as prospective generativity.

## 7. Primary outcomes

### Non-compensatory

- false scientific completion;
- unsafe transport;
- evidence/source corruption;
- criterion drift;
- invalid uncertainty strengthening;
- authority/custody violation;
- critical native-parent capability loss;
- hidden outcome leakage.

### Scientific quality

- justified-terminal rate;
- known-answer or expert-adjudicated correctness;
- correct `CANNOT_CHECK`;
- weakest-link diagnosis;
- selective reopening accuracy;
- useful discriminator value;
- calibration;
- parent recovery;
- generativity;
- cross-domain transfer;
- unnecessary refusal/escalation.

### Cost

`Cost = (latency, compute, memory, annotation, expert_time, implementation, maintenance, failure_surface)`.

No average cost or quality gain compensates a critical validity or authority failure.

## 8. Theory-dominance decision

Apply the executable theory-dominance semantics after the frozen case outcomes.

### `ABSORPTIVE_SUPERTHEORY_CANDIDATE`

Requires:

- no critical regressions;
- native parent non-inferiority;
- at least one protected cross-parent strict gain over F0;
- at least one prospective generative or integrative gain;
- independent adjudication;
- at least two materially different domains;
- correct local deference;
- causal component/bridge attribution.

### Other valid terminals

- `INTEGRATIVE_THEORY_ADVANCE`;
- `INTERFIELD_SPECIALIZATION`;
- `ENGINEERING_EFFICIENCY_ADVANCE`;
- `FEDERATED_PARENT_EQUIVALENT`;
- `PARENT_COMPOSITION_SUFFICIENT`;
- `DOMAIN_SPECIFIC_ADVANCE`;
- `DECEPTIVE_UNIFICATION`;
- `OVERGENERALIZED_THEORY`;
- `REDUNDANT_DRAG`;
- `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## 9. Statistical and adjudication plan

- freeze primary outcomes and minimum practically important differences;
- report effect sizes and uncertainty intervals rather than only significance;
- use repeated seeds/runs where stochasticity is material;
- stratify by case family and domain;
- preserve per-case failures instead of only averages;
- analyse candidate × domain interactions;
- use blinded paired expert adjudication for semantic outputs;
- report inter-rater agreement and unresolved cases;
- correct or hierarchically model multiple comparisons where appropriate;
- retain parent wins and negative generativity.

## 10. Publication mapping

### Flagship Perspective

Uses only published/frozen specialist evidence and the field-level comparison. It cannot contain substantial unpublished primary results.

### P-A/P-B

Own parent reconstruction, embeddings, recovery and relation/counter-probe results.

### P-C/P-D

### P-E
