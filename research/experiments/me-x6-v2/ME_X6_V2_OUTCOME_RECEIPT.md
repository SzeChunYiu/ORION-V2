## 1. What was asked

V1's provenance receipt §7 left one question open and forbade guessing it:

> §4 shows a **hand-set** zeroing suffices. Whether a *learned* capacity-matched parent
> would discover that zeroing from the development split is unknown and must be measured,
> not assumed. Nothing here licenses the guess.

It is now measured. **A learned capacity-matched untyped comparator discovers the zeroing
and reproduces M's capability verdict on every one of 1400 protected instances.**

## 2. The comparator, and why it is not a published-method parent

`B8_CAPACITY_MATCHED_BEST` is **constructed for this study**. It carries no fidelity
receipt and the absence is correct: ME-X6's generator emits per-period integer channel
counts with no bibliographic substrate, so no published science-of-science estimator is
natively computable on this input and none was manufactured. It is named for what it is —
a **learned capacity-matched untyped aggregate** over the same 16 channels, the same
late-minus-early half difference, and the same decision rule every V1 arm uses.

Two standard, pre-specified, RNG-free procedures were registered and frozen before any
protected instance existed:

| arm | fitted vector (development split, n = 56) | dev | zeroed |
|---|---|---|---|
| `B6_GREEDY_SUBSET_UNTYPED` | `formal_artifacts +1, replications_passed +1, retractions −1, independent_rederivations +1` | 56/56 | **12/16** |
| `B7_L1_PATH_UNTYPED` | `disruption +0.0150, formal_artifacts +0.0016, replications_passed +0.0279, retractions −0.0185, independent_rederivations +0.0202` | 56/56 | 11/16 |
| `B8` (selected: `B6`, frozen rule, tie → B6) | as `B6` | 56/56 | 12/16 |
| `B4X_..._UNIT_SIGN_LEARNED_CONTROL` (V1's own comparator, refitted by V1's own `fit_signs`) | 13 nonzero channels | 28/56 | 3/16 |

**The learned vector is not M's.** M weights eight channels and uses `retractions = −2`;
`B6` uses **four** channels and `retractions = −1`, dropping `downstream_reuse`,
`corrections`, `solution_cost` and `replications_failed` entirely. The finding is
therefore not "the fitter rediscovered the mechanism" but the stronger "a **different**,
**sparser** untyped rule, learned from data, also decides the conjunction exactly."

## 4. What this says about ME-X6 V1 — and what it does not

**G2 is V2's own contribution, and it is the load-bearing gate.** Both arms in it are
**fitted from the same development split**; only the weight class differs. The
capacity-matched learned comparator beats the unit-sign learned comparator 1400 to 700,
winning 14 cells and losing none, on **700 discordant pairs**. The 14 cells it wins are
exactly the seven decoupled strata — `I1`, `I2`, `I3`, `I4`, `I5`, `I7`, `I8` — at both
scales, which are exactly the 14 cells V1's protected receipt recorded as the separation.

So V1's separation is reproduced arm-for-arm, and **attributed**: it is a **capacity**
effect, not a typing effect. Holding learnedness fixed and varying only whether the class
contains zero moves the comparator from 700/1400 to 1400/1400.

**What V1 got right, and keeps.** V1 never claimed a residual
(`ME_X6_RESIDUAL_CLAIMED = FALSE`), disclosed that M is exact by construction, published
its comparator's frozen signs, and its provenance receipt already corrected
`CANNOT_REPRESENT` → `DOES_NOT_RECOVER` and attributed the failure to the inability to
zero a channel. Every V1 gate verdict, denominator, rate, ablation and ladder count stands
untouched: this study re-ran nothing of V1's, refitted none of its signs, wrote nothing
into its directory, and changed no gate, number, terminal or authorization. V1's 41 tests
pass unchanged.

**What now falls.** V1's design §1.2 — *"the only difference between them is typing"* —
does not survive as stated. The difference was capacity, and once capacity is matched the
difference is gone. V1's provenance receipt §5 kept the study's strongest defence, that
one may read "typing" as *the a-priori assignment of signed roles including the zeros*.
That defence survives only as a definition: under it "typing" is exactly "being allowed to
drop a channel", and V2 shows a fitter allowed to drop channels **learns which ones** from
28 public instances per cell. The a-priori assignment is not what carried the result.

**Stated plainly because it is unfavourable to the mechanism:** on ME-X6's world, the
typed collective state has **no measurable advantage** over an untyped weighted aggregate
at matched information and matched capacity. ME-X6 contracts to an interpretive framework.

## 5. Pre-run reachability audit, recorded as a pre-outcome correction

Written into the frozen design before the protected split existed, and reproduced in the
selftest report (`reachability_audit`):

1. **A clause "the comparator is AHEAD of M" was deliberately NOT registered.** M is exact
   by construction on this generator (V1 design §1.3), so such a clause is unreachable — a
   gate that could not fire. `G0d` is therefore a **validity** check: a failure there is
   generator drift, not a result. The two live outcomes were the tie and M-ahead.
2. **The comparator class contains a failing member and an exact member.** All-+1 weights
   score 28/56 on development; V1's hand-set zeroing scores 56/56. The arm could lose and
   could win.
3. **Both fitters are heuristics that can miss a member of their own class**, demonstrated
   on constructed data rather than asserted: forward selection reaches **5/6** where the
   hidden in-class rule `{f2 +1, f3 −2}` reaches 6/6 and exhaustive enumeration finds it;
   the lasso path reaches **6/8** where the same rule reaches 8/8. **An exhaustive search
   over a class containing M's own vector was deliberately not registered** — it could not
   fail, and the tie would then have been an identity rather than a measurement. This is
   the tautology V2 was most at risk of becoming, and avoiding it is why the tie is
   evidence.
4. **The activity half is not scored and not reported.** V1 computes it from the activity
   channels by the same call for every arm, so it is equal by construction and is not
   evidence. V2 declines to publish a 100% agreement no arm could have lost.
5. **The protected seed governs instance generation only.** The comparator fit is a
   deterministic, RNG-free function of the public development split, frozen before the
   seed was revealed. Disclosed rather than implied.
6. **The exact binomial tail is computed in rationals**, because the float form overflows
   at n = 1400 and would return `inf`, making a `p > α` hard gate incapable of failing.
   The selftest asserts the tail is finite at protected scale.

## 7. Executable assertions, and the controls that keep each zero honest

`tests/unit/test_me_x6_v2_capacity_matched.py`: **18 passed, exit status 0**, read from
`$?` with no pipe. `ruff` clean on the whole study directory and the test file. V1's
`tests/unit/test_me_x6_exact_study.py`: **41 passed, exit 0**, unchanged.

- **The tie is controlled.** `test_un_zeroing_the_six_channels_destroys_the_tie` restores
  the comparator's six dropped channels to the control's own signs and the score falls
  below ceiling — so 1400/1400 is a property of the fitted vector, not of the scoring path.
- **The capacity control is controlled.** `test_the_unit_sign_control_reproduces_V1s_own_
  frozen_signs` compares V2's refit against the signs committed in **V1's design JSON**,
  so the capacity control is verifiably V1's comparator and not a lookalike. They match
  exactly, across two different development seeds.
- **Every terminal is reachable.** Four routing tests exercise the separation terminal, a
  failed hard gate, neither-live-clause, and an unexercised cell; each lands where
  registered.
- **The heuristics' failure modes are asserted**, not described.
- **Denominators published with every rate.** The one figure that is *not* evidence — the
  activity half — is not published at all rather than published with a caveat.

**What could not be checked, kept distinct from what was checked and is fine:** whether a
capacity-matched comparator ties M on any world other than ME-X6's generator (not run, not
claimed); and whether a published-method science-of-science parent would tie M (**not
computable here** — §2 — and manufacturing one would be the defect this study exists to
avoid).
