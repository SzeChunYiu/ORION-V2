# P-A / P-B — Standalone Survival Gate Disposition Receipt V1

**Disposition: `CANNOT_CHECK_INDEPENDENT_ADJUDICATION` — a precisely-named blocker, not a
verdict.** Recorded 2026-09-04.

Both papers were carrying registry reasons that read as neglect —
`STANDALONE_SURVIVAL_GATE_UNEXECUTED` (P-A) and `DECISIVE_NATURALISTIC_GATE_UNEXECUTED`
(P-B) — as though the gate were simply waiting for someone with compute. It is not. This
receipt establishes what the registered gate actually is, why it cannot be executed, why no
second gate can substitute, and what was repaired in it while it is still pre-outcome.

**No verdict is claimed for either paper.** A blocked gate yields a blocker. Reporting one as
a scientific null would file an unbuilt study as a tested one.

## 1. The registered gate, and the justified scope of that claim

> define the minimum naturalistic evidence that could support the surviving P-A and P-B
> standalone claims

and §9 opens *"P-A/P-B may be promoted beyond `HOLD` only if all are satisfied"*, with seven
numbered conditions. One gate, two papers, jointly.

**"Only gate" is an absence claim, so the scope is stated rather than implied.** Both repos
were searched in full at `ORION-V2` main `ec3a13e` and `ORION-paper` main `f226386`, by
basename with `find` and by content, each negative paired with a control pattern that had to
match on the same files.

different classes. Only one is executable:

## 2. Why FM80 cannot be executed

Terminal `BLOCKED_ELIGIBILITY_PRECONDITIONS_UNSATISFIED`, filed in
`research/experiments/fm-exact/FM80_BLOCKED_WITH_REASON_RECEIPT.md` and independently
re-verified clause-by-clause on 2026-09-03 by a reader who did not write it (two reporting
imprecisions corrected there; the disposition stood). That receipt is relied on here rather
than re-litigated, and its §7 names the single blocking artifact:

> §7 has no hatch. *"at least two qualified adjudicators who did not build the evaluated
> arm"* is a property of persons. Independence-from-construction cannot be manufactured by
> computation.

**The human gate is multiply determined — four independent clauses, not one.** This audit
adds that count:

| clause | what it requires of a person |
|---|---|
| §4.4 | an independent adjudicator accepts the donor is scientifically relevant — so `REMOTE` is **not machine-decidable** |
| §7 | ≥ 2 qualified adjudicators per domain who did not build the arm, blinded, + a third for terminal disagreements |
| §9.4 | ≥ 1 winning domain contains genuinely `REMOTE` donors *under §4* — inheriting §4.4 |
| §9.7 | independent adjudication finds no stronger omitted parent collapsing the residual |

At three domains × two adjudicators plus a third for disagreements, the lane needs six to nine
qualified, conflict-declared people independent of arm construction. §10 anticipates exactly
this and names `CANNOT_CHECK_INDEPENDENT_ADJUDICATION` a valid recordable terminal.

**A machine-checkable surrogate for remoteness was considered and rejected.** It is the
obvious move and it is wrong: §4.4 makes `REMOTE` non-machine-decidable *by construction*, so
a surrogate would **replace** the clause rather than satisfy it, and §9.4 would then be
unsatisfiable in principle — a structurally guaranteed negative reading as an empirical
finding, which is precisely the defect §4 of this document exists to prevent. §2 forbids
substituting a generated suite for a domain; §8's scoped pilot "cannot grant the standalone
top-tier terminal" and needs eligible cases regardless, of which there are zero; §12 forbids
relaxing the protocol on the strength of a negative elsewhere. The blockage is structural for
the lane as frozen.

**The standing compute authorization does not bind here.** No amount of computation
substitutes for a person who did not build the arm. This is the one shape of blocker that
more compute cannot move.

## 3. Why E80/E90 cannot substitute

E80 ("P-A hidden remote-parent benchmark") and E90 ("P-B formal relation result and two
naturalistic cases") are registered as the decisive evidence in the frozen survivor matrix, so
they are not nothing. But they are **registered-but-unspecified**: no frozen protocol, no
study design, no arms, no endpoints, no pass thresholds, no terminal vocabulary, and
`runnable_now: false` with unmet external requirements (a held-out remote-domain corpus,
expert donor-card replication, domain reviewers). A `done_when` sentence is not a gate.

Specifying them now would be a **post-null design**, and the portfolio's own frozen contract
prohibits it in terms. `NEXT_PROTECTED_EVIDENCE_DECISION_GATES_V1.md`, P-A "Prohibited
rescue", verbatim:

> inventing a new donor benchmark after the registered decisive cells are null

and its global rule:

> A result may strengthen, narrow, contract, merge or kill a paper. It may not create a new
> endpoint after outcome access merely to preserve a paper count.

Writing E80's design today, with the generated-battery null already read, is that move exactly.
**Declining to specify E80/E90 is a finding, not an omission**: the papers' remaining
registered evidence is unreachable both ways — the specified gate is human-blocked, and the
unspecified cells cannot be specified without breaking the rule that makes any of this
evidence rather than storytelling.

## 4. What was repaired while the gate is still pre-outcome

FM80 has never been dispatched, so it is still open to correction, and one was needed. Full
analysis in `FM80_PRE_OUTCOME_DESIGN_CORRECTION_V1.md`; in short:

**§9.1 and §9.2 are jointly unsatisfiable at §8's registered sample.** §9.1 asks for ≥ 10
percentage points in ≥ 2 of 3 domains; §9.2 asks the paired 95% interval to exclude zero under
Holm; §8 sets 30 cases per domain with exact paired tests. At n = 30, 10 pp is 3 net cases, and
the most favourable table consistent with that — **zero** adverse discordance — gives an exact
two-sided p of 0.25. Holm's rungs are 0.0167 and 0.025. A domain landing exactly on §9.1's bar
fails §9.2 with certainty, at every possible pairing.

Any true effect in [10 pp, 20 pp) would satisfy the clause the protocol calls scientifically
meaningful and necessarily fail the clause that certifies it, terminating on a §10 contraction
vocabulary — most likely `PARENT_OR_RETRIEVAL_SUFFICIENCY` — which would be read as evidence
that typed donor transfer adds nothing. **That is a manufactured finding**, and §10 forbids
redesigning the same cases after outcome access, so catching it post-run would be too late.

Repair options, both stated because they differ: raise the sample to **≥ 61 eligible cases per
domain (183 total, against a registered 90)** to keep the 10 pp bar; or raise the bar to the
sample's actual resolution of **23.3 pp at n = 30 (2.3× the stated bar)** and say plainly that
FM80 as sampled detects only large effects. A third option — swapping the exact paired test for
a more permissive one — must not be taken merely because it is cheapest; that would be relaxing
a frozen protocol, which §12 forbids.

No case has been assembled, so no branch is foreclosed. **This correction restores a choice the
current freeze would have quietly removed**, and it changes nothing about the block: it makes
the study sound for whoever can finally run it.

`scripts/verify_fm80_section9_reachability.py` reproduces the arithmetic with three-way exit
codes; `--self-test` asserts it can alarm on the registered design, **stay silent** on sound
designs (n = 120 at 10 pp; n = 30 at 40 pp), refuse rather than report a negative on
out-of-range inputs, and remain monotone in n. 7/7 pass.

## 5. Second defect: the paper-side gates and FM80 are not bound to each other

`grep -rln "FM80"` over the whole paper repo returns 7 files, **none** under
`v2-papers/P-A-structural-donor-discovery/` or `v2-papers/P-B-context-relative-relations/`
(control: `grep -c "P-"` on both READMEs returns 3 each, so those directories are searchable).
Each paper's README and post-null brief states survival conditions in its own words, and P-A's
five README conditions do not match FM80 §9's seven.

Nothing currently keeps them in step. If FM80 §9 is amended — as §4 above says it must be —
four other documents will silently continue to state the old conditions. Raised as a binding
task, not repaired here, because editing paper-side survival language is a manuscript act and
this lane holds no claim authority over it.

## 6. What is claimed, and what is not

**Claimed:** the sole registered, specified standalone survival gate for P-A and P-B is FM80
§9; it is blocked on a human-adjudication precondition required independently by four of its
clauses; no second registered gate is specified enough to substitute, and specifying one now is
prohibited by the portfolio's own frozen rescue rules; and the gate as frozen carries a
sample/effect-size defect that would have manufactured a negative, now corrected pre-outcome.

**Not claimed:**
- **no scientific verdict on either paper.** No arm ran. §10's contraction terminals are
  outcomes of an *executed* study and none applies. Filing this as a null would misreport an
  unbuilt study as a tested one;
- not that a residual does not exist — nothing here bears on that either way;
- not `PARENT_SUFFICIENT`, which is a legitimate terminal only for a study that ran;
- not that the papers should be reopened, and not that they should be released.
  `publication_disposition` is unchanged and `submission_authorized` remains False.

The existing generated-battery nulls stand undisturbed: P-A's false-analogy battery null and
P-B's relation battery null (full interface 99/112 vs simple/static 100/112, paired exact
McNemar p ≥ 0.75) remain the papers' recorded evidence. What this receipt changes is the
*reason* the papers are closed — from "nobody ran the gate" to "the gate is blocked on people,
and here is the defect it carried."
