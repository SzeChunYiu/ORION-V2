# ORION-V2 Atom Re-Derivation — Wave 01

**Status:** provisional donor-reduction study; no atom is admitted.  
**Principle:** no V1 operation enters V2 by inheritance. The unit of analysis is a scientific obligation, not a familiar function name.

## Review roles

- native parent-field owner;
- formal-contract reviewer;
- statistical/measurement reviewer;
- workflow/resource reviewer;
- authority/provenance reviewer;
- hostile “already solved / unnecessary atom” reviewer.

For every candidate atom, the hostile outcome `REPLACE_WITH_PARENT_METHOD` is a positive research result.

---

## A01 — Compile problem/frontier contract

### Obligation
Represent what must be decided or produced, why it matters, which constraints and evidence classes apply, what resources/authority are available, and which non-success terminals are acceptable.

### Parent owners
Requirements engineering, systems engineering, decision analysis, legal scope/standing, clinical question formulation, design briefs and research-protocol methodology.

### Why V1 is insufficient as the final object
V1 framing supplies scoped problem state and invariants, but V2 must distinguish requested output from scientific validation, decision utility, agenda authority, resource/capacity and reopen conditions.

### Candidate formal object
`ProblemContract = (identity, target_decisions, scope, obligations, blockers, evidence, actions, resources, capacity, authority, terminals, epoch, replay)`.

### Provisional disposition
`GENERALIZE_WITH_NEW_COORDINATE`

### Falsifiers
- a standard requirements/decision specification fully expresses every candidate field;
- the extra contract coordinates add no protected decision value;
- contract compilation induces needless refusal on direct problems.

---

## A02 — Orient and select a representation

### Obligation
Determine problem boundary, relevant domains, observables/interventions, scale, time regime, candidate problem class and representations adequate to express required distinctions.

### Parent owners
Situation awareness, initial exploration, problem structuring methods, conceptual modelling, ontology engineering, systems architecture, human sensemaking and scientific model selection.

### Candidate formal object
A portfolio of representation hypotheses with adequacy partitions, observable/action interfaces and explicit unknown context.

### Provisional disposition
`SPLIT_BY_CONTEXT`

### Falsifiers
- ordinary schema/model selection parent solves all cases;
- representation portfolio gives no gain beyond one strong model with uncertainty;
- orientation overhead harms simple controls.

---

## A03 — Decompose and recombine

### Obligation
Split a problem only when interfaces, shared variables, independence/coupling, authority, resources and recomposition are explicit.

### Parent owners
Systems architecture, mathematical decomposition, divide-and-conquer algorithms, operations research, modular design, workflow nets and category/sheaf-style gluing.

### Candidate formal object
`DecompositionReceipt` binding subproblems, interfaces, coupling graph, shared evidence/resources, local terminals, global obligations and merge/obstruction rule.

### Provisional disposition
`GENERALIZE_WITH_NEW_COORDINATE`

### Falsifiers
- standard architecture/decomposition formalism suffices;
- local solutions are treated as global without a gluing check;
- decomposition costs exceed direct solution on negative controls.

## A04 — Acquire information

### Obligation
Obtain literature, data, observations, measurements, experiments, computations or expert input while preserving source identity, coverage and censoring.

### Parent owners
Information retrieval, systematic review, survey sampling, data acquisition, experimental science, active learning and sensor management.

### Candidate formal object
Not one atom: a typed family of acquisition actions sharing provenance, coverage, cost and authority contracts.

### Provisional disposition
`SPLIT_BY_CONTEXT` plus `REPLACE_WITH_PARENT_METHOD`

### Falsifiers
- ORION-native acquisition underperforms the strongest domain method;
- route absence is silently converted to evidence of absence;
- source count substitutes for independent information.

## A05 — Interpret and absorb native evidence

### Obligation
Transform observations or documents into structured candidate scientific objects without erasing qualifiers, ambiguity, measurement conditions or native representation.

### Parent owners
Scientific information extraction, semantic parsing, measurement theory, hermeneutics, entity resolution, data integration and laboratory data reduction.

### Candidate formal object
Source projection plus typed candidate interpretation and unresolved bindings; integration remains a separate act.

### Provisional disposition
`PRESERVE_V1_SOURCE_PROJECTION` and `SPLIT_BY_CONTEXT`

### Falsifiers
- an existing native parser/data model preserves all decisive judgments;
- normalization changes the donor’s native verdict;
- unresolved evidence is promoted as an integrated claim.

## A06 — Compare structures, hypotheses and solutions

### Obligation
Determine exactly how two scientific objects relate under a declared probe, intervention, decision, target, resource and epoch context.

### Parent owners
Bisimulation, structural equivalence, experiment comparison, causal equivalence, model selection, rough sets, abstraction and metrology.

### Candidate formal object
Plural context-relative relation receipts, not one similarity metric.

### Provisional disposition
`MERGE_INTO_DEEPER_OBJECT`

### Falsifiers
- one parent family supplied with the same interface covers all required relations;
- the unified object collapses native distinctions;
- learned distance cannot produce witnesses or counter-probes.

## A07 — Construct a plural scientific state

### Obligation
Maintain competing models, explanations and solutions; exact support/defeaters; source projections; uncertainties; dependencies; coverage; resources; authority and history.

### Parent owners
Bayesian model portfolios, truth maintenance, knowledge representation, blackboard systems, model ensembles, provenance and scientific databases.

### Candidate formal object
`SolverState`/`ScientificState` with explicit plural alternatives and no forced canonical answer.

### Provisional disposition
`MERGE_V1_STATE_OBJECTS__GENERALIZE`

### Falsifiers
- state becomes a maximal ontology with no downstream sufficiency role;
- a simpler belief/model state matches every protected action decision;
- plural state cannot be maintained/replayed deterministically.

## A08 — Generate candidate hypotheses, designs and solutions

### Obligation
Produce a portfolio of plausible candidates within a declared language/closure, including parent methods and negative controls.

### Parent owners
Abduction, Bayesian model generation, design theory, program/theorem synthesis, symbolic regression, generative models, case-based reasoning and quality-diversity search.

### Candidate formal object
Plural proposal portfolio with origin, grammar/search regime, assumptions, expected consequences, donor lineage and verification obligations.

### Provisional disposition
`REPLACE_WITH_DONOR_PRODUCT` candidate

### Falsifiers
- generic/parent candidate generator dominates;
- diversity rewards useless dispersion;
- proposals after evaluator access leak protected outcomes;
- generated labels/macros are counted as method invention.

---

## A09 — Select a discriminating action

### Obligation
Choose an observation, query, computation, proof, simulation, experiment or expert request that most usefully separates live alternatives or discharges obligations under cost/risk/authority constraints.

### Parent owners
Bayesian experimental design, active learning, query learning, POMDPs, metareasoning, optimal diagnosis and sensor management.

### Candidate formal object
Action proposals with expected obligation reduction, distinguishing power, justified-reach gain, option/diversity value, costs, risk and authority exposure.

### Provisional disposition
`REPLACE_WITH_DONOR_PRODUCT` unless the scientific non-compensatory interface separates.

### Falsifiers
- strongest parent policy ties V2 under identical information;
- selected actions optimize proxy information but cannot affect the target decision;
- resource/capacity and censoring are ignored.

---

## A10 — Execute and observe

### Obligation
Perform computational, observational or physical actions under exact environment/tool/device identity and retain outputs, side effects, costs and failures.

### Parent owners
Scientific workflows, laboratory automation, distributed/durable execution, reproducible computing, instrumentation and process control.

### Candidate formal object
Typed `ScientificStepReceipt` and domain adapter; generic execution is parent-owned.

### Provisional disposition
`PRESERVE_V1_EXECUTION_INTEGRITY` plus parent adapters.

### Falsifiers
- execution success is promoted to scientific validity;
- partial side effects are omitted;
- retries fabricate a single clean occurrence;
- device/model/environment identity is not bound.

## A11 — Verify, validate and challenge

### Obligation
Test candidate content, predictions, constraints, measurement validity, robustness, replication and authority through appropriately independent routes.

### Parent owners
Formal verification, metrology, psychometrics, systems V&V, scientific replication, adversarial testing, audit and peer review.

### Candidate formal object
Separate verification, validation, calibration, reviewer-dependence and adoption records.

### Provisional disposition
`SPLIT_BY_CONTEXT`; preserve V1 anti-laundering boundary.

### Falsifiers
- correlated validators are counted as independent;
- checker acceptance substitutes for construct validity;
- correct content self-authorizes adoption.

## A12 — Diagnose responsibility and obstacle class

### Obligation
Determine whether an unresolved result is caused by access/data, implementation, local method, model class, representation, objective, instrument, workflow, framework, constitution or interacting causes.

### Parent owners
Model-based diagnosis, causal inference, medical differential diagnosis, multiple-fault reliability, quality root-cause analysis and system control.

### Candidate formal object
`ResponsibilityDiagnosis` with plural topology and discriminating intervention set.

### Provisional disposition
`GENERALIZE_V1_SPECIAL_CASE`

### Falsifiers
- one-stage attribution is forced under interaction failure;
- a poor score is treated as an objective/framework diagnosis;
- no intervention separates responsibility levels.

---

## A13 — Repair, compensate, rollback or reopen

### Obligation
Apply the minimum valid change while preserving independent support and immutable failure history; account for partial/irreversible effects.

### Parent owners
Program repair, truth maintenance, belief revision, database recovery, workflow compensation, maintenance/reliability and configuration management.

### Candidate formal object
A family of recovery transitions with affected-support hypergraph, alternative support, side-effect/compensation state and authority.

### Provisional disposition
`PRESERVE_V1_SELECTIVE_REOPEN_AS_SPECIAL_CASE`

### Falsifiers
- full reset or no reset performs equally under all cases;
- repair deletes failures;
- a reversible state repair is used for irreversible experimental effects;
- alternative support is ignored.

---

## A14 — Reframe, remodel or change abstraction

### Obligation
Change problem/model/representation only when the incumbent distinctions or closure are shown inadequate and lower-level expansion is insufficient.

### Parent owners
Insight/representational change, C-K/TRIZ, model criticism, abstraction refinement, causal model discovery, planning abstraction and ontology evolution.

### Candidate formal object
Level-specific transformation proposal with old ceiling, correspondence, affected commitments, preservation/reopening and predicted consequences.

### Provisional disposition
`MERGE_INTO_JUMP_LADDER`

### Falsifiers
- missing evidence is misdiagnosed as representation failure;
- model expansion suffices;
- new terminology creates no new reach;
- old commitments are silently reinterpreted.

## A15 — Escalate/Jump

### Obligation
Select the minimum responsible change level J0–J8 after a witnessed lower-level ceiling, and test against strongest parent products.

### Parent owners
M-open model discovery, representation change, design theory, CEGAR/CEGIS, program/instrument invention, open-ended search, objective evolution, meta-skill/self-code evolution and governance.

### Candidate formal object
`JumpTrigger`, `JumpProposal`, `JumpEvaluationReceipt`.

### Provisional disposition
`CANDIDATE_COMPOSITE_POLICY__NO_PRIMITIVE`

### Falsifiers
- lower level succeeds;
- trigger is low score, timeout or censoring;
- donor composition ties the proposal;
- current gains harm future successor ability;
- adoption is self-certified.

---

## A16 — Stop with a typed terminal

### Obligation
Stop for the correct scientific reason and preserve what would reopen the problem.

### Parent owners
Decision-theoretic stopping, sequential testing, open-world coverage, diagnosis, proof systems, resource-bounded planning and legal/authority procedure.

### Candidate formal object
Typed terminal taxonomy separating solution, partial result, plural alternatives, observational indeterminacy, structural non-identifiability, representation/method insufficiency, incomplete/censored coverage, resource bound, authority required, obstruction, refutation and `CANNOT_CHECK`.

### Provisional disposition
`GENERALIZE_V1_TERMINALS`

### Falsifiers
- terminal precedence converts censoring to non-identifiability;
- “resource bound” hides policy failure;
- a correct proposal is called adopted without authority;
- plural alternatives are collapsed to one answer.

---

## A17 — Learn and revise the solver

### Obligation
Use immutable episodes to improve retrieval, experiment selection, methods, workflows or meta-skills while preventing selected-success bias and self-promotion.

### Parent owners
Meta-learning, library/skill learning, organizational learning, DGM/meta-skill systems, safe self-modification and continual learning.

### Candidate formal object
Versioned proposal with causal attribution, fresh transfer, negative-history migration, non-regression and external adoption.

### Provisional disposition
`PRESERVE_V1_PROPOSAL_ADOPTION_BOUNDARY__PARENT_METHODS`

### Falsifiers
- same-owner evaluation;
- replaying training/derivation tasks as transfer;
- deleting harmful revisions;
- current performance rises while later-successor generation worsens.

# Wave result

The current V1 root names do not form a justified final V2 atomization. The emerging deeper objects are:

1. problem/frontier contract;
2. plural scientific state;
3. context-relative structural relations;
4. coupled hypothesis/action portfolios;
5. protected action-selection policy;
6. execution and observation receipts;
7. verification/validation/authority separation;
8. plural responsibility and recovery;
9. witnessed regime/Jump transitions;
10. typed scientific terminals;
11. protected solver evolution.

This list is itself provisional. Every object remains subject to parent collapse, split, merge or deletion.
