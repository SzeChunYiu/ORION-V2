# Research Provenance V0 — How ORION-V2 Was Reached

**Purpose:** preserve the reasoning path so future contributors do not receive only a finished architecture and invent a retrospective story.

## 0. Initial concern

The starting concern was that ORION had absorbed extensive AI, formal-methods, epistemology, verification, search and measurement literature, but this did not guarantee adequate learning from political science, linguistics, sociology, management, engineering, humanities, arts and other fields.

The hypothesis was symmetric:

- an external field may contribute a missing mechanism or coordinate; or
- attempting to apply ORION to that field may reveal a stronger native formulation and force an ORION claim to contract.

## 1. Repository audit

Before declaring omissions, the current ORION repository was inspected. It already contained:

- a parent-domain catalogue and changed-vocabulary search seeds;
- open computational-linguistics and measurement-science programmes;
- donor assimilation and donor-envelope assessments;
- extensive nearest-work pressure ledgers;
- local/global gluing and sheaf-related donors;
- regime-revision and Jump negative results;
- V1 P1–P15 theorem and component ledgers.

This changed the question from “which fields are missing?” to:

> Which faces of each discipline are missing, which native problems are already partially absorbed, and what independent coverage protocol prevents ORION vocabulary from selecting its own parents?

## 2. First high-value remote donors

### Quantitative politics and psychometrics

Latent positions or traits may be identifiable within one model but not comparable across times, institutions or forms without anchors and invariance assumptions. This pressured ORION's learned coordinates and cross-generation framework transport.

### Diachronic linguistics

Representation continuity does not guarantee meaning continuity. Semantic drift can preserve symbols while changing their scientific interpretation.

### Stemmatology and evolution

Lineage may be contaminated or reticulate; a child can inherit different components from multiple parents. Simple commit ancestry is not enough for scientific provenance.

### Organizational learning and institutions

Exploration, exploitation, escalation and learning-about-learning have mature organizational parents. Framework change is not uniquely an AI self-improvement problem.

## 3. Remote structures became neighbours

The research then moved beyond isolated donors. Structures from different subjects appeared close when compared by role, probes or future consequences:

- structural/regular equivalence in networks;
- bisimulation in transition systems;
- contextual/interface equivalence in semantics and engineering;
- predictive equivalence in computational mechanics;
- observational and Markov equivalence in causal inference;
- decision-relative comparison of experiments;
- rough indiscernibility under an attribute family;
- viability/reachability equivalence;
- local compatibility with global obstruction.

This produced a central correction: ORION knowledge does not live in one unconditional metric space. The relevant relation depends on what can be observed, changed, decided, preserved and afforded.

## 4. Scientifically opposite cases were separated

Several pairs that look identical at a superficial level have opposite meanings:

- **gauge/representation redundancy:** different descriptions represent one underlying state;
- **inverse-problem equifinality:** different underlying states remain observationally indistinguishable;
- **safe abstraction:** discarded distinctions are proved irrelevant to protected consequences;
- **unsafe coarse-graining:** the current target survives but later scientific queries fail;
- **independent evidence:** multiple observations add information;
- **correlated agreement:** multiple validators repeat one failure mode.

These distinctions became mandatory hostile controls.

## 5. The search method was itself reduced

Cross-domain structural hunting initially looked like a paper-level novelty. Further search found mature parents:

- structure mapping and analogy;
- TRIZ;
- design-by-analogy;
- General Morphological Analysis;
- Function–Behaviour–Structure methods;
- C-K design theory;
- literature-based discovery;
- science-of-science work on unusual combinations.

Therefore the candidate V2 contribution is not “use analogy.” It is narrower:

`independent coverage + native donor reconstruction + typed contextual structure + strongest donor-product reduction + counter-probes + provenance + authority + machine replay + protected scientific evaluation`.

## 6. Donors challenged V1 procedures

V1's earliest-stage failure attribution is useful for serial pipelines: downstream ranking cannot explain a miss when candidate generation recovered nothing. Multiple-fault diagnosis, distributed control and interaction attribution show this cannot become a universal causal ontology.

The emerging V2 responsibility space must allow:

- single cause;
- ordered upstream cause;
- multiple independent causes;
- distributed control;
- interaction-only responsibility;
- latent shared cause;
- unresolved attribution.

This was a concrete example of the intended programme: remote donors can scope or replace a V1 rule rather than merely add a feature.

## 7. Solver and Jump synthesis

The full problem-solving study then compared scientific-discovery dual search, Bayesian experimental design, POMDPs, systems engineering, blackboard/cognitive architectures, medicine/diagnosis, CEGIS/CEGAR, design theory, quality-diversity/open-ended search, self-improving agents and self-driving laboratories.

The current synthesis is a nested, obligation-driven scientific control system with:

- problem/frontier contract compilation;
- plural scientific state;
- coupled hypothesis and experiment/action spaces;
- adaptive ordinary solving;
- independent verification and authority;
- minimum-responsible-level Jump;
- protected meta-solver learning;
- typed scientific terminals and reopening.

V1 Jump evidence remains binding: representation transition was real on the bounded worlds, but the strongest regime-revision parent tied the ORION composition. V2 therefore treats Jump as a donor-reduced escalation policy, not a restored universal mechanic.

## 8. Why a separate repository

A separate ORION-V2 repository prevents prospective work from contaminating frozen V1 history and keeps new publication identities distinct. It also makes the gate visible: research and protocol work may proceed now, while implementation and outcome-generating evaluation remain blocked until the exact V1 handoff.

## 9. Current uncertainty

The current architecture and paper candidates are hypotheses. All may be merged, split, replaced by donors or terminated as `CANNOT_CHECK`. The repository exists to make those changes traceable rather than to defend the first design.
