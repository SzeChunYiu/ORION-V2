# Witnessed Scientific Jump Research Programme V0

**Status:** candidate frontier programme; blocked by V1 freeze.

## 1. V1 inheritance

V1 established a bounded difference between additional search/model expansion inside a frozen representation and a validated transition beyond that representation. It did not establish incremental ORION value over the strongest verified regime-revision parent. V1 also decomposed broad labels such as tension, thought experiment and concept invention into multiple witness and transformation families.

V2 therefore must not implement `if stuck: be creative()`.

## 2. Definition hypothesis

A Jump is a governed proposal to change the search space in which solving occurs. It is serious only when:

1. the incumbent closure/level is explicit;
2. an obstruction, ceiling, non-identifiability or mismatch is witnessed;
3. narrower repair and ordinary expansion are tested or ruled out;
4. the transformation and its parent lineage are explicit;
5. correspondence, preservation and reopening obligations are constructed;
6. newly reachable protected consequences are predicted before outcome access;
7. protected falsifiers and strongest donor products are frozen;
8. adoption authority remains external.

## 3. Candidate escalation ladder

- `J0 ACTION/PARAMETER`: another move, route, query or experiment.
- `J1 LOCAL REPAIR/COMPOSITION`: implementation/interface/evidence repair or known-method composition.
- `J2 MODEL/HYPOTHESIS EXPANSION`: enlarge the model class inside the governing representation/evaluation regime.
- `J3 REPRESENTATION/ABSTRACTION/REGIME`: change state variables, scale, quotient, relation structure or coordinate system.
- `J4 PROBLEM/OBJECTIVE/CONSTRAINT`: reformulate target obligations or proxies after responsibility is shown.
- `J5 METHOD/OPERATOR/TOOL/INSTRUMENT`: invent a new algorithm, inference rule, DSL, program primitive, experiment or measurement channel.
- `J6 WORKFLOW/CONTROL/META-SKILL`: change action generation, selection, scheduling, evaluation, repair or learning.
- `J7 FRAMEWORK`: revise schema, operator grammar, dependency/provenance/history semantics or research policy.
- `J8 CONSTITUTION`: propose a change to protected validation/authority/integrity; self-certification is forbidden.

The minimum sufficient level wins. Higher escalation is overreach.

## 4. Strong trigger candidates

- certified expressive ceiling;
- structural non-identifiability under current probes;
- predictive checks/model criticism showing model-family inadequacy;
- genuine counterexample not removable by local repair;
- local solutions with global obstruction;
- repeated protected residuals across independent routes;
- responsibility assigned to representation/objective/method/workflow/framework;
- remote donor operation outside incumbent closure;
- evidenced exploration collapse;
- strategic response invalidating evaluator/model assumptions;
- semantic or scale drift invalidating old certificate transport.

## 5. Insufficient triggers

- poor score alone;
- timeout or budget exhaustion;
- unavailable provider/search route;
- failure to retrieve a known answer;
- anomaly or disagreement alone;
- a new name, prompt, macro or parameterization;
- generated novelty language;
- evaluator/denominator/constraint changes that raise reward;
- proposal after protected-outcome leakage.

## 6. Transformation portfolios

### Counterfactual/problem operations

Intervention, assumption relaxation, reference-frame transformation, system coupling, formal counterexample worlds, limiting cases, symmetry change and scale change.

### Concept/representation operations

Latent-object introduction, contextual splitting, quotienting, relational lifting, bridge construction, coordinate/observable change, dimension addition/removal and tree-to-reticulate transformation.

### Method/process operations

Model grammar expansion, symbolic operator/program/theorem synthesis, new instruments, experiment-policy revision, protected objective evolution, skill/meta-skill evolution and transactional framework revision.

Each family must be reduced against its native parents before ORION ownership.

## 7. Candidate machine receipts

### `JumpTrigger.v2`

Binds current level/closure, witness, responsibility hypotheses, discriminator, coverage/censoring and proposal-only status.

### `JumpProposal.v2`

Binds base state/framework, level, transformation family, donors, old-ceiling witness, new objects/operators, correspondence, affected commitments, preservation/reopening, predicted reach, negative-history migration, costs, risks, tests, falsifiers and rollback.

### `JumpEvaluationReceipt.v2`

Binds frozen proposal/evaluator, incumbent and donor-product baselines, old-ceiling result, fresh reach, preservation, semantic fidelity, cost/risk, false-escalation controls, replay and external adoption state.

## 8. Governed lifecycle

1. detect impasse without calling it a Jump;
2. diagnose responsibility across levels and causal topologies;
3. test minimum repairs first;
4. certify/bound incumbent insufficiency;
5. search native and remote donors;
6. generate a diverse proposal portfolio plus non-Jump controls;
7. construct correspondence/preservation before protected outcomes;
8. freeze proposals, budgets, evaluators and authority;
9. run cheap formal/simulation counter-probes;
10. run protected fresh evaluation;
11. externally adopt, reject, defer or retain incomparable successors;
12. monitor future-generation performance and reopen on drift.

## 9. Quantitative measurements

- genuine-insufficiency trigger precision/recall;
- minimum-responsible-level accuracy;
- false-Jump and missed-Jump rate;
- old-closure ceiling correctness;
- incremental justified reachability;
- newly discharged obligations per cost;
- preservation and selective-reopening correctness;
- semantic fidelity/comparability;
- distinguishing-probe quality;
- useful frontier breadth versus useless dispersion;
- donor discovery and donor-product tie rate;
- resource/risk-adjusted value;
- fresh transfer;
- later-successor generation;
- replay and authority-integrity violations.

## 10. Protected tier ladder

Tiers 0–8 force lower levels to win on their native cases, from direct methods through local repair, model expansion, representation transition, objective reformulation, method/tool invention, workflow/meta-skill evolution, framework revision and constitution interaction.
