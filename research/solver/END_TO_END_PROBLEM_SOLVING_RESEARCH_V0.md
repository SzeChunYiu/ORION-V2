# End-to-End Scientific Problem Solving Research V0

**Status:** deep donor synthesis open; specification only; blocked by V1 freeze.

## 1. Scientific question

When ORION-V2 receives a problem—or scans a frontier—what machine process leads from the initial request/observation to a justified solution, partial result, new research question, regime transition or honest non-solution terminal?

The solver must preserve the full useful V1 capability surface and add frontier capability without treating the V1 root sequence as the final ontology.

## 2. Parent-field synthesis

No single parent supplies the whole system. The principal owners are:

- scientific discovery as coupled hypothesis and experiment search;
- Bayesian experimental design, active learning, POMDPs and metareasoning;
- systems engineering, requirements, verification/validation and change control;
- business-process management, operations, scheduling and exception recovery;
- blackboard/cognitive architectures and impasse-triggered subgoaling;
- differential and multiple-fault diagnosis;
- theorem proving, CEGIS/CEGAR and formal synthesis;
- design theory, analogy, TRIZ and representation change;
- quality-diversity/open-ended search and stepping-stone archives;
- autonomous laboratories and scientific workflow systems;
- scientific governance, independent validation and adoption.

The possible V2 residual is a donor-faithful integration under typed obligations, plural scientific state, source-bound evidence, protected authority, witnessed escalation and machine-replayable terminals.

## 3. Candidate nested architecture

### L0 — protected constitution and execution substrate

Tools, devices, environments, side effects, resource ceilings, protected evaluators, safety/legal constraints, execution receipts and adoption authority.

### L1 — problem/frontier contract compiler

Two entry modes:

- contracted problem: compile a request into exact success, evidence, scope, constraint and terminal obligations;
- frontier scouting: propose research opportunities from anomalies, contradictions, unexplained regularities, missing bridges, model inadequacy and unmet scientific needs.

### L2 — plural scientific state

Maintain source-bound observations, multiple representations, live hypotheses/models/solutions, support/defeaters, uncertainty, identifiability, dependence, coverage, costs, authority, provenance and history.

### L3 — ordinary solving policy

Choose among admitted search, observation, experiment, computation, proof, comparison, synthesis, verification, expert request, decomposition, repair, reopening and stopping actions.

### L4 — scientific discovery/model loop

Couple hypothesis generation, prediction, discriminating experiment selection, execution, analysis, model criticism and model-class expansion.

### L5 — Jump/escalation policy

Invoke only after lower-level insufficiency is witnessed. See the Jump programme.

### L6 — meta-solver learning

Learn retrieval, context, experiment, method, repair, scheduling and meta-skill policies from immutable episodes; changes remain proposal-side until protected fresh transfer.

### L7 — synthesis, independent validation and adoption

Assemble complete bounded solutions and keep content correctness, scientific validity, utility, authority and publication distinct.

## 5. Reference lifecycle

This is a partial-order reference, not a mandatory serial pipeline.

- **E0 Bind:** actor, scope, tools, authority and resources.
- **E1 Compile obligations:** define justified success and non-success terminals.
- **E2 Orient:** identify decision class, contexts, observables, interventions and scales.
- **E3 Acquire:** retrieve literature, data, formal libraries, prior experiments, donors and negative episodes.
- **E4 Model:** construct decompositions, alternatives, constraints, dependencies and equivalence classes.
- **E5 Select action:** compare admissible moves by protected value.
- **E6 Execute:** bind exact action/environment/tool/device, observations, cost and side effects.
- **E7 Interpret:** update source projections, hypotheses, uncertainty, coverage and closures.
- **E8 Challenge:** check, replicate, counterexample, sensitivity-test and independently review.
- **E9 Diagnose obstacle:** information, implementation, method, model, representation, objective, instrument, workflow, framework, constitution or multi-cause.
- **E10 Continue class:** continue, branch, parallelize, repair, acquire new evidence, expand model, Jump, ask externally or stop.
- **E11 Synthesize candidate terminal:** complete evidence-bound solution with limitations.
- **E12 Validate/adopt:** independent scientific and authority decision.
- **E13 Monitor/reopen:** preserve episodes and reopen under drift, revocation or new evidence.

## 6. Action selection

A provisional non-scalar value vector is:

`Value(a|Z) = (hard_gate_pass, obligation_reduction, distinguishing_power, delta_JReach, scientific_value, option_value, diversity_value, -cost, -risk, -authority_exposure)`.

Different parent theories may justify lexicographic, constrained, robust, Pareto, viability or other policies. The solver must preserve incomparability rather than force one reward.

## 7. Problem decomposition

A decomposition requires:

- subproblem identities/interfaces;
- shared variables, resources and evidence;
- independence or coupling assumptions;
- cross-subproblem constraints;
- recomposition/gluing rules;
- authority/provenance boundaries;
- non-commuting effects;
- conditions where local solutions fail globally.

Support independent parallel work, coupled joint problems, competing hypotheses, hierarchical refinement, global obstructions and interaction-only failure.
