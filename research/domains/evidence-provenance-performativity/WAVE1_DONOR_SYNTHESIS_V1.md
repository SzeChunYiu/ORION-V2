# Evidence Dependence, Reticulate Provenance and Performative Evaluation Synthesis V1

**Status:** first integrated donor round.

## Dependence-aware evidence

Three supporting sources or validators may share data, models, instruments, prompts, institutions or copied claims. Counting them as independent confirmations can create false authority.

Parent fields include dependent-effects meta-analysis, graphical models, survey design effects, common-cause reliability, ensemble diversity, correlated-fault systems and sociology of copying.

The implemented conservative reference graph connects declared dependencies into components. Component count is diagnostic, not authority. Missing required pair dispositions yields unknown.

**Limit:** connected components do not estimate partial correlation or effective sample size; real domains need quantitative dependence models.

## Reticulate provenance

A paper, dataset or framework may inherit representation, method, evidence, calibration and evaluator from different parents. Tree ancestry can hide component-specific revocation.

Parents include W3C PROV, data/process provenance, SBOM/version control, workflow lineage, stemmatology and phylogenetic networks.

The implemented DAG labels parent–child edges by relation and component, supports multi-parent ancestry and component-scoped descendant/revocation queries.

Open residual: decision-relevant affected-commitment calculation for claims/certificates/closures.

## Performative evaluation

A published evaluator, policy or prediction may change behaviour and the data-generating process. A metric can improve while the protected outcome worsens.

Parents include Lucas critique, Goodhart/specification gaming, mechanism design, strategic classification and performative prediction.

The implemented finite diagnostic measures total-variation distribution shift, metric/protected-outcome change and whether a control/intervention identity exists.

Fail-closed terminals:

- `PROXY_IMPROVES_PROTECTED_OUTCOME_WORSENS`
- `PERFORMATIVE_SHIFT_DETECTED_CAUSE_CANNOT_CHECK`
- `PERFORMATIVE_SHIFT_DETECTED_CONTROL_BOUND`
- `NO_MATERIAL_PERFORMATIVE_SHIFT_UNDER_BOUND`

## Integrated implications

1. Evidence promotion needs dependence structure.
2. Validator diversity is model/data/instrument/lineage diversity.
3. Provenance identifies inherited components.
4. Revocation propagates along component-relevant descendants.
5. Evaluation epochs matter because deployment changes targets.
6. Pre/post scores need response and measurement analysis.
7. Strategic response may reopen models/evaluators/objectives but does not authorize Jump.

## Known-answer cases

- three sources, two sharing data, yield at most two components;
- missing dependence dispositions remain unknown;
- representation/method inherited from different parents;
- component-scoped revocation;
- metric improves while protected outcome falls;
- shift without control leaves cause `CANNOT_CHECK`.
