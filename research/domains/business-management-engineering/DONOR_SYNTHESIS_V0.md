**Status:** first native-problem reduction; not saturation and not V2 novelty authority.

## Expert cell and delegated roles

- **Operations-research scientist:** sequential decisions, scheduling, queues, resource allocation, robust optimization and value of information.
- **Business-process and operations-management scientist:** workflow nets, conformance, exception handling, capacity, bottlenecks and service systems.
- **Systems/safety engineer:** requirements, interfaces, architecture, verification, validation, configuration control and hazards.
- **Reliability/quality engineer:** FMEA, fault trees, statistical process control, corrective action and multiple-fault recovery.
- **Organizational-learning and innovation researcher:** routines, exploration/exploitation, escalation, absorptive capacity and ambidexterity.
- **Governance/accounting/internal-control researcher:** custody, segregation of duties, audit evidence, authorization and management override.
- **Supply-chain/resilience researcher:** propagation, dependency, inventory, bullwhip, disruption recovery and option value.

Each lane was asked to identify what it already owns, what it would reject in the provisional V2 solver, and what machine variables it requires.

## 1. Operations research and sequential decisions

### Native problem

Choose actions, experiments, allocations or schedules under objectives, constraints, uncertainty, costs, finite resources and future consequences.

### Native objects

- state and information state;
- action and feasible-action correspondence;
- resource/capacity vector;
- objective or vector of objectives;
- transition/observation model;
- policy, stopping time and recourse;
- robust/adversarial scenario set;
- dual prices, shadow costs and opportunity costs.

### Strong mechanisms to absorb

- dynamic programming and approximate control;
- POMDP/belief-state planning;
- stochastic and robust optimization;
- multi-armed/active information acquisition;
- queueing and scheduling;
- value of information/computation;
- decomposition, column generation and recourse;
- Pareto/lexicographic constrained decisions.

### Pressure on V2

A fixed ORION stage order is not justified when several admissible actions compete under cost and information. V2 must make action selection a policy problem, but it must not claim generic optimal control or value of information.

### Candidate V2 residual

Scientific action selection with non-compensatory validity/authority/integrity constraints, source-bound evidence, representation changes, honest epistemic terminals and preservation/reopening.

### Falsifier

If a strongest POMDP/robust decision parent supplied with the same typed scientific state and constraints matches every V2 policy judgment, use the parent and delete a distinct solver-control claim.

## 2. Business process management and process mining

### Native problem

Specify, execute, monitor, audit and improve multi-stage organizational processes with concurrency, decisions, exceptions, roles and compliance obligations.

### Native objects

- events, cases and traces;
- tasks/activities;
- gateways and partial orders;
- roles/resources;
- process model and conformance relation;
- exception/compensation paths;
- service-level and compliance constraints;
- process variants and drift.

### Strong mechanisms to absorb

- workflow/Petri-net semantics;
- soundness, deadlock and liveness analysis;
- process discovery and conformance checking;
- partial-order/concurrent execution;
- exception, compensation and escalation handling;
- process variant comparison and drift detection;
- provenance-rich event logs.

### Pressure on V2

`FRAME -> SEARCH -> ...` is one process model, not the ontology of scientific work. Every surviving edge needs a causal, informational, authority or resource justification. Scientific state may commute while audit order remains distinct.

### Candidate V2 residual

Obligation-driven process adaptation in which changing the process can invalidate scientific closures, require semantic transport and remain proposal-side until protected adoption.

### Falsifier

If an established workflow/process formalism with typed data, policies and scientific state can express all required transitions and terminals, V2 should orchestrate that formalism rather than invent another workflow calculus.

## 3. Systems engineering

### Native problem

Transform stakeholder needs into traceable requirements, logical/physical architecture, interfaces, implementation, integration, verification, validation, transition and lifecycle control.

### Native objects

- stakeholder expectation and operational concept;
- requirement and verification method;
- functional/logical architecture;
- interface/control document;
- design alternative and trade study;
- configuration baseline and change request;
- verification versus validation evidence;
- risk and technical-performance measure.

### Strong mechanisms to absorb

- recursive requirements decomposition;
- bidirectional traceability;
- architecture and interface management;
- trade-space analysis;
- verification matrices;
- configuration/change control;
- lifecycle reviews and readiness gates;
- independent validation.

### Pressure on V2

A `ProblemContract` must trace requested outcomes to obligations, observations, designs, tests and terminals. Verification that a candidate satisfies a formal requirement is different from validation that the requirement captures the scientific need.

### Candidate V2 residual

Open-world scientific requirements that can themselves be diagnosed, reframed and reopened while preserving exact history and external adoption authority.

### Falsifier

If systems-engineering lifecycle and change-control parents already solve the complete scientific contract problem under the same uncertainty and authority interface, ORION’s contribution contracts to an implementation/domain adapter.

## 4. Reliability, diagnosis and safety engineering

### Native problem

Predict, detect, localize, contain and recover from component, interaction, process and common-cause failures while preserving acceptable service and safety.

### Native objects

- component/function;
- failure mode, cause and effect;
- fault tree/event tree;
- hazard and barrier;
- diagnostic hypothesis;
- detectability and test;
- redundancy/common-cause factor;
- maintenance/repair/rollback action.

### Strong mechanisms to absorb

- FMEA/FMECA;
- fault-tree and event-tree analysis;
- reliability block and Markov models;
- model-based and multiple-fault diagnosis;
- common-cause/dependent failure modelling;
- defence in depth and safety cases;
- corrective/preventive action;
- graceful degradation and fault containment.

### Pressure on V2

The solver cannot universalize one earliest responsible stage. Failures may be serial-upstream, independent multiple faults, common-cause, distributed, interaction-only or unresolved. Agreement among validators may be a common-mode failure rather than independent assurance.

### Candidate V2 residual

Responsibility diagnosis across epistemic, semantic, authority and framework levels, connected to minimum justified repair and selective reopening.

### Falsifier

If native diagnosis/safety cases supplied with V2’s exact coordinates make all relevant responsibility and recovery decisions, retain those parents and remove distinct ORION ownership.

## 5. Quality management and continuous improvement

### Native problem

Maintain process capability and customer/scientific fitness while identifying assignable causes, reducing variation and improving processes without outcome manipulation.

### Native objects

- customer/fitness requirement;
- process and measurement system;
- common versus special cause;
- control limit and capability;
- defect/nonconformance;
- root-cause hypothesis;
- corrective/preventive action;
- standard work and improvement cycle.

### Strong mechanisms to absorb

- statistical process control;
- measurement-system analysis;
- design of experiments;
- PDCA/PDSA and DMAIC;
- root-cause and corrective-action discipline;
- process capability and acceptance sampling;
- standardization after validated improvement.

### Pressure on V2

An observed failure may be process variation, measurement error, special cause or inadequate specification. “Improve until positive” is prohibited; baseline, measurement and acceptance criteria must be frozen before outcome access.

### Candidate V2 residual

Versioned scientific improvement in which negative results and prior standards remain addressable, objectives/evaluators cannot be changed to manufacture success, and framework-level change requires independent adoption.

## 6. Organizational learning, innovation and R&D management

### Native problem

Allocate attention and resources between exploitation and exploration, preserve organizational memory, absorb external knowledge and change routines/structures when local learning is insufficient.

### Native objects

- routine and capability;
- exploration/exploitation portfolio;
- aspiration/reference level;
- organizational memory;
- absorptive capacity;
- project/experiment portfolio;
- escalation and governance level;
- innovation pipeline and stage gate.

### Strong mechanisms to absorb

- exploration/exploitation models;
- organizational learning curves and routines;
- absorptive capacity;
- ambidexterity and portfolio governance;
- stage-gate/real-options R&D management;
- postmortems and learning from failure;
- dynamic capabilities and strategic renewal.

### Pressure on V2

Self-improvement and Jump are not unprecedented. V2 must distinguish local exploitation, model exploration, process redesign, capability invention, organizational/framework revision and externally governed constitutional change.

### Candidate V2 residual

Machine-executable, evidence-bound and preservation-aware escalation across these levels, evaluated on future scientific reach and later-successor ability rather than current reward alone.

## 7. Governance, audit, accounting and internal control

### Native problem

Ensure assets, decisions, reports and changes are authorized, evidenced, segregated, auditable and resistant to override, fraud and conflicts of interest.

### Native objects

- principal, role and mandate;
- transaction and approval;
- control objective and control activity;
- evidence and audit trail;
- segregation of duties;
- materiality/risk;
- exception and management override;
- assurance opinion.

### Strong mechanisms to absorb

- separation of initiation, custody, approval and review;
- authorization matrices;
- audit sampling and evidence sufficiency;
- reconciliations and exception reports;
- internal-control frameworks;
- independent assurance;
- change/override logging.

### Pressure on V2

The candidate that benefits from a scientific transition cannot solely author, execute, evaluate and adopt it. A correct result can remain unauthorized. Audit evidence is not the same as scientific truth, but it is required for accountable adoption.

### Candidate V2 residual

Typed scientific authority and evidence-domain non-fungibility across assertion, experiment, publication, tool action and self/framework change.

# Cross-donor synthesis

The strongest common object is not a fixed workflow but a governed transition system:

`W = (S, O, E, Pre, Eff, Info, Cost, Risk, Auth, Dep, Hist, Inv, Cap)`

where `Cap` records people/tool/instrument/queue capacity. Different parents own projections of this object.

## Candidate structural consequences for ORION-V2

1. **Workflow is a partial order/policy, not a universal list.**
2. **Problem contracts need requirements-to-evidence traceability.**
3. **Verification and validation remain separate.**
4. **Responsibility topology is plural.**
5. **Capacity, queues and opportunity costs are scientific-control variables.**
6. **Change levels need minimum-responsible-level escalation.**
7. **Audit/segregation protects adoption but does not create truth.**
8. **Improvement must preserve negative history and frozen acceptance criteria.**
9. **Resilience requires substitution, containment and option-value modelling.**
10. **Business/engineering parents may replace substantial V2-native machinery.**
