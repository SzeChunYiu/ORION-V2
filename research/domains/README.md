# External Knowledge Domains

This subtree recursively stores native-domain research. Use the pattern:

```text
<domain>/
  README.md
  canonical-problems/
  concepts/
  quantitative-models/
  mechanisms/
  mappings-to-v2/
  donor-reconstructions/
  evidence/
  benchmarks/
  open-residuals/
  history/
```

Only create subfolders when they contain real artifacts.

Initial priority domains include:

- business process and operations management;
- organizational learning, governance and strategy;
- systems, control, reliability and design engineering;
- political methodology and institutions;
- psychometrics, metrology and survey methodology;
- diachronic/computational linguistics;
- causal inference and inverse problems;
- formal learning/query theory;
- cognitive science, design theory and problem finding;
- open-ended search and autonomous science;
- law/evidence/authority;
- stemmatology, cultural evolution and provenance;
- sociology, epidemiology and distributed dependence.

A domain programme is a research obligation, not a V2 framework change.
