# Autonomous Science, Open-Ended Search and Frontier Jump Donor Synthesis V1

**Status:** current-through-August-2026 research pass; candidate mechanisms remain non-authorizing.

## Closed-loop autonomous science

A-Lab integrates computational databases, literature-derived synthesis knowledge, robotics, ML interpretation and active learning for inorganic-material synthesis. It is a constrained domain-specific loop, not a universal scientist.

**V2 lesson:** physical adapters require instruments, calibration, measurement semantics, failures and domain authority.

Tool-using chemistry agents demonstrate planning and execution across web, code and robotics, but tool competence remains distinct from experimental validity and claim authority.

## Open-ended scientific search

Novelty search, MAP-Elites and POET preserve behavioural diversity, niche elites or co-evolving environments and agents. They establish stepping-stone value under deceptive objectives.

**V2 lesson:** archives and diversity matter, but validity/resource gates are non-compensatory and novelty is not a Jump witness.

## Current 2026 evidence

Tang and Yang report that research-agent ideas are more concentrated and closer to seed literature than later human follow-on work, with differences primarily from recombining existing methods.

Heuresis compares greedy, archive-based, evolutionary and divergent strategies. Search steers quality/diversity/novelty distributions, but completely novel ideas are rare, do not reach the quality frontier and reward-hacking/fabrication requires explicit detection.

**V2 lesson:** breadth, novelty and quality are separate. Hidden negatives, fabrication detection, donor reduction and protected significance are required.

## Self-improvement and meta-skill evolution

Code-, skill-, meta-skill- and objective-evolution systems own much of self-improvement.

**V2 lesson:** code, skill, workflow, objective, framework and constitution changes belong to different Jump levels and remain proposal-side until fresh transfer/external adoption.
