## Central question

When can evidence identify the target, which query/intervention separates live alternatives, and when is continued acquisition of the same information type provably useless?

## Donor families

### Query learning

Angluin distinguishes membership, equivalence, subset, superset, disjointness and exhaustiveness queries. Learning power depends on the query interface.

**V2 consequence:** `DISCRIMINATE` chooses among typed probes and records which equivalence classes each can separate.

### Teaching dimension

Teaching dimension asks for a smallest labelled set uniquely identifying a target in a concept class.

**V2 consequence:** identifying-evidence cost is relative to a frozen hypothesis class and protocol.

### Statistical identifiability and experiment comparison

Distinct parameters/structures must induce distinguishable observables under the registered design. Blackwell/Le Cam asks what decision value is preserved or lost when substituting experiments.

**V2 distinctions:** insufficient sample; observational equivalence; structural non-identifiability; decision-equivalent information; interventionally distinguishable alternatives.

### Causal discovery

Observational Markov-equivalent graphs can encode different causal structures; interventions or assumptions may separate them.

**V2 consequence:** observational position is not causal identity.

### Rough indiscernibility

Objects are equivalent relative to selected attributes; lower/upper approximations express definite/possible membership.

**V2 consequence:** `UNKNOWN` may be a probe-relative equivalence class, not scalar confidence.

### Computational mechanics

Histories inducing the same target-future distribution can be merged for prediction.

**V2 consequence:** retain state coordinates for declared future sufficiency, not maximal description.

### Viability/reachability

Viability kernels and capture basins own constrained safe reachability.

**V2 consequence:** ORION's residual is evidence, authority, provenance and reopening around parent-owned geometry.

## Hostile controls

- more same-type samples cannot separate equivalent candidates;
- observationally equivalent causal models separate under intervention;
- predictive equivalence with different authority/provenance;
- current-safe quotient destroys a later obligation;
- low-cost and high-information probes remain incomparable;
- censored route is not non-identifiability;
- misspecified hypothesis class invalidates query/teaching guarantees.
