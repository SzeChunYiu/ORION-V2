## F1 · P1 · capability-level revocation over ⊕ warrants

**Objects.** A capability `c` (here: a word is interpretable) rests on the profile `P_c = ⊕_i w_i`, one alternative per lesson (`w_i = {e_i}`, each lesson its own INSTRUCTION evidence id, as `ChatSession._teach` admits it). A revocation notice names one id `e` and comes from a speaker with authority `Auth ⊆ E` (the ids the speaker may revoke: authored by them, or all under administrative authority) and an intent `∈ {LESSON, CAPABILITY, UNSPECIFIED}`. Three readings, each a policy on the lattice: `revoke_named` (R ∪ {e}), `revoke_all` (R ∪ (evidence(P_c) ∩ Auth)), and `report_remainder` (revoke the named id and name the alternatives still LIVE). `notice_semantics(intent, …)` is the licensed reply: LESSON → revoke-named, remainder reported if non-empty; CAPABILITY → revoke-all, `CAPABILITY_REMOVED` iff nothing survives else `CAPABILITY_PERSISTS_UNAUTHORIZED_REMAINDER` naming the survivors; UNSPECIFIED → revoke-named and `CLARIFY_REMAINDER` (ask) iff something survives. An unauthorized id is refused under every reading.

**Theorem** (exhaustive: 85 antichains of ≤ 3 alternatives of size ≤ 2 over 4 ids × every named id × 16 authority sets × 5 prior revocations = 21 760 cases, 10 880 authorized). (i) Revoke-named kills the capability iff the named id is a *cut* of the live alternatives (belongs to every one of them); on the M12 two-sense word it is not a cut, so the word stays interpretable — the observed `revoked_stops` 4/6. (ii) Revoke-all kills the capability iff the speaker's authority is a *hitting set* of the live alternatives; otherwise no licensed policy removes it and the only honest outputs name the unauthorized survivors. (iii) Monotone: revoke-all revokes a superset of revoke-named, its remainder is exactly the unauthorized alternatives. (iv) Under every intent the reply claims removal (`REVOKED`/`CAPABILITY_REMOVED`) iff the capability is DEAD afterwards, and the reported remainder equals the surviving alternatives (32 640 checks; 7 488 `CLARIFY` cases). **Proof.** (i)–(iii) are the definition of `live` on the antichain (an alternative dies iff it meets `R`); (iv) by construction; all four enumerated. ∎

**Hostile** `mutant_revoke_named_silent` (the runtime's `revoke <eid>` shape: revoke one id, answer "Revoked.", never report): applied and caught on all 10 584 cases where a CAPABILITY/UNSPECIFIED notice leaves a live remainder — the capability persists silently; on the M12 word (`{L_early}, {L_late}`, revoke `L_late`) the honest reply is `CLARIFY_REMAINDER` (unspecified) or `CAPABILITY_REMOVED` (capability intent with authority over both) or `CAPABILITY_PERSISTS_UNAUTHORIZED_REMAINDER` (authority over `L_late` only); the mutant says `REVOKED` while the word is LIVE. **No-alarm:** a single-warrant capability — all readings coincide, nothing to report (the fresh-arm 6/6 shape). **Tightened:** "which reading is licensed" has no lattice answer; the lattice fixes only (i)–(iii); the licensed reading is the *intent × authority* policy above, and the one universally forbidden output is a live remainder that is not reported. Parent: ATMS environments/nogoods (de Kleer 1986), minimal hitting sets (Reiter 1987) — PARENT_OWNED for the lattice facts.

## F2 · P2 · the unit of inference for lifetime residuals

**Objects.** A lifetime is one path; its tasks are dependent through shared state. The M12 orderings O1–O3 re-order one frozen task stream on a deterministic machine with phase A always first; the recorded phase-A vectors are byte-identical across O1–O3 for every arm (`M12_LIFETIME_EVAL_V2.json` `phases.*.*.A.post_deployment`). For a predeclared direction, the discordant-pair statistic is the one-sided exact sign test `P(Bin(n_d, ½) ≥ wins)`; the M12 `revoked_stops` row (OCM 4/6, parent 6/6) is `(wins_OCM, wins_parent) = (0, 2)`.

**Theorem.** (i) *Pooling re-orderings is pseudo-replication with exact inflation in the enumerated tables:* pooling `k` identical replicas multiplies `n_d` by `k` and never raises the selected-tail statistic `P(Bin(k n_d, 1/2) >= k max(w,l))` (54 tables `n_d ≤ 6`, `k ∈ {2,3}`); on the M12 row `p = 1/4, 1/16, 1/64` for `k = 1, 2, 3` — a selected-tail threshold crossing at `k = 3` from zero new information; the valid two-sided values are `1/2, 1/8, 1/32`, also crossing at `k = 3` (between-replica variance 0; Kish design effect with `ρ = 1` gives `n_eff = n`); 16 of the 54 tables cross `α` under pooling. (ii) *Item-level inference under within-lifetime dependence:* if the `n` items of one lifetime fall into blocks of size `b` each decided by one latent fair coin, the item-level sign test at `α` has exact size `P(Bin(n/b, ½) ≥ ⌈c_α(n)/b⌉)`; at `n = 54` (the M12 conversations family) the size is `≤ α` only at `b = 1` and equals `65/256, 11/32, 1/2, 1/4, 1/2` at `b = 6, 9, 18, 27, 54`; a lifetime that is one latent coin has size `1/2` at every `n ≥ 5`. (iii) *Valid design:* `m` independent paired lifetimes (distinct stream seeds, OCM and parent on the same stream, one paired difference per lifetime), sign test over lifetimes: exact size `≤ α` for every `m ∈ 5…30`, no rejection region below `m = 5`; exact power `P(Bin(m, p) ≥ c_α(m))` — at `p = 0.9`: `m = 5 → 0.590, 8 → 0.813, 12 → 0.889, 20 → 0.989`; at `p = 0.7`: `m = 5 → 0.168, 20 → 0.416`. Pooling genuinely independent lifetimes is valid (size `≤ α` at `k·6`). (iv) Within-lifetime permutation over task order has the registered ordering family as its exact reference distribution and tests *order sensitivity*, not the residual; on the deterministic replicas the statistic is constant and `p = 1`. **Proof.** Exact binomial arithmetic; enumeration. ∎

**What the M12 numbers can and cannot support.** Every non-language family: DESCRIPTIVE, as recorded (S32) — `n_lifetimes = 1`, and the three orderings add nothing. The conversations family (`n = 54`, RESIDUAL_A): an *item-level* claim whose size is `α` under independent fair discordant signs under the null (or an independently justified randomization distribution); exchangeability alone is insufficient; P1 establishes potential shared-state dependence (item `k` depends on item `j < k` through the lexicon), and the direction of that dependence is unsigned, so the claim stands as "RESIDUAL_A conditional on an independently justified sign-test null, `n_lifetimes = 1`". A lifetime-level residual needs ≥ 5 paired lifetimes to reject at all and 8 for power > 0.8 at `p = 0.9`. **Hostile** `mutant_pool_orderings` reports `RESIDUAL_SUPPORTED` (p = 1/64) on three identical replicas; the honest unit accounting returns `(1, DESCRIPTIVE)`. **No-alarm:** six independent lifetimes all favouring OCM → `RESIDUAL_SUPPORTED` (p = 1/64) is valid. Parent: Hurlbert 1984 (pseudoreplication), Kish 1965 (design effect), exact sign test / McNemar — PARENT_OWNED; batch-4 D1 for the decision rule.

## F3 · P3 · observational limits of self-diagnosis (batch-5 E2 grammar)

**Objects.** The observational record of a failed run is its stage-field vector `f` (9 Boolean fields, 512 observations); the counterfactual coordinates are the three registered lower-level attempts and the ceiling witness (250 completions per observation). `C(f)` = the set of E2 verdicts consistent with `f` over all completions.

**Theorem.** (i) *Identifiability partition:* 504 observations have `|C(f)| = 1` (the six non-method classes are decided by `f`); the 8 nominal observations have `C(f) = {REPRESENTATION, m(f)}` exactly, where `m(f)` is the observational method verdict (router → operator → adapter → INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE). (ii) *No observational update:* both hypotheses predict `f` with certainty, so the likelihood ratio is 1 and the posterior on REPRESENTATION after `k` repeated identical failures equals the prior for every `k` (under a uniform prior over completions it is `1/250`: exactly one completion per nominal `f` is a valid certificate). (iii) *Minimal counterfactuals:* the adaptive policy "run the registered alternatives in ORDER, stop at the first success; if all fail read the attempt warrants from the ledger and evaluate the witness" returns the E2 verdict on all 65 536 fully-tried traces, uses 0 runs on the 504 identified observations, at most 3 runs, and `7/4` runs on average over the nominal traces; no fixed design with fewer than three runs identifies — for each of the 7 proper subsets of the alternatives there is a pair of nominal traces agreeing on `f`, on the subset and on the witness with verdicts REPRESENTATION vs INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. **Proof.** Enumeration of the grammar; (ii) is Bayes with likelihood 1. ∎

**Hostiles** `mutant_posterior_from_repetition` (odds double per repeated failure: `1/250 → 32/281` after 5) — caught against the constant honest posterior; `mutant_two_run_design` (router + operator only, then certify) — caught on the 16 traces where the untried adapter alternative restores the task (E2's "restoring operator" shape). **No-alarm:** 64 512 identified traces need zero counterfactual runs. **Tightened:** the packet's "bounds on the diagnosis distribution" is exactly (ii): from failure records alone the distribution over {REPRESENTATION, m(f)} is the prior, whatever the frequency; the diagnosis `weights` must be ablation-derived (as `diagnose.diagnose` already does) and a frequency term is provably inert. Parent: identifiability / likelihood principle (classic); batch-5 E2 (PROVED).

## F4 · P4 · false-structural-alarm lemma

**Objects.** An execution path of `k ≤ 3` objects, each with 1–2 alternative singleton warrants over 3 ids and a *used* alternative; `R ⊆ E`; an operator defect flag; a representation ceiling flag. A run succeeds iff every used warrant is LIVE, no defect, no ceiling. Layers in the OCM `diagnose.ORDER` prefix: D1 reroute a dead object to a LIVE alternative; D2 restore the operator (reinstate the used warrant's evidence / clear the defect); D3 lift the ceiling; `minimum_sufficient` = first layer whose single ablation restores the run. `certificate_status` = E3's LIVE clause first (`REINSTATE_FIRST` when any used warrant is DEAD), then the witness.

**Lemma** (26 208 fixtures, 24 498 failed runs). A failure *explained* by a DEAD warrant on the path (some used warrant DEAD and the run with it reinstated succeeds) has `minimum_sufficient ∈ {D1, D2}` and its certificate reads `REINSTATE_FIRST` — 4 842/4 842. **Converse conditions.** (a) A dead warrant alone does not explain the failure when a defect or ceiling coexists (14 526 fixtures): the certificate is still `REINSTATE_FIRST`; after reinstatement the fresh verdict is the true one (D2 for a defect, D3 with a valid `OBSTRUCTION` certificate for a ceiling, `None` when both remain) — reinstatement precedes escalation as a strict prefix, and a certificate issued before it is invalid even when the ceiling is real (4 842 such fixtures). (b) `minimum_sufficient ≤ D2` does not imply a dead warrant (3 420 plain-defect fixtures). (c) The certificate never reads `OBSTRUCTION` while any used warrant is DEAD (19 368 checks). **Proof.** Enumeration. ∎ **Hostile** `mutant_dead_is_structural` (M11 S5 / M12 F shape: one revoked dependency fails every task, read as D3) — caught on all 4 842 explained fixtures. **No-alarm:** on the 5 130 failed runs without a dead warrant the mutant equals the honest verdict. Parent: batch-5 E2/E3 (PROVED); model-based diagnosis with fault modes (Reiter 1987) — PARENT_OWNED.

## F5 · P5 · epistemic identity of a persistent machine

**Objects.** A ledger is an append-only event chain with genesis `h_0 = H(root)` and head `h_t = H(h_{t−1}, e_t)`; component fingerprints and adoption lineage are *derived from the ledger* (ADOPT events). Identity of machine `M` at `t`: `I_t = (genesis, h_t, F_t, L_t)`. A machine `M'` after a restart is the *same machine* iff `genesis' = genesis`, `h_t` lies on the reopened chain (prefix), the running fingerprints equal the ledger-derived `F'`, and `L'` extends `L_t`. A commitment made after the restart is *attributable* to `M` iff every evidence id it cites resolves on the active chain to the pre-restart bytes and the chain extends `h_t`.

**Theorem** (20 seeded machines, 92 atoms). (i) An honest restart (persist, reopen the same chain) is the same machine; every atom's liveness is unchanged (a restart is not a revocation); later commitments are attributable. (ii) Necessity of each coordinate: a restart that reopens a *different root* (the M12 S31 harness split) breaks genesis/prefix and its commitments cite ids that do not resolve — not attributable (20/20); a *same-path, replaced log* keeps the genesis and breaks the prefix (20/20); an *out-of-band component swap* keeps root and prefix and breaks `F` (20/20); *deleting an adoption event* rewrites the chain (20/20); a lineage forged onto a prefix-preserving chain is refused when the recorded `prev` contradicts the ledger. (iii) Lineage must live *in* the ledger: after an honest restart, identity passes yet a rollback artifact kept only in process memory is gone (20/20), while the ledger-derived lineage still permits rollback. **Proof.** Hash-chain prefix property (collision resistance assumed) and enumeration of the five restart variants. ∎

**Hostiles** (M12 S31): `stale_handle_check` (identity = object identity of a runtime handle) passes the split on all 20 machines — the machine still holds the old handle while writing to a fresh root; `path_check` (identity = root path string) passes the replaced log on all 20. Both caught by `same_machine`. **No-alarm:** an honest extension of the chain (a new adoption appended with the correct `prev`) is the same machine. **Tightened:** "ledger root" must mean the *chain* (genesis + head), never the path; the OCM `identity()` reports a path and a handle test. Parent: hash chains / linked timestamping (Haber–Stornetta 1991), fork consistency (Mazières–Shasha 2002; candidate, unverified), event sourcing — PARENT_OWNED; the identity triple is the OCM reading.

## F6 · MEG-02 graded half · the (max, ×) graded antichain is exact; scalar retraction is not a function

**Objects.** Grades `g(e) ∈ (0, 1]`; the (max, ×) value of a derivation family `D` (sets of ids) under `R` is `max{∏_{e∈d} g(e) : d ∈ D, d ∩ R = ∅}` (0 if none). The *graded antichain label* attaches a grade to each monomial of `canon(D)`; retraction drops the monomials that meet `R` (KS-T04b's 0/1 gate) and takes the max of the survivors — the grade rides outside the gate.

**Theorem** (469 families over 4 ids × 8 gradings × 16 revocations = 60 032 cases). (i) Absorption is exact for grades ≤ 1: the value over `canon(D)` equals the value over `D` (a superset monomial never outgrades its subset); necessary — a grade of 2 breaks it (witness recorded). (ii) Retraction on the graded label equals recomputation over the surviving derivations — exact-share retraction lifts to (max, ×) verbatim, with the same gates as KS-T04b. (iii) The graded value is positive iff the antichain is LIVE under `R` (KS-T21 unchanged; navigation gating untouched). (iv) *Scalar impossibility:* there is no function of (scalar value, grade of the revoked id) that yields the retracted value — smallest witness by exhaustive search over families on `{a, b}`: `D_1 = {{a}}` and `D_2 = {{a},{b}}` with `g(a) = 9/10, g(b) = 3/5` share the scalar `9/10`; revoking `a` gives `0` vs `3/5`. So the batch-5 R3 witness is exact for *scalars* and false for *graded antichains*: the graded half of MEG-02 has a witness, not an impossibility. (v) The (+, ×) reading is not a semiring value once sharing exists: read as a *measure* (independent evidence, revoked ids forced false, exact by world enumeration) it retracts exactly on the antichain (2 814 checks) and reproduces R3's `3/8`; the homomorphic sum equals the measure iff at most one derivation survives (840 cases) and strictly exceeds it otherwise (1 974 cases; union bound). **Proof.** (i) monotonicity of products of factors ≤ 1; (ii)–(iii) by (i) and the definition of `live`; (iv)–(v) enumeration. ∎

**Hostile** `mutant_scalar_subtract` ("retraction by subtraction" on the collapsed scalar) returns `0` on the witness where survivors give `3/5` — caught. **No-alarm:** a single-derivation family — subtraction and monomial retraction agree, and the (+, ×) sum equals the measure. **Status:** PROVED for (max, ×) graded antichains (the OCM's `WarrantProfile` can carry a grade per monomial); the (+, ×) measure is PARENT_OWNED (inclusion–exclusion; exact evaluation is #P-hard in general, Valiant 1979) and is *not* a semiring homomorphism — MEG-02's request for a graded *semiring* with exact-share retraction is answered only for source-indexed graded annotations and exact retraction. Scalar (max, times) evaluation is not a semiring homomorphism when evidence is shared: `e tensor e = e` but `g(e)^2 != g(e)` for `0 < g(e) < 1`; probability sums likewise fail. Parent: provenance semirings and specialisation (Green–Karvounarakis–Tannen 2007), absorptive/PosBool provenance, Viterbi semiring — PARENT_OWNED.

## F7 · MEG-28 · ceilings for J2/J3 on the 3-input Boolean tower (MEG-07 left OPEN)

**Objects.** The 256 Boolean functions of three inputs; ANF (Möbius) degree. A nested tower with a decidable ceiling per level: level 1 = affine (J1, composition closure of the registered features, 16 functions; batch-5 E3's fixture generalised), level 2 = degree ≤ 2 (J2 hypothesis expansion, 128), level 3 = all (J3 representation regime, 256). `C_ℓ(q)` := `q ∉ S_ℓ` with witness = a monomial of degree > ℓ whose sub-cube parity is odd (checked independently of the ANF). `minimum_level(q)` walks the ceiling chain; `assess_jump` = trigger admissibility (built from the incumbent level's certificate and nothing else) → lower level sufficient → proposed level insufficient → CANDIDATE.

**Theorem** (256 targets). (i) `minimum_level(q) = max(1, deg q)`; every ceiling certificate carries a checkable witness (368 checks). (ii) A governed Jump from incumbent `ℓ` to `ℓ'` is `CANDIDATE_FOR_PROTECTED_EVALUATION` iff `ℓ' = minimum_level(q) > ℓ` (768 checks): a jump above the minimum is refused as `NO_JUMP_NEEDED_LOWER_LEVEL_SUFFICIENT` (S6 harmful high-level Jump: 112 degree-2 targets proposed to level 3), a jump below it as `PROPOSED_LEVEL_INSUFFICIENT` (128), and a target inside the incumbent level yields no trigger (`INCUMBENT_INSUFFICIENCY_NOT_IDENTIFIED`). (iii) *The registered sequential algorithm can return the minimum when every queried ceiling is available:* with the level-2 predicate unavailable, `minimum_level` is `CANNOT_CHECK` for all 240 targets outside level 1 — never a jump to 3. (iv) Batch-5 E3 embeds: 2-input AND lifted to 3 inputs has a level-1 ceiling with witness `{x, y}` and minimum level 2; XOR is level-1 sufficient. **Proof.** ANF is a bijection; degree is monotone along the nested tower; enumeration. ∎

**Hostiles** `mutant_partial_level2` (level-2 enumeration forming only `xy`, `yz`) certifies a false level-2 ceiling on the 64 degree-2 targets with an `xz` term — caught (E3(iii)'s "enumeration must be the registered closure" at level 2); `mutant_poor_score_trigger` refused on all 256. **No-alarm:** the 16 affine targets produce no trigger at any proposed level. **Status:** PROVED for J2/J3 on this tower; the finite sequential procedure is calibrated by (iii). This does not prove the stated iff: a mathematical minimum can exist while its oracle is unavailable, and another decision procedure may still compute it. OPEN: ceilings for J4+ (problem reformulation, tool invention) and **MEG-07 per-source normalisation — not attempted in this batch**. Parent: Reed–Muller / ANF degree (classic), CEGAR / BMC completeness thresholds — PARENT_OWNED; batch-2 B8 and batch-5 E3 (PROVED).

## F8 · P6 · information-budget binding for a reference arm

**Objects.** Registered finite class ALL16 (Boolean functions of two inputs); `k` registered examples fix `k` truth-table rows, so the version space has exactly `2^{4−k}` members and identification needs `k = 4` (batch-4 D2's teaching-dimension bound). Channels carry budgets in bits; a channel whose budget cannot be measured (pretraining exposure) is `UNMEASURED`. Labels: `REFERENCE` (any unmeasured channel), `BELOW_LOWER_BOUND_UNDECLARED_CHANNEL` (zero-error identification guaranteed for every target at `k < 4` with only registered channels declared), `CONSISTENT_WITH_MATCHED` (at the bound; never *certified* matched — absence of evidence). A comparison report places the reference result *beside* the batch-4 D1 paired decision, never inside it.

**Theorem** (16 targets × all example sets, 256 checks). (i) A zero-error identifier guaranteed to distinguish every target compatible with `k` distinct registered example rows needs at least `4 − k` additional binary bits in the worst case. One correct output does not demonstrate identification or extra information: it can be a lucky guess among `2^{4-k}` hypotheses. (ii) A foundation-model arm is a *reference* and not a matched comparator whenever its information channels include one the protocol cannot bound; its number is reported only as an unmatched reference, not a proven performance upper bound. (iii) The label rule is exact on all 10 (declaration × k) cases; a matched parent identifying exactly at the bound raises nothing. **Proof.** Counting; enumeration. ∎ **Hostiles** `mutant_reference_as_matched` (the reference arm enters the paired decision as the parent) flips the M12-shaped verdict from `RESIDUAL_SUPPORTED` (matched 9–1) to `PARENT_DOMINATES` (reference 0–10) — the honest report refuses with CANNOT_CHECK; `mutant_prompt_matching_is_matching` (identical registered inputs ⇒ "matched") is caught by the version-space audit: a uniform zero-error identification guarantee at `k = 2` would require 2 additional bits in the worst case; observed success alone returns `IDENTIFICATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED`. **No-alarm:** the declared reference reported beside a `DESCRIPTIVE` decision alarms nothing. **Tightened:** "matched" is never certifiable from outcomes; the sound labels are `CONSISTENT_WITH_MATCHED`, `REFERENCE`, or `CANNOT_CHECK` (M12's choice). Parent: Hartley counting, teaching dimension (Goldman–Kearns 1995), batch-4 D1/D2 (PARENT_OWNED); benchmark-contamination audits (candidate parents, unverified).
