# Frontier after the corrected foundation

The objective is a useful, resource-bounded science of warranted machine behavior, not a renamed collection of parent algorithms. The strongest applicable parent product receives the same observations, tools, storage, checker access and compute. Definitions and simulations are useful foundations but not evidence of a strict residual.

## F1 — Sequentially valid statistical operators under adaptive shift

**Target.** Construct actual pre-action bounds q_t for selected OCM operators while prompts, representations, policies and environment distributions evolve. T06 gives the conditional bound `min(1,(alpha_t+tau_t+d_t)/pi_t)` and T05 composes it; the hard question is obtaining sound, useful component bounds from available data, rather than declaring their validity.

**First decidable study.** Finite covariate/label worlds with known transition kernels; compare a fixed-family union-bound contract, conditional-risk oracle ceiling, confidence-sequence parent, naive marginal-certificate reuse and the candidate. Withhold labels until after each registered action; include drift, adaptive selection, correlated calibration and missing-checker cases. Report valid-action coverage, any-error probability and total calibration/checking costs. Parent replacement has first refusal.

**Theorem frontier.** Under explicitly bounded conditional shift and finite action families, derive a nonvacuous sample/risk/work bound; alternatively prove the information needed to keep q_t below a chosen threshold. Unknown shift must produce a vacuous bound or refusal. Do not assume the very conditional validity the learner is meant to establish.

**Parents.** S3-S5 and the recent abstract-only S10 lead. Full-text reconstruction of S10 is still required before any priority claim.

## F2 — Minimum sufficient memory for future evidence and criterion changes

**Target.** Retain enough information for registered future revocations, target changes and risk-certificate revalidation without storing every historical bit. The natural object is future-response equivalence over a declared intervention/query family, not current answer agreement alone.

**Study.** Start with T12's affine operators, then a structured program/library family where supports overlap and revocations are nonrectangular. Compare explicit antichains, compiled Boolean representations, indexed incremental recomputation and a strongest equal-interface recurrent/memory implementation. Measure query, update, certificate, index and reconstruction costs separately.

**Theorem frontier.** A representation/update trade-off including compilation cost and collateral retraction, with a matching lower bound under a named access model. Unrestricted equal-information expressivity separation is not the target; restricted finite-resource trade-offs remain meaningful. Existing counting bounds and ATMS/knowledge-compilation results must be subtracted first.

## F3 — Certified local graded navigation and abstraction

**Target.** Turn T07's global residual into an output-local stopping certificate and adaptive sparse update algorithm. Prove approximation error for the actual extracted query object, not just a matrix norm. Preserve fixed-normalization or provide explicit normalization transport.

**Study.** Directed weighted hypergraphs with held-out growth/revocation events, fixed-point oracle on small instances, sparse parent methods on larger ones, and degree/fan-out/tie/near-threshold stressors. Compare dynamic recomputation, directed PageRank parents, safe region restriction and the candidate at matched accuracy. An undirected parent is not automatically a fair comparator.

**Theorem frontier.** Complexity dependent on a certified affected region, restart and decision margin, including index maintenance. Identify families forcing global work. Quotient lumpability is only a navigation condition; separately prove preservation of solver answers and semantic commitments. Reconstruct S11's exact assumptions before claiming an algorithmic residual.

## F4 — Proof-carrying learned procedures beyond enumerated truth tables

**Target.** Scale query-specific nonvacuous supports to structured procedures, control flow and recursive libraries. Separate the warrant of one observed execution from a universal correctness claim about a procedure.

**Study.** A finite typed instruction language with a bounded interpreter and independent verifier; curriculum lessons, held-out compositions, partial/corrupt lessons, checker changes and targeted revocations. Compare version-space/KWIK/synthesis parents and the strongest parent composition. Charge hypothesis enumeration and certificate information, not just inference steps.

**Theorem frontier.** A learnability or maintenance bound under bounded corruption and a named verification interface. A verifier containing the hidden program invalidates the comparison. Infinite grammar identification remains a separate problem with explicit data assumptions, not a consequence of finite-class soundness.

## F5 — Semantically faithful language under unresolved uncertainty

**Target.** A codec may preserve both possible meanings and their decision-relevant warrants while permitting answers common to all admissible meanings. This is stronger than seed equality, and different from forbidding all ambiguity.

**Study.** Typed propositions with negation, quantities, scope, attribution and time; independent parser/renderer implementations; hidden semantic equivalence classes; cases with identical wording/different meanings and different wording/same meaning. Include witnessed correction, ambiguous-but-answerable and inconsistent-context cases. Exact bounded semantics precede open-language claims.

**Theorem frontier.** Composition of parsing uncertainty, query agreement and renderer entailment under a registered semantic fragment, with selective coverage and error/cost bounds. Read-only capability proofs cannot substitute for semantic fidelity. Prefix admissibility needs both continuation feasibility and commitment semantics.

## F6 — Executable epistemic control and causal evidence

**Target.** Choose among answer, clarify, search, inspect a source, run an experiment, abstain and propose a representation change under actual evidence/risk/work constraints. Reduce finite versions to constrained decision processes and value-of-information parents before proposing new laws.

**Study.** Finite causal microworlds with observationally indistinguishable models separated by a registered intervention; multiple action costs; no-information feedback; unsafe or unauthorized experiment controls. Distinguish an observed outcome from evidence for its generating mechanism. Use a dynamic-programming oracle as a ceiling, not as a beatable baseline.

**Theorem frontier.** Conditions for identifiable action value and safe information acquisition under partial models; impossibility when no admissible action separates models. A chosen utility function or experiment channel is a declared task contract, not universal epistemic truth. This package does not yet supply the planner or a causal-identification theorem.

## F7 — Runtime refinement, distributed evidence and self-change

**Target.** Prove that a real event-sourced OCM implementation refines T16 through concurrency, cache use, crash/restart, schema migration, checker drift and governed representation change.

**Study.** State-machine and failure-injection traces: revoke during query, stale cache, concurrent evidence updates, interrupted risk reservation, duplicate action receipt, incompatible support contexts and missing remote checker. For multi-agent testimony, include shared-source aliases and conflicting provenance; independence is measured or assumed explicitly, never inferred from agent count.

**Theorem frontier.** A refinement proof with explicit linearization/durability assumptions and an effect boundary; quantified cost of safe selective reopening versus full replay. Structural and normative self-modification remain externally authorized. A system's own proof of correctness cannot certify that its proof checker and external interpretation are sound.

## Completion discipline

For each frontier, freeze the exact question, access/resource model, parent, checker, falsifier and admissible negative terminal before the decisive evaluation. Keep exploratory counterexample search distinct from protected tests. A result may be parent-sufficient, an impossibility, a bounded interface contribution or a strict resource residual. Neither a new vocabulary nor a passing pipeline chooses the terminal.

Foundation closure means all declared claims have checked scope, proofs/evidence, counterexamples, dependency records and review dispositions. It does not mean no future scientific question remains. The immediate unfinished obligations are independent assumption review, broader mechanization, source reconstruction for partial-access parents, real conditional-risk construction, scalable support/navigation algorithms, semantic-language evidence and runtime refinement. All remain explicit in `ATLAS_MAP.json`.
