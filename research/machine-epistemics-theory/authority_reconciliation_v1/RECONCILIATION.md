## New counterexample: B1 T5(iii) loses evidence after absorption

Let both intervals be exact, with lower equal to upper:

\[
P=\{\{a\}\},\quad Q=\{\{a\},\{b\}\}.
\]

The antichain product first produces `{{a},{a,b}}`; minimization removes the
second support because `{a}` is its strict subset. Thus

\[
P\otimes Q=\{\{a\}\},\qquad
\operatorname{ev}(P\otimes Q)=\{a\}\subsetneq\{a,b\}
 =\operatorname{ev}(P)\cup\operatorname{ev}(Q).
\]

For shared alphabet `Sigma={b}`, the right operand cites shared evidence while
the product does not. This refutes T5(iii)'s footprint equality and its claimed
iff for shared-footprint membership, even with exact lower/upper intervals. The
truth-product identity remains correct: `a AND (a OR b) = a`. Every retained
product support is a union of operand supports, so the valid general relation is
footprint **inclusion**, and product-shared implies at least one operand-shared.
This does not refute the conservative interference/reopening upper bound in
T5(i). It does prevent treating its candidate cone as an exact impact set.

This finding was separately checked by the evidence-audit teammate against the
source definitions. That is same-team hostile review, not independent external
scientific replication. `test_antichain_product_footprint_is_not_union` retains
the strict-subset witness; no frozen theorem text is replaced.
