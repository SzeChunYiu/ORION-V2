# OCM absorption contract — proposed, not implemented

This package changes ORION-V2 research only. Neither merge nor a passing checker grants OCM adoption, real-model statistical validity, exact truth, or external action authority. Do not change active OCM files from this contribution.

## A. Required objects

A proposed `RiskTransportRequest` must bind: old and proposed subject manifests; the registered semantic failure predicate and domain; reference distribution/error-table identity and their evidence; mutable-event region and its justification; distribution drift and event-change budgets and their justification; any exact no-change audits and their current dependency state; a computation/resource policy; and the task's separately provided risk tolerance. Scope/epoch and calibration provenance are mandatory.

A proposed `RiskTransportResult` must retain: input hashes and proof/checker version; exact conditional bound; an attaining model when exactness is claimed; model-class restrictions and realizability status; charged resource use; and a typed outcome. The outcome distinguishes `BINDING_MATCH_ONLY`, conditional mathematical bound, `REVALIDATE`, `CANNOT_CHECK`, and a separate externally authorized decision. A scalar risk never substitutes for those coordinates.

The current Python binding checker verifies only syntactic manifest agreement and dependency revocation. It does not authenticate evidence, fetch executable bytes, enforce operating-system isolation, or implement a complete attestation verifier. OCM must supply those boundaries independently.

## B. Inference rules that must not appear

No rule may infer an individual fact from marginal coverage, infer population P from empirical frequencies without a sampling theorem, infer unchanged behavior from an unchanged operator label, infer no possible errors from zero reference mass, or infer external permission from an outcome receipt. An after-action receipt is not a pre-action permit.

A checker failure event is not automatically the task's true failure event. Their relationship needs a soundness/refinement certificate or a scoped missed-violation risk bound. Missing correspondence means `CANNOT_CHECK` for the stronger claim.

## C. Adoption acceptance tests

1. Bind this study's final commit, THEORY.md/CLAIMS.json and exact checker digests; keep proof, calibration, parent ownership and independent-review states separate.
2. Reproduce all primary and supplementary finite cells through an OCM adapter. Do not let the adapter add a hidden error oracle or drop zero-mass atoms.
3. Change each manifest coordinate independently. Changed subject/configuration/checker/calibration/selection policy/epoch must require revalidation; unknown fields require a schema successor, not silent acceptance.
4. Revoke a required audit. Recompute the risk under the enlarged event class; preserve unrelated claims, and do not automatically convert missing warrant into falsehood.
5. Give an incomplete manifest, a missing drift certificate, a sample substituted for P, or an unattested checker verdict. Each must refuse the deployment claim even if a numeric conditional calculation can run.
6. Preserve the distinction between the empty event and a nonempty zero-mass event, and reject a diminishing-returns assumption contradicted by the two-audit witness.
7. Keep a changed-task mapping explicit. Common string labels are not a proof of common semantic failure predicates.
8. Exercise actual persistence/replay, transactional update, stale-cache invalidation and pre-action gating with the bound manifests. These runtime properties were NOT tested here.

## D. What this closes, and what it does not

This gives MEG-02/03 a precise finite risk-transport object, MEG-17/19/22 a scoped revalidation-cost example, and MEG-30/31/33 explicit cost and audit-selection boundaries. It does not close those atlas rows wholesale. It does not repair the old `LIVE`-from-coverage implementation, settle graded warrant, solve general learning or language, or change any OCM milestone.

Next research contracts: finite-sample uncertainty sets for P and U; conditionally valid adaptive selection; imperfect and dependent checker evidence; a realizable-backend restriction; and measured artifact-to-execution attestation. Those require new prospective identities and strongest-parent comparisons, not tuning this exact-model study into an empirical success.

## E. Finite-sample route

`SAMPLING.md` supplies a conservative alternative when complete P/F tables are unavailable. OCM must bind fixed sample sizes, sampling/selection policy, model-family identity, reference label validity, paired-evaluation records, confidence-budget allocation, and a joint-deployment-drift premise. The count function verifies none of those facts. It returns only a conditional risk calculation; rejection of unknown drift and the adaptive-selection counterexample must survive integration. Shared samples do not justify independence claims, and a guarantee about input marginals does not cover label shift.
