This is a science-to-machine handoff contract, not an OCM implementation or adoption authorization. Work division remains #304.

## Safe implementation consequences now

- **FD-01:** every internal derivation includes the operator/bridge authority factor; internal operations cannot mint external commit authority.
- **FD-02:** score/confidence/navigation/statistical coverage remain outside individual truth warrant unless a separate registered truth certificate exists; nogoods filter joint supports after composition.
- **FD-03:** store explicit dependency/provenance sufficient for the claimed local reopening behavior; treat dependency completeness as an assumption/certificate, not an invisible fact.
- **FD-04:** navigation implementations may use the fixed-point perturbation bound for activation/cache invalidation and numerical decision margins, but never as a truth/warrant guarantee.
- **FD-05:** preserve immutable evidence identities and append-only/quarantined structural lineage. Reinstatement can restore the semantic projection; full audit/history/cost state is never rewound. Relearn under a new identity is not reinstate.
- **FD-06:** commitment gating should be expressible as a typed safe/viability predicate; the generic viability algorithm remains parent-owned.
- **FD-08:** summary/multiscale reuse requires registered lumpability/intertwining plus warrant/answer measurability and revision commutation across the declared family; noncommutation returns `REFINE_REQUIRED` or reopen.
- **FD-09:** preserve source/dependence/authority through testimony. Do not infer independence from distinct model/prompt/process/source ids.
- **FD-10:** self-model/shadow/proposal state has no object-world or external-commit write authority; adoption remains external and rollback lineage-bound.
- **FD-11:** runtime/status receipts expose the whole registered resource vector rather than only token/time/parameter fragments.
- **FD-12:** every solve/control path needs an acyclic stage order or a checked decreasing fuel/rank; finite state count alone does not imply termination. `CANNOT_CHECK` stays absorbing for conclusions requiring the missing premise.

## Not ready for machine doctrine

- **FD-07 general information/interface conservation:** `OPEN_RESEARCH`.
- a universal probabilistic/graded truth-warrant algebra: not established by graded navigation or marginal calibration.
- quantitative multi-agent evidence fusion without a registered dependence/coupling model.
- real-world open-system closure/freshness without a registered upper revision/model envelope.
- infinite-class lifecycle learnability.
- novelty/architecture residual for the unified field object.

## Runtime architecture implied by the field object

OCM should keep separate interfaces for the seven transition families:

`F fast inference | E evidence/revision | L learning | R representation/organisation | T environment/update ingestion | M communication/codec | G governance/action/self`.

They may share storage, but their write authorities and receipts must be distinguishable. In particular F cannot write warrant/authority, M cannot mint world truth, self-model G proposals cannot adopt themselves, and T is exogenous evidence/model change rather than an internal inference step.

## Parity tests worth adding when OCM reaches the relevant milestone

1. fast inference changes activation but not warrant/authority;
2. revoke/reinstate restores the semantic projection while preserving changed audit history; relearn-new-id is lifecycle-distinct;
3. stale dependency/model/scope certificate fails closed;
4. quotient accepted exactly under registered dynamic + warrant + answer sufficiency;
5. self/shadow has zero object-store/commit side effects;
6. statistical action can be permitted while the candidate remains truth-UNKNOWN;
7. typed CANNOT_CHECK survives persistence/replay/codec/action boundaries.

No OCM milestone is closed by this document.
