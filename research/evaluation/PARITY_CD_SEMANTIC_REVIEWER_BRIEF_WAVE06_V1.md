# PARITY-C / PARITY-D Independent Semantic Reviewer Brief — Wave 06 V1

**Status:** pre-outcome reviewer brief. Reviewer identities are not yet bound. This document does not authorize a parity run or confer scientific authority.

## Purpose

The frozen V1-native source audit is strong for most of the 59-cell parity surface but deliberately weak for two semantic families:

- **PARITY-C:** scientific-language absorption, claim/context binding and representation mapping;
- **PARITY-D:** reconstruction/gluing, plural portraits and search-universe expansion.

Four post-subject hostile cases were therefore frozen after the immutable V2 subject commit and before paired outcome access. They can falsify V2 but cannot establish V1-native non-regression without independent scientific adjudication.

The task of the semantic reviewers is narrow: decide whether a blinded output satisfies the already frozen invariant for the given case. Reviewers do not rank ORION, select papers, grant novelty, or change the criterion.

## Frozen cases

### PARITY-C

`C2_SAME_WORDS_DIFFERENT_NATIVE_STRUCTURE`

Required judgment:
- lexical identity must not force scientific-structure identity;
- the source projection must remain recoverable;
- an ambiguous mapping must remain unresolved rather than be normalized by convenience.

`C3_DIFFERENT_WORDS_SAME_REGISTERED_STRUCTURE`

Required judgment:
- different vocabulary may map to the same registered structure when there is a witness;
- the mapping may not grant stronger identity/equivalence than its frozen context permits.

### PARITY-D

`D2_LOCAL_COMPATIBILITY_GLOBAL_OBSTRUCTION`

Required judgment:
- pairwise/local compatibility is not enough for a globally valid reconstruction;
- a genuine global obstruction must be surfaced instead of forcing one portrait.

`D3_NEW_REPRESENTATION_OPENS_ROUTE`

Required judgment:
- a scientifically valid new representation may expand the search universe;
- earlier absence under the old representation must not be rewritten as prior complete coverage.

## Reviewer eligibility

Each primary reviewer must declare before receiving any paired subject output:

1. relevant scientific/formal background for the assigned cases;
2. whether they authored or materially designed ORION-V2, the frozen V2 subject, these specific parity cases, or the scoring rule;
3. whether they have reviewed or seen identifiable V1/V2 outputs for the cases;
4. material dependence on another reviewer through the same model, data, answer key, hidden source, laboratory, or adjudication process;
5. any conflict that could make a reviewer effectively a second copy of the same evaluator.

The first two reviewers for a case cannot both depend on the same undisclosed material evaluator source. A reviewer need not be statistically independent in every possible sense, but material dependence must be declared and dispositioned before output access.

## Packet and blinding

The review coordinator will provide:

- frozen case ID and criterion;
- source material/evidence required to judge the criterion;
- two or more candidate output packets in randomized order;
- neutral packet IDs that do not identify V1, V2 or a parent comparator;
- the frozen label vocabulary and rationale form.

Where implementation artifacts themselves would trivially reveal identity, the packet should present the smallest scientifically sufficient projection and record why stronger blinding was infeasible.

## Allowed labels

Exactly one per invariant/output packet:

- `PASS`
- `FAIL`
- `CANNOT_CHECK`

`CANNOT_CHECK` is required when the packet lacks evidence needed to decide the frozen invariant. It is not a weak pass.

## Required rationale

Each label must identify the exact evidence that supports it: source span, graph relation, consistency witness, obstruction, mapping witness, ambiguity, or missing information.

Confidence may be recorded descriptively but cannot override the label.

## Agreement rule

Two agreeing eligible independent primary labels decide the invariant.

If the primary reviewers disagree, a pre-bound or subsequently bound-before-access independent tiebreak reviewer receives the same blinded packet and frozen rubric. Majority decides. If a valid majority cannot be established, the case remains `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## Prohibited reviewer actions

A reviewer may not:

- change the case, criterion, evaluator rule, subject commit or resource budget;
- request a favorable rerun after seeing an output;
- infer identity and then use general impressions of ORION/V1 as evidence;
- fill missing evidence from private knowledge that was not part of the frozen packet unless the review protocol explicitly creates a new run identity;
- trade a semantic failure against speed, elegance, novelty, expected usefulness or other secondary value;
- grant scientific truth, kernel adoption, novelty, field status or publication authority.

## Suggested reviewer backgrounds

For PARITY-C, useful backgrounds include scientific information extraction, knowledge representation/ontology alignment, domain scientific semantics, measurement/context semantics, or formal semantics.

For PARITY-D, useful backgrounds include graph consistency/constraint systems, sheaf/local-to-global methods, scientific model reconstruction, multi-model/representation reasoning, or formal knowledge integration.

A single reviewer can cover both campaigns only if eligibility/dependence is acceptable, but the programme should prefer distinct expertise when available.

## Identity-binding record

Before paired output access, the evaluator registry must be updated with immutable identity or accountable pseudonymous identity, declared expertise, dependence/conflict disposition, criterion/scorer digest and authority ceiling.

The current evaluator registry remains `registry_bound=false` until the four primary slots are filled.

## Authority boundary

A semantic adjudication is evidence that a packet met or failed one frozen evaluator criterion. It does not establish the global truth of the scientific theory, the superiority of ORION-V2, novelty, publication authority, or a new scientific field.
