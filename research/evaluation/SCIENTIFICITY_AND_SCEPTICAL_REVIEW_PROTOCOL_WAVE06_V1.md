# Scientificity and Sceptical Review Protocol — Wave 06 V1

**Status:** binding claim-governance protocol for Machine Epistemics / ORION-V2. This protocol grants no scientific truth, novelty, field status, superiority, publication readiness or journal acceptance.

## 1. Purpose

Machine Epistemics contains unfamiliar terms and candidate structures. Careful scientists are justified in treating them sceptically. The programme must therefore make it easier—not harder—for a sceptic to show that a concept is unnecessary, parent-owned, non-identifiable, overgeneralized or wrong.

The governing rule is:

> **Novel terminology carries zero scientific credit. A new concept earns status only through operational definition, discriminating evidence or formal necessity, strongest-parent comparison, explicit falsifiers and independent scrutiny.**

The programme distinguishes inspiration, metaphor, engineering interface, empirical hypothesis, formal proposition, empirical result, normative boundary and field-level theory. These categories must never be silently promoted into one another.

## 2. Metaphor boundary

Daily experience, craft knowledge and analogies may generate useful research hypotheses. They do not become evidence merely because the analogy is vivid.

Examples:

- `organism` is a donor metaphor;
- `protein digestion` is a donor analogy for decomposition and selective assimilation;
- `recycling centre` is a donor analogy for separation, recovery and recombination;
- `knowledge metabolism` is acceptable as a provisional name for a measurable lifecycle only after the lifecycle is defined independently of the metaphor.

The scientific object is therefore not “ORION is an organism”. The testable object is a lifecycle such as:

`INGEST -> DECOMPOSE -> SORT -> NATIVE_RECONSTRUCT -> REDUCE -> ABSORB -> RECOMBINE -> CHALLENGE -> ASSIMILATE_OR_RECYCLE`.

That lifecycle must outperform or explain something beyond simpler and parent controls. If it does not, the metaphor remains inspiration and the scientific claim contracts.

## 3. Scientificity ladder

### S0 — `INSPIRATION_OR_METAPHOR`

Useful for question generation only. No claim of mechanism, truth or field status.

### S1 — `OPERATIONALLY_DEFINED_CONCEPT`

The object, inputs, outputs, measurement procedure, scope and non-examples are explicit. It may still be unnecessary or parent-owned.

### S2 — `DISCRIMINATING_HYPOTHESIS`

The concept changes a prospective prediction, decision, theorem target or failure expectation relative to named alternatives.

### S3 — `PROSPECTIVELY_TESTED_RESULT`

A preregistered/frozen test has been executed under matched resources, with outcome-independent criteria, negative controls, uncertainty and retained failures.

### S4 — `INDEPENDENTLY_REPLICATED_OR_CROSS_DOMAIN`

The result survives genuinely independent analysis/replication and a materially different domain or regime where the claim is meant to generalize.

### S5 — `FOUNDATION_PROPOSITION_CANDIDATE`

A proposition has parent recovery, strongest-parent comparison, formal/empirical support, known scope, counterexamples and external hostile review. It remains revisable.

No internal state is named `PROVEN_UNIVERSAL_LAW` or `ESTABLISHED_FIELD`.

## 4. Required concept card

Every novel ORION/Machine-Epistemics term that appears in a headline claim must bind:

- `concept_id`;
- plain-language definition;
- claim type;
- closest parent concepts and strongest parent product;
- what is genuinely different, if anything;
- operationalization;
- observable decision or prediction changed;
- measurement/evaluator and its sensitivity/blind spots;
- simple/direct control;
- negative control;
- strongest-parent control;
- non-application case where the concept should not help;
- falsifier or contraction condition;
- known failure modes;
- cost/complexity burden;
- preregistration/protocol identity;
- independent evaluator/replication requirement;
- present scientificity level;
- allowed manuscript wording.

A concept without such a card remains vocabulary, not science.

## 5. Strongest sceptic requirement

For every central claim, appoint at least one reviewer whose role is to defend the strongest plausible alternative rather than improve ORION.

Required adversarial positions include where applicable:

- `F0 advocate`: strongest parent federation is sufficient;
- `simple-control advocate`: complexity is unnecessary;
- `domain-native scientist`: ORION terminology distorts native semantics;
- `measurement sceptic`: the evaluator cannot detect the claimed difference;
- `statistics sceptic`: result depends on analyst/specification choices;
- `systems sceptic`: gain is hidden compute, latency or engineering complexity;
- `field sceptic`: this is an interdisciplinary programme, not a distinct field.

Before protected outcome access, proponents and sceptics should agree on:

1. what result would favour each theory;
2. what result would be inconclusive;
3. what observation would kill or contract the new concept;
4. which analysis choices are defensible;
5. which evidence source has decision authority.

This is an adversarial-collaboration principle, not reviewer voting.

## 6. Confirmatory versus exploratory custody

Exploration is legitimate but cannot be laundered into confirmation.

Every analysis/result is labelled:

- `EXPLORATORY` — generated after outcome access or during open-ended search;
- `CONFIRMATORY` — hypothesis, endpoint, comparator, exclusion rule and analysis frozen before relevant outcome access;
- `REPLICATION` — independently executed against a registered prior claim;
- `FORMAL` — theorem/proof/counterexample relative to an explicit formal system;
- `DESCRIPTIVE` — summarizes observed data without confirmatory promotion.

Important confirmatory ORION studies should be prepared in Registered-Report-like form where journal format permits: question, hypotheses, sampling/task plan, exclusion rules, estimands, analyses and falsifiers frozen before substantive result access.

All deviations require a dated deviation receipt and both planned and changed analyses where feasible.

## 7. Measurement and oracle adequacy

A result cannot be stronger than the evaluator's ability to expose relevant error.

Every headline result binds:

- target construct;
- evaluator/oracle identity;
- known-answer or external criterion where available;
- perturbation/alternative family;
- test sensitivity or known blind spots;
- false-positive/false-negative failure modes;
- evaluator dependence on the subject model/data/source;
- authority ceiling.

A correctly frozen but non-discriminating benchmark is not strong evidence.

## 8. Robustness to reasonable analysis choices

Before publication-level promotion, every empirical headline claim must undergo a reasonable-specification audit.

At minimum inspect, when applicable:

- valid exclusion rules;
- alternative but defensible scoring thresholds;
- missing-data handling;
- stochastic aggregation choices;
- evaluator variants;
- resource normalization;
- project/domain stratification;
- dependence assumptions;
- uncertainty interval method.

The purpose is not to search until the preferred result disappears. It is to show which conclusions are invariant and which are specification-dependent.

If defensible analyses materially disagree, the disagreement becomes part of the result.

## 9. Replication hierarchy

Evidence strength increases through distinct forms of independence:

1. exact rerun/reproduction;
2. fresh execution from frozen artifacts;
3. independent analyst using the same data;
4. independent implementation;
5. independent evaluator/domain expert;
6. materially different dataset/problem family;
7. materially different scientific domain;
8. external group replication.

Same-model self-critique is dependent evidence and cannot substitute for independent replication.

## 10. Anti-overclaim language rules

Before S4/S5, avoid:

- `new law of science`;
- `proves ORION is superior`;
- `establishes Machine Epistemics as a field`;
- `general scientific intelligence`;
- `not memorization` as an absolute claim;
- `understands` when only benchmark success is observed;
- `autonomous scientist` when human/evaluator/authority inputs are material.

Prefer bounded formulations:

- `candidate mechanism`;
- `prospective hypothesis`;
- `supported in the registered debugging tranche`;
- `cross-domain evidence is consistent with...`;
- `outperforms comparator X under contract C`;
- `converging evidence against direct-copy explanations`;
- `field hypothesis`.

## 11. New-concept admission test

A concept remains outside the stable foundation unless it satisfies one of:

### Empirical necessity

Removing or parent-replacing it produces a reproducible protected scientific failure in a registered stratum.

### Formal necessity

A theorem, impossibility result or counterexample shows that the distinction is required under explicit assumptions.

### Predictive/generative necessity

The concept prospectively produces a discriminator, prediction, experiment or failure boundary unavailable from the strongest comparator under matched information.

### Native-fidelity necessity

Without it, the higher theory cannot preserve a material parent/domain-native judgment.

Otherwise the default disposition is merge, adapter, heuristic, metaphor, parent-owned or removal.

## 12. Component and concept kill criteria

A new concept/component is removed or demoted when any of the following holds after adequate testing:

- no measurable protected decision change;
- effect is reproduced by a simpler control;
- mature parent method reproduces the effect more cleanly;
- apparent gain is explained by additional compute, tools, context or human input;
- benefit appears only under the concept's own evaluator vocabulary;
- domain-native semantics erase the gain;
- reasonable analyses reverse the conclusion;
- it creates critical failures or unacceptable resource drag;
- independent reviewers cannot identify the claimed construct reliably;
- the concept cannot state a plausible non-application case.

## 13. Paper-level cautious-scientist review

Before R4, every surviving manuscript receives three independent review packets:

### Packet A — parent scientist

Can the native parent science already explain the result? Are parent assumptions and limitations represented fairly?

### Packet B — methodological sceptic

Could measurement, leakage, flexibility, evaluator dependence, multiple testing or resource mismatch explain the result?

### Packet C — hostile editor

Is the advance important enough for the claimed venue after all rhetoric and ORION-specific terminology are removed?

Reviewers must be allowed to recommend:

- narrower claim;
- rename concept;
- return result to parent field;
- merge paper;
- downgrade field claim;
- further experiment;
- `CANNOT_CHECK`;
- rejection.

## 14. Reproducibility and transparency

For all result-bearing papers, prepare:

- frozen protocol/preregistration record;
- raw or minimally processed result data where legally/ethically shareable;
- task/dataset identities and checksums;
- environment and model identities;
- prompts/configurations/tool contracts where disclosure is permitted;
- code required to reproduce analysis and figures;
- exclusion/deviation log;
- negative-result and failure ledger;
- data availability statement;
- code availability statement;
- AI-use disclosure and human accountability statement;
- restrictions/custody statements for non-public data.

## 15. Current high-risk concepts

The following require especially cautious language until their registered evidence arrives:

- `Machine Epistemics` — field hypothesis, not established field;
- `absorptive supertheory` — aspirational theory candidate;
- `knowledge metabolism` — operational lifecycle hypothesis inspired by metaphor;
- `machine-native scientific intelligence` — mechanism-level empirical hypothesis;
- `scientific warrant factorisation` — formal/assurance candidate;
- proposed universal transition propositions — candidates, not laws.

## 16. Scientificity terminal

A manuscript cannot enter R4 when a headline concept is below S3, and a broad field/theory claim cannot enter R4 when its central empirical propositions are below S4 and external demarcation is incomplete.

Allowed terminals remain:

```text
SUPPORTED_BOUNDEDLY
SUPPORTED_WITH_SPECIFICATION_DEPENDENCE
INTERFIELD_ADVANCE_ONLY
PARENT_SUFFICIENT
SIMPLE_CONTROL_SUFFICIENT
DOMAIN_SPECIFIC_ONLY
METAPHOR_ONLY
REDUNDANT_OR_HARMFUL
FAILED_REPLICATION
CANNOT_CHECK
```
