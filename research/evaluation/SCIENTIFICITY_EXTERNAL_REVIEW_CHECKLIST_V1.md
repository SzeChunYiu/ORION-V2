# External Scientificity Review Checklist V1

This checklist is for independent reviewers who are not asked to endorse ORION. Their task is to identify overclaim, parent sufficiency, construct failure, measurement weakness and unjustified field rhetoric.

## A. Concept legitimacy

- [ ] The concept has a plain-language definition independent of ORION jargon.
- [ ] Non-examples and non-application cases are provided.
- [ ] Nearest parent concepts are reconstructed in their native terminology.
- [ ] The claimed residual is not merely a renamed parent mechanism.
- [ ] Metaphor and evidence are explicitly separated.

## B. Falsifiability

- [ ] A prospective discriminator exists.
- [ ] A parent/simple-control win is possible by design.
- [ ] A kill/merge/return-to-parent terminal is stated.
- [ ] The claim scope would contract after a failed domain transfer.

## C. Measurement

- [ ] The evaluator/oracle measures the intended construct.
- [ ] Relevant blind spots and error-detection sensitivity are stated.
- [ ] Subject/evaluator dependence is disclosed.
- [ ] The benchmark is not defined so that ORION terminology automatically wins.

## D. Confirmatory custody

- [ ] Primary question and endpoint were frozen before outcome access.
- [ ] Inclusion/exclusion rules were frozen.
- [ ] Comparators and resource budgets were frozen.
- [ ] Deviations are logged and original analyses remain visible.
- [ ] Exploratory analyses are labelled separately.

## E. Robustness and uncertainty

- [ ] Effect sizes and intervals are reported, not only binary significance.
- [ ] Reasonable alternative analyses have been examined.
- [ ] Stochastic repetitions are not treated as independent tasks.
- [ ] Missingness/timeouts/evaluator failures are retained.
- [ ] Negative, parent-win and inconclusive cases remain visible.

## F. Independence and replication

- [ ] At least one evaluator/analyst is genuinely independent of the subject system.
- [ ] Same-model self-critique is not labelled independent evidence.
- [ ] Broad claims have cross-domain or independent replication support.
- [ ] Domain-native experts can reproduce the semantic verdict without adopting ORION vocabulary.

## G. Resource fairness

- [ ] Information/tool access is matched.
- [ ] Compute/token/time/human assistance are reported.
- [ ] Efficiency and implementation burden are separated from scientific quality.
- [ ] A gain caused solely by additional resources is not described as a theoretical advance.

## H. Manuscript language

- [ ] “field hypothesis” is used unless external demarcation supports stronger language.
- [ ] “candidate proposition” is used instead of “law” before S5.
- [ ] Anti-memorization language reports controls rather than impossibly proving absence from training.
- [ ] Human and machine processes are not treated as neutral normative baselines.

## Allowed reviewer terminals

```text
SCIENTIFICALLY_WELL_POSED_AND_READY_FOR_PROTECTED_TEST
SUPPORTED_BOUNDEDLY
SUPPORTED_BUT_SPECIFICATION_DEPENDENT
PARENT_SUFFICIENT
SIMPLE_CONTROL_SUFFICIENT
DOMAIN_SPECIFIC_ONLY
MEASUREMENT_INADEQUATE
UNDERPOWERED
DECEPTIVE_UNIFICATION
METAPHOR_ONLY
FAILED_REPLICATION
MORE_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED
CANNOT_CHECK
```

No reviewer terminal grants journal acceptance or establishes the field.
