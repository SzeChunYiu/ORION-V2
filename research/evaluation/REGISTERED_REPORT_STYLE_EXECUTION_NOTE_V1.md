# Registered-Report-Style Execution Note V1

**Status:** prospective execution guidance for confirmatory ORION studies. This is not journal acceptance-in-principle and does not claim that the target journal will use the Registered Report format for a particular manuscript.

## Why use this structure

For controversial new concepts, credibility improves when the scientific question, task/sample plan, comparators, endpoints, exclusion rules and analyses are judged before the results are known. The ORION programme should therefore package its key confirmatory studies in a Stage-1-like form even when the eventual venue uses an ordinary Article format.

## Stage-1-like package

Before substantive outcome access, freeze:

1. scientific question and competing hypotheses;
2. construct definitions and scientificity levels;
3. strongest parent/simple comparators;
4. task/dataset sampling plan;
5. model/checkpoint/tool/resource identities;
6. primary and secondary endpoints;
7. evaluator/oracle and sensitivity assumptions;
8. exclusions/invalid-task rules;
9. statistical estimands and uncertainty methods;
10. multiplicity handling;
11. component ablations;
12. negative/non-application cases;
13. anti-copy controls where relevant;
14. decisive result regions favouring F0/F1/F2/F3;
15. kill/merge/contraction conditions;
16. planned reasonable-specification robustness analyses.

The package receives independent methods and parent-field review before the confirmatory run where feasible.

## After results

Report:

- every registered analysis;
- all deviations and why they were necessary;
- exploratory analyses in a separate section;
- effect sizes and uncertainty;
- parent/simple wins;
- hard failures and missingness;
- robustness/specification results;
- independent evaluation/replication;
- exact claim-level promotion decisions.

## ORION-specific restriction

A positive confirmatory result can promote a bounded empirical claim. It cannot by itself:

- establish Machine Epistemics as a field;
- prove F2 a universal supertheory;
- establish general scientific intelligence;
- authorize deployment/adoption;
- make a contingent paper automatically independent enough for publication.

Those require separate cross-domain, independent and demarcation evidence.
