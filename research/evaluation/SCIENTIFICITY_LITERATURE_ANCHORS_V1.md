# Scientificity Methodology Anchors V1

**Purpose:** methodological anchors for cautious evaluation of novel ORION/Machine-Epistemics concepts. This is not a claim that every cited method applies identically across all ORION studies.

## Prospective custody and Registered Reports

- Nature Portfolio reporting policies support preregistration of study and analysis plans and require transparent data/code availability for published original research.
- Nature announced in 2026 that Registered Reports are expanding across the fields it publishes, emphasizing strong questions, sound methods and analyses before knowing outcomes.
- Nature Human Behaviour's Registered Reports guidance requires the research question, hypotheses, procedures, analysis pipeline, sampling/power plan and pilot information where applicable before Stage-1 review.

### ORION donation

Use Stage-1-like packets for controversial confirmatory claims even if the final target content type is a standard Article.

## Analytical robustness

- Specification-curve analysis demonstrates that defensible analysis decisions can materially alter empirical conclusions.
- Large-scale independent reanalysis work published in Nature in 2025 found substantial variation across reasonable reanalyses of the same questions/data.

### ORION donation

A single favourable analysis is insufficient for a new field claim. Headline results need reasonable-specification robustness audits, with disagreement preserved.

## Reproduction and replication

- Nature Human Behaviour and the Institute for Replication have emphasized reproduction and robustness replication at scale.
- Replication must be distinguished from exact rerun, independent analysis, independent implementation and cross-domain generalization.

### ORION donation

Use a replication hierarchy rather than calling repeated seeds or same-model critique independent replication.

## Adversarial collaboration

- Nature's 2025 editorial on adversarial collaboration emphasizes proponents of rival theories agreeing to tests while accepting the possibility of being wrong.

### ORION donation

For the F0/F1/F2/F3 programme, freeze what outcomes favour each candidate before protected execution and include explicit advocates for the strongest rival explanation.

## Reporting and code availability

- Nature Portfolio policies state that readers should be able to replicate and build upon published claims and require data/code availability statements and access to central custom code for review where applicable.
- Nature Methods reiterated in 2026 that AI-generated material must be carefully checked and that accountability remains with human authors.

### ORION donation

Every R4 paper needs a reproducibility package, AI-use disclosure and human accountability. AI-assisted manuscript or analysis generation never substitutes for scientific responsibility.

## Caution from failed “rigour” claims

A Nature Human Behaviour article claiming high replicability from rigour-enhancing practices was retracted in 2024, and a Matters Arising response emphasized that claims about scientific rigour themselves require rigorous evidence.

### ORION donation

Do not claim that preregistration, big teams, elaborate receipts or many reviewers automatically make ORION correct. Each methodological safeguard is itself subject to effectiveness tests and failure analysis.

## Practical rule

```text
RIGOUR_PROCESS != SCIENTIFIC_TRUTH
PREREGISTRATION != VALID_MEASUREMENT
REPLICATION != GENERALITY
MANY_REVIEWERS != INDEPENDENCE
NEW_VOCABULARY != NEW_SCIENCE
OPEN_CODE != CORRECT_CODE
```

Each safeguard reduces a particular class of risk; none self-authenticates the theory.
