## What “conceptual foundation complete” means

It means only that the bounded search/synthesis programme has a stable, explicit object, parent universe, alternative theories, candidate propositions, falsifiers, non-claims, measurement plans and reopening rules sufficient to begin protected science without further framework invention by default.

It does **not** mean:

- F2 is superior to F0;
- Machine Epistemics is an established field;
- K0–K6 are universal laws;
- the knowledge-metabolism mechanism works;
- machine-native intelligence has been demonstrated;
- any paper is R3 or R4;
- any top-tier venue will accept the work.

## Reopening triggers

Closed non-execution work reopens only on a named material trigger:

1. protected execution exposes a missing coordinate/relation/failure that changes a registered decision;
2. a strongest parent invalidates an ownership or novelty assumption;
3. independent domain review shows a native semantic loss;
4. a concept is harmful/redundant and its removal changes the foundation factorization;
5. a new parent/domain/source mode produces a material contradiction not representable by the current theory;
6. a journal/editorial requirement changes the scientific object rather than merely its presentation.

New ideas without one of these triggers go to an idea ledger, not directly into the kernel, foundation or paper portfolio.
