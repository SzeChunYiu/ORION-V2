## 1. Frozen scientific identity

Working title:

> **Beyond Predictive Sufficiency: A Prospective Revision Audit for Autoregressive Representations**

Frozen question:

> **After a representation is already matched on the declared linguistic prediction target and the registered current responsibility decision, can later evidence expose a revision failure caused by historical information that the representation failed to retain?**

The paper is an **assessment-framework paper**, not a new generic theory of predictive, decision, belief, information, or recurrent state.

## 2. Primary supported result

There exists a finite process and two representations that are:

1. equally adequate for the declared complete linguistic prediction target;
2. equally zero-regret for the current registered responsibility decision;
3. unequally adequate for revision after the same later evidence.

Canonical witness:

```text
C_stat^* = 0 bits
C_dyn^*  = 1 bit
Omega_dyn = 1 bit
```

with a unique current action.

Therefore:

> **present prediction + present decision cannot, in general, certify prospective revision adequacy.**

Human-readable proof: `papers/llm-machine-epistemics/PROOF_APPENDIX_V1.md`, Theorem G.1.  
Mechanical witness: merged #56–#59 receipt corpus.

No claim is made that a deployed LLM currently exhibits this failure.
