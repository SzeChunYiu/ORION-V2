## 4. Primary candidate paper claims

The claim ledger currently treats these as load-bearing and still unearned:

### P1 — responsibility-relative state cost

For a declared responsibility decision family, quantify the additional state beyond `S_P` by the conditional entropy of its decision quotient rather than raw auxiliary labels.

### P2 — current sufficiency versus future revision sufficiency

A representation may preserve the complete linguistic future and every current responsibility decision yet fail future epistemic revision because a currently dormant history distinction was discarded.

### P3 — dynamic optionality state cost

The extra exact state needed solely for correct recursive future responsibility updates is the entropy of the stable right-congruent refinement relative to the current predictive–responsibility state.

### P4 — bounded-responsibility necessity

No nontrivial compressed state can be exactly sufficient for unrestricted future responsibility families; as responsibilities become predictive-fibre separating, required state saturates at full non-predictive history.

### P5 — typed deficiency audit

Separate absent-at-ingress information, current representation loss and future revision-option loss because they imply different interventions.

These remain candidate **package-level** contributions; individual mathematical ingredients may be classical.
