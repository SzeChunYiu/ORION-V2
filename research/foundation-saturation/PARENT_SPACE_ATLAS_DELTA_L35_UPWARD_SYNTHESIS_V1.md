## Sub-lanes

### L35.1 — Interfield theory

- explain relations between fields;
- answer questions unavailable within either field alone;
- redirect attention to new domain items;
- generate cross-field predictions.

### L35.2 — Explanatory unification

- derive broader accepted consequences from fewer stringent patterns;
- measure economy and scope;
- reject unification that loses causal, mechanistic, measurement or decision relevance.

### L35.3 — Mechanistic integration

- preserve native entities and activities;
- use mechanism schemas to connect fields or levels;
- require causal/operational bridge witnesses rather than vocabulary similarity.

### L35.4 — Reduction, emergence and multiple realization

- distinguish reduction, approximation, constraint, emergence and coexistence;
- allow stable higher-level regularities;
- classify parent disagreement rather than assuming lower or higher theory wins.

### L35.5 — Integrative pluralism

- preserve several partial models when one representation is insufficient;
- integrate without mandatory total unification;
- allow `INTEGRATION_NOT_DESIRABLE_OR_NOT_POSSIBLE`.

### L35.6 — Theory virtues and progress

- accuracy;
- consistency;
- scope;
- simplicity/compression;
- fruitfulness/generativity;
- robustness and resilience;
- problem-solving effectiveness;
- resource and implementation cost;
- prospective independent corroboration.

### L35.7 — Conservative extension and recovery

- parent embedding;
- parent-domain recovery;
- explicit scope and revision;
- no unsupported new consequences in the old domain;
- counter-probes for lost distinctions.

### L35.8 — Metatheory and absorptive supertheory

- native parent federation;
- typed bridge layer;
- cross-parent transition laws;
- machine-native implementation freedom;
- empirical cross-domain field science.

## Materiality condition for higher-order synthesis

A synthesis is materially upward only if it produces at least one of:

- a new cross-parent explanatory relation;
- a prospective prediction or discriminator;
- a formal bridge/composition/impossibility result;
- a protected scientific decision gain beyond F0;
- a major reduction in ad hoc coordination without native loss;
- a new naturalistic measurement or benchmark;
- a principled account of why different parents apply in different contexts;
- a machine-native strategy predicted from the integrated theory.
