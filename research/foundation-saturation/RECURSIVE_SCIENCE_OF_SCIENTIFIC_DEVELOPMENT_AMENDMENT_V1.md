# Recursive Science of Scientific Development Amendment V1
## Learning general principles from populations of scientific trajectories

**Framework role:** candidate meta-level extension above Transfer Discovery, Conceptual Development and Formalism Genesis.  
**Scientificity:** S1 operational interface + S2 discriminating research hypothesis.  
**Authority:** non-authorizing. Population regularities, citation impact, prizes and historical fame are not scientific truth labels.

## 1. Target object

The target is not how one famous theory was discovered. It is the distribution of successful, failed, partial and redirected scientific-development trajectories across very large populations of researchers, teams, fields, epochs and institutional environments.

A scientific-development episode is a trajectory

\[
\mathcal E_i = (S_{i,0}, a_{i,0}, x_{i,0}, S_{i,1},\ldots,S_{i,T_i}),
\]

where each state may bind problem/frame, concepts/formalism, knowledge sources, methods, instruments, collaborators, resources, obligations, failures, evaluations and authority. Public papers are only one observable projection of this trajectory.

The research question is:

> Can ORION infer reusable mechanisms and higher-order principles of scientific development from many trajectories, use them to predict and improve held-out scientific-development decisions, then recursively generalize those principles without collapsing into unfalsifiable universal rhetoric?

## 2. Why population scale matters

Individual breakthrough stories are selected after success and are vulnerable to hindsight, survivorship and hero narratives. Population-scale science-of-science work already shows cross-field regularities in novelty combinations, risk-taking, exploration/exploitation, team structure and career dynamics. These findings motivate a larger programme, but they remain noisy observational associations.

V2 must therefore ingest not only celebrated successes but also ordinary work, nulls, abandoned routes, negative results, unsuccessful combinations, failed replications, retractions, corrections, long delays, near-misses and `CANNOT_CHECK` trajectories where accessible.

## 3. Breakthrough is a vector, not one label

Do not use citation count, prizes or disruption score as the target by themselves. Represent a development outcome by a non-compensatory vector such as

\[
B_i=(V_i,N_i,G_i,T_i,C_i,R_i,A_i,Z_i),
\]

where `V` is validity/reproducibility, `N` bounded novelty, `G` generativity of new questions/results, `T` transfer, `C` explanatory/compressive change, `R` robustness/retention, `A` adoption or downstream use, and `Z` resource/time burden. Bibliometric impact can be an observed proxy coordinate, never a truth coordinate.

## 4. Learn development operators, do not hard-code them

Each trajectory can be segmented into candidate development operators

\[
o:S_t\rightarrow S_{t+1}.
\]

Possible discovered operator families may include representation change, abstraction, decomposition, counterexample-driven refinement, transfer, instrumentation, new-variable introduction, scale separation, generalized-object construction, constraint relaxation/tightening, theorem/experiment selection, failure diagnosis, collaboration change or exploitation of a promising route. This list seeds search only; it is not the ontology.

An operator receives scientific status only if its inferred preconditions and consequences predict or improve held-out trajectories better than strongest simpler sequence/process models.

## 5. Recursive abstraction hierarchy

Let `D_0` denote raw/parsed scientific-development episodes. Define recursively

\[
D_{k+1}=\mathcal G_k(D_k),
\]

where `G_k` proposes a more abstract representation of recurring transition structure at level `k`.

One possible hierarchy is:

- `L0` — episodes/actions/events;
- `L1` — development operators;
- `L2` — operator families and preconditions;
- `L3` — meta-policies selecting/sequencing operators;
- `L4` — principles governing conceptual/formal/empirical development;
- `L5+` — recursively discovered constraints over lower-level principles.

Levels are earned by evidence, not declared in advance.

For candidate abstraction `H`, use a multi-objective selection criterion rather than one scalar truth score:

\[
\mathcal J(H)=
(\mathcal L_{heldout},\mathcal L_{reconstruction},\mathcal L_{transfer},\mathcal L_{intervention},C_{critical},C_{complexity},C_{resource}).
\]

Historical data can estimate held-out prediction/reconstruction/transfer. Intervention loss requires prospective or quasi-experimental evidence; it cannot be fabricated from citation history.

## 6. Recursive stopping and the meaning of “ultimate”

There is no internally authorized metaphysical final theory. The strongest admissible analogue is a **recursive-stability candidate**.

Let `A_k` be the best supported abstraction at level `k`. A higher abstraction is retained only if it yields a material held-out gain, stronger compression without loss of critical distinctions, new prospective consequences, or lower resource cost.

A bounded fixed-point candidate satisfies approximately

\[
A^* \simeq \mathcal G(A^*)
\]

under declared transformations of domain, epoch, researcher/team identity, representation and problem class, with no material new residual found by the registered search routes.

This is always relative to a declared evidence universe and can be reopened by new science. `ULTIMATE` is therefore an asymptotic research direction, not an allowed truth label.

## 7. Why breakthroughs and non-breakthroughs must be paired

A useful principle must explain both why a path can work and why superficially similar paths fail. The core observational object is therefore not a breakthrough alone but a contrast set:

\[
(\mathcal E^+,\mathcal E^-)
\]

matched or stratified by field, epoch, problem maturity, resources, team structure, prior knowledge and available instrumentation where possible.

The system should learn conditions under which an operator changes the protected outcome distribution, while preserving uncertainty about unobserved confounding.

## 8. Known population-level donors are hypotheses, not framework laws

Existing science-of-science results supply candidate regularities to reproduce and then challenge:

- high-impact work often combines a conventional knowledge base with atypical combinations;
- risky innovative strategies have higher variance and can yield exceptional impact while often failing or being ignored;
- exploration followed by exploitation is associated with hot-streak onset, while either phase alone is insufficient in those data;
- smaller teams and larger teams occupy different disruptive/development niches;
- the scientific literature has become narrower in the portions of prior knowledge it uses in some large-scale analyses.

V2 must not encode these as universal prescriptions. It must ask whether they replicate under alternative outcome definitions, causal controls, fields, epochs and prospective task environments.

## 9. Data universe

The programme should be built from multiple source modes, with separate provenance and licensing:

- broad scholarly graphs and author/citation histories;
- PubMed/MEDLINE and domain-specific curated corpora;
- preprint/version histories;
- patents and software artifacts where lawful;
- grants, registered reports and protocols where accessible;
- corrections/retractions and replication records;
- benchmark, code and data lineage;
- curated historical process records/lab notebooks only where rights permit;
- prospective ORION/human scientific-development episodes.

Bibliographic graphs alone cannot identify reasoning processes and systematically omit failed/unpublished work.

## 10. Three evidence layers

### Observational meta-science
Discover robust population regularities with time/domain/team/author splits, missingness analysis and alternative outcome definitions.

### Mechanism reconstruction
Use richer process traces, historical records and controlled tasks to identify candidate development operators rather than only correlations in final publications.

### Prospective intervention
Freeze a meta-principle, use it to choose a research action/representation/transfer/formalism strategy on unseen tasks, and compare protected outcomes against strongest parent/meta-science baselines.

Only the third layer can support causal claims about using a learned principle to improve scientific development.

## 11. Interaction with existing V2 layers

- **Knowledge Metabolism:** learns what information to retain/recombine/reject inside an episode.
- **Transfer Discovery:** learns what structure may transfer between episodes/domains.
- **Conceptual Development:** learns when concepts/distinctions should change.
- **Formalism Genesis:** learns when the language itself requires extension/replacement.
- **Scientific Development Meta-Learning:** learns population-level conditions and sequencing principles governing when those lower-level mechanisms tend to help or fail.
- **Scientific-State Transition:** remains the authority boundary for what may actually be concluded.

This is a hierarchy of research objects, not a claim that every level is necessary.

## 12. Kill conditions

Contract this layer if:

- population regularities fail under time/domain/outcome robustness;
- simple bibliometric/network/process parents reproduce the same predictions;
- learned principles cannot predict failed as well as successful trajectories;
- gains vanish when author/team/institution/field leakage is removed;
- the learned abstraction cannot improve prospective scientific decisions;
- higher recursive levels add vocabulary/complexity without protected residual;
- apparent principles are artifacts of publication/citation/reward systems rather than scientific development.

## 13. Current terminal

`RECURSIVE_SCIENTIFIC_DEVELOPMENT = S2_RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS`

No “ultimate principle,” field law, breakthrough predictor or R4 paper claim is authorized. The next step is a protected population-scale and prospective programme that recursively learns, tests, contracts and re-learns development principles.
