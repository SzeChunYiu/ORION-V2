# Conceptual Development and Transfer Discovery Amendment V2
## Open-world scientific transfer with mathematics as a primary structural donor

**Supersedes:** the donor-universe framing of `CONCEPTUAL_DEVELOPMENT_AND_TRANSFER_DISCOVERY_AMENDMENT_V1.md` while preserving its non-authorizing scientific contract.  
**Framework role:** candidate amendment to the bounded Machine Epistemics foundation.  
**Scientificity:** S1 operational interface + S2 discriminating mechanism hypothesis.  
**Paper status:** shared infrastructure for P-A/P-B/P-C/P-D and the flagship; no new paper identity is granted by this document.

## 1. Correction of emphasis

Conceptual Development and Transfer Discovery are **not physics-derived mechanisms**. Physics is one donor family among many. The donor universe is open-world and must include mathematics and the full range of scientific disciplines.

Mathematics has a particularly important role because many of its historical developments explicitly concern the discovery of structure itself: abstraction from examples, invariant characterization, equivalence, structure-preserving maps, universal constructions, dual descriptions, obstruction and impossibility, counterexample-driven refinement, proof-generated concepts, and movement between representations. These are not pre-written lessons to inject into ORION. They are source-bound cases from which a transfer-discovery system may or may not infer useful abstractions.

The central research question is therefore:

> Can ORION discover which relational/formal/scientific structures are transferable from an open donor universe, including mathematics, and prospectively demonstrate that the discovered transfer improves a new decision, prediction, proof obligation or conceptual representation without corrupting native meaning?

## 2. Mathematics as a primary structural donor, not a rulebook

### 2.1 Structural mathematics

Modern structuralist mathematics provides historical cases where attention shifts from individual objects or calculation procedures toward structures and structure-preserving relations. Examples include the development from equation solving toward groups/fields in Galois theory, Dedekind/Noether-style structural methods, and later categorical approaches.

ORION must not encode `structuralism is always superior`. Instead, mathematical donor records should expose the original objects, mappings, invariants, failed formulations, and later abstractions. Transfer Discovery receives credit only if it reconstructs the native mathematical move and independently finds a useful target mapping.

### 2.2 Invariants and transformations

Klein's Erlangen programme is a useful historical donor because it connects geometries with transformation groups and their invariants. More generally, mathematics repeatedly uses invariants to separate substantive structure from representation-dependent detail.

This motivates an open discovery problem:

`candidate transformation family -> candidate invariant -> hidden cases -> preserve/reject`.

No specific invariant or transformation family is built into Machine Epistemics.

### 2.3 Proofs, refutations and proof-generated concepts

Lakatos's reconstruction of mathematical discovery provides a donor pattern in which conjectures, attempted proofs, counterexamples, hidden lemmas and revised concepts co-develop. This is useful for Conceptual Development because a failed proof may expose not only a false proposition but a missing condition or an inadequate concept boundary.

The framework therefore allows a counterexample to trigger a **concept-revision search**, but never automatically treats a counterexample as evidence for any particular revision.

### 2.4 Abstraction and universal characterization

Category theory is a major donor family because categories, functors and natural transformations were introduced to formalize recurring relations between mathematical structures, and universal properties can characterize constructions independently of a particular implementation. This supplies historical cases for discovering when an object should be represented by what it does in a web of morphisms rather than by internal construction details.

Again, ORION must discover whether such a move is relevant in a target problem. It must not contain `use category theory` as a universal heuristic.

### 2.5 Equivalence, duality and representation

Mathematics distinguishes equality, isomorphism, equivalence, natural isomorphism and many weaker/stronger forms of sameness. These distinctions provide unusually clean donor cases for the V2 problem of determining whether two representations support the same protected scientific decision.

A Transfer Discovery hypothesis must bind the exact proposed equivalence relation and test what is preserved and what is not.

### 2.6 Obstruction, impossibility and local-to-global failure

Mathematics often learns by proving that a desired construction cannot exist, by identifying an obstruction, or by finding conditions under which local solutions fail to extend globally. These are important donor cases because scientific intelligence must learn that a failure can reveal structure rather than merely trigger more search.

An obstruction transfer receives scientific credit only if it prospectively predicts a target impossibility/failure boundary and survives hidden cases.

### 2.7 Generalization by mappings rather than vocabulary

The mathematics-first principle is:

> Prefer discovery of a structure-preserving map, invariant, universal property, obstruction or counterexample pattern over mere shared terminology.

This is a methodological hypothesis for protected testing, not an established universal law.

## 3. Open-world donor universe

The donor search space is not a closed list. At minimum, calibration and prospective search should support:

- pure mathematics: algebra, geometry, topology, analysis, number theory, combinatorics, logic, foundations, probability, dynamical systems, category theory;
- applied mathematics and statistics;
- theoretical and experimental physics;
- chemistry and materials science;
- molecular/cellular/evolutionary/ecological biology;
- medicine and biomedical sciences;
- earth, climate and planetary sciences;
- astronomy and astrophysics;
- computer science and scientific computing;
- engineering and control;
- cognitive and behavioural sciences;
- social sciences where concepts, measurements and causal structures can be scientifically evaluated;
- metrology, methodology, metascience, data governance and other sciences of scientific practice.

The list defines calibration coverage, not an ontology ceiling. A new domain may enter if its source identity and native semantics can be bound.

## 4. Source-bound conceptual-development records

Each historical or contemporary donor episode should be represented as a sequence rather than as one hindsight summary:

`problem state -> native concepts -> anomaly/problem -> attempted representation -> counterexample/constraint -> new abstraction or restriction -> new consequences -> later limitations`.

The record must distinguish:

- what participants knew at the time;
- later reconstruction;
- native mathematical/scientific meaning;
- heuristic interpretation proposed by ORION researchers;
- actual transferable structure, if any, established only by protected target evidence.

This prevents famous historical cases from becoming hand-written universal lessons.

## 5. Transfer Discovery pipeline

The reference lifecycle remains:

`encounter -> structural description -> candidate discovery -> relational/formal alignment -> bounded projection -> native recovery -> negative-transfer challenge -> prospective consequence -> hidden evaluation -> transfer-memory update`.

For mathematics, `prospective consequence` may be a theorem condition, counterexample prediction, invariant, obstruction, equivalence class, useful representation, proof obligation or algorithmic decision rather than an empirical prediction.

For empirical sciences it may be a measurement, model, experiment, diagnosis, causal claim, transport decision, uncertainty representation or action recommendation.

## 6. Mathematics-specific evidence modes

Mathematical transfer requires evidence modes different from empirical transfer. The framework must preserve this type distinction.

Possible mathematical witnesses include:

- mechanically checked proof;
- independently checked proof;
- generated counterexample;
- model/countermodel;
- exact computation;
- equivalence/isomorphism witness;
- invariant-preservation witness;
- obstruction/non-existence proof;
- reduction to a known theorem with verified assumptions.

These do not automatically authorize empirical claims. Conversely, empirical replication does not prove a theorem.

## 7. Conceptual Development object

`ConceptState` remains versioned and source-bound. For mathematics, operational links may be formal rather than physical measurements. A concept state may therefore bind:

`identity, primitives, relations, axioms/assumptions, scope, constructions, morphisms/maps, invariants, equivalence notions, examples, counterexamples, parent concepts, proof obligations, unresolved anomalies, authority/source identity`.

The existing transition operators remain candidate reference operators:

`SPECIALIZE, GENERALIZE, SPLIT, MERGE, BRIDGE, REPARAMETERIZE, REVISE, DEPRECATE`.

They are descriptive interface types, not a claim that all mathematical/scientific development follows eight stages.

## 8. Stronger admission gate

A discovered transfer or conceptual transition earns protected scientific credit only if it passes the relevant non-compensatory gates:

1. donor/source identity bound;
2. native donor reconstruction/recovery;
3. target problem and criterion frozen;
4. relation/equivalence type explicit;
5. assumptions and scope explicit;
6. preserved invariants/old valid cases checked;
7. losses and forbidden projections recorded;
8. strongest parent/simple comparator executed;
9. prospective new consequence or formal necessity frozen before evaluation;
10. counterexample/negative-transfer search executed;
11. hidden or independently checked evaluation passes;
12. resource cost retained;
13. no authority or measurement corruption.

`NEW_VOCABULARY != NEW_SCIENCE` remains binding.

## 9. Discovery must beat fixed lessons

The decisive comparison is not only `F2 vs F0`. It includes:

- target-only reasoning;
- semantic retrieval;
- fixed lesson injection;
- strongest analogy/structure-mapping parent;
- strongest mathematical formal parent where applicable;
- F0 federation;
- static F2;
- transfer-discovery F2.

If hand-written mathematics/science lessons match the discovery mechanism at lower cost, the discovery hypothesis contracts to `FIXED_LESSON_SUFFICIENT`.

If the strongest mathematical/scientific parent already performs the transfer, the terminal is `PARENT_SUFFICIENT`.

## 10. Mathematics-first prospective challenge families

The confirmatory programme should include fresh cases that require discovery of structures analogous to, but not labelled as:

- invariance under transformation;
- quotient/equivalence formation;
- structure-preserving mapping;
- universal characterization;
- decomposition/recomposition;
- local-to-global compatibility;
- obstruction/non-existence;
- dual representation;
- hidden-lemma/counterexample-driven concept restriction;
- abstraction from several examples;
- change of representation that reduces proof/search complexity;
- identifying irrelevant versus essential structure.

Task tokens and domain semantics must be generated or held out so the correct move cannot be obtained by recognizing a famous theorem name.

## 11. All-science prospective challenge families

In parallel, fresh and naturalistic cases should test discovery from chemistry, biology, earth science, medicine, statistics, computation, engineering and other domains. The relevant transfer may concern mechanism, measurement, scale, causal structure, conservation, robustness, feedback, selection, uncertainty, experimental design, representation, failure topology or governance.

No domain family is privileged by the framework. Mathematics is privileged only as a rich source of formally checkable structural-development cases and as a source of tools for representing transfer.

## 12. Transfer Memory learns what *not* to transfer

Transfer Memory must retain successful and failed mathematical/scientific mappings:

`donors -> induced abstraction -> target -> prospective consequence -> witness/failure -> counterexample -> parent verdict -> cost -> disposition`.

Negative transfer should alter future retrieval/ranking without becoming an absolute universal ban unless the relevant impossibility has actually been established.

## 13. Connection to existing K0-K6

No new hard-coded science layer is introduced.

- **K0:** version concept/problem/regime identities.
- **K1:** store source-bound mathematical/scientific concept states, exemplars, proofs, counterexamples and abstractions.
- **K2:** represent discovered maps, invariants, equivalence relations, transport losses and forbidden projections.
- **K3:** preserve native disciplinary/community ownership of meaning and authority.
- **K4:** estimate value of structural transfer search versus local solution methods.
- **K5:** anomalies/counterexamples can trigger search, never justify a revision by themselves.
- **K6:** open-world saturation prevents donor search from collapsing to familiar physics or researcher-preferred subjects.

Knowledge Metabolism supplies decomposition, native reconstruction, recombination and recycling. Conceptual Development changes the representation/concept system itself. Transfer Discovery searches for source-bound structure that may justify such a change.

## 14. Scientific kill conditions

The mathematics/all-science extension fails as an independent mechanism if:

- semantic retrieval is sufficient;
- a fixed lesson library is sufficient;
- strongest mathematical/scientific parent methods are sufficient;
- induced abstractions fail on surface-permuted fresh tasks;
- false analogy rises materially;
- native mathematical/scientific meaning is corrupted;
- old valid cases are lost without explicit scope restriction;
- formal witnesses cannot be reproduced;
- empirical consequences do not replicate;
- transfer cost dominates without protected quality gain;
- historical calibration works but fresh/prospective transfer does not.

Valid terminals remain positive, negative, contextual, parent-sufficient, fixed-lesson-sufficient, merge/drop, or `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## 15. Literature anchors for the mathematics-first correction

- Imre Lakatos, *Proofs and Refutations*: mathematical concepts and proofs can develop together through counterexamples and proof analysis.
- Felix Klein, Erlangen programme: transformation groups and invariants as a unifying viewpoint for geometry, with historically complex reception rather than a simple universal template.
- Historical work on Dedekind and Emmy Noether: methodological structuralism and conceptual mathematics emphasize relations, mappings and abstract structure.
- Eilenberg and Mac Lane / category theory: categories, functors and natural transformations emerged from the need to formalize recurring structure-preserving relations; later universal constructions and equivalences generalized this style across mathematics.
- Historical development of Galois theory: movement from equation-solving procedures toward groups, fields and automorphisms is a canonical case of conceptual reorganization toward abstract structure.

These sources justify mathematics as a rich calibration/research domain. They do **not** prove that the corresponding lessons transfer to Machine Epistemics.
