## 2. Atlas-wide rules

A lane is reconstructed only when the programme can state:

- its native problem and object of study;
- canonical mechanisms and impossibility/negative results;
- strongest executable parent products;
- assumptions, scope and known failure modes;
- the exact ORION claim or transition it threatens;
- a protected case capable of separating parent sufficiency from a residual;
- the correct contraction terminal.

Citation count is not a coverage metric. A new term is not a new coordinate. A useful interface is not a scientific law. Agreement, formal acceptability, robustness or evidence certainty never automatically create scientific truth or authority.

## 5. Cross-lane deductions that are not foundation laws

### 5.1 Agreement is weaker than correctness

Distributed consensus can establish an ordered common state under declared fault assumptions. It cannot establish that the agreed scientific interpretation is true. This reinforces—not replaces—the execution/evidence, dependence and authority boundaries.

### 5.2 An accepted argument is weaker than a valid scientific conclusion

Argumentation semantics can identify acceptable sets relative to attacks, inference rules and preferences. They do not establish the truth of premises, appropriateness of the scientific model or legitimacy of the decision authority.

### 5.3 Robust action can be rational without a precise posterior

Deep-uncertainty methods show why preserving options, monitoring signposts and avoiding catastrophic regret can be preferable to optimizing one contested probabilistic model. This is parent pressure on action policy, not a license for arbitrary conservatism.

### 5.4 Evidence certainty is not the decision

Evidence grading, recommendation strength and legitimate decision authority are separate. The same scientific evidence can support different contextual recommendations without making the evidence itself subjective; the values, resources and mandates must be explicit.

## 6. Strongest-parent products now required

Protected studies must include at least these composed alternatives:

1. **Multi-agent transition product:** replicated log/consensus + provenance/dependence + native scientific evaluator.
2. **Assurance product:** SACM/GSN/CAE + defeasible argumentation + evidence/provenance + independent validity checks.
3. **Deep-uncertainty control product:** RDM/DAPP or minimax-regret parent + monitoring/signposts + native domain simulator/evaluator.
4. **Evidence-to-decision product:** evidence certainty/quality + contextual decision criteria + explicit authority root.
5. **Statistical credibility product:** Bayesian/statistical workflow + SBC/VVUQ + severity/sensitivity + robustness routes.
6. **Minimality product:** MDL/sufficiency/model-selection parent + native relation/transport method.

A proposed Machine Epistemics residual is not credible until the strongest relevant product receives the same information, tools and resources.
