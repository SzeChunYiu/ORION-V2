# Machine Epistemics — Bounded Foundation Candidate V1
## An Absorptive Federation for the Scientific Control of Machine-Mediated Inquiry

**Canonical foundation issue:** #41  
**Selected architecture:** F3 native/domain layer → F0 parent federation → F1 typed bridges → F2 absorptive transition candidate  
**Status:** internally complete bounded conceptual foundation candidate. It is not an externally established field, proven supertheory, final kernel or publication-ready result.

## Abstract

Machine-mediated research is becoming capable of searching, modelling, proving, simulating, experimenting, interpreting, reviewing and recommending across an entire scientific episode. The central problem is no longer only whether a machine can perform these tasks, but under what conditions their outputs may change scientific commitments, problem frames or research agendas. The relevant knowledge is already distributed across mature parent sciences: epistemology and learning theory; statistics and experimental design; causality, measurement and metrology; control, diagnosis and resilience; formal verification and numerical analysis; software testing and security; cognitive and social epistemology; research methodology and metascience; data governance and law; automated discovery and machine-native intelligence. Machine Epistemics is the bounded hypothesis that these parent sciences can be organized as an absorptive federation with a small set of cross-parent transition constraints. The foundation preserves domain-native practices and mature parent methods, introduces typed bridges only where their outputs interact, and treats a higher transition theory as valid only if it recovers parent verdicts, deactivates when local methods suffice, and produces prospective protected gains beyond the strongest parent federation. Seven candidate commitments define the internal foundation: bound transition identity and authority; layered warrant and detection adequacy; typed conservation through composition; plural support and selective reopening; constructive exploration separated from confirmation and bounded closure; parent recovery and upward theory dominance; and machine-native internal freedom with external scientific witnesses and governed use. This document completes the conceptual synthesis relative to a declared 22-family knowledge universe and three independent no-material-addition passes. Empirical superiority, external demarcation and field authority remain open.

## 1. The object of the field

### 1.1 From output evaluation to transition evaluation

An AI system can produce a correct string, proof, simulation, model, laboratory action or recommendation while the corresponding scientific transition remains invalid. Examples include:

- replicated consensus on a scientifically inadequate model;
- exact replay of a numerically wrong computation;
- successful proof of the wrong specification;
- high benchmark performance under an evaluator that cannot expose the deployment failure;
- semantically lossy ontology alignment;
- exploratory discovery narrated as confirmation;
- strong evidence used without legitimate data or action authority;
- secure, signed provenance for malicious or scientifically false content;
- a machine-native strategy whose gain disappears against a strong domain baseline.

The field object is therefore not the model output alone. It is the **scientific-state transition** produced or proposed inside a bounded research episode.

### 1.2 Machine-epistemic episode

Use the comparison interface:

\[
E=(P,F,S,O,A,R,M,V,X,H,K),
\]

where:

- \(P\) — problem, target and comparison/criterion identity;
- \(F\) — current problem and representation frame;
- \(S\) — plural scientific state: observations, claims, alternatives, uncertainty, competence and unresolved distinctions;
- \(O\) — active scientific obligations and blockers;
- \(A\) — admissible epistemic, computational, experimental and governance-relevant actions;
- \(R\) — resources, tools, instruments, access, time and compute;
- \(M\) — evidence identity, provenance, dependence, source mode and memory;
- \(V\) — validation, test/oracle, assurance and evaluator contracts;
- \(X\) — world, tool, human and institutional responses;
- \(H\) — append-only transition, failure, incident and negative-history record;
- \(K\) — externally rooted authority, custody, consent and constitutional constraints.

The tuple is not claimed as the final ontology of science or intelligence. It is a comparison boundary that makes certain otherwise hidden transition failures observable. Each domain can project it into its own objects and omit irrelevant coordinates with an explicit non-application argument.

### 1.3 Transition object

A candidate transition is:

\[
T_t:(E_t,a_t,x_t)\rightarrow(E_{t+1},\rho_t),
\]

where \(a_t\) is an action, \(x_t\) the resulting observation/output, and \(\rho_t\) a receipt binding the proposed scientific consequence.

A transition may:

- add, weaken, reject or reopen a claim;
- alter an uncertainty representation;
- revise the problem frame or model;
- transport a result to another context;
- change an experiment, instrument or computation;
- open or close a research route;
- recommend—but not self-authorize—an institutional action.

Execution proposes information. Interpretation proposes a scientific transition. Neither execution nor interpretation grants truth or authority automatically.

## 2. The absorptive federation

### 2.1 Layer 0 — native domain sciences and authority

Every scientific domain retains ownership of its:

- entities, quantities and mechanisms;
- instruments, measurements and error models;
- experimental and computational methods;
- professional skills and practical competence;
- source and data-governance rules;
- evaluators and standards of evidence;
- institutional decision rights.

This is the F3 constitutional layer. Machine Epistemics cannot replace pharmacometrics, numerical fluid dynamics, causal epidemiology, theorem proving, chemistry, metrology, Indigenous data governance or any other native discipline with a generic score or ontology.

### 2.2 Layer 1 — strongest mature parent federation

The F0 layer contains the strongest relevant parent methods, including:

- reliable inquiry, formal learning and belief revision;
- experimental design, sequential testing and metareasoning;
- causal transport, measurement invariance and metrology;
- formal verification, program synthesis and automated reasoning;
- numerical analysis, VVUQ and scientific-software validation;
- provenance, dependence-aware evidence, robustness and triangulation;
- assurance cases and defeasible argumentation;
- control, diagnosis, resilience and deep-uncertainty decision methods;
- cognitive, social and institutional epistemology;
- FAIR/CARE, research ethics, evidence-to-decision and law;
- open-ended, evolutionary and machine-native search;
- cybersecurity, privacy and secure research supply chains.

F0 is the strongest current empirical null. When a parent solves a case completely and more efficiently, the higher layers must remain inactive.

### 2.3 Layer 2 — typed interfield bridges

F1 bridges connect parent outputs only where a protected scientific decision spans them. A bridge binds:

`(native parents, shared domain, registered decision, relation, assumptions, witnesses, counter-probes, losses, recovery, expiry, authority)`.

Leading bridge families include:

- execution/computation → scientific evidence;
- relation/transport → revalidation and selective reopening;
- dependence/robustness → assurance and argument;
- uncertainty form → action and adaptation;
- exploratory construction → later confirmation;
- scientific support → recommendation and authority;
- machine-native internal process → external scientific witness;
- secure provenance/privacy → usable scientific evidence.

Bridge theories are genuine scientific objects when their validity conditions and counterexamples are not owned by one parent alone. They must not become a free-floating adapter catalogue.

### 2.4 Layer 3 — F2 absorptive transition metatheory candidate

F2 proposes a small set of constraints over cross-parent transitions. It does not implement the native algorithms. It attempts to explain recurring composition failures and generate discriminators that are difficult to see from one parent perspective.

F2 is **absorptive** only if it:

1. embeds or recovers native parent views;
2. preserves their valid verdicts and limitations;
3. exposes bridge conditions and failure propagation;
4. predicts cross-parent consequences prospectively;
5. remains locally inactive when unnecessary;
6. adds justified reach without critical regressions or universal drag.

F2 is currently a selected candidate, not an established superior theory.

## 3. Seven candidate foundation commitments

These are the minimal internally synthesized commitments after contraction of nine F2 propositions and the Pass-H–K findings. They are not yet universal laws.

## C1 — Bound transition identity and externally rooted authority

### Statement

A scientific transition binds the identity of its subject, problem and criterion, source/evidence basis, representation and evaluator epoch, affected claims and authority scope. A silent material mutation creates a new comparison identity or an invalid transition. No local computation, confidence, consensus, evidence aggregate or model output can create scientific, institutional, custodial or publication authority beyond its legitimate roots.

### Why it is needed

Without bound identity:

- changed criteria can be laundered into improvement;
- different model/data/instrument epochs can be compared as though identical;
- a local checklist, benchmark or ontology can inherit the authority of a canonical one;
- privacy or custody changes can disappear during reuse;
- a quorum of lower-authority agents can substitute for an unavailable authorized evaluator.

### Parent owners

Provenance, configuration management, formal specification, metrology, benchmark custody, law, research ethics and data governance.

### Falsifier

If native configuration/provenance/metrology/governance products reproduce every protected identity and authority decision with equal or lower cost, the common constraint contracts to an engineering convention.

---

## C2 — Layered warrant and claim-relative detection adequacy

### Statement

When a scientific claim materially depends on source identity, semantic interpretation, model adequacy, numerical/computational validity, oracle or test adequacy, argument structure, transport and authority, these links remain separately evaluable. The warranted scope cannot exceed a necessary invalid or unresolved link. A pass supports only the error classes the evaluator or oracle had a warranted capacity to expose under the registered cases, perturbations, stopping and selection history.

### Important clarification

“Weakest link” is not a universal scalar minimum. Different links can be categorical, set-valued, probabilistic, defeasible or authority-bound. The commitment means that validity at one layer cannot be borrowed to fill an invalid or unknown layer.

### Examples

- a stable algorithm solves an inadequate model;
- exact provenance reproduces a scientific bug;
- a metamorphic relation passes both correct and faulty programs;
- a formal proof verifies the wrong specification;
- an assurance graph has a false premise;
- a benchmark cannot distinguish the deployment failure;
- signed evidence is malicious;
- a private release has unreported bias/coverage loss.

### Parent owners

Assurance cases, formal argumentation, severe testing, numerical analysis, scientific-software V&V, metamorphic testing, VVUQ and epistemology of evidence.

### Falsifier

If a strongest assurance + VVUQ + oracle + native-evaluator product makes all the same decisions and diagnoses at lower cost, C2 adds no independent theory.

## C3 — Typed conservation through composition

### Statement

Relation family, direction, context, approximation, numerical/semantic loss, uncertainty form, evidence dependence, argument modality, security/privacy transformation and unresolved distinctions cannot silently strengthen, disappear or become exact through composition.

### Consequences

- observational equivalence cannot become causal equivalence;
- textual equivalence cannot become procedural competence;
- approximate transport cannot become exact because several links are chained;
- an interval bound cannot become a probability;
- ignorance cannot become a precise posterior without justified modelling;
- several dependent reviewers cannot become independent corroboration;
- semantic interoperability cannot erase local constraints;
- privacy noise/clipping must propagate into downstream uncertainty;
- authenticated provenance cannot become content validity;
- a recommendation cannot become authority.

### Parent owners

Abstract interpretation, simulation/bisimulation, causal transport, measurement invariance, metrology, imprecise probability, evidence dependence, ontology matching, numerical error analysis, privacy and formal argumentation.

### Falsifier

If correct native compositions already conserve every materially relevant type and loss, the common rule contracts to documentation.

---

## C4 — Plural sufficient support with selective invalidation and reopening

### Statement

Claims can have several sufficient support families. When evidence, relation, computation, evaluator, source governance or security state is invalidated, only commitments whose complete sufficient support has failed must reopen. Independent valid support remains standing. Unknown dependence or support identity prevents unjustified retention.

### Consequences

- one revoked source need not erase independently supported claims;
- global reset is an error when selective reopening is possible;
- a compromised dependency reopens every derivation materially dependent on it;
- changed calibration reopens only claims whose relevant measurement relation expired;
- multiple acceptable arguments remain plural when no discriminator resolves them;
- a raw failure log does not become a transferable lesson without attribution and scope.

### Parent owners

TMS/ATMS, belief revision, model-based diagnosis, support hypergraphs, argumentation, provenance and incident/revocation systems.

### Falsifier

If existing support-family and truth-maintenance systems handle every heterogeneous invalidation without semantic loss, the cross-parent commitment is redundant.

---

## C5 — Constructive exploration separated from confirmation, with bounded closure

### Statement

Exploratory and constructive actions may legitimately create new observables, representations, instruments, source routes and problem frames. Their outputs remain proposal/anomaly state until later discriminating evidence supports a claim. Closure is always relative to a declared search and evaluation universe, residual uncertainty, censored routes and reopening conditions.

### Consequences

- serendipitous encounters can open inquiry but do not prove explanations;
- representation change may be required when current variables cannot express a discriminator;
- reframing must not silently change the success criterion;
- open-ended or machine-native search can preserve diverse candidates without equating novelty with truth;
- a route-specific stopping rule cannot establish universal absence;
- inaccessible community, proprietary or security-sensitive routes remain visible as censored;
- a material new parent/source/criterion reopens the bounded foundation.

### Parent owners

Exploratory experimentation, abduction, active learning, automated discovery, open-ended learning, problem finding, systematic review, formal learning/stopping and qualitative saturation.

### Falsifier

If native experimental-design/discovery/stopping methods already track proposal status and bounded closure across all relevant modes, the common commitment adds no value.

## C6 — Parent recovery, local deference and upward theory dominance

### Statement

A higher-order Machine Epistemics theory is valid only if it recovers critical native parent judgments within their scope, states explicit bridge relations where translations are lossy, and remains inactive when a native parent or direct method is sufficient. It advances upward only through prospective cross-parent strict gains, explanatory compression, transfer, efficiency/resilience and independent evaluation.

### Required dominance dimensions

1. native verdict recovery;
2. protected scientific non-inferiority;
3. cross-parent strict gain over F0;
4. prospective generativity;
5. cross-domain transfer;
6. causal attribution to a bridge/commitment;
7. correct local deference;
8. resource and maintenance cost;
9. critical-failure and authority preservation.

### Consequences

- framework complexity is not evidence of superiority;
- a parent win is a successful contraction result;
- F2 must be replaceable by F0/F1/F3 where they dominate;
- a component that creates no protected decision value becomes parent adapter, contextual gate, redundant drag or removal candidate;
- human cognition is a donor and control, not the target architecture;
- a machine-native system does not win merely by being different.

### Parent owners

Interfield theory, reduction/emergence, conservative extension, integrative pluralism, model selection and component-value methodology.

### Falsifier

F2 is rejected as a superior foundation if F0/F1/F3 reproduce all decisions and generative consequences with equal or lower cost, or if parent recovery/naturalistic transfer fails.

## C7 — Machine-native internal freedom with external scientific witness and governed use

### Statement

Machine inquiry may use internal representations, search topologies, memories, synthetic experience and coordination processes that do not resemble human reasoning or natural-language traces. A scientific transition is adoptable only when a claim-relative external witness is sufficient for the required evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, relation, computation, affected commitments, security/privacy and legitimate source/action authority.

### Consequences

- human trace conformity is not a correctness criterion;
- opaque internal state is not automatically unacceptable, but claim adoption cannot rely on unexplained performance alone;
- exact formal certificates may be sufficient for formal claims but not empirical-world claims;
- resource-matched strong parent baselines and leakage audits are mandatory;
- tool actions must remain causally attributable to trusted intent rather than untrusted content;
- FAIR availability does not erase CARE, consent or custody;
- machine-generated confidence, value or consensus does not self-authorize action;
- the strongest system can be human–machine complementary rather than human-mimetic or machine-only.

### Parent owners

Automated reasoning, theorem proving, program synthesis, interpretability/accountability, scientific-software validation, human–AI collaboration, security/privacy and data governance.

### Falsifier

The commitment contracts if domain-native certificates and governance interfaces are always sufficient, or if machine-native gains disappear under resource, validation and authority controls.

## 4. Foundation-level invariants

The seven commitments jointly imply the following internal invariants:

1. **No self-authentication:** a process cannot validate its scientific correctness solely through its own confidence, reflection, consensus or internally generated evaluator.
2. **No cross-layer authority amplification:** evidence, computation and agreement cannot mint permission, consent or institutional authority.
3. **No type laundering:** approximation, uncertainty, dependence, semantic loss and modality cannot silently strengthen through composition.
4. **No forced single winner:** unresolved alternatives, conflicting evidence and multiple argument extensions remain representable.
5. **No silent criterion mutation:** legitimate reframing creates an explicit new identity or relation to the old problem.
6. **No execution/truth conflation:** successful operation, secure provenance and reproducibility are not scientific correctness.
7. **No human-architecture constitution:** human cognition supplies mechanisms, failure evidence and collaboration requirements, not the optimal machine design.
8. **No universal activation:** higher-layer machinery remains inactive when a native parent or simple control is sufficient.
9. **No literal civilizational saturation:** all closure is bounded and revisable.

These invariants remain candidates until protected cases and external review establish their necessity and independence.

## 8. Field demarcation

### Machine Epistemics is not

- general epistemology of machines;
- a replacement for domain science;
- generic workflow engineering;
- provenance or reproducibility alone;
- metareasoning or experimental design alone;
- machine consciousness or human cognitive imitation;
- a universal evidence score;
- a universal ontology of knowledge;
- an automatic authority or policy system.

### Candidate residual

The residual is the **scientific theory of cross-parent state transitions in executable machine-mediated inquiry**: how validity, uncertainty, dependence, relation, computation, exploration, reopening, closure and authority interact when no single parent owns the entire decision.

### Field falsifiers

The field contracts if:

1. F0/F1/F3 reproduce all protected decisions and generative outputs with equal or lower cost;
2. the commitments disappear when native domain semantics are restored;
3. the common layer adds documentation but no scientific decision value;
4. parent recovery fails;
5. machine-native gains are only compute, leakage, weak baselines or evaluator exploitation;
6. cross-domain transfer fails;
7. the framework creates unacceptable refusal, latency, attack surface or maintenance burden;
8. independent reviewers cannot identify a stable residual.

A contraction to an integration discipline would still be a useful scientific outcome.
