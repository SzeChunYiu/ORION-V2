# Machine Epistemics Foundation Synthesis Gate V1

**Status:** blocked until issue #41's atlas and post-material-addition conditions are satisfied. This document defines how the programme must think after saturation work; it does not perform or authorize the final synthesis.

## 1. Why synthesis needs its own gate

A large literature map can still produce a weak foundation. It can become:

- a catalogue without a theory;
- a vocabulary union that hides parent differences;
- an ontology inflated by every interesting idea;
- a manifesto whose claims are not tied to protected decisions;
- an elegant framework selected before the contradictory evidence was reconstructed.

The synthesis phase must therefore be delayed enough to reduce anchoring, but structured enough to do more than summarize the atlas.

The purpose is to derive the **smallest defensible set of foundation propositions** that explains recurring scientific-control failures, changes protected decisions across materially different domains, survives strongest-parent composition and exposes its own falsifiers.

## 2. Entry criteria

Global foundation synthesis may begin only when:

1. the declared parent lanes are reconstructed or explicitly censored;
2. each current K0–K6 interface, foundation law and paper contribution has a reverse-ancestry/ownership card;
3. strongest parent products are specified for every surviving residual;
4. contradictions and major literature disputes are recorded rather than resolved by preference;
5. the last material addition is identified;
6. three independent post-material-addition omission passes produce no material new coordinate, relation, obstruction, failure, benchmark family, parent contraction, authority distinction or paper identity;
7. the independent hostile ownership review is complete or explicitly unavailable;
8. naturalistic domain restoration exists for any proposition that claims cross-domain scientific value.

If an entry criterion fails, the terminal is `RETURN_TO_SATURATION` or `CANNOT_CHECK`, not “synthesis by best judgment”.

## 3. Synthesis cell

The final synthesis uses separate roles:

1. **parent compressor** — clusters terms/mechanisms already owned by mature fields;
2. **contradiction curator** — preserves incompatible accounts and identifies what evidence could discriminate them;
3. **cross-domain structural analyst** — searches for invariant decision structures after native semantics are restored;
4. **formalizer** — states candidate propositions and countermodels precisely;
5. **empirical methodologist** — identifies protected tests, estimands and evaluator custody;
6. **systems/efficiency reviewer** — checks whether the proposition improves harness value or creates drag;
7. **authority/governance reviewer** — separates descriptive, epistemic and normative claims;
8. **hostile field editor** — attempts to contract the proposed field to parents or domain-specific methods;
9. **independent domain reviewers** — assess whether the common abstraction distorts their sciences.

No one role can promote a proposition alone.

## 4. Synthesis sequence

### Stage S1 — Freeze and normalize the evidence surface

Freeze the atlas version, source cutoff, unresolved routes and latest material addition. Create content-bound identities for each reconstruction card.

Normalization must preserve native meanings. It may align fields such as:

- object/state;
- evidence/observation;
- action/intervention;
- claim/decision;
- relation/transport;
- failure/obstruction;
- authority/custody;

but it may not declare two native terms equivalent without a relation receipt and counter-probe.

### Stage S2 — Parent-owned compression

Group every candidate concept into:

- directly parent-owned;
- parent composition;
- domain-specific specialization;
- implementation/interface convention;
- benchmark/failure fixture;
- candidate residual;
- contradictory/unstable;
- `CANNOT_CHECK`.

The target is aggressive contraction. A smaller foundation with explicit adapters is preferred to a larger ontology that duplicates mature science.

### Stage S3 — Contradiction matrix

For every disputed foundation question, record at least:

- competing propositions;
- source identities;
- assumptions and domains;
- whether they are genuinely inconsistent or context-relative;
- observations/interventions that could discriminate them;
- consequences for ORION decisions;
- current terminal.

Key contradiction families include:

- universal method versus methodological pluralism;
- explicit rule versus skill/practice;
- internal cognition versus distributed cognition;
- coherence versus plural model portfolios;
- compression/simplicity versus explanatory/causal adequacy;
- robustness through diversity versus dependence among routes;
- transparency through structured method versus method-induced inconsistency;
- automated authority versus institutionally supplied authority;
- machine-native optimization versus human interpretability/collaboration.

Synthesis must not average these into vague compromise statements.

### Stage S4 — Blind structural clustering

Where feasible, cluster reconstruction cards using structural signatures without field labels, prestige or ORION terminology. Then restore labels and examine:

- clusters spanning materially different domains;
- surprising splits among similarly named concepts;
- apparent invariants caused by shared language or data;
- missing source modes or historical periods.

This is a discovery aid, not evidence of equivalence. Human/domain review and counter-probes remain required.

### Stage S5 — Generate multiple foundation candidates

Construct at least three alternative formulations:

1. **parent-composition interpretation** — no new field; only an integration discipline;
2. **minimal residual interpretation** — a small set of cross-parent scientific-transition laws;
3. **domain-federation interpretation** — no universal laws, but a common interface among domain sciences.

Additional candidates may include an assurance-science interpretation or machine-native-inquiry interpretation. The programme may not evaluate only the formulation it prefers.

### Stage S6 — Foundation proposition cards

Each candidate proposition binds:

`FoundationProposition = (`
`id, statement, native_parents, residual, changed_decision, status_type, evidence, counterevidence, assumptions, scope, costs, hostile_case, falsifier, benchmark_or_proof, authority_ceiling, failure_disposition)`.

#### Status types

- formal theorem/definition;
- empirical regularity/hypothesis;
- engineering interface requirement;
- normative/governance boundary;
- programme heuristic;
- unresolved conjecture.

A proposition cannot slide between types. For example, an engineering fail-closed rule is not automatically an empirical law of science.

### Stage S7 — Protected decision sufficiency

For every candidate proposition ask:

1. Which exact scientific transition changes when it is included?
2. Does `MINUS-proposition` create a protected regression?
3. Does the strongest parent composition recover the decision?
4. Is the proposition necessary, parent-replaceable, contextual, redundant drag, harmful or uncheckable?
5. Does it survive a materially different domain?
6. What new cost/failure surface does it create?

No proposition survives merely because experts find it philosophically attractive.

### Stage S8 — Coherence and non-overlap proof obligations

The resulting foundation must show:

- no two propositions own the same transition without a declared specialization;
- no proposition silently mints evidence or authority;
- approximation and uncertainty cannot disappear through composition;
- `CANNOT_CHECK` cannot be promoted by aggregation;
- problem/criterion changes preserve comparison identity or create a new one;
- human and machine-native cognition remain alternative mechanisms under common scientific criteria, not normative rankings;
- the foundation can be implemented by more than one architecture.

### Stage S9 — Hostile external review

Provide reviewers with:

- the parent atlas;
- the three alternative foundations;
- strongest parent products;
- negative and contradictory evidence;
- protected benchmark definitions;
- exact non-claims and contraction terminals.

Ask each reviewer to return one of:

- `PARENT_COMPOSITION_SUFFICIENT`;
- `MINIMAL_RESIDUAL_PLAUSIBLE`;
- `DOMAIN_FEDERATION_ONLY`;
- `FOUNDATION_OVERREACH`;
- `CANNOT_CHECK`.

Disagreement is recorded with dependence/source identity; it is not converted automatically to a majority verdict.

### Stage S10 — Foundation and manuscript contraction

Only after S1–S9:

- freeze a minimal foundation candidate;
- update K0–K6 or contract them;
- decide whether P-F or any other paper identity survives;
- rewrite the flagship around the surviving propositions;
- preserve failed candidate foundations as negative history.

## 5. Foundation quality criteria

A strong foundation should be:

- **parent-faithful** — native fields remain recognizable;
- **minimal** — each proposition has a decision witness;
- **typed** — formal, empirical, engineering and normative claims are separate;
- **falsifiable** — field and proposition contraction terminals are explicit;
- **non-anthropomorphic** — human cognition is neither ignored nor treated as optimal architecture;
- **machine-permissive** — internal representations may be nonhuman while interfaces remain scientifically auditable;
- **authority-bounded** — evidence never self-grants permission;
- **resource-aware** — validity and efficiency are measured separately;
- **resilient** — disturbance, recovery and graceful degradation are testable;
- **revisable** — new material parents or source modes reopen the foundation.

## 6. Maximum public complexity

The public foundation should be much smaller than the atlas.

The final flagship should normally expose:

- one bounded object of study;
- three to five organizing scientific questions;
- a small number of foundation propositions/laws justified by the synthesis;
- a parent-ownership map;
- a falsifiable empirical programme;
- explicit non-claims and contraction terminals.

The number of laws is not fixed in advance. A law survives because it is necessary and supported, not because the paper needs a symmetrical list.

## 7. Prohibited synthesis shortcuts

- selecting only literature that fits the existing K0–K6 factorization;
- counting citations as coverage;
- treating three similar keyword searches as independent saturation passes;
- converting inaccessible knowledge into absence;
- importing philosophical claims as empirical findings;
- treating one top-performing benchmark as evidence of a general foundation;
- inferring novelty from unfamiliar vocabulary;
- expanding the paper to demonstrate reading breadth;
- hiding parent wins, negative results or component drag;
- declaring machine-native cognition superior because it differs from human cognition.
