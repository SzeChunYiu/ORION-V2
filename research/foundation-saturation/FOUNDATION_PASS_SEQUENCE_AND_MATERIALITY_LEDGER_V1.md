## 3. Latest-material rule

The clean-pass counter begins **after Pass K**, because K adds a broad family and benchmark/failure structures. Passes H–K are retained as material discovery history and cannot be reclassified after outcome access.

A future material finding in L, M or N:

1. is assigned the next canonical pass identity;
2. expands or revises the declared knowledge universe;
3. resets the consecutive full-universe clean-pass counter to zero;
4. blocks internal synthesis until three later independent clean passes are completed.

## 4. Identity rule

Every pass receipt binds:

- canonical pass ID;
- content digest/commit identity;
- declared family universe version;
- routes and changed vocabularies;
- source/dependence ledger;
- expert-role review surface;
- finding kinds;
- materiality decision and rationale;
- predecessor latest-material pass;
- clean-pass counter before/after;
- authority ceiling.

File name or chronological order alone cannot determine saturation state.
