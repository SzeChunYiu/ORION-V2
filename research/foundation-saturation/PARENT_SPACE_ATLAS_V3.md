## 4. Atlas deductions that remain hypotheses

### 4.1 Scientific integrity is layered

A research transition can fail because of source governance, semantic mapping, scientific model choice, numerical problem/implementation, missing oracle, adversarial compromise, uncertainty representation, interpretation or authority. Passing one layer never certifies the others.

### 4.2 Honest uncertainty is representationally plural

A precise distribution, credal set, interval enclosure, conflict state and complete ignorance answer different questions. The foundation should conserve uncertainty form through composition unless a justified transformation is provided.

### 4.3 Security is not scientific correctness

Authenticated, confidential, fault-tolerant and attack-resistant operation can still produce scientifically invalid claims. Scientific validity also often depends on secure identities and uncorrupted artifacts. The two are coupled but non-equivalent.

### 4.4 Openness is not unrestricted reuse

Machine accessibility and reproducibility can conflict with privacy, CARE, consent, collective authority or security. These constraints must remain externally rooted and context-specific.

### 4.5 A superior field theory must absorb these parents without swallowing their native validity criteria

L35's upward-synthesis programme cannot achieve superiority through vocabulary coverage. A candidate supertheory must recover the parent verdicts of L01–L41, expose bridge conditions and add prospective cross-parent decision value under matched resources.
