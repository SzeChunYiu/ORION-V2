# Mathematics and All-Science Conceptual Development Donor Atlas V1

**Purpose:** open-world calibration/research atlas for Conceptual Development and Transfer Discovery.  
**Status:** donor atlas only; no entry is a Machine Epistemics law.  
**Rule:** historical reconstruction may motivate candidate abstractions, but only prospective protected target evidence can establish transfer value.

## A. Mathematics — primary structural donor family

### M01 Proofs, counterexamples and concept revision
Representative development: proof attempts expose hidden assumptions; counterexamples force refinement of theorem, proof or concept boundary.
Candidate structures to discover: hidden lemma, scope restriction, proof-generated concept, counterexample taxonomy.
Do not hard-code: `counterexample -> specialize`.

### M02 Transformation groups and invariants
Representative development: geometry classified/reframed through transformations and invariant properties.
Candidate structures: transformation family, invariant, orbit/equivalence class, preserved decision.
Do not hard-code: `invariance is always the right abstraction`.

### M03 Galois-theoretic reorganization
Representative development: shift from algorithms for solving individual equations toward fields, groups and automorphisms encoding solvability structure.
Candidate structures: move from object-level computation to symmetry/automorphism structure; impossibility/solvability criterion.
Do not hard-code: `replace computation by group structure`.

### M04 Dedekind/Noether structural methods
Representative development: definitions and proofs organized around relations, mappings, axiomatic structure and generality rather than special representations.
Candidate structures: isomorphism-invariant description, structure-preserving map, abstraction across examples.

### M05 Category/functor/natural-transformation discovery
Representative development: recurring structure-preserving relationships across mathematical fields formalized by categories, functors and natural transformations.
Candidate structures: map between systems, map between maps, naturality/coherence, compositionality.
Do not hard-code category-theoretic vocabulary into target domains.

### M06 Universal properties
Representative development: characterize a construction by its relation to all relevant objects/maps rather than a particular construction history.
Candidate structures: representation-independent characterization, uniqueness up to appropriate equivalence, minimal sufficient interface.

### M07 Equivalence and levels of sameness
Representative development: equality, isomorphism, equivalence, natural equivalence and weaker notions preserve different structures.
Candidate structures: typed sameness relation, preserved/forgotten structure, decision-relative equivalence.

### M08 Duality
Representative development: one mathematical structure can have a systematically related dual description.
Candidate structures: reversible/partially reversible representation change, swapped roles, proof/search simplification.
Do not hard-code: `every problem has a useful dual`.

### M09 Local-to-global reasoning
Representative development: local solutions or descriptions may or may not glue into a global object; obstructions characterize failure.
Candidate structures: local compatibility, gluing condition, obstruction, witness of non-globalizability.

### M10 Quotients and equivalence classes
Representative development: identify distinctions irrelevant to a chosen structure by quotienting under an equivalence relation.
Candidate structures: representational redundancy versus substantive difference.

### M11 Topological invariants and deformation
Representative development: classify objects by properties preserved under allowed deformation rather than coordinates.
Candidate structures: robust feature under transformation, representation independence, boundary of allowed perturbation.

### M12 Algebraic abstraction
Representative development: recurring operations/relations across number systems and concrete problems become groups, rings, fields, modules and other structures.
Candidate structures: common laws, homomorphism, factorization, extension/restriction.

### M13 Analysis and limiting processes
Representative development: convergence, completeness, approximation, continuity and generalized function concepts repair inadequate finite/algebraic intuitions.
Candidate structures: limit-sensitive concept revision, approximation with error, completion.

### M14 Measure/probability conceptualization
Representative development: probability and integration gain formal structure through sigma-algebras, measures and convergence theorems.
Candidate structures: event structure, additive/measure semantics, uncertainty-type conservation.

### M15 Logic/model theory
Representative development: separate syntax, semantics, models, proof systems and expressive strength.
Candidate structures: satisfiability versus provability, countermodel, conservative extension, definability, model dependence.

### M16 Type theory/foundations
Representative development: propositions/objects/constructions can be represented through typed formal systems with explicit formation and proof obligations.
Candidate structures: type-safe composition, proof-relevant identity, construction witness.

### M17 Combinatorial extremal/probabilistic methods
Representative development: existence and structure can be shown indirectly by counting, extremality or probabilistic arguments.
Candidate structures: proof-method transfer across surface-diverse objects.

### M18 Dynamical systems and qualitative structure
Representative development: phase portraits, stability, bifurcation and invariant sets expose qualitative regimes beyond closed-form solutions.
Candidate structures: regime transition, attractor/stability class, bifurcation trigger.

### M19 Optimization/variational principles
Representative development: diverse problems represented through objectives, constraints, dual variables and optimality conditions.
Candidate structures: constraint-active regime, primal/dual witness, trade-off surface.

### M20 Computational mathematics / theorem proving
Representative development: symbolic computation, numerical methods and proof assistants create machine-checkable witnesses but require specification and error discipline.
Candidate structures: executable proof/witness, formal specification, numerical-versus-symbolic validity.

## Cross-donor discovery dimensions

The Transfer Discovery system may search for candidate common structure along dimensions such as:

- transformation and invariant;
- equivalence/sameness relation;
- abstraction/generalization;
- specialization/scope restriction;
- decomposition/recomposition;
- local/global relation;
- mechanism/function;
- causal/associational relation;
- obstruction/impossibility;
- representation change;
- scale/regime;
- uncertainty representation;
- measurement/latent relation;
- proof/counterexample;
- feedback/control;
- selection/adaptation;
- robustness/failure topology;
- authority/provenance.

These dimensions are **search coordinates**, not guaranteed universal categories. New coordinates can be induced from donor data; stale coordinates can be removed if they do not improve protected transfer.

## Anti-hard-coding rule

The atlas may seed search coverage and evaluator construction. It may not provide the solver with the intended transfer label in confirmatory studies.

The scientific question is not whether ORION can recite these lessons. It is whether ORION can recover native source structure, induce a useful abstraction, find it again in a fresh target, reject decoys, and improve a protected consequence under matched resources.
