## 3. Finding H1 — inquiry has multiple epistemic action modes

Exploratory experimentation can establish regularities, stabilize phenomena or help form concepts before a sufficiently developed theory supports a clean confirmatory test. DENDRAL/BACON-style systems likewise expose that discovery can involve representation and search-space construction rather than only testing a fixed hypothesis.

Candidate interface:

`EpistemicActionMode = {CONFIRMATORY, DISCRIMINATIVE, EXPLORATORY, CONSTRUCTIVE, CALIBRATIVE, REPLICATIVE}`.

Material distinction:

- exploratory/constructive output may open new concepts, observables or obligations;
- it does not automatically confirm an explanation;
- replication requires a declared variation/transport scope;
- calibrative progress should not be silently scored as target discovery.

Candidate failures:

- `EXPLORATION_LAUNDERED_AS_CONFIRMATION`;
- `HYPOTHESIS_SPACE_LOCK_IN`;
- `CONSTRUCTED_PHENOMENON_TREATED_AS_UNMEDIATED_OBSERVATION`;
- `REPLICATION_SCOPE_UNDECLARED`.

Reduction: K4/K5 interface and benchmark pressure; native ownership remains with experiment/design/discovery parents.

## 4. Finding H2 — standards, concepts and instruments can improve iteratively

Historical measurement work on epistemic iteration shows that imperfect standards, concepts and instruments can constrain and improve one another. This is not permission for criterion drift. It requires visible circular dependence, preserved history, independent constraints and affected-claim revalidation.

Candidate object:

`CalibrationIterationReceipt = (target_construct, standard_before, instrument_or_evaluator_before, discrepancy, evidence, proposed_revision, standard_after, instrument_or_evaluator_after, uncertainty, independent_constraints, affected_claims, comparison_identity)`.

Candidate failures:

- `CALIBRATION_CIRCLE_HIDDEN`;
- `OLD_RESULTS_SILENTLY_REINTERPRETED`;
- `IMPROVEMENT_WITHOUT_INDEPENDENT_CONSTRAINT`;
- `CRITERION_ITERATION_LAUNDERED_AS_SAME_EVALUATION`.

Reduction: K0 identity, K2 cross-epoch relation, K3 revalidation and K6 evaluator custody.

## 5. Finding H3 — evaluator identity is not evaluator adequacy

A scientific pass is weak when the test would rarely expose the error relevant to the claim. Severe-testing/error-statistical traditions make this explicit. A criterion and evaluator can be perfectly bound yet scientifically non-identifying.

Candidate object:

`TestSensitivityProfile = (target_claim, relevant_error_classes, test_or_evaluator, perturbation_or_alternative_family, detection_probability_or_bound, blind_spots, calibration_evidence, scope, epoch)`.

Candidate failures:

- `WEAK_PASS_LAUNDERED_AS_STRONG_EVIDENCE`;
- `BOUND_EVALUATOR_NONIDENTIFYING`;
- `TEST_DISTRIBUTION_NARROWER_THAN_CLAIM`;
- `ERROR_CLASS_DEFINED_AFTER_OUTCOME`.

Reduction: P-D/K6 evaluation pressure; no universal severity scalar.

## 6. Finding H4 — the experimental system can generate scientific state

In model-based reasoning, laboratory studies and scientific infrastructure, instruments, model systems, data pipelines and representations do more than execute a pre-existing plan. They determine observable variables, possible interventions, artifact classes and sometimes the phenomena that become scientifically stable.

Candidate object:

`ExperimentalSystemState = (material_system, instruments, models, data_infrastructure, operators, calibration, observables, interventions, generated_artifacts, known_artifacts, safety_authority, epoch)`.

Candidate failures:

- `TOOL_LIST_LAUNDERED_AS_AFFORDANCE`;
- `APPARATUS_ARTIFACT_MISTAKEN_FOR_TARGET_PHENOMENON`;
- `INFRASTRUCTURE_CHANGE_SILENTLY_CHANGES_SEARCH_UNIVERSE`;
- `MODEL_SYSTEM_SCOPE_OVERGENERALIZED`.

Reduction: K1/K4 state-action context and K2 target transport; no K7.

## 7. Finding H5 — coordination does not require one universal ontology

Boundary-object and epistemic-culture work shows that an artifact can coordinate multiple practices through shared identity and limited common structure while retaining different local meanings. A universal receipt schema can become a hidden epistemic monoculture if shared labels are treated as universal semantics.

Candidate object:

`BoundaryInterfaceReceipt = (shared_identity, participating_practices, local_projections, common_invariants, translation_rules, unresolved_meanings, local_authorities, coordination_decision, prohibited_cross_projection_inferences)`.

Candidate failures:

- `SHARED_LABEL_MISTAKEN_FOR_SHARED_MEANING`;
- `UNIVERSAL_ONTOLOGY_ERASES_NATIVE_VERDICT`;
- `LOCAL_AUTHORITY_EXPORTED_GLOBALLY`;
- `BOUNDARY_OBJECT_PROMOTED_TO_EXACT_EQUIVALENCE`.

Reduction: K2/K3 interface pluralism.

## 8. Finding H6 — no thinking method is neutral across every problem class

Formal induction, Solomonoff/MDL traditions and No-Free-Lunch results pressure any claim of a universally best human or machine reasoning method. The conclusion is not that all methods are equal. It is that performance depends on representation, prior/bias, problem distribution and resources.

Candidate object:

`InductiveBiasDeclaration = (method, representation_language, prior_or_regularizer, search_order, invariances, assumed_problem_family, resource_regime, advantage_strata, failure_strata, fit_evidence, expiry)`.

Candidate failures:

- `UNIVERSAL_METHOD_WITHOUT_PROBLEM_DISTRIBUTION`;
- `REPRESENTATION_BIAS_HIDDEN_AS_NEUTRAL_REASONING`;
- `BENCHMARK_MATCH_LAUNDERED_AS_GENERAL_INTELLIGENCE`;
- `HUMAN_MIMICRY_TREATED_AS_UNBIASED_BASELINE`;
- `MACHINE_DIFFERENCE_TREATED_AS_VALUE_BY_ITSELF`.

Reduction: K0/K4/K5/K6 contract and evaluation pressure; materially strengthens P-F.

## 9. Finding H7 — machine-native internal freedom requires an external scientific witness

AlphaTensor, FunSearch, AlphaEvolve and work on latent/continuous reasoning demonstrate useful computation that need not resemble a human verbal trace. These examples do not establish general scientific intelligence. They establish that human process imitation is not the only route to valuable output.

The framework must reject both:

- human-trace conformity as correctness;
- opaque novelty/high score as self-authenticating discovery.

Candidate constraint:

> A machine may use internal representations and search processes optimized for its substrate, but every scientific-state transition must expose a source/evidence/assumption/relation/uncertainty/authority witness sufficient for the claim being adopted.

The obligation is claim-relative and does not require exposing every activation.

Candidate failures:

- `HUMAN_TRACE_CONFORMITY_LAUNDERED_AS_CORRECTNESS`;
- `OPAQUE_NOVELTY_LAUNDERED_AS_DISCOVERY`;
- `BRUTE_FORCE_GAIN_HIDDEN_BY_PROCESS_LABEL`;
- `SCIENTIFIC_CLAIM_WITHOUT_RECOVERABLE_WITNESS`.

Reduction: P-C/P-F experiments and K0–K6 output boundary; no paper admission.

## 10. Finding H8 — saturation requires its own evidence

Methodological research on saturation warns against declaring it from source count or vague repetition. ORION's foundation stop must expose the declared universe, new-information definition, source dependence, changed-vocabulary passes, censored routes and omission challenges.

The canonical #41 requirement is therefore retained:

- three complete post-material-addition full-universe changed-vocabulary hostile passes with no new coordinate, relation, obstruction, failure, benchmark family or parent contraction;
- reproducible independent ownership review;
- synthesis only after those passes.
