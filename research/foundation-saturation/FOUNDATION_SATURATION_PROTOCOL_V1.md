# Machine Epistemics Foundation Saturation Protocol V1

**Status:** mandatory research gate opened by issue #41. This protocol does not establish saturation, a field, a kernel, a foundation law or publication readiness.

## 1. Purpose

The foundation of Machine Epistemics must not be assembled from the vocabulary that ORION-V2 already prefers. It must be earned through the same discipline ORION applies to scientific claims:

1. bind the question and coverage universe;
2. reconstruct candidate parents in their native terms;
3. search remote fields and changed vocabularies;
4. recover negative, contradictory and censored evidence;
5. compare the strongest parent compositions;
6. contract duplicated mechanisms;
7. stop adding concepts long enough to synthesize;
8. expose the propositions, limits and falsifiers that remain.

The objective is not to read an arbitrary maximum number of documents. It is to make it progressively harder for a material parent, failure mode, knowledge form or scientific practice to remain invisible merely because it uses a different vocabulary, source ecology or representation.

## 2. Two different gates: saturation and synthesis

### Foundation saturation

A bounded search state in which repeated adversarial changes of vocabulary, taxonomy, source mode, historical period and problem framing produce no new material:

- coordinate;
- relation;
- obstruction;
- failure mode;
- transition type;
- benchmark family;
- parent-ownership contraction;
- authority distinction;
- paper identity.

Saturation is always relative to a declared universe. Literal saturation of all human knowledge is not claimable.

### Foundation synthesis

A separate reasoning stage after the search frontier stabilizes. Synthesis asks:

- Which distinctions are genuinely irreducible?
- Which are different names for one parent-owned operation?
- Which only matter in one domain or source mode?
- Which propositions change a protected scientific decision?
- Which propositions are merely good governance advice?
- Which surviving claims are formal, empirical, normative or unresolved?
- What would falsify the field-level residual?

More reading cannot substitute for synthesis, and elegant synthesis cannot excuse an inadequately searched parent space.

## 3. Review cell

Every material finding is cross-examined by at least these roles:

1. **Formal epistemology and learning-theory reviewer** — reliable inquiry, identification in the limit, dynamic epistemic logic, belief change, impossibility and information requirements.
2. **Philosophy and history of science reviewer** — experimentation, modelling, scientific representation, plural styles of reasoning, severe tests, robustness and theory change.
3. **Statistics, causal and measurement reviewer** — workflow, model checking, calibration, selective inference, design, triangulation, transport and metrology.
4. **Control, systems and assurance reviewer** — observability, controllability, fault diagnosis, safety/assurance cases, resilience, adaptation and fail-closed operation.
5. **AI and machine-native intelligence reviewer** — automated reasoning, program synthesis, search, meta-learning, self-play, open-endedness, non-linguistic representations and resource policies.
6. **Cognitive and nonhuman intelligence reviewer** — metacognition, memory, skill, development, animal cognition, evolutionary search and collective intelligence.
7. **Social, institutional, legal and intelligence-analysis reviewer** — testimony, criticism, incentives, standards of proof, structured analysis, authority and strategic response.
8. **Anthropology and practice reviewer** — apprenticeship, craft, repair, oral/local/Indigenous knowledge, human factors and distributed work.
9. **Hostile parent/editor reviewer** — seeks older ownership, false analogy, field inflation, weak baselines and concepts that do not change protected decisions.

The component or field advocate may define a hypothesis, but cannot be the sole owner of its final parent or semantic disposition.

## 6. Per-lane reconstruction card

Every lane records:

1. native problem;
2. native object(s) of study;
3. definitions and distinctions;
4. canonical positive mechanisms;
5. strongest formal/empirical results;
6. failure and impossibility results;
7. source and evaluator identities;
8. historical development and major disputes;
9. strongest current implementation or executable method;
10. relation to other lanes;
11. exact ORION/Machine-Epistemics collision;
12. candidate donation;
13. hostile case where the donation should not apply;
14. protected downstream decision, if any;
15. disposition: parent-owned / absorbed / benchmark-only / contradictory / new residual candidate / cannot check.

A slogan, abstract or secondary summary cannot close a lane whose detailed mechanism is material to the foundation.

## 7. Required search routes

### 7.1 Native route

Use the lane's own terminology, canonical problems and primary sources.

### 7.2 Reverse-ancestry route

Start from every proposed ORION primitive, law, failure mode and paper contribution. Search backward for older theories that already own it.

### 7.3 Changed-vocabulary route

Rewrite the current concept without ORION, AI-agent or epistemology vocabulary. Search by its input/output, transition, failure and decision structure.

### 7.4 Remote-neighbour route

Search materially different domains in which the same structural problem has operational consequences.

### 7.5 Strongest-composition route

Do not compare a proposed integrated idea with one parent at a time. Construct the strongest information- and resource-matched parent product.

### 7.6 Non-paper route

Deliberately examine practice, incident, standard, instrument, demonstration, oral and environmental sources.

### 7.7 Plural-epistemic route

Reconstruct non-Western and culturally distinct epistemic practices without treating Western academic categories as the neutral universal language.

### 7.8 Nonhuman and machine-native route

Search animal, collective, evolutionary and machine-native mechanisms so that human process is not mistaken for the definition of intelligence.

### 7.9 Hostile efficacy route

Search whether the proposed method has failed, added inconsistency, increased cost, become ritualized, or performed no better than a simpler control. Structured methods do not receive value merely for making reasoning look disciplined.

## 8. Omission challenges

Each pass is designed to make a specific omission likely to surface. Examples:

- **old-wine challenge:** find the older field that already owns the operation;
- **same-word/different-problem challenge:** reject lexical similarity;
- **different-word/same-problem challenge:** recover remote ownership;
- **human-default challenge:** identify machine or nonhuman alternatives;
- **paper-default challenge:** find practice or environmental knowledge;
- **formalism-default challenge:** find tacit, institutional or material dependencies;
- **pluralism challenge:** vary scientific style, method and epistemic practice;
- **independence challenge:** reveal common causes among apparent confirmations;
- **severity challenge:** ask whether the test could have exposed the proposed error;
- **workflow challenge:** inspect model-building, checking and analysis choices rather than only the final estimator;
- **assurance challenge:** distinguish evidence from the argument connecting it to a bounded claim;
- **resilience challenge:** test recovery and graceful degradation, not only nominal success;
- **authority challenge:** separate support from permission, consent and allocation;
- **cost challenge:** identify concepts that reduce validity or efficiency;
- **discordance challenge:** preserve conflicting evidence rather than averaging it away.

## 9. Evidence and coverage ledger

Coverage is tracked as a vector, not a single percentage:

`Sat_t = (`
`lane_gap, native_source_gap, primary_source_gap, source_mode_gap, cultural_gap, historical_gap, reverse_ancestry_gap, changed_vocabulary_gap, strongest_composition_gap, negative_evidence_gap, naturalistic_gap, evaluator_gap, new_coordinate_rate, parent_contraction_rate, open_censored_routes)`.

A lane marked `covered` must expose its evidence level:

- primary sources directly reconstructed;
- authoritative reconstruction only;
- empirical/formal cross-check complete;
- current implementation audited;
- independent parent reviewer complete;
- unresolved/censored.

## 10. Materiality test

A finding is material only if it changes at least one of:

- protected scientific decision;
- necessary information or relation;
- failure/terminal semantics;
- benchmark design;
- parent ownership;
- component activation policy;
- paper thesis or non-claim boundary;
- authority/custody requirement.

Interesting vocabulary without a changed decision becomes a parent note, curriculum item or historical context—not a new kernel coordinate.

## 11. Post-material-addition stopping rule

After the last material addition, complete at least three whole-pass omission challenges that deliberately vary different axes. A valid trio must include:

1. one formal/statistical/systems pass;
2. one human/social/practice/plural-epistemic pass;
3. one machine-native/nonhuman/remote-domain pass.

Each pass must add no material coordinate, relation, obstruction, failure, benchmark family, parent contraction or paper identity. Repeating similar keyword searches does not count as an independent pass.

A route that is inaccessible, proprietary, culturally restricted or untranslatable is recorded as censored/unknown. It cannot be counted as negative evidence.

## 12. Synthesis moratorium

Once the post-material-addition passes begin:

- no new kernel family is admitted;
- no foundation law is promoted;
- no new standalone paper is admitted;
- no flagship expansion is made merely to include another literature;
- new search is limited to resolving explicit contradictions, missing ownership or evaluator challenges.

The programme then clusters and contracts the atlas before writing the foundation.

## 13. Foundation proposition gate

Every surviving proposition must bind:

- proposition identity;
- native parent owners;
- exact residual beyond parent composition;
- scientific decision changed;
- formal, empirical, normative or engineering status;
- strongest supporting evidence;
- strongest counterevidence;
- assumptions and scope;
- cost/complexity implications;
- hostile counterexample;
- falsifier;
- benchmark or proof obligation;
- authority ceiling;
- disposition if falsified.

Possible terminals:

- `PARENT_OWNED`;
- `INTEGRATION_INTERFACE_ONLY`;
- `DOMAIN_SPECIFIC_METHOD`;
- `CANDIDATE_CROSS_DOMAIN_LAW`;
- `GOVERNANCE_REQUIREMENT`;
- `BENCHMARK_ONLY`;
- `CONTRADICTED`;
- `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## 14. Paper and architecture propagation

### Flagship

The Machine Epistemics Perspective may describe the field as a falsifiable hypothesis, but cannot present the foundation as settled until issue #41 closes.

### P-A–P-F

Every paper contribution must point to an atlas lane, strongest-parent composition and omission challenge. A paper identity does not survive merely because the literature is large.

### Kernel

K0–K6 remain candidates. The atlas may contract, gate or parent-replace them. It cannot expand the kernel through terminology alone.

## 15. Current known high-priority gaps

The first unified atlas pass must deepen:

- statistical workflow and analysis-decision provenance;
- severe testing and test sensitivity;
- robustness/triangulation under dependence and discordance;
- assurance cases and claim–argument–evidence structure;
- resilience and graceful extensibility under surprise;
- structured analytic techniques, including evidence that they can add inconsistency;
- law/evidence and standards of proof;
- styles of scientific reasoning and methodological pluralism;
- MDL/algorithmic complexity as representation and model-selection parents;
- domain-naturalistic validation beyond constructed ORION fixtures.
