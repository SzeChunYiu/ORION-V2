## 1. Reopening reason

The early foundation was derived largely from explicit hypotheses, evidence, reasoning, relation/transport, review and closure. Expanded human/practice research added skill, framing, self-monitoring, communication, distributed cognition and surprise. Pass H adds further material structure:

- exploratory and constructive experiment before a stable hypothesis space;
- epistemic iteration among instruments, standards and concepts;
- test sensitivity/error-detection adequacy;
- experimental systems/infrastructure as generative scientific state;
- boundary interfaces among epistemic cultures;
- inductive-bias/problem-class dependence;
- machine-native internal computation with an external scientific witness.

Under ORION's no-silent-closure rule, a material addition resets the synthesis gate.

## 2. Existing candidate invariants retained but unfrozen

The current principles remain strong candidates:

- comparison identity cannot change silently;
- execution is not evidence by itself;
- support binds to actual source identity;
- local computation cannot amplify authority;
- transport is typed/context-relative;
- invalid support triggers selective reopening;
- dependence is not independence;
- censored search is not evidence of absence;
- closure is bounded to a declared universe;
- `CANNOT_CHECK` is a legitimate outcome.

They are not revoked, but they are no longer treated as the final ten laws.

## 3. New candidate invariants under reconstruction

- **inductive-bias visibility:** methods bind representation, prior/search preference, problem family and resources;
- **error-detection adequacy:** an evaluator pass supports only the errors the evaluator could expose;
- **exploratory-action legitimacy:** exploration/construction may expand the scientific state without confirming an explanation;
- **calibration-iteration visibility:** standards/evaluators may improve only with preserved history, uncertainty and revalidation;
- **interface pluralism:** shared machine structure may require local projections rather than universal semantics;
- **machine-native internal freedom with external witness:** non-human computation is admissible, but scientific transitions require claim-sufficient external receipts.

These may merge into existing candidates, be returned to parents or fail protected tests. No final law count is authorized.

## 4. Working object remains provisional

`E = (P, F, S, O, A, R, M, V, X, H, K)` remains useful, but Pass H asks whether action mode, experimental-system state, inductive-bias declaration, test sensitivity, calibration iteration and local practice projections are:

- optional fields;

The tuple must not grow by literature accumulation. Every addition faces FULL/MINUS/PARENT/MERGED and cost tests.

## 6. Synthesis custody

The saturation chamber may not freeze foundation prose. The synthesis chamber sees frozen reconstruction artifacts only. A hostile court then seeks old-wine ancestry, parent sufficiency, anti-ORION cases, overhead and naturalistic counterexamples.

This operationalizes “saturate, then think” as a gate rather than an intention.
