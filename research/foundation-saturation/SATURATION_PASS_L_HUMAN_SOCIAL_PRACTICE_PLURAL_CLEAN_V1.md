## 4. Specific hostile reductions

### L1 — manufactured ignorance

Agnotology shows that ignorance can be deliberately produced through secrecy, selective funding, misleading studies, vocabulary control or saturation with doubt. This is not a new epistemic state beyond ORION's censored routes, negative knowledge, strategic dependence, adversarial integrity and performative institutions. It does strengthen one failure:

`MANUFACTURED_IGNORANCE = strategic actions make a relevant route absent, distorted or prohibitively costly while preserving an appearance of ordinary uncertainty`.

This is a benchmark subtype, not a new family or kernel enum.

### L2 — judgment aggregation impossibility

Individually coherent reviewers can produce an incoherent collective judgment under proposition-wise aggregation. This directly attacks majority-vote review. It is already absorbed by F03/F08/F11, L31 consensus and L32 argumentation:

- vote count is not argument consistency;
- group rationality depends on aggregation rule and agenda;
- no universal aggregation rule satisfies all desired properties;
- plural unresolved state can be correct.

No new coordinate survives.

### L3 — maintenance and repair

Maintenance studies make invisible infrastructure, tacit diagnostic work and unequal burdens visible. The donation is already represented by:

- failure lessons;
- negative history;
- recovery and resilience;
- distributed cognitive episodes;
- component-value/maintenance cost;
- infrastructure and supply-chain identity.

A `MaintenanceBurdenProfile` would add reporting convenience but no protected decision not expressible through F10/F13/F17/F19. It is not admitted.

### L4 — high-reliability organizations

Preoccupation with failure, reluctance to simplify, sensitivity to operations, resilience and deference to expertise are strong organizational parents for monitoring and recovery. They do not establish a universal scientific method and can become ritualized. Their protected value belongs in P-C resilience and method-efficacy controls.

### L5 — participation and epistemic justice

Pass J already found that evidence systems can exclude sources before aggregation and that participation, competence, dependence and authority must remain separate. Further reconstruction adds no new coordinate. Participation remains contextual and must be tested against burden, noise, tokenism and delayed agenda access.

### L6 — CARE and community custody

CARE strengthens the authority/custody semantics already recorded. It does not become a universal score or evidence law. Some native/community routes remain censored to ordinary open-web research and therefore remain explicit reopening conditions.

## 5. Negative and non-application controls

- an exact formal proof task where participatory deliberation adds no scientific value;
- a simple numerical transformation where maintenance sociology should not trigger extra workflow;
- an independent expert source correctly receives greater evidential weight than an uninformed source;
- a lawful/authorized open dataset where CARE-specific collective restrictions are not applicable;
- a majority vote that is coherent because the proposition agenda is separable;
- a breakdown with no transferable lesson beyond local repair;
- an agnotology accusation unsupported by evidence of strategic production.
