# Machine Epistemics Parent-Space Atlas V1

**Status:** first unified atlas under issue #41. This is a coverage and ownership map, not a claim that any lane is saturated. `STRONG_INITIAL` means the programme already contains substantial native reconstruction; it does not mean the lane is complete or independently reviewed.

## 1. Why a parent-space atlas is needed

Machine Epistemics sits at the intersection of many mature disciplines. That makes ordinary literature review especially dangerous: the programme can appear novel because the same problem is divided across incompatible vocabularies, or it can flatten genuinely different scientific relations into one general framework.

The atlas therefore records, lane by lane:

- the native question;
- current ORION coverage;
- likely ownership or collision;
- the protected decision that could justify further integration;
- the next omission challenge;
- the current honest disposition.

A lane is not credited for the number of citations it contains. It is credited when its native mechanisms, counterexamples and strongest executable methods have been reconstructed well enough to challenge ORION's proposed decisions.

## 2. Coverage status vocabulary

- `STRONG_INITIAL` — substantial reconstruction and direct programme use exist; independent saturation remains open.
- `PARTIAL` — important sources/mechanisms are mapped, but native breadth or current methods remain incomplete.
- `MATERIAL_NEW_PRESSURE` — the unified pass found a parent or distinction that changes benchmark, interface or paper design.
- `OPEN` — only preliminary or adjacent coverage exists.
- `SENSITIVE_OR_CENSORED` — access, consent, translation, proprietary or community-custody conditions prevent ordinary open-corpus treatment.
- `PARENT_OWNED` — current evidence places the core mechanism in a mature parent; ORION may use an interface or adapter but does not own the mechanism.
- `RESIDUAL_OPEN` — a cross-parent protected decision remains plausible but untested.

## 4. First unified findings

### 4.1 The analysis workflow is part of the epistemic object

A final estimator, model score or posterior is insufficient evidence about the validity of a scientific analysis. Model construction, computational implementation, checking, sensitivity, comparison and troubleshooting can change the result and its interpretation. ORION therefore needs analysis-decision provenance and computational-validation obligations in addition to source provenance.

### 4.2 A test result is only as informative as its capacity to expose the error

A pass from a weak or non-identifying test cannot support the same transition as a pass from a severe, high-sensitivity test. Evaluator identity must be supplemented by a claim about what error classes the evaluator could have detected under the case.

### 4.3 Multiple methods do not automatically create robustness

Robustness and triangulation depend on variation of assumptions and on independence conditions. Several methods can share the same hidden defect, and genuinely discordant evidence should remain visible rather than being summarized into artificial consensus.

### 4.4 Evidence requires an argument to support a bounded claim

Assurance-case traditions make explicit the chain from claim, through argument and assumptions, to evidence. ORION receipts contain many of these ingredients, but the programme has not yet shown that a separate scientific-assurance object changes protected decisions. This is now a benchmark hypothesis, not a component admission.

### 4.5 Structured reasoning can become ritual or add error

A documented structured technique can improve transparency while failing to improve—and sometimes degrading—judgment consistency or accuracy. Therefore ORION cannot validate a reasoning component merely because it forces decomposition, documentation or explicit matrices. Every reasoning aid must pass the same component-value and negative-control protocol as any other mechanism.

### 4.6 Scientific control needs resilience measures, not only nominal correctness

A system can pass normal cases yet fail abruptly when assumptions, resources or event severity change. Recovery time, graceful degradation, adaptive-capacity sharing and capability preservation are distinct from nominal accuracy and should enter protected harness evaluation.

### 4.7 Simplicity/compression is a parent, not a universal law of truth

MDL and related principles provide strong model/representation selection baselines balancing fit and complexity. They do not by themselves establish scientific truth, causal adequacy, authority or safety. Any ORION minimal-envelope claim must compare against these parents while preserving decision-relative semantics.

## 5. Candidate new research objects — not admitted components

The unified pass creates four objects for hostile testing:

1. `AnalysisWorkflowReceipt` — model/data/code/evaluator decisions and checks across an analysis episode.
2. `TestSeverityReceipt` — target error class, detection capability, assumptions and limits of a pass/fail.
3. `ScientificAssuranceCase` — bounded claim, argument, evidence, assumptions, defeaters and confidence/revalidation plan.
4. `ResilienceProfile` — nominal capability, disturbed capability, degradation, recovery, adaptive-capacity sources and cost.

Each remains outside the kernel until removal/replacement experiments show a protected decision not already recovered by K0–K6 plus mature parent adapters.

## 6. Cross-paper pressure

- **P-A:** compare minimal envelopes and donor reductions against MDL/compression parents; add analysis-workflow provenance where donor conclusions depend on modelling decisions.
- **P-B:** add test sensitivity and robustness under changed assumptions to transport evidence; preserve discordance.
- **P-C:** add analysis-workflow diagnosis, method-efficacy negative controls and resilience/recovery endpoints.
- **P-D:** incorporate robustness independence, assurance arguments and structured-method common-cause failures.
- **P-E:** opportunity discovery must distinguish robust anomaly from workflow artifact and use prospective severe discriminators.
- **P-F:** machine-native difference must outperform MDL/search/automated-reasoning parents and demonstrate resilience rather than only benchmark peaks.
- **Flagship:** the field foundation must remain provisional until the atlas and synthesis gate close.
