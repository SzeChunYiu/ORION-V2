## 4. System-level reductions

### M1 — automatically verified discovery

AlphaTensor discovers exact tensor decompositions; AlphaDev searches assembly programs; FunSearch and AlphaEvolve combine generative models, evolutionary search and automated evaluators. Their common structure is:

`human/machine-defined problem representation + candidate generator + population/archive + evaluator + selection/mutation + externally inspected survivor`.

This structure is already represented by F03/F04/F06/F13/F14/F18/F19. The evaluator defines the accessible notion of improvement; if it omits numerical stability, safety or scientific adequacy, the search can optimize the omission.

### M2 — machine-native difference is not value

A strategy differing from human traces is neither superior nor inferior by itself. Protected value requires:

- matched information/tools;
- strong native algorithmic parent;
- resource curve;
- causal component ablation;
- cross-domain or changed-context transfer;
- external witness sufficient for the claim;
- failure and authority controls.

This is already the P-F survival gate and F21 dominance test.

### M3 — quality diversity and open-endedness

QD archives preserve diverse high-quality candidates, avoiding premature single-winner collapse. However, behavior descriptors, quality metrics and environments determine what diversity exists. Open-endedness can generate unlimited novelty without scientific relevance. F13/F14/K5 already represent the proposal portfolio, exploration signal and noise trap.

### M4 — self-referential improvement

Gödel-machine-style proposals prove self-rewrites relative to a formal utility function, axioms and hardware description. Their guarantee does not establish that the utility function captures scientific truth or legitimate authority. The distinction is absorbed by F01/F03/F06/F18/F21 and the criterion-conservation law candidate.

### M5 — neural algorithmic reasoning

Neural systems can learn latent execution patterns across algorithms, but benchmark performance can remain underspecified and fail out of distribution. The existing F15/F19 pipeline-validity and stress-test structures cover this without requiring human-readable internal traces.

### M6 — world models and synthetic experience

Synthetic environments can improve a policy or search process. They remain evidence about the simulator/model unless a relation supports transport to the target world. This is already execution/evidence separation plus K2 transport and F05/F07.

## 5. Hostile controls

- exact evaluator rewards a numerically unstable algorithm;
- discovered program exploits evaluator bug;
- apparent gain disappears against optimized human/native baseline;
- more compute explains the full gain;
- behavior descriptor excludes the useful solution class;
- self-play becomes detached from target-world evidence;
- proof establishes utility under a misspecified objective;
- latent strategy succeeds on benchmark and fails changed representation/hardware;
- human-readable translation alters the discovered algorithm;
- machine-only arm violates source custody or authority while hybrid arm does not.
