## 1. Domain restoration set

The pass restored native semantics in four materially different domains:

1. **Computational physics / ML for PDEs** — numerical approximation, conservation, stability, error–cost matching and strong numerical baselines.
2. **Model-informed drug development and clinical evidence-to-decision** — model risk/impact, evaluation/documentation, evidence certainty, benefits/harms, values, regulatory recommendation and authority.
3. **Autonomous materials and chemical laboratories** — computational screening, literature-derived procedures, active learning, instruments/robotics, characterization, failure diagnosis and safety/dual-use.
4. **Formal mathematics and algorithm discovery** — exact specifications/evaluators, proof or exhaustive verification, machine-native search, hardware-relative efficiency and mathematical versus empirical authority.

These domains were selected because they differ in ontology, evidence form, error mechanism, experiment relation, computational validity and authority.

## 4. Domain-native findings and contractions

### N1 — computational physics

ML PDE claims cannot be evaluated by wall-clock speed or benchmark error alone. The native decision requires matched numerical accuracy, stable/optimized baselines, conditioning, conservation, discretization, hardware, setup/training cost and reporting of failed/negative cases. The 2024 systematic review by McGreivy and Hakim found that 60 of 76 sampled fluid-PDE ML papers claiming superiority used a weak baseline, demonstrating why strongest-parent binding is non-optional.

**Contraction:** generic "machine faster than numerical method" claims return to F15/F19/component-value analysis. No new field coordinate.

### N2 — model-informed drug development and clinical recommendations

ICH/FDA M15 treats model-informed evidence as a planned, evaluated and documented contribution whose risk and impact depend on its decision context. GRADE/EtD separates evidence certainty from recommendation strength, values, resources, feasibility and authority.

**Contraction:** the foundation already represents evidence-to-decision, model risk, uncertainty, context and externally supplied authority. Clinical/regulatory specifics remain domain adapters.

### N3 — autonomous materials and chemistry

A-Lab integrates computation, historical data, active learning, robotics and characterization; Coscientist integrates retrieval, code, planning and laboratory execution. Their results depend on target selection, synthesis/characterization semantics, instruments, active-learning loops and safety boundaries. Failures can reveal kinetic, computational, procedural or instrumentation limitations.

**Contraction:** the episode object, distributed cognition, failure lessons, opportunity/experiment selection, computational validity, source dependence and authority cover the decision structure. Chemistry/materials methods remain native parents.

### N4 — formal algorithm discovery

AlphaTensor, AlphaDev and FunSearch show that machine-native search can discover exact or verifiably superior algorithms under formal evaluators. Their scientific strength comes from evaluator clarity and formal verification; their limitation is dependence on the specified representation/search space/objective and often large compute.

**Contraction:** machine-native search and exact proof are already F06/F14/F18/F19. Formal truth does not establish empirical or authority claims.

## 5. Negative controls

- an exact theorem proof where clinical EtD criteria are irrelevant;
- a clinical recommendation where formal proof of software correctness does not decide patient values;
- an ML PDE solver that loses to an optimized numerical method;
- an autonomous lab success that depends on a human-selected target and instrument interpretation;
- a formally correct algorithm slower on a different hardware/compiler;
- a model-informed decision correctly remaining conditional despite a technically valid model;
- restricted data whose absence prevents universal cross-domain comparison.
