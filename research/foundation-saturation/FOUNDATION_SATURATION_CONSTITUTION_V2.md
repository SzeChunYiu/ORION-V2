# ORION Foundation Saturation Constitution V2

**Canonical owner:** issue #41  
**Material Pass-H child:** issue #42

**Status:** operational constitution supplement to `FOUNDATION_SATURATION_PROTOCOL_V1.md`. It grants no foundation freeze, kernel authority, field status, scientific truth, novelty or publication readiness.

## 1. Constitutional method

The Machine Epistemics foundation must be subjected to ORION rather than merely described by it:

`divergent search -> native reconstruction -> hostile ancestry/parent search -> contradiction -> contraction -> minimal synthesis -> protected intervention -> external demarcation`.

A large bibliography is not saturation. A polished synthesis is not a foundation. A foundation candidate is admissible only after the declared universe has stopped producing material structure under independent changed-vocabulary and hostile-parent passes.

Literal exhaustion of all knowledge is not claimable. The valid target is bounded saturation over a declared, revisable universe with explicit open and censored routes.

## 2. Two chambers and one hostile court

### Saturation chamber

May produce only native donor cards, primary-source ledgers, source-dependence graphs, contradictions, candidate structures, parent ownership and open/censored routes. It may not freeze laws or ontology.

### Synthesis chamber

Receives frozen reconstruction artifacts only after the saturation gate. It derives the smallest invariant set from independently reconstructed traditions and protected decision requirements. It may not use journal prestige or ORION vocabulary as evidence.

### Hostile court

Attempts to destroy the synthesis through old-wine ancestry, strongest-parent composition, simple controls, leave-one-family-out sensitivity, cost accounting, external parent review and naturalistic counterexamples.

## 3. Expert cell

Every complete pass requires these independent roles:

1. historian/philosopher of discovery, experiment and measurement;
2. formal epistemologist/learning theorist;
3. experimental, statistical and metrological reviewer;
4. cognitive, social, institutional and practice epistemologist;
5. plural/non-Western/Indigenous knowledge reviewer;
6. machine-learning and machine-native systems reviewer;
7. assurance, reliability and component-value reviewer;
8. hostile parent-field/editorial reviewer.

The originating author may propose mappings but may not be the sole semantic evaluator.

## 4. Coverage and route requirements

The declared machine-readable family registry is:

`research/foundation-saturation/FOUNDATION_KNOWLEDGE_UNIVERSE_V2.json`.

Coverage varies at least:

`ProblemClass × EpistemicFunction × Representation × SourceMode × AcquisitionMode × ActorTopology × TemporalScale × FailureMode × EvaluationMode × AuthorityRegime × Domain × HistoricalEpoch × EpistemicPractice × MachineAffordance × AdversarialCondition`.

Each material family requires, where applicable:

- native primary source or executable artifact;
- canonical reconstruction and criticism;
- changed-vocabulary search;
- historical ancestry/reverse primitive search;
- strongest hostile parent/composition;
- remote practice and non-paper route;
- implementation/instrument/workflow route;
- cross-domain contradiction or non-application case.

`OPEN`, `CENSORED`, `UNKNOWN` and `NOT_APPLICABLE` are distinct. Censored routes require reasons and never become negative evidence.

## 5. Native reconstruction card

Each donor records:

- native problem and objects;
- positive method/mechanism;
- strongest result or argument;
- assumptions, counterexamples and failure boundary;
- source and dependence identity;
- historical context and later criticism;
- executable or experimental artifact where available;
- candidate ORION collision;
- protected decision that might change;
- strongest parent replacement;
- hostile non-application case;
- disposition: parent-owned, absorbed, contradicted, benchmarked, candidate residual or `CANNOT_CHECK`.

A slogan, abstract or derivative review is not a completed native reconstruction.

## 6. Anti-confirmation procedures

- use multiple non-identical taxonomies and source-mode classifications;
- construct a source/citation/idea-dependence graph;
- perform reverse ancestry from every proposed ORION primitive;
- derive candidate foundations independently from formal, experimental/practice, social/plural and machine-native starting points;
- preserve a contradiction ledger rather than averaging incompatible claims;
- run leave-one-family-out foundation sensitivity;
- search explicitly for ORION-induced overhead, method monoculture, false closure and parent duplication;
- compare with the strongest information/resource-matched parent products;
- keep simple/direct controls and cases where ORION should lose.

## 7. Synthesis moratorium

The flagship, K0–K6 factorization, episode tuple, ten candidate laws and four public questions remain provisional while any complete pass finds a new:

- coordinate;
- relation;
- obstruction;
- transition;
- failure mode;
- parent contraction;
- benchmark family.

New literature cannot become a kernel family, foundation law or standalone paper by naming alone.

## 8. Foundation-admission rule

A candidate invariant enters protected testing only if at least one condition holds:

- independent traditions converge on the same decision-relevant constraint;
- a formal impossibility/counterexample requires it;
- removing it creates a protected cross-domain scientific failure;
- it is necessary to preserve native parent verdicts in composition.

It is not admitted because it resembles human thinking, differs from human thinking, sounds philosophically deep or improves one average score.

## 9. Close and synthesis gates

Internal synthesis is permitted only when:

1. every declared family/lane is reconstructed or explicitly open/censored;
2. every proposed primitive has an ownership/collision card;
3. strongest parent products and contradictions are dispositioned;
4. the latest **three complete post-material-addition passes**, each covering the full declared universe with changed vocabulary and hostile parent search, add no material structure;
5. source-dependence and omission-challenge ledgers are current;
6. no undisclosed open route remains.

External demarcation additionally requires reproducible independent hostile review and protected component-value evidence. Neither gate grants field authority.

## 10. Reopening triggers

Reopen on any material new family, source/acquisition mode, parent theory, naturalistic failure, component harm/redundancy, evaluator/criterion drift, machine substrate, epistemic practice or evidence that the search universe was outcome-conditioned.

## 11. Paper discipline

Writing may continue as hypothesis and experiment design. No final foundation rewrite is allowed during synthesis moratorium. Manuscript promotion requires changed parent ownership, new evidence/counterevidence, protected results, valid claim contraction or independent reviewer findings—not stylistic version churn.
