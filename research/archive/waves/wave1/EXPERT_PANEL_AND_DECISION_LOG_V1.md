# ORION-V2 Wave 1 — Expert Panel and Decision Log V1

**Status:** completed first cross-check round; research conclusions remain provisional and non-authorizing.

**Scope:** translate the founding programme into concrete donor reductions, machine objects, known-answer fixtures and paper gates without pre-committing the final V2 architecture.

## Panel

### Formal systems and semantics lead

**Background:** modal/process semantics, bisimulation, abstract interpretation, proof obligations, countermodel construction and formal verification.

**Role:** veto any “same structure” claim that lacks an explicit relation, context, witness and counter-probe. Verify that receipts cannot self-authorize truth or adoption.

### Statistics, causal inference and measurement lead

**Background:** identifiability, comparison of experiments, optimal design, psychometrics, metrology, measurement invariance and uncertainty propagation.

**Role:** distinguish missing data, observational equivalence, structural non-identifiability, measurement drift and decision-relative information loss.

### Systems engineering, operations and management lead

**Background:** lifecycle engineering, BPM, operations research, scheduling, process control, project/program governance, quality management and organizational learning.

**Role:** attack inherited workflow order, scalar optimization, single-cause responsibility and weak change-control assumptions.

### Scientific discovery and cognitive/design lead

**Background:** dual-space scientific discovery, insight and representational change, analogy, C-K/FBS/TRIZ, CEGIS/CEGAR and open-ended search.

**Role:** reduce broad “creativity” and “Jump” claims into parent-owned operations; identify only the governed cross-level residual.

### Evidence, provenance and institutional authority lead

**Background:** W3C PROV, data/process lineage, dependent evidence, common-cause reliability, legal/institutional authority and strategic response.

**Role:** keep source count distinct from independence, ancestry distinct from valid transport, agreement distinct from truth and policy score distinct from protected scientific outcome.

### Autonomous-science and experimental execution lead

**Background:** self-driving laboratories, active learning, robotic experimentation, scientific agents, execution receipts, protected holdouts and research evaluation.

**Role:** test whether proposed architecture actually closes literature–model–experiment–analysis loops while preserving instrument semantics, failure history and independent validation.

### Native-domain rotating reviewer

**Role:** reproduce the donor's decisive native judgments before accepting any common ORION representation. May reject a mapping that looks elegant but erases the donor's strongest assumptions.

## Decision protocol

Every finding passes through:

1. native reconstruction;
2. known-answer reproduction;
3. structural signature;
4. ORION collision audit;
5. strongest donor-product test;
6. counter-probe;
7. machine disposition;
8. paper-boundary review.

## Decisions reached in Wave 1

### D1 — V1 freeze handoff is sufficient for reference implementation

The exact architecture/local-formalism freeze commit and its control plane are content-bound. It authorizes local reference objects, deterministic tests and separately frozen pilots. It does not authorize protected external evaluation, scientific superiority, novelty or final paper identity.

**Disposition:** `HANDOFF_BOUND_REFERENCE_IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORIZED`.

### D2 — one universal knowledge embedding is rejected

Role equivalence, bisimulation, observational equivalence, predictive equivalence, decision dominance, safe quotienting and underdetermination have different semantics and composition laws.

**Disposition:** `MULTI_RELATIONAL_CONTEXTUAL_SPACE_REQUIRED_AS_RESEARCH_OBJECT`.

### D3 — source count is not evidence independence

Evidence units connected by declared common-cause, shared-data, shared-model, copying or organizational dependence form one conservative dependence component unless a stronger model justifies more information.

**Disposition:** `ADD_DEPENDENCE_COORDINATE`; no authority follows from the component count.

### D4 — scientific ancestry is component-level and reticulate

A successor may inherit representation, method, evidence, calibration and evaluation from different parents. A single Git parent or generic provenance edge is insufficient for selective transport/reopening.

**Disposition:** `ADD_COMPONENT_LEVEL_RETICULATE_PROVENANCE_REFERENCE_OBJECT`.

### D5 — evaluation can alter the world being evaluated

A published metric, policy or deployed predictor may shift the data-generating distribution. Metric improvement can accompany deterioration of a protected outcome.

**Disposition:** `ADD_PERFORMATIVE_EVALUATION_DIAGNOSTIC`; causal attribution remains `CANNOT_CHECK` without a control or intervention design.

### D6 — cross-generation reuse needs comparability, not identity alone

Persistent identifiers and provenance do not establish that constructs, scales or meanings are comparable after a representation/framework change.

**Disposition:** `ADD_COMPARABILITY_CERTIFICATE_REFERENCE_OBJECT` with exact, partial, non-comparable and unresolved states.

### D7 — broad Jump remains contracted

Poor score, timeout, censored search or novelty language are not Jump triggers. A Jump candidate requires a witnessed incumbent ceiling/non-identifiability/obstruction, lower-level dispositions, correspondence, preservation/reopening, prospective consequences and donor-product comparison.

**Disposition:** `WITNESSED_MINIMUM_LEVEL_ESCALATION_ONLY`.

### D8 — non-compensatory action selection is the safe default research scaffold

Actions that violate hard authority/integrity gates are not rescued by high predicted value. Non-dominated actions may remain incomparable rather than being forced into a scalar ranking.

**Disposition:** `PARETO_OR_CONSTRAINED_POLICY_REFERENCE`; final control theory remains parent-dependent.

### D9 — initial paper priority

The strongest current candidates are cross-domain donor hunting, context-relative structural knowledge, obligation-driven scientific solving, witnessed Jump and cross-generation comparability.

**Disposition:** detailed prospectuses created, all `CANNOT_CHECK` for paper authority.
