## Parent reconstructions

### Structural and decision-relative equivalence

- Structural/regular equivalence: entities may occupy corresponding relational roles without sharing identity.
- Bisimulation: observations and every labelled move can be recursively matched.
- Blackwell comparison: one experiment is at least as informative as another for a decision family when the latter is a garbling of the former.
- Le Cam deficiency: approximate comparison quantifies simulation loss.
- Rough indiscernibility: equivalence is relative to selected attributes.
- Computational mechanics: histories are equivalent when they induce the same target future distribution.

**Reduction:** native relations remain separate solvers. V2 may coordinate contexts and receipts but cannot claim their mathematics.

### Scientific action selection and workflows

Dual-space discovery, Bayesian design, POMDP/metareasoning, systems engineering, BPM/process mining, diagnosis and CEGIS/CEGAR own most individual operations.

**Reduction:** a fixed ORION sequence is not novel. The open residual is a source-bound scientific control plane with honest terminals, preservation and authority.

### Open-ended search and Jump

Representational-change theory, C-K/TRIZ, novelty search, MAP-Elites/POET and self-improving systems own creativity, diversity and search-space evolution mechanisms.

**Reduction:** Jump survives only if level responsibility, lower-level insufficiency, old-state preservation and external adoption are jointly demonstrated.

### Evidence, provenance and response

PROV, dependent-evidence/common-cause models, reticulate inheritance and performative prediction own the native structures.

**Reduction:** current code implements conservative reference checks; real use needs domain-specific quantitative models and protected causal designs.
