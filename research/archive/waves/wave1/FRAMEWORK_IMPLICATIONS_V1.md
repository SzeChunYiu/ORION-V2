# ORION-V2 Wave 1 — Framework Implications V1

**Status:** architecture pressure map, not final V2 specification.

## What survives as obligations

- proposal and adoption remain separate;
- local compatibility does not imply global consistency;
- open-world closure requires coverage assumptions;
- diagnosis requires separating interventions;
- representation changes require preservation/reopening;
- execution, validity and authority remain separate;
- negative and `CANNOT_CHECK` histories remain addressable.

## Immediate changes

### Plural context-relative state

V2 cannot rely on one canonical graph or embedding. It must retain source projections, multiple representations, relation certificates, live alternatives and contexts/epochs.

### Adaptive partially ordered policy

The V1 root sequence becomes a baseline. Operations may be required, conditional, parallel, commuting on one projection, history-sensitive, merged, split or absent.

### Typed epistemic limitations

The solver distinguishes reducible uncertainty, censored access, observational indeterminacy, structural non-identifiability, representation insufficiency, method insufficiency, authority blockage and resource bounds.

### Dependence-aware support

Evidence and validation require common-cause/lineage modelling; nominal validator count is not independence.

### Component-level reticulate provenance

A successor can inherit different scientific components from different parents. Reopening follows component-specific dependencies.

### Performative evaluation

Deploying a metric, model or policy can change the process being evaluated and invalidate comparisons.

### Minimum-level escalation

Jump is a controlled transition above the incumbent level after lower-level insufficiency is witnessed.

## Revised provisional state

`Z_t = (P, X, O, H, M, Q, A, U, B, R, G, V, EDep, Prov, Resp, kappa)`

This state remains too large to freeze; coordinate-removal and sufficiency tests are mandatory.

## Candidate architecture boundaries

- **Scientific control plane:** obligations, admissibility, resources and terminals.
- **Native-method substrate:** mature retrieval, statistics, causal, optimization, proof, diagnosis and instrument solvers.
- **Structural/donor layer:** mappings, remote neighbours, counter-probes and claim contraction.
- **Preservation/authority layer:** dependence, provenance, transport, revocation and adoption.
- **Frontier evolution layer:** level diagnosis, transformation proposals and protected Jump evaluation.

## Scientific risks

- oversized ontology without downstream sufficiency;
- no value beyond donor composition;
- over-conservative dependence/provenance;
- intractable or underdetermined action policy;
- Jump triggers not prospectively identifiable;
- diversity causing useless dispersion/fabrication;
- V1 direct/simple performance regression;
- V2 papers duplicating V1 or parents.
