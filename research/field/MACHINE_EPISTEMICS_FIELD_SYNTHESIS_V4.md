# Machine Epistemics — Field Synthesis V4

**State date:** 2026-09-01  
**Status:** provisional, falsifiable field hypothesis; no novelty, adoption, publication or scientific-truth authority  
**Supersedes for current design:** V3. Earlier syntheses remain historical records.

## 1. Field question

Machine Epistemics studies a bounded control problem in machine-mediated inquiry:

> **When may an epistemic machine change a scientific commitment, representation, method, experiment, evaluator or problem in response to partial and fallible interaction with a world that is distinct from the machine's representation of it?**

The programme makes no claim that the world is a machine. The external target is the referent of inquiry; the machine is the learner/reasoner/controller. A distinct field is justified only if recurring cross-transition constraints change protected scientific decisions beyond the strongest faithful composition of mature parents. Parent sufficiency, bridge theory, plural federation, integration engineering and rename remain valid outcomes.

## 2. Four-layer separation

### External target

Let the relevant target state be

\[
\omega_t\in\Omega.
\]

An ontic transition \(\omega_t\to\omega_{t+1}\) is a change in the target, not epistemic learning and not evidence that nature is computational.

### Observation

The machine receives observations through a declared context and interface:

\[
x_t\sim\mathcal O(\cdot\mid\omega_t,c_t,i_t).
\]

A changed observation can reflect target change, intervention, calibration drift, noise, sampling, instrument failure or other channel effects. Observation is not identical to target state.

### Epistemic/scientific state

For a bounded episode,

\[
E_t=(P_t,S_t,O_t,A_t,R_t,M_t,V_t,X_t,H_t,K_t),
\]

with problem/criterion, plural scientific state, obligations, admissible actions, resources, evidence/provenance/dependence, evaluator contracts, observations, history and external authority.

A static target can coexist with epistemic learning:

\[
\omega_{t+1}=\omega_t,\qquad E_{t+1}\ne E_t.
\]

Conversely, target change does not imply that the machine has evidence capable of detecting it.

### Generative regime

When decision-relevant,

\[
\widetilde E_t=(E_t;\Gamma_t,\Pi_t,\mathcal A_t),
\]

with generative regime, locality/perspective and bounded atlas. A candidate machine self-revision is

\[
\mu_t:\Gamma_t\to\Gamma_{t+1}.
\]

This is not an ontic change in the external world. Contemporary representational-regime and model-discovery work owns much of this territory; \(\Gamma_t\to\Gamma_{t+1}\) cannot carry Machine-Epistemics novelty by itself.

## 3. Scientific transition

The candidate unit is

\[
T_t:(\widetilde E_t,a_t,x_t)\mapsto(\widetilde E_{t+1},\rho_t).
\]

A non-authorizing receipt \(\rho_t\) can record evidence/state/obligation deltas, replay identities, active falsification/evaluator conditions and authority ceiling. Receipt completeness supports audit and later revision; it does not make the recorded interpretation true.

Two key non-implications are

\[
\text{successful execution}\not\Rightarrow\text{warranted scientific transition}
\]

and

\[
E_{t+1}\ne E_t\not\Rightarrow\omega_{t+1}\ne\omega_t.
\]

## 4. Discrepancy locus: the missing diagnostic object

Before deciding how to revise itself, a scientific machine should represent competing responsibility hypotheses for a witnessed discrepancy. V4 uses the operational family

\[
\mathcal L=\{
\text{target},
\text{observation},
\text{model},
\text{representation},
\text{problem/criterion},
\text{evaluator},
\text{process}
\}.
\]

The corresponding reference labels are:

1. `TARGET_WORLD` — external target materially changed;
2. `OBSERVATION_MEASUREMENT` — sensing, sampling, calibration or measurement relation changed/failed;
3. `EPISTEMIC_MODEL` — current scientific/model family is inadequate;
4. `REPRESENTATION_REGIME` — representational/operator regime blocks warranted progress;
5. `PROBLEM_CRITERION` — problem, objective, success criterion or formal specification is wrong/inadequate;
6. `EVALUATOR_VALIDATION` — scientific test/oracle/evaluator is blind, invalid or mis-scoped;
7. `PROCESS_TOOL_WORKFLOW` — implementation, tool, orchestration or workflow is responsible.

`CANNOT_IDENTIFY` is first-class whenever available evidence cannot discriminate the live hypotheses.

This family is not claimed to be a universal ontology. Native-domain review can merge, split or reject a locus. Its scientific value must come from better intervention decisions, not taxonomy elegance.

## 5. Scientific evaluator versus diagnostic evaluator

If the scientific evaluator is a candidate failure locus, it cannot validate its own adequacy. Therefore the framework distinguishes:

- the scientific evaluator/validation contract that may be under diagnosis; and
- the diagnostic evaluator or independent discriminator used to assess competing responsibility hypotheses.

If the latter is inadequate, the locus remains unresolved. This blocks evaluator self-certification.

## 6. Minimum-sufficient escalation

The existing Jump ladder is a candidate intervention interface, not a field axiom:

0. action/parameter;
1. local repair/composition;
2. model/hypothesis expansion;
3. representation-regime transition;
4. problem/objective reformulation;
5. method/tool/instrument invention;
6. workflow/meta-skill revision;
7. framework revision;
8. constitution proposal under external authority.

A higher-level proposal becomes eligible only after a witnessed obstruction, prospective discriminator and lower-level disposition are available, with preservation/falsifier contracts and strongest-parent comparison. Locus diagnosis only suggests candidate action families; it does not authorize adoption.

This creates a two-axis scientific problem:

\[
\text{what is responsible?}\quad\times\quad\text{what is the minimum sufficient intervention?}
\]

ME-X2 tests whether the first axis causally improves the second.

## 7. Other retained mechanics

### Typed transport

A cross-context claim specifies the relation type, prospective target consequences, forbidden transfers, scope/loss and falsifiers. Causal transport, measurement invariance, formal refinement and other native parents remain authoritative locally.

### Selective reopening

Defeated sufficient support should reopen affected commitments without globally resetting independently supported claims. Truth-maintenance/belief-revision/assurance parents receive priority; no ME residual exists if they recover the same decisions.

### Local-to-global atlas

Local compatibility does not establish a global witness:

\[
\text{pairwise compatibility}\not\Rightarrow\text{witnessed global section}.
\]

Obstructed, unresolved, matching-only and separately witnessed global outcomes remain distinct.

### Epistemic horizon

For a bounded candidate family, useful probes can be represented as strict refinements of a decision-relevant observational partition. This is a bounded distinguishability object, not a universal knowledge metric.

## 8. Parent-owned territory

The programme does not own, in isolation:

- epistemic control;
- automated/agentic scientific discovery;
- scientific state-transition language;
- provenance and reopenability;
- verification/admissibility workflow gates;
- model-family inadequacy and hypothesis expansion;
- value-of-information experiment selection;
- representational-regime transition and preservation;
- generic diagnosis or metareasoning;
- belief revision;
- causal transport, metrology or formal refinement.

Direct contemporary collisions include type-safe scientific execution graphs, verification-first autonomous science, reopenable provenance, Model Discovery Agent, AutoResearch AI and verified representational-regime transition systems.

## 9. Field hypothesis and strongest null

### H_ME

There exist recurring scientific decisions for which coupling evidence/provenance, evaluator validity, dependence, discrepancy locus, representation/model state, problem/criterion state, transport, obligations, selective reopening, stopping and authority yields protected decisions that cannot be reproduced as simply or reliably by the strongest faithful parent composition under matched information and resources.

### H0

An information-matched composition of formal learning, measurement diagnostics, model-based diagnosis, metareasoning/experimental design, belief revision, formal methods, causal transport, provenance, dependence-aware evidence synthesis, workflow verification and external governance makes the same protected decisions with equal or better reliability/cost.

If H0 wins, the field-level claim contracts.

## 10. What counts as a real residual?

A surviving residual must be more than terminology. It should take at least one form:

1. a nontrivial theorem or impossibility result not reducible to a parent theorem;
2. a stable cross-domain benchmark exposing composition failures missed by the strongest parent federation;
3. a control mechanism whose causal ablation changes protected decisions under matched resources;
4. a cross-transition interaction showing that individually sound parent-local decisions compose incorrectly without an additional coupling condition.

## 11. Decisive studies

- **ME-X1 — Transition Coupling:** locally valid operations, different warranted downstream transitions.
- **ME-X2 — Locus + Minimum Escalation:** target/observation/model/representation/problem/evaluator/process diagnosis versus strongest diagnosis/metareasoning federation.
- **ME-X3 — Formal Mathematics:** search versus lemma/model versus representation versus specification mismatch under Lean verification and semantic-intent checks.
- **ME-X4 — Dynamic Selective Reopening:** exact support-graph updates under retraction, dependence, calibration, transport and evaluator change.
- **ME-X5 — Cross-Domain Residual:** native-reviewed formal, experimental/computational and evidence/measurement modes against a real strongest-parent federation.
- **ME-X6 — Collective Epistemics:** scientific outputs such as arXiv as noisy channels of a latent distributed epistemic process, not knowledge counts.
- **ME-X7 — External Witness Sufficiency:** claim-sufficient external witnesses for machine-native internal computation.

## 12. Mathematics

Formal mathematics is a strong first laboratory because local proof validity can be exact while problem/specification fidelity remains separate. ME-X3 therefore tests proof validity, intent fidelity, obstruction/locus diagnosis, intervention choice, reuse/transfer and resource cost. A gain that disappears against matched proof-search, lemma-generation, abstraction or autoformalization parents is parent sufficiency.

## 13. Collective epistemics

A scientific community may be modeled as a distributed epistemic system while preserving

\[
\text{world}\ne\text{scientific community}\ne\text{scientific artifacts}.
\]

ArXiv, papers, citations, formal libraries and software releases are noisy emissions shaped by incentives, filtering, duplication and error. Any latent capability model must beat strong science-of-science baselines, survive duplicate/paraphrase/invalidation/popularity/field-rate tests and predict later validated capability under strict temporal holdout.

## 14. Candidate transition constraints

Retain only as testable hypotheses:

- criterion and identity conservation;
- execution/evidence separation;
- source/evidence binding and dependence visibility;
- non-amplifying external authority;
- typed context transport;
- selective reopening;
- bounded closure with honest unresolvedness;
- **ontic/epistemic responsibility separation before escalation**, including problem/criterion and evaluator loci;
- **no evaluator self-certification** when evaluator validity is itself contested.

## 15. Field kill conditions

Contract the programme if:

- strongest parent composition ties or wins the protected quality-cost frontier;
- the common transition object dissolves under native-domain semantics;
- explicit locus diagnosis adds no decision value beyond ordinary diagnosis/metareasoning;
- diagnostic evaluation cannot distinguish the proposed loci reliably;
- receipt fields add documentation but no omission-specific causal value;
- regime mechanics reduce to current self-revising/model-discovery parents;
- selective reopening reduces exactly to mature TMS/assurance products;
- atlas/horizon semantics add notation but no protected decision;
- collective metrics reduce to publication/citation/semantic-novelty proxies;
- mathematical gains reduce to extra compute, retrieval or standard abstraction search;
- external reviewers cannot recover a stable residual after parent ownership is explicit.

## 16. ORION and publication boundary

ORION-V2 is one candidate experimental framework, not the field. Its reference objects are allowed to fail or contract. The flagship Perspective should present the minimum formal core and falsifiable field wager, not report the prospective X1-X7 outcomes as results.
