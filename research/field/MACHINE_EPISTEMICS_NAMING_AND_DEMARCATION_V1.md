# Machine Epistemics — Naming and Demarcation Audit V1

**Status:** preferred working name; no disciplinary, novelty, publication, or external scientific authority.

## Decision

Preferred working field name:

# **Machine Epistemics**

Tagline:

> **The control science of AI-driven discovery.**

Operational definition:

> Machine Epistemics studies and engineers how AI and hybrid human–machine research systems acquire, transform, compare, test, preserve, revise, reopen, and close scientific commitments under explicit evidence, uncertainty, provenance, resource, and authority constraints.

The earlier working label **Computational Scientific Control (CSC)** remains a historical derivation name. It described the object well but sounded primarily like control engineering and did not immediately signal modern AI. Machine Epistemics better names the scientific object while retaining control-theoretic content as a parent rather than the public identity of the field.

## Naming panel

Five lenses were used.

1. **AI/agent-systems lens** — does the name sound native to current AI research rather than legacy workflow engineering?
2. **epistemology/metascience lens** — does it accurately name knowledge-state dynamics without claiming ownership of epistemology itself?
3. **control/formal-methods lens** — can the field still contain observability, controllability, stopping, repair, transport, and escalation?
4. **publication/branding lens** — is the name memorable, concise, searchable, and suitable for a field-defining paper?
5. **prior-art lens** — does it avoid established programmes, standards, laboratories, or active field labels?

## Rejected or demoted names

### Scientific Intelligence / Scientific Intelligence Systems

Rejected as primary branding. IEEE P4117, approved in 2026, is already developing a **Scientific Intelligence Systems Reference Architecture** and explicitly defines terminology and conceptual boundaries for SISs. Using this as the field name would create avoidable ownership and search collisions.

### Agentic Science

Rejected as field name because it is already an active emerging paradigm for autonomous scientific agents and discovery workflows. Machine Epistemics may study the validity and control semantics of agentic science, but should not rename that parent programme.

### Autonomous Science

Rejected as field name because national laboratories and autonomous-laboratory programmes already use it for AI + automation + instrumentation closed loops.

### Epistemic AI / AI for Epistemics

Rejected as primary name. Epistemic AI is already the name of a major European research programme on uncertainty, and “AI for epistemics” is an active contemporary policy/research label. These names are also broader than scientific research systems.

### Scientific Agentics / Agentics

Rejected because “Agentics” is already used for an agent-oriented scientific society/research paradigm and would over-emphasize agent architecture rather than scientific warrant.

### Epistemic Systems Engineering

Rejected as public field name because the phrase has existing non-scientific/commercial use and sounds primarily like organizational engineering.

## Why Machine Epistemics survives

### 1. It is AI-native without being model-specific

“Machine” includes LLM agents, symbolic systems, theorem provers, autonomous laboratories, retrieval systems, simulators, workflow engines, learned policies, and future machine research systems. The field therefore does not depend on the current agent/LLM paradigm.

### 2. “Epistemics” points to operations, not ownership of philosophy

The term signals the dynamics and infrastructure of knowing: evidence acquisition, claim formation, uncertainty, dependence, comparison, invalidation, revision, and justified closure. Philosophy of science and social epistemology remain parent fields and normative authorities.

### 3. It names the missing composition-level object

AI for Science primarily asks how AI can accelerate or improve scientific tasks. Machine Epistemics asks a different systems question:

> When may a machine-mediated scientific process legitimately move from one epistemic state to another, what must be preserved or reopened, and what authority does the resulting commitment possess?

### 4. It naturally supports a mathematical programme

Candidate subjects include:

- epistemic observability and non-identifiability;
- scientific controllability and justified reachability;
- evidence-dependence topology;
- context-relative relation and transport;
- selective belief/claim reopening;
- bounded epistemic stopping;
- minimum sufficient escalation;
- criterion and evaluator stability;
- replay identity and provenance;
- authority-safe delegation.

### 5. It admits honest contraction

If the strongest composition of AI-for-Science, metareasoning, truth-maintenance/belief revision, formal methods, provenance, evidence synthesis, control, and scientific governance fully reproduces these decisions, Machine Epistemics should contract to an integration/engineering label rather than claim a new science.

## Field demarcation

Machine Epistemics does **not** own:

- generic AI agents or multi-agent orchestration;
- generic autonomous laboratories;
- generic Bayesian optimization or experimental design;
- generic truth-maintenance or belief revision;
- generic model-based diagnosis;
- generic provenance standards;
- generic workflow durability;
- generic causal inference or causal transportability;
- generic formal verification;
- generic philosophy of science or epistemology;
- generic systematic review methodology.

It can claim a distinct field only if the **composition-level control of scientific commitments** requires stable abstractions, laws, benchmarks, or empirical results not reducible to those parents in straightforward composition.

## Preferred terminology

- **Machine Epistemics (ME)** — the provisional field.
- **Machine-Epistemic System (MES)** — a machine/hybrid system whose scientific-state transitions are explicitly governed.
- **Machine-Epistemic Episode (MEE)** — one bounded research/control episode.
- **Epistemic transition receipt** — a replayable record of a proposed or admitted scientific-state transition.
- **Epistemic control policy** — a policy over scientific actions subject to non-compensatory validity and authority constraints.
- **Epistemic closure** — bounded, typed stopping under a declared searched basis and residual uncertainty; never universal truth or completeness.

The acronym **ME** is intentionally not used as a brand in paper titles where ambiguity is likely; titles should normally spell out “Machine Epistemics.”

## Field-establishment gate

The label advances from `PREFERRED_WORKING_NAME` to `CANDIDATE_FIELD` only if all of the following survive protected evaluation:

1. at least two distinct scientific domains require the same machine-epistemic control abstraction;
2. at least one formal result or falsifiable systems law is not a trivial restatement of a parent theory;
3. the integrated framework improves a protected scientific decision over the strongest parent-composed baseline;
4. V1 non-regression and authority/integrity gates pass;
5. independent reviewers agree that the residual object is coherent and non-redundant;
6. the flagship manuscript can state the field boundary without relying on ORION-specific vocabulary.

Otherwise the terminal is one of:

- `MACHINE_EPISTEMICS_AS_INTEGRATION_ENGINEERING_ONLY`;
- `SUBSUMED_BY_AGENTIC_OR_AUTONOMOUS_SCIENCE`;
- `SUBSUMED_BY_EPISTEMOLOGY_PLUS_CONTROL_FORMAL_METHODS`;
- `FIELD_BOUNDARY_TOO_FRAGMENTED`;
- `CANNOT_CHECK_FIELD_SEPARATION`.
