## H-EXT-1 — Conditional activation with channel-external witnesses (S2)

**Claim under test.** For each epistemic-control mechanism with a demonstrated conditional benefit (dependence modelling; obligations), there exists a cheap, *input-computable* gate (not the oracle label, not the loop's own feedback) whose activation policy recovers the positive-stratum gain while removing the negative-stratum drag, Pareto-dominating both always-on `M` and always-off `B5`.

**Falsifier.** Gated-M fails to beat always-on M on the pooled quality–cost frontier under a clean baseline plus a shuffle-equal-n gate null (a gate that merely selects favourable instances is not a mechanism). Gate must be frozen on a development split or a fresh generator seed, never on the stratum labels of the frozen outcome.

**Strongest parent.** Contextual/adaptive control, mixture-of-experts routing, selective prediction, and P-D's own "conditional mechanism" wording. The delta is only that the gate is *channel-external and prospectively frozen*.

**Terminal if negative.** `ACTIVATION_POLICY_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_FROM_INPUTS` — conditional value is real but not exploitable without oracle regime knowledge (a boundary claim for P-D/P-C).

## H-EXT-2 — Internal-salience Goodhart in agentic scientific loops (S2)

**Claim under test.** In a closed feedback loop around a native scientific learner, the loop's internally generated salience/"significance" signal is *negatively* correlated with the quantitative truth metric across cycles, and the correlation sign is stable across native learners and substrates. (E40 m5′ observed this in-sample on one learner; direction inversion was correctly forbidden there. A replication under a **different native learner and/or substrate, with the sign pre-registered**, is a new prospective identity.)

**Falsifier.** Pre-registered directed Spearman ρ(salience, truth) ≤ 0 on the fresh cell, or sign instability across the two cohorts.

**Strongest parent.** Goodhart/specification gaming, reward hacking, p-hacking-as-selection, Lucas critique. The delta is the *mechanism locus*: the signal is produced by the same loop that consumes it, so any truth-anchor must be channel-external (ties to Stage-2b replica consistency and to H-EXT-1).

**Terminal if negative.** `SALIENCE_ANTI_RANKING_NOT_REPLICATED` — the m5′ observation becomes a single-learner artefact and is reported as such.

## H-EXT-3 — The field residual is an interface-information residual, not a control residual (S1→S2)

**Claim under test.** In exact known-answer worlds (ME-X1 family I, ME-X4), the advantage of `M` over the strongest faithful parent federation `B5` is a monotone function of *what crosses module boundaries*: verdict-only → +provenance → +dependence ancestry → +typed transport status → full structure. Prediction registered both ways: (a) the advantage vanishes at full-structure exchange (**parent sufficiency at the interface level**: Machine Epistemics contracts to interface standards, which is a successful terminal), or (b) it persists (a genuine control residual). Either outcome is decisive for the flagship's wager and cannot be produced by a weak-baseline comparison.

**Falsifier.** Non-monotone or flat ladder; or `M` advantage that depends on information `B5` cannot be given at any rung.

**Strongest parent.** Sheaf/local-to-global obstruction, ATMS environments, provenance semirings, contract-based design. Formal target: a finite separation example where every verdict-exchange federation fails a global obstruction that structure exchange detects.

**Terminal if negative.** `RESIDUAL_IS_INTERFACE_STANDARD_NOT_CONTROL` (contracts the field; keeps the interface contribution).

## H-EXT-4 — Quantitative prospective-revision premium (PRA theory extension) (S1)

**Claim under test.** Beyond the existence witness (1 bit), there is a non-trivial inequality linking the prospective state premium `Ω_dyn` (bits) to the minimal future regret of any representation-measurable rule, with an approximate/ε-acceptable version and a multi-step (recursive) case. A Fano-type lower bound on regret in terms of the dormant variable's conditional entropy given `Z` is the candidate.

**Falsifier / honest terminal.** The bound is either a direct corollary of decision-sufficiency/Blackwell results (`PARENT_OWNED`), or trivial (`NO_NONTRIVIAL_BOUND`). Only a machine-checked statement with a finite tight example counts. Manuscript changes are not authorized by this row.
