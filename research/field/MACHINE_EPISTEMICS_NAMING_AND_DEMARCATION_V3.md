# Machine Epistemics — Naming and Demarcation Audit V3

**Status:** preferred working umbrella under high conceptual collision. No field, novelty, publication or scientific-truth authority.

## Updated conclusion

The name **Machine Epistemics** remains useful as a compact public label, but the literature no longer supports treating the surrounding scientific problem as sparsely named or newly discovered.

By 2026, adjacent work explicitly uses:

- **epistemic control** for machine-learning-based science;
- **epistemic control architectures** for AI-assisted empirical research;
- **epistemic control** as a dimension of evaluating LLM-assisted research;
- **provenance and reopenability** as foundations for trust in autonomous science;
- long-established **automated scientific discovery** as a field connecting AI and the scientific method.

Machine Epistemics therefore advances only as an **emerging convergence hypothesis**:

> several established and newly converging traditions may share a common object—the controlled change of scientific commitments inside executable AI/hybrid research processes—and a common cross-domain research programme may be useful if it preserves the native theories rather than replacing them.

## Revised public definition

> **Machine Epistemics** is the provisional study of scientific-state transitions in AI-mediated research: how evidence, models, representations, measurements, tools, evaluators and prior commitments jointly warrant—or fail to warrant—a change in what a research process scientifically commits to.

This definition is intentionally narrower than:

- machine epistemology in general;
- computational epistemology/formal learning;
- epistemic control of ML-based science broadly;
- provenance/reproducibility infrastructure;
- trustworthy or autonomous science as a whole;
- AI for Science.

## Tagline disposition

Earlier tagline:

> The control science of AI-driven discovery.

Current disposition:

`MEMORABLE_BUT_HIGH_COLLISION`.

Because *epistemic control* is now explicit prior terminology in multiple 2026 sources, this phrase may still be used informally but should not carry a novelty or demarcation burden in a foundation paper.

Preferred technical subtitle:

> **Scientific-State Transitions in AI-Driven Discovery**

Possible broad-audience title:

> **How AI Research Systems Change What Science Knows**

The latter requires editorial caution because scientific knowledge is distributed/institutional and cannot be reduced to one machine's internal state.

## Direct 2026 convergence evidence

### Ratti — epistemic control and ML-based science

Emanuele Ratti's 2026 work explicitly formulates epistemic control in machine-learning-based science using tracking and tracing conditions.

Disposition:

`GENERIC_EPISTEMIC_CONTROL_IN_ML_SCIENCE = DIRECT_PARENT`.

Machine Epistemics must not claim to introduce epistemic control as the central concern of AI-mediated science.

### Wojarnik — spec-driven architecture of epistemic control

A 2026 scoping review/preprint combines preregistration, registered reports, multiverse/specification analysis, reproducible research, provenance/FAIR principles and AI-assisted workflows into an architecture of epistemic control for empirical research.

Disposition:

`INTEGRATED_EMPIRICAL_RESEARCH_EPISTEMIC_CONTROL = DIRECT_CONTEMPORARY_PARENT_THREAT`.

Because this is a preprint, scientific status and final claims must be represented accurately; it nevertheless matters for priority/demarcation.

### MacKnight et al. — provenance grounds trust in autonomous science

A 2026 *Nature Computational Science* Comment argues that trust in autonomous science should rest on a complete, reopenable provenance record of reasoning, action and measurement that can be audited and corrected.

Disposition:

`PROVENANCE_REOPENABILITY_FOR_AUTONOMOUS_SCIENCE = DIRECT_TOP_TIER_PARENT`.

The possible Machine Epistemics residual cannot be 'complete provenance plus reopening'. It must test scientific-validity decisions that remain after provenance is already supplied.

### Kim & Park — epistemic control in LLM-assisted research evaluation

A 2026 *Research Evaluation* article explicitly asks what is being evaluated when research activities become partially automated and frames the issue partly through epistemic control.

Disposition:

`EPISTEMIC_CONTROL_AS_RESEARCH_EVALUATION_COORDINATE = DIRECT_CONTEMPORARY_PARENT`.

### Kramer et al. — automated scientific discovery

A 2026 *Machine Learning* Review traces automated scientific discovery back to the 1970s and surveys equation discovery, closed-loop science and autonomous discovery systems.

Disposition:

`AUTOMATED_SCIENTIFIC_DISCOVERY = ESTABLISHED_HISTORICAL_PARENT_FIELD`.

Machine Epistemics must not frame automation of the scientific process itself as an intellectual novelty.

## Stronger residual under test

After these contractions, the field hypothesis is narrowed to:

### Cross-mode scientific transition semantics

Can one scientific-process framework coordinate, without erasing:

- formal proof/refinement;
- causal/observational inference;
- measurement and uncertainty;
- retrieval/evidence synthesis;
- experiment/action selection;
- provenance/replay;
- evidence dependence;
- evaluator response;
- selective revalidation/reopening;
- bounded scientific stopping;

such that it changes protected scientific decisions beyond the strongest existing compositions?

The proposed residual is **not** that all these topics should be discussed together. It requires a stable decision object, law, benchmark, impossibility result or empirical gain.

## Revised field hypothesis ladder

### Level 0 — terminology only

`LABEL_ONLY` — reject.

### Level 1 — useful synthesis

`USEFUL_UMBRELLA_RESEARCH_PROGRAMME` — a coherent map of converging work improves communication/benchmark design but no new scientific mechanism is established.

This is already a potentially worthwhile Perspective outcome.

### Level 2 — emerging interdiscipline

`EMERGING_INTERDISCIPLINARY_FIELD_SUPPORTED` — independent review and cross-domain evidence show a stable object, methods/benchmarks and nontrivial residual beyond parents.

### Level 3 — established field

Requires sustained external community uptake, independent publications, curricula/venues or other scientific institutionalization. ORION-V2 cannot grant this level.

Current evidence does not support Level 3.

## Submission gate

Before using **Machine Epistemics** in a field-defining top-tier Perspective:

1. include the 2026 epistemic-control/provenance/autonomous-discovery literature prominently rather than burying it;
2. avoid priority claims for epistemic control, provenance/reopenability or automated discovery;
3. show what cross-mode transition question is not answered by those programmes;
4. bind independent demarcation review;
5. conduct a fresh exact/changed-vocabulary search immediately before submission;
6. permit a Perspective conclusion of `USEFUL_UMBRELLA_RESEARCH_PROGRAMME` even if distinct field status remains unearned.
