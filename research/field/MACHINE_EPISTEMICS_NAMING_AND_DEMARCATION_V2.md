**Status:** preferred working name under reopened conceptual-collision review. No disciplinary, novelty, publication, or scientific authority.

## Updated decision

Preferred working label remains:

# **Machine Epistemics**

Tagline:

> **The control science of AI-driven discovery.**

However, the V2 audit corrects the earlier claim that naming collision risk was simply low. Exact-phrase collision appears limited in the search routes run so far, but **conceptual naming collision is material** because adjacent traditions already use:

- **machine epistemology** for philosophical/pragmatist accounts of machine learning, inquiry and knowledge;
- **computational epistemology** for formal/computational approaches to learning, induction and reliable inquiry;
- **epistemic engineering** for engineered/distributed systems that construct or operationalize knowledge;
- **epistemic AI / computational epistemology for causal AI** for AI systems evaluated explicitly as knowledge-producing systems.

Therefore the naming terminal is not “unused concept”. It is:

`DISTINCT_WORKING_LABEL_WITH_STRONG_ADJACENT_EPISTEMOLOGY_PARENTS`.

## Why the label can still survive

Machine Epistemics is retained only under a **narrow operational demarcation**:

> Machine Epistemics studies controlled scientific-state transitions in executable AI/hybrid research processes: what scientific commitment changes, under which evidence/semantic/dependence/representation conditions, what must reopen, when the process may close, and what externally supplied authority constrains the transition.

This is narrower than computational epistemology's general study of learnability/reliable inquiry and narrower than machine epistemology's broad philosophical questions about machine knowledge. It is also more specifically scientific-process-oriented than epistemic engineering in the broad systems/cognition sense.

If this operational distinction does not survive the full literature and expert review, the public name should change or the field claim should contract.

## Newly explicit adjacent parents

### Computational epistemology

Formal epistemology already studies knowledge acquisition, inductive problems, convergence/reliability and computational constraints. This is a direct intellectual parent for Machine Epistemics questions about identifiable scientific questions, admissible methods and successful inquiry.

**ME residual under test:** executable multi-stage scientific processes with source-bound evidence, changing representations, tool/experimental receipts, dependence, selective reopening, evaluator custody and externally constrained adoption/closure.

### Machine epistemology

Existing work uses “machine epistemology” to analyse how machine learning relates to inquiry, uncertainty and knowledge. Machine Epistemics must not claim the general philosophical question “can machines know?” or the pragmatist account of knowledge as control.

**ME residual under test:** operational transition semantics and measurable control failures in scientific research systems rather than general machine knowledge.

### Epistemic engineering

Existing work uses epistemic engineering for distributed/systemic processes that create or organize knowledge.

**ME residual under test:** scientific-claim state changes under explicit evidence, validation, comparability, resource, history and authority constraints.

### Causal AI / computational epistemology

Recent work explicitly frames causal AI as knowledge-producing and develops epistemic standards around assumptions, identifiability, invariance and causal queries.

**ME residual under test:** coordination across causal and non-causal scientific modes, including retrieval, experiment, theorem/proof, simulation, measurement, evidence synthesis and representation change.

## Rejected interpretations of the name

Machine Epistemics must **not** be defined as:

- the epistemology of all machines;
- a general theory of machine knowledge;
- computational epistemology renamed for AI;
- epistemic logic for agents;
- philosophy of AI with engineering language;
- “truth maintenance plus provenance”;
- a synonym for trustworthy AI or AI safety;
- a synonym for AI for Science, agentic science or autonomous science.

Any manuscript sentence that implies one of these interpretations should fail the demarcation audit.

## Stronger anti-columbusing comparison set

A field-level submission must explicitly reconstruct at least these neighbors:

1. computational epistemology / logical reliability;
2. machine epistemology;
3. epistemic engineering;
4. cybernetics/control theory;
5. rational metareasoning;
6. truth-maintenance systems and belief revision;
7. model-based diagnosis;
8. active experimentation/optimal design;

10. causal identification and transportability;
11. provenance/workflow/reproducibility;

13. performative/strategic evaluation;
14. metascience/science of science;
15. philosophy and social epistemology of science;
16. AI for Science / agentic / autonomous science.

The proposed field survives only if an information-matched composition of these parents leaves a stable scientific-process transition residual.

## Naming alternatives kept in reserve

The naming problem is not closed forever. If independent demarcation review finds that “Machine Epistemics” is too easily confused with machine/computational epistemology, candidates should be evaluated against the same collision protocol rather than chosen by preference. Possible reserve directions include phrases built around **scientific state transitions**, **AI research control**, or **scientific assurance**, but none is promoted in V2 because the current label remains stronger on memorability and AI relevance.

## Naming survival gate

Retain **Machine Epistemics** for external submission only if:

1. the manuscript explicitly distinguishes computational epistemology, machine epistemology and epistemic engineering;
2. at least two independent domain/field reviewers can explain the ME object without ORION-specific language;
3. those reviewers do not predominantly interpret the name as a synonym for existing computational/machine epistemology;
4. the strongest-parent composition leaves a nontrivial scientific-process transition residual;
5. a fresh search immediately before submission finds no newly established field/programme with the exact label and materially overlapping scope.

Otherwise the paper may retain its scientific results while changing the field label or contracting to an integration Perspective.
