# Machine Epistemics — Field Synthesis V3

**State date:** 2026-09-01  
**Status:** provisional, falsifiable field hypothesis; no novelty, adoption, publication or scientific-truth authority  
**Supersedes conceptually:** V2 only where the ontic/epistemic boundary and September-2026 parent collisions require correction. V1/V2 remain historical records.

## 1. Central question

Machine Epistemics studies a bounded control problem in machine-mediated inquiry:

> **When may an epistemic machine change a scientific commitment, representation, method, experiment or problem in response to partial and fallible interaction with a world that is distinct from the machine's representation of it?**

The proposed field is not a theory that the world is a machine. The world is the referent or target of inquiry. The machine is the learner/reasoner/controller that maintains and revises scientific state.

The field hypothesis is useful only if this common transition object changes protected scientific decisions beyond the strongest faithful composition of mature parent methods. If not, the programme contracts to bridge theory, integration engineering, benchmark infrastructure or a different name.

## 2. Four-layer formal separation

### 2.1 External target

Let the relevant external target state be

\[
\omega_t\in\Omega.
\]

This is an ontic object, not a claim that nature is digital, computational or machine-like. If the target changes, write schematically

\[
\omega_t\rightarrow\omega_{t+1}.
\]

The acting machine does not generally observe \(\omega_t\) directly.

### 2.2 Observation channel

The machine receives an observation through a declared context and instrument/interface:

\[
x_t\sim\mathcal O(\cdot\mid \omega_t,c_t,i_t).
\]

A changed observation may result from target change, intervention, calibration drift, sampling variation, measurement failure or another channel effect. Observation is therefore distinct from both target state and machine belief.

### 2.3 Machine epistemic state

For a bounded research episode retain

\[
E_t=(P_t,S_t,O_t,A_t,R_t,M_t,V_t,X_t,H_t,K_t),
\]

where \(P_t\) binds problem/criterion, \(S_t\) plural scientific commitments and alternatives, \(O_t\) obligations/blockers, \(A_t\) admissible actions, \(R_t\) resources, \(M_t\) evidence/provenance/dependence, \(V_t\) evaluator contracts, \(X_t\) observations, \(H_t\) history and \(K_t\) external authority.

An epistemic transition may occur even when the target is static:

\[
\omega_{t+1}=\omega_t,\qquad E_{t+1}\neq E_t.
\]

Conversely, target change does not imply that the machine has evidence capable of detecting it.

### 2.4 Machine generative regime

When decision-relevant, extend the state by

\[
\widetilde E_t=(E_t;\Gamma_t,\Pi_t,\mathcal A_t),
\]

where \(\Gamma_t\) is the machine's representation/model/operator/search/tool regime, \(\Pi_t\) its locality/perspective frame and \(\mathcal A_t\) a bounded epistemic atlas.

A candidate regime revision is

\[
\mu_t:\Gamma_t\rightarrow\Gamma_{t+1}.
\]

This is machine self-revision, not ontic world change. Recent representational-regime-transition work directly owns much of this move; Machine Epistemics cannot claim novelty for \(\Gamma_t\to\Gamma_{t+1}\) by itself.

## 3. Scientific transition and receipt

The candidate unit of evaluation remains a machine-mediated scientific transition driven by observable information:

\[
T_t:(\widetilde E_t,a_t,x_t)\mapsto(\widetilde E_{t+1},\rho_t).
\]

A non-authorizing receipt may make recoverable the evidence/state/obligation deltas, replay or witness identities, still-active evaluator/falsification conditions and authority ceiling. Completeness of a receipt makes a transition inspectable; it does not make the interpretation true.

The central non-implication remains:

\[
\text{successful execution}\not\Rightarrow\text{warranted scientific transition}.
\]

A second boundary now becomes explicit:

\[
E_{t+1}\neq E_t\not\Rightarrow \omega_{t+1}\neq\omega_t.
\]

That is, machine revision need not mean the target changed.

## 4. New missing object: discrepancy locus

Before escalating, the system should keep separate responsibility hypotheses for the observed discrepancy. Define the candidate locus family

\[
\mathcal L=\{\text{target},\text{observation},\text{model},\text{representation},\text{process}\}.
\]

The machine may maintain several live hypotheses or return unresolved/`CANNOT_IDENTIFY`. A locus diagnosis is not an ontological truth declaration; it is a bounded responsibility hypothesis that must be supported by discriminating evidence.

This matters because the same residual can imply incompatible actions:

- genuine target change may require new measurement or experiment;
- calibration drift may require measurement repair rather than theory revision;
- model-family inadequacy may justify hypothesis expansion;
- representation inadequacy may justify a regime transition;
- tool/workflow failure may justify process repair;
- non-identifiability may justify a new probe or an honest unresolved terminal.

The reference semantics live in `src/orion_v2/ontic_epistemic_boundary.py`; they are deliberately non-authorizing and are not part of the frozen kernel API.

## 5. Obstruction and minimum-sufficient escalation

The existing ORION-V2 Jump ladder remains a candidate control interface, not a field axiom. Higher-level transformation becomes eligible only after:

1. a witnessed obstruction or discrepancy;
2. competing responsibility hypotheses with a prospective discriminator;
3. disposition of relevant lower-level repair routes;
4. preservation/falsifier contracts for the proposed transformation;
5. strongest-parent comparison.

ME-X2 now tests two distinct axes: **locus diagnosis** and **minimum responsible intervention level**. An agent that correctly says “something is wrong” but chooses the wrong locus or intervention has not demonstrated the proposed mechanism.

## 6. Transport, reopening, atlas and horizon

The remaining formal objects retain their conservative status.

### Typed transport

A transport claim must specify its relation type, target consequences, forbidden transfers, scope/loss and falsifiers. Causal transport, measurement invariance, formal refinement and other parent theories remain locally authoritative.

### Selective reopening

When registered sufficient support is defeated, reopen affected commitments without globally resetting independently supported claims. TMS/ATMS and belief-revision parents receive strongest-parent priority; Machine Epistemics earns no residual if they recover the same mixed-mode decisions.

### Local/global atlas

Local compatibility is not a global witness:

\[
\text{pairwise compatibility}\not\Rightarrow\text{witnessed global section}.
\]

The atlas records obstruction, unresolved correspondence, matching-only and separately witnessed globality rather than manufacturing a global ontology.

### Epistemic horizon

For a bounded candidate family, useful probes can be represented as strict refinements of the current decision-relevant observational partition. This formalizes distinguishability, not universal knowledge or a total space of possible epistemic systems.

## 7. Parent-owned territory after the September 2026 reopen

The following cannot carry field novelty by themselves:

- epistemic control in AI-mediated science;
- automated/agentic scientific discovery;
- scientific state-transition language;
- typed execution graphs and provenance;
- verification/admissibility workflow gates;
- reopenable provenance;
- hypothesis-class inadequacy and model-family expansion;
- value-of-information experiment selection;
- representational-regime transition with preservation;
- generic belief revision, diagnosis, causal transport, metrology or formal refinement.

The strongest direct/contemporary collisions include formal learning/computational epistemology, rational metareasoning, truth maintenance, model-based diagnosis, verification-first autonomous science, provenance-centered autonomous science, scientific execution graphs, model-discovery agents and verified representational-regime transitions.

## 8. Sharpened field hypothesis

### H_ME — cross-transition epistemic control

There exist recurring scientific decisions for which coupling evidence/provenance, evaluator validity, dependence, locus diagnosis, representation/model state, transport, obligations, selective reopening, stopping and authority yields protected decisions that cannot be reproduced as simply or reliably by the strongest faithful composition of parent-native methods under matched information and resources.

### H0 — strongest-parent sufficiency

An information-matched composition of formal learning, diagnosis/metareasoning, experiment design, TMS/belief revision, formal methods, causal transport, measurement/metrology, provenance, dependence-aware evidence synthesis, scientific workflow verification and external governance makes the same protected decisions with equal or better reliability/cost.

If H0 wins, the field-level claim contracts.

## 9. What would constitute a real residual?

A distinct scientific residual must take at least one non-rhetorical form:

1. a theorem or impossibility result not reducible to a parent theorem;
2. a stable cross-domain benchmark object exposing composition failures missed by the strongest parent federation;
3. a control mechanism whose causal ablation changes protected scientific decisions under matched resources;
4. a cross-transition interaction showing that individually sound parent-local decisions compose incorrectly without an additional coupling condition.

A vocabulary, taxonomy or software schema alone is not enough.

## 10. Decisive study programme

### ME-X1 — transition coupling

Construct episodes where local operations are correct but the downstream scientific transition differs because of provenance/dependence, evaluator fitness, transport, support defeat, authority or local/global obstruction. Primary comparator: strongest faithful parent federation.

### ME-X2 — discrepancy locus and minimum escalation

Use paired cases with similar symptoms but different responsible loci. Test whether target/observation/model/representation/process diagnosis causally improves minimum-intervention choice beyond failure diagnosis + metareasoning/model discovery. See `ME_X2_LOCUS_DIAGNOSIS_PROTOCOL_V1.md`.

### ME-X3 — formal mathematical discovery

Use Lean as a strong local verifier to test when to remain in proof search versus invent/use a lemma, change representation or declare specification mismatch. Proof validity and intent fidelity are separate outcomes.

### ME-X4 — dynamic selective reopening

Use known-answer support graphs with shared ancestry, retraction, calibration, transport and evaluator changes to test exact reopening/preservation against TMS/assurance parents.

### ME-X5 — cross-domain field residual

Require native reconstruction in multiple epistemic modes and compare against a real B5 federation with engineering glue allowed. This is the main field-level discriminator.

### ME-X6 — collective epistemics

Treat arXiv and other scientific artifacts only as noisy observation channels of a latent distributed epistemic process. Publication volume is activity, not knowledge. The study is exploratory and does not gate the flagship.

### ME-X7 — claim-sufficient external witnesses

Test whether machine-native internal computation can remain scientifically accountable through structured external witnesses without requiring complete human-style reasoning traces.

## 11. Collective epistemics boundary

A scientific community may be modeled as a distributed epistemic system, but retain

\[
\text{world}\neq\text{scientific community}\neq\text{scientific artifacts}.
\]

ArXiv submissions, papers, citations, formal libraries and software releases are emissions/measurements of the collective process, distorted by incentives, filtering, duplication, error and publication practice. Any latent knowledge/capability model must survive duplicate, paraphrase, invalidation, popularity and field-rate controls and predict downstream validated capability beyond standard scientometric baselines.

## 12. Mathematics as a hard testbed

Formal mathematics is useful precisely because a local proof checker can be exact while the scientific transition can still be wrong at a different layer—for example, a valid proof of a statement that does not faithfully encode the intended problem. ME-X3 therefore separates:

- proof validity;
- specification/intent fidelity;
- obstruction diagnosis;
- representation/lemma intervention;
- transfer/reuse;
- resource cost.

A theorem-solving gain that disappears against matched proof-search, lemma-generation or abstraction parents is parent sufficiency, not evidence for a new field.

## 13. Candidate transition constraints

Retain the following only as testable hypotheses:

- criterion/identity conservation;
- execution/evidence separation;
- source/evidence binding and dependence visibility;
- non-amplifying external authority;
- typed context transport;
- selective reopening under defeated sufficient support;
- bounded closure with honest unresolvedness;
- **ontic/epistemic locus separation before escalation**.

The last item is new to V3. It does not claim that locus diagnosis itself is novel; its proposed value is in preventing category-error interventions in coupled scientific transitions.

## 14. Field kill conditions

The programme contracts if:

- strongest parent composition ties or wins the protected quality-cost frontier;
- the common transition object dissolves under native-domain semantics;
- locus diagnosis is recoverable by ordinary diagnosis/metareasoning with no added decision value;
- transition receipts add documentation but no omission-specific causal value;
- representation/regime mechanics reduce to current self-revising/model-discovery parents;
- selective reopening reduces exactly to mature TMS/assurance products;
- atlas/horizon semantics add notation but no protected decision;
- collective metrics reduce to publication/citation/semantic-novelty proxies;
- math gains reduce to extra compute, retrieval or standard abstraction search;
- external reviewers cannot recover a stable residual after parent ownership is made explicit.

## 15. Relationship to ORION-V2 and the flagship

ORION-V2 is one candidate experimental framework, not the field. Its reference objects are allowed to fail or contract.

The flagship Perspective should present the minimum sufficient formal core and the falsifiable field wager, not claim that the field is established or import prospective study outcomes as results. The V3 correction that *world is not machine* should enter the next flagship candidate because it changes the formal interpretation of the transition object and prevents a category mistake.
