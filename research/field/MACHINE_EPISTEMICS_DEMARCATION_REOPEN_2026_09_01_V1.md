# Machine Epistemics — Demarcation Reopen 2026-09-01 V1

**Status:** changed-vocabulary hostile reopen; no field/novelty authority  
**Trigger:** new/contemporary work materially overlaps scientific-state transitions, verified representational-regime change, provenance/reopenability, verification-first workflow control and hypothesis-class expansion.

## 1. Decision

The working name **Machine Epistemics** remains usable, but the distinct-field claim is under **higher collision pressure** than recorded in the previous naming/demarcation pass.

The following formulations are no longer defensible as novelty-bearing slogans:

- “AI science should be evaluated as state transitions rather than outputs” without further qualification;
- “scientific state must be validated/transferred/recorded”;
- “scientific discovery can require representational-regime change”;
- “provenance should support reopening/correction”;
- “hypothesis-space inadequacy can trigger model-family expansion and new experiments”;
- “verification/admissibility gates should control workflow state updates”.

All now have direct parent/contemporary neighbors.

The sharpened residual is therefore:

> **Do recurring cross-transition constraints produce protected scientific decisions beyond the strongest faithful composition of parent-native transition mechanisms?**

This residual is open, not established.

## 2. New severe/high collisions

### D-01 — Wang & Buehler, Self-Revising Discovery Systems for Science (arXiv:2606.01444)

**Collision:** `SEVERE_FORMAL`.

The paper defines scientific discovery as revision of the **representational regime** in which evidence, artifacts, operations and verifiers are typed. Fixed-regime state is represented category-theoretically; discovery is a verified regime transition that transports/preserves old artifacts and identifies residual content beyond transport. The systems include typed skills/artifacts/open needs, workflow mutation, gates, stress tests and proof-carrying knowledge-computation structure.

**ORION consequences:**

- `Gamma_t -> Gamma_{t+1}` cannot carry novelty by itself;
- “representation change with preservation obligations” cannot carry novelty by itself;
- typed verifiers/artifacts/provenance around regime change are directly overlapped;
- a formal parent-recovery mapping is mandatory.

**Residual candidate:** witnessed minimum-responsible escalation across multiple failure levels; cross-mode selective reopening/authority/local-global obligations; strongest-parent contraction semantics.

### D-02 — Bai et al., El Agente Gráfico (arXiv:2602.17902; 2026-08 revision)

**Collision:** `SEVERE_SYSTEM`.

The revised abstract explicitly states that scientific agents need rules for how scientific state is **validated, transferred and recorded** across heterogeneous operations, and proposes typed execution graphs that enforce **admissible scientific state transitions**, record provenance and confine model judgment to explicit decision points.

**ORION consequences:**

- scientific-state transition language is not itself distinctive;
- type-safe execution/provenance/admissibility is a strong system baseline;
- ME must construct cases where the runtime executes validly but the *scientific warrant* still differs because of evidence dependence, evaluator fitness, transport, support defeat, authority or global obstruction.

### D-03 — Tie et al., AutoResearch AI (arXiv:2605.23204)

**Collision:** `HIGH_PROGRAMME`.

The survey frames the shift from task-level AI for Science to long-horizon workflow automation and identifies evidence preservation, reproducibility, validation, weak-direction rejection, provenance, cross-domain robustness and accountable scientific closure as unresolved bottlenecks.

**ORION consequence:** workflow-level reliable scientific closure is a convergent programme concern, not an ME priority claim.

### D-04 — Murphy, Model Discovery Agent (arXiv:2608.09696)

**Collision:** `HIGH_MECHANISM`.

The agent works in M-open settings; predictive checks flag inadequacy of the current hypothesis class, an LLM proposes expanded model structures and Bayesian value-of-information chooses experiments to distinguish mechanisms.

**ORION consequence:** model-family inadequacy -> hypothesis expansion -> discriminating experiment is parent/neighbor-owned.

**Residual candidate:** classify when the blocker is *not* model-family inadequacy—representation, probe, evaluator, measurement, objective, tool/workflow, global obstruction—and choose the minimum responsible intervention level.

### D-05 — MacKnight et al., Provenance grounds trust in autonomous science (Nature Computational Science, 2026-08-20)

**Collision:** `HIGH_CONTROL`.

The paper argues for a complete **re-openable** record of what was reasoned, done and measured, enabling audit and correction.

**ORION consequence:** provenance + reopenability is parent-owned. ME receipts must be justified only by transition-decision value beyond provenance.

### D-06 — Liu & Ou, Verification-first autonomous catalysis (npj Artificial Intelligence, 2026)

**Collision:** `HIGH_CONTROL`.

Structured mechanisms, evidence ledgers, admissibility/consistency/provenance checks, uncertainty gates and workflow-state revision are explicit.

**ORION consequence:** verification-first state control is a strong domain baseline and belongs in the parent federation.

### D-07 — FormalScience (ACL 2026)

**Collision:** `IMPORTANT_SPECIFICATION_BOUNDARY`.

FormalScience demonstrates that perfect formal validity can coexist with semantic drift such as notational collapse and abstraction elevation. This strongly supports the non-implication:

`formal verification != faithful scientific specification`.

It also means this non-implication is not ME priority. ME can use it as a decisive case family for cross-transition evaluator/specification control.

### D-08 — Discover and Prove / theorem-discovery systems (ACL/NALOMA 2026)

**Collision:** `HIGH_MATH_BASELINE`.

Hard-mode theorem proving explicitly separates answer discovery from proof, while conjecturing-proving systems reuse verified discoveries to improve later proof success.

**ORION consequence:** a mathematical ME study must beat strong discovery/proof/lemma-generation baselines; “proof search plus reflection” is not enough.

## 6. Residual claims that remain worth testing

### R-02 — minimum-responsible-level diagnosis
