# Primary Source Ledger — Wave 04 Native Recovery

**Status:** canonical mechanism anchors for constructed recovery cases. This ledger does not claim exhaustive literature coverage.

## Process and workflow

- W. M. P. van der Aalst, “The Application of Petri Nets to Workflow Management,” Journal of Circuits, Systems and Computers 8(1), 1998. Native object: workflow-process structure and analysis using Petri nets.
- W. M. P. van der Aalst et al., workflow-net soundness literature. Native object: completion reachability, proper completion and absence of dead transitions/tasks.

## Multiple-fault diagnosis

- Johan de Kleer and Brian C. Williams, “Diagnosing Multiple Faults,” Artificial Intelligence 32(1), 1987. Native object: multiple fault candidates and sequential measurements.
- Raymond Reiter, “A Theory of Diagnosis from First Principles,” Artificial Intelligence 32(1), 1987. Native object: logical consistency-based diagnosis.

## Correspondence and comparability

- NIST metrological-traceability guidance. Native object: documented unbroken calibration chain, references and uncertainty at every link.
- Joshua Clinton, Simon Jackman and Douglas Rivers, “The Statistical Analysis of Roll Call Data,” American Political Science Review 98(2), 2004. Native object: latent ideal-point inference and identification.
- William L. Hamilton, Jure Leskovec and Dan Jurafsky, “Diachronic Word Embeddings Reveal Statistical Laws of Semantic Change,” ACL 2016. Native object: quantitative temporal semantic change.
- Psychometric measurement-invariance and equating traditions remain primary parents for common-scale interpretation; exact full-text card and ownership audit remain open.

## Viability and learning spaces

- Jean-Pierre Aubin, Viability Theory and related viability-kernel/capture-basin work. Native object: states from which admissible controls preserve constraints and reach targets.
- Jean-Claude Falmagne and Jean-Paul Doignon, Knowledge Spaces and learning-space theory. Native object: feasible knowledge states and prerequisite-constrained progression.

## Local/global and information comparison

## Evidence dependence

- Leslie Kish, survey-sampling/design-effect work. Native object: clustering/design changes effective variance and information.

- Joel Lehman and Kenneth Stanley, “Abandoning Objectives: Evolution Through the Search for Novelty Alone,” Evolutionary Computation 19(2), 2011. Native object: novelty and stepping stones in deceptive search.
