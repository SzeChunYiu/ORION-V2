# ORION-V2 Wave 04 — Native Recovery and Target Adaptation Corpus V0

**Status:** constructed exact-world research protocol and transparent reference tests. No naturalistic performance, novelty, or adoption authority.

## 1. Research question

Wave 03 can compile domain theories into decision-preserving envelopes. Wave 04 asks the harder reverse question:

> Can the generalized object recover the native field's own decisions and counterexamples as a special case, without importing irrelevant industry assumptions into other fields?

A generalized theory that cannot reconstruct its donors is merely inspired by them. A generalized theory that reconstructs donors but cannot expose target-specific missing roles, calibrations, actions or tests is unsafe for transfer.

## 2. Recovery diagram

For native theory `T_D`, generalized family `G_C`, adapter `A_D`, registered native judgment `J_D`, and generalized judgment `J_G`:

```text
T_D --A_D--> G_C
 |            |
J_D          J_G
 |            |
 +---- terminal map ----+
```

A recovery case succeeds only when:

1. the native source identity is bound;
2. every registered native assumption is mapped or explicitly dispositioned;
3. every declared native counterexample remains reflected;
4. the native terminal maps to the computed generalized terminal;
5. exact versus sound/set-valued preservation is typed;
6. no authority or novelty is created by agreement.

## 3. Expert cell

### Native-domain reviewers

Separate reviewers own manufacturing/process management, law/procedure, reliability engineering, medicine, metrology, psychometrics, political methodology, linguistics, control, education, causal/local-global methods, statistics, evidence synthesis, performative evaluation, and R&D portfolio management.

Veto: a generalized terminal cannot replace a native judgment unless the native expert accepts the terminal map and assumption ledger.

### Formal semantics and testing

Role: verify exact terminal maps, set-valued soundness, counterexample reflection, and aggregate suite accounting.

Veto: passing examples without negative controls do not count as recovery.

### Transfer and governance

Role: test target adaptation contracts separately from native recovery.

Veto: source recovery never authorizes target use.

## 4. Recovery modes

```text
EXACT
SOUND_OVER_APPROXIMATION
```

`EXACT` requires equality after the explicit native-to-generalized judgment map.

`SOUND_OVER_APPROXIMATION` permits a generalized set-valued result only when it contains the mapped native result. It must not be reported as exact recovery.

## 6. Wave-04 constructed corpus

The initial corpus contains finite exact worlds, not naturalistic empirical claims.

### Process and authority

- manufacturing quality/release workflow — sound completion;
- manufacturing missing-quality-evidence control — no completion measurement;
- legal/administrative signatory control — authority ceiling blocks completion.

### Diagnosis and responsibility

- engineering pump-versus-valve diagnosis — multiple hypotheses separable by a probe;
- medical interaction-only syndrome — joint cause identified without reducing to either component alone.

### Cross-generation correspondence

- metrological calibration link — exact within the constructed link;
- psychometric form link — partial due semantic/item loss;
- political latent-scale link — noncomparable when the required scale invariant is violated;
- diachronic linguistic alignment — cannot check when sense identity is unresolved.

### Viability and reachability

- robust control — risky boundary excluded under adversarial transitions;
- existential control — the same boundary remains viable when one safe successor suffices;
- educational prerequisite path — all declared learning states can safely reach mastery.

### Other Wave-03 families

- parity contextual model — local validity with global obstruction;
- statistical experiment comparison — perfect signal dominates an uninformative signal;
- shared-model validator cluster — effective evidence is lower than source count;
- performative policy world — deployment response reverses the static winner;
- frontier portfolio — importance-heavy and information-heavy opportunities remain incomparable Pareto choices.

## 7. Target adaptation controls

The first target-adaptation cases require:

- complete source-role to target-role maps;
- target calibration identities;
- target-native known-answer/hostile tests;
- external authority binding;
- source and target epochs.

Controls include:

- manufacturing gate logic adapted to scientific review only after evidence/authority roles and scientific validation tests are rebound;
- engineering diagnosis adapted to medicine blocked when clinical calibration/risk tests are absent;
- scale/correspondence transfer blocked when target anchors or epoch are missing.

A complete contract earns only `READY_FOR_TARGET_NATIVE_VALIDATION`.

## 8. Quantitative outputs

The suite reports:

- exact recovery count;
- sound recovery count;
- decision-drift count;
- assumption-erasure count;
- counterexample-loss count;
- cannot-check count;
- per-domain and per-family coverage;
- target-adaptation blocker distribution.

A future protected corpus must additionally measure false transfer, unnecessary refusal, adaptation cost, target-native performance, and expert disagreement.

## 9. What this wave does not establish

- that the native fixtures are complete models of their industries;
- that the generalized theories outperform native methods;
- that the terminal maps transfer to real datasets or institutions;
- that the source citations are exhaustive;
- that cross-domain adaptation is beneficial;
- that C11 or any generalized family is novel.

## 10. Iteration rule

Every later domain lane must add at least:

1. one positive native recovery case;
2. one native hostile/counterexample case;
3. one assumption that must remain domain-specific;
4. one target adaptation blocker;
5. one strongest-parent comparison;
6. one paper/framework disposition.
