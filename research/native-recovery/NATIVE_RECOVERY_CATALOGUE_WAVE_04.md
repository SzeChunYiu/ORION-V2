## Native assumptions not generalized away

### Process

- resource units and capacity;
- regulatory/legal role identities;
- evidence standards;
- deadlines, compensation and appeals.

### Diagnosis

- component/disease ontology;
- causal model;
- probe risk and calibration;
- standards of responsibility or blame.

### Correspondence

- anchors and reference standards;
- target population and construct;
- latent-scale identification;
- sense inventory and corpus sampling;
- uncertainty propagation rule.

### Viability

- physical/ecological/educational safety set;
- action availability;
- nondeterminism interpretation;
- target definition.

### Evidence, performativity and portfolios

- covariance or common-cause model;
- strategic response function;
- protected outcome;
- scientific importance and harm;
- agenda authority.

## False-transfer controls

1. A sound manufacturing workflow cannot be transferred to science by renaming `quality gate` to `peer review`; scientific validity and evidence tests must be rebound.
2. Engineering fault probabilities do not become clinical disease probabilities.
3. Psychometric anchors do not become political or linguistic anchors without a target identification argument.
4. A robust physical safety kernel does not define educational or epistemic safety.
5. A survey design-effect formula is not a universal evidence-dependence model.
6. A performative-prediction response map is not a complete institutional or strategic model.
7. Novelty-search diversity is not scientific agenda authority.

## Current disposition

The corpus establishes finite non-vacuity and negative controls for generalized interfaces. Every real-domain claim remains open.
