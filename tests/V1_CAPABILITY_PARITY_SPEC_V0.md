# V1 Capability Parity Specification V0

**Status:** census/protocol design; exact V1 identities unbound until freeze.

## Purpose

Prevent a more elegant V2 architecture from silently losing a useful V1 capability.

## Capability families to bind

### Problem/state

Problem contract, framing, scope, decomposition, resources, invariants, exact run/problem identity and deterministic typed routes.

### Search/acquisition

Residual inspection, typed queries, heterogeneous providers, route expansion/stopping, censoring, domain/source-family tracking and search-universe growth.

### Interpretation/representation

Source projections, identity/reference/context resolution, ambiguity, typed mappings, evidence/provenance and unresolved quarantine.

### Reconstruction

Plural/global portrait, compatibility, contradiction, gluing/obstruction and universe expansion.

### Diagnosis/discrimination

Typed gaps, experience/negative history, competing responsibility, discriminating experiment/action and evidence-bound narrowing.

### Change/recovery

Minimal reframe, incumbent comparison, dependency impact, selective staling/reopening, unaffected-state preservation and failure retention.

### Stopping/saturation

Substantive growth, route/domain/freshness/lineage coverage, omission challenge, bounded stop and `CANNOT_CHECK`.

### Cross-cutting

Memory/context selection, immutable episodes, independent review, benchmark interpretation, experiment selection, authority/promotion, execution receipts, side-effect control, persistence, self-change proposal/adoption and failed-execution recovery.

## Allowed dispositions

- `PRESERVE_NATIVE`
- `PRESERVE_AS_SPECIAL_CASE`
- `MERGE_INTO_DEEPER_OBJECT`
- `SPLIT_BY_CONTEXT`
- `REPLACE_WITH_PARENT_METHOD`
- `REPLACE_WITH_DONOR_PRODUCT`
- `GENERALIZE_WITH_NEW_COORDINATE`
- `DEPRECATE_AFTER_PROTECTED_NONINFERIORITY`
- `CANNOT_CHECK`

## Admission condition

For frozen V1 contract suite `C_V1`, matched budget `B` and constitution `kappa`, V2 must not reduce protected justified reach or worsen mandatory gate coordinates beyond frozen bounds.

A new frontier capability cannot compensate for:

- false completion;
- authority/integrity regression;
- semantic/provenance loss;
- loss of selective reopening;
- worse `CANNOT_CHECK` calibration;
- unreplayable execution;
- unnecessary escalation on simple controls.
