# ORION-V2 Paper Programme Delta — Wave 03

## Programme-level change

Wave 03 changes the publication programme from “find structurally similar donor theories” to “compile the smallest decision-preserving envelope and separately validate target adaptation.”

This strengthens several papers but also increases parent-subsumption risk.

## C02 — Context-Relative Structural Knowledge Space

### New formal content

- directional Blackwell-style information order;
- local/global obstruction;
- scale maps with current-target and future-query safety;
- decision-relative versus exact equivalence.

### Admission effect

C02 remains high priority. It must now compare its integrated relation interface against the union of experiment comparison, contextual equivalence, causal abstraction and local/global methods.

## C04 — Obligation-Driven Solver

### New formal content

- solver consumes compiled envelopes rather than untyped donor theories;
- source methods require target adaptation contracts;
- frontier selection returns Pareto portfolios;
- performative response may expire a previous policy evaluation.

### Admission effect

The strongest donor-composed solver baseline becomes stronger. C04 must beat or simplify that product without V1 parity loss.

## C06 — Cross-Generation Comparability

### New formal content

- no lossy envelope is future-query universal;
- exact, decision-relative and sound abstraction grades;
- link composition with conservative authority and uncertainty.

### Admission effect

C06 remains high priority. Shared theory with C11 needs one owner.

## C07 — Dependence-Aware Evidence

### New formal content

- explicit evidence networks;
- effective information under a declared dependence model;
- `DEPENDENCE_UNIDENTIFIED` rather than assumed independence;
- contradiction retained after dependence adjustment.

### Admission effect

C07 remains high priority, but the finite design-effect adapter is only a benchmark mechanism. The paper needs stronger covariance and lineage models.

## C08 — Reticulate Provenance

### New formal content

- component-level lineage;
- semantic-transport requirement on non-copy edges;
- authority ceilings;
- descendant-specific reopening.

### Admission effect

C08 remains merge-by-default. It survives only if typed reticulation changes protected transport/reopening decisions beyond W3C PROV and existing provenance systems.

## C09 — Performative Scientific Evaluation

### New formal content

- static versus performative optima;
- performative stability;

### Admission effect

C09 is strengthened as a possible standalone paper, but parent ownership from performative prediction and policy evaluation is very high.
