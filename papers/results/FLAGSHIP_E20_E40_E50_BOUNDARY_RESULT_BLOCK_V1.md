# Flagship E20/E40/E50 Boundary Result Block V1

**Use:** manuscript-ready empirical-boundary insert for the Machine Epistemics flagship.  
**Status:** protected pilot/native inputs only; field-level empirical claim remains unearned.

## Empirical boundary insert

The first protected real-problem pilot did not show an advantage for the absorptive F2 architecture. Across three gold-blind BugsInPy tasks, raw registered-test success was 1/3 for F2, 1/3 for the strongest-parent federation F0, and 2/3 for both direct generation and same-model reflection. The paired F2-F0 success-risk difference was 0.000, whereas the observed differences versus the two simpler controls were -0.333; the pilot was deliberately underpowered and all corresponding intervals were maximally broad. We therefore treat this result as an adverse boundary condition, not as evidence for or against a field-level supertheory.

The pilot also revealed that some apparent problem-solving failures occur at the artifact interface rather than the scientific diagnosis layer. Two F2 outputs failed during unified-diff application before compilation or testing; one contained a substantively plausible edit with malformed path headers and another contained incorrect hunk counts. A SIMPLE output also failed at patch serialization. Because end-to-end executability is itself part of agent competence, the raw failures remain primary. For mechanism attribution, however, E30 prospectively separates raw executable success from an arm-blind syntax-only sensitivity analysis.

Two scientific-domain native studies further sharpen the lower bound without yet testing F2. In CausalBench, the native causal learner produced non-empty observational networks but empty partial-interventional outputs in both evaluated cell lines, creating a concrete adequacy/escalation stress case. In Matbench Discovery, ORB v2 was the strongest of the three evaluated native models on the recorded F1, precision and accuracy metrics, strengthening the parent/native baseline that any higher-order control layer must respect. Neither result demonstrates that ORION diagnoses or improves these native outcomes; those matched F0/F2 studies remain open.

## Field-status consequence

These results narrow rather than expand the claim. Machine Epistemics remains a research programme hypothesis until a higher-order residual survives strongest-parent comparison in confirmatory real-problem studies and at least two materially different scientific domains. If F0 or simpler controls reproduce the protected decisions with lower cost, the flagship must contract to interfield integration, domain federation or parent sufficiency. A negative or contracted terminal is scientifically valid and must remain visible in the main text.
