# ORION-V2 / Machine Epistemics Publication Targets and Fallbacks V1

**Status:** editorial strategy only. Venue assignment does not grant paper survival, novelty, scientific correctness, or acceptance probability.

## Policy

Every surviving ORION-V2 primary paper is developed to the evidential and presentation standard of its **Tier-1 target journal**. A **Tier-2 fallback** is selected in advance so that rejection does not trigger scientific claim inflation, benchmark replacement, or hurried venue shopping.

“Tier-1” and “Tier-2” are programme-internal submission priorities, not universal journal rankings.

A venue rejection may trigger presentation/scope changes but may not change frozen outcomes, protected criteria, negative results, or strongest-baseline dispositions.

## Shared top-tier manuscript standard

Before first Tier-1 submission, every primary paper must have:

1. one crisp scientific thesis and one explicit non-claim boundary;
2. exact ORION V1 ownership/non-overlap;
3. strongest parent-composed comparator, not a convenience baseline;
4. prospective or outcome-blind protocol identity where applicable;
5. matched resources and non-compensatory validity/integrity metrics;
6. multiple hostile/simple controls, including cases where added V2 complexity should lose;
7. at least one fresh or remote-domain evaluation beyond authored fixtures;
8. independent/blinded semantic adjudication where exact scoring is impossible;
9. statistical uncertainty, ablations, effect sizes and failure analysis appropriate to the study;
10. complete code/data/receipt/replay package where legally possible;
11. negative, parent-tie and `CANNOT_CHECK` results retained in the paper rather than hidden;
12. a title/abstract intelligible to the target journal’s broad audience without ORION-specific vocabulary.

## P-A — Structural Donor Discovery, Reduction and Conservative Generalization

### Tier-1 target

**Nature Machine Intelligence — Article**

Fit: the paper is fundamentally an AI/scientific-discovery method: machine-assisted discovery of remote structural parents, donor-faithful reduction, false-analogy rejection and correct novelty contraction. Nature Machine Intelligence explicitly covers AI/ML and their impact on scientific discovery.

### Tier-2 fallback

**Artificial Intelligence (AIJ) — Regular Paper**

Fit: broad AI, automated reasoning, knowledge representation, search, planning, reasoning under uncertainty and principled new views of AI problems, with value/effectiveness demonstrations.

### Top-tier survival package

- hidden known-parent benchmark with semantic-distance strata;
- lexical, citation, embedding, expert-local, LBD/analogy and strongest-union controls;
- remote-parent recall and false-analogy precision;
- native-verdict preservation as a hard gate;
- novelty-contraction accuracy rather than novelty-maximization;
- human-expert reproduction study for donor cards;
- at least two remote scientific domains not used to design the structural signature;
- explicit cases where the strongest parent search method wins.

### Tier-1 kill condition

If performance gain comes mainly from more search budget or expert labour rather than the typed reduction method, do not submit to Tier-1 as a general AI advance.

## P-B — Context-Relative Scientific Relations, Comparability and Transport

### Tier-1 target

**Nature Computational Science — Article**

Fit: a broadly reusable computational/mathematical framework for scientific relation and transport across representations, domains and epochs, with applications across multiple sciences. Nature Computational Science explicitly seeks foundational algorithms, tools, frameworks and mathematical models that advance multidisciplinary scientific research.

### Tier-2 fallback

**Artificial Intelligence (AIJ) — Regular Paper**

Fit: the relation calculus touches knowledge representation, automated reasoning, uncertainty, abstraction, planning/action semantics and new principled views of AI representation problems.

### Top-tier survival package

- exact parent-known-answer suites for bisimulation, Blackwell-style comparison, abstraction, causal/observational equivalence and local/global obstruction;
- typed relation selection under context rather than one universal metric;
- exact and approximate transport with downstream decision-stability tests;
- cross-generation/epoch drift and selective reopening;
- embedding-distance and persistent-identifier controls;
- cross-parent cases where no single parent theory is sufficient;
- native-judgment preservation hard gate;
- naturalistic representation-change cases from at least two sciences.

### Tier-1 kill condition

If the integrated interface merely wraps mature relation theories without changing a protected scientific decision, publish only as a methods/resource contribution or contract to parents.

## P-C — Obligation-Driven Scientific Problem Solving with Witnessed Escalation

### Tier-1 target

**Nature Machine Intelligence — Article**

Fit: an AI control architecture for scientific agents/research systems with adaptive action selection, plural hypotheses, explicit obligations, diagnosis, minimum-level escalation and protected scientific stopping.

### Tier-2 fallback

**npj Artificial Intelligence — Article**

Fit: transformative/interdisciplinary AI frameworks bridging theoretical advances and practical AI-driven scientific inquiry.

### Top-tier survival package

- exact frozen V1 non-regression over all protected capabilities;
- strongest donor-composed solver baseline rather than a fixed workflow strawman;
- simple cases penalizing unnecessary decomposition/search/escalation;
- hidden mechanistic discovery and multiple-fault cases;
- formal synthesis/proof cases and governed tool-execution cases;
- minimum sufficient escalation accuracy, false/missed escalation and cost;
- justified-terminal rate and false-completion as non-compensatory metrics;
- fresh scientific tasks with independent expert adjudication;
- replayable action/state/terminal receipts.

### Tier-1 kill condition

If the strongest parent-composed solver matches V2, report `USE_DONOR_COMPOSED_SOLVER` and do not market architecture integration as a top-tier algorithmic advance.

## P-D — Dependence-Aware Scientific Evidence and Dynamic Evaluation

### Tier-1 target

**Nature Machine Intelligence — Article**

Fit: a core reliability/evaluation problem for AI systems and scientific agents: shared-source evidence, dependent evaluators, criterion churn, dynamic/performative evaluation and false-authority prevention.

### Tier-2 fallback

**npj Artificial Intelligence — Article**

Fit: AI evaluation, reliability, governance and interdisciplinary impact are directly within scope.

### Top-tier survival package

- copied-source and shared-model/data evaluator controls;
- known dependence graphs plus hidden/common-cause cases;
- independent-support positive controls preventing over-conservatism;
- criterion-churn and evaluator-mutation hostile cases;
- performative environment-response cases;
- calibration and false-authority outcomes, not source-count accuracy alone;
- support-family selective reopening;
- blinded evaluator study where semantic judgment is unavoidable;
- comparison against graphical/statistical dependence parents plus provenance deduplication.

### Tier-1 kill condition

If ordinary dependence modelling plus provenance already produces the same scientific decisions, merge the result into a broader evaluation/resource paper rather than claim a new evidence theory.

## P-E — Frontier Opportunity Discovery and Problem Finding

### Status

**Contingent paper.** It does not receive a standalone submission merely because a draft exists.

### Tier-1 target if it survives

**Nature Computational Science — Article**

Fit: computational discovery of scientifically valuable research opportunities across domains, if the system demonstrates prospective value rather than retrospective interestingness.

### Tier-2 fallback

**npj Artificial Intelligence — Article**

Fit: AI methods reframing research challenges and enabling future AI-driven scientific inquiry.

### Top-tier survival package

- historically time-sliced cases where future outcomes were unavailable at proposal time;
- blinded expert prospective scoring;
- anomaly, novelty, literature-based discovery and portfolio baselines;
- importance, falsifiability, tractability, decisive-probe value and agenda authority kept separate;
- negative interesting-but-not-useful controls;
- multi-objective/non-scalar opportunity portfolios;
- longitudinal follow-up or strong proxy validation.

### Standalone kill condition

If prospective value cannot be measured independently of eventual success, merge P-E into P-C as opportunity-generation methodology.

# Flagship — Machine Epistemics: the Control Science of AI-Driven Discovery

## Role

Field-defining synthesis, **not a sixth primary-results slot**.

The specialist papers carry original experimental/formal claims. The flagship asks whether their surviving residuals plus the external literature define a coherent new field.

## Tier-1 target

**Nature Machine Intelligence — Perspective**

Why: Nature Machine Intelligence publishes peer-reviewed Perspectives on fast-moving/emerging topics and explicitly covers AI’s impact on scientific discovery. A Perspective is suitable for a balanced but forward-looking field proposal and should contain minimal unpublished primary data.

### Candidate title

**Machine Epistemics: The Control Science of AI-Driven Discovery**

Alternative title:

**Beyond AI Scientists: Machine Epistemics for Reliable Scientific Discovery**

## Tier-2 fallback

**npj Artificial Intelligence — Perspective or Review**

Why: npj Artificial Intelligence explicitly accepts peer-reviewed Perspectives and Reviews, including forward-looking, interdisciplinary AI research directions.

## Flagship evidence standard

The flagship is submitted only after enough specialist results are frozen to support—not merely speculate about—the field boundary. It should include:

- a systematic neighboring-field map;
- formal primitive object and foundation laws;
- exact parent ownership and non-claims;
- a taxonomy of machine-epistemic failure modes;
- at least two cross-domain demonstrations from surviving primary papers;
- quantitative evidence that composition-level control changes scientific decisions;
- a basic curriculum and benchmark roadmap;
- explicit field falsifiers and contraction terminals;
- independent outside commentary/review before submission.

## Flagship kill condition

If protected evidence shows no distinct composition-level residual, retitle as an integration Perspective on reliable agentic science rather than claiming Machine Epistemics as a new field.

# Venue escalation and fallback rule

For each paper:

1. prepare to Tier-1 standard first;
2. optional pre-submission inquiry only where the journal supports it;
3. submit Tier-1 without changing frozen evidence to fit perceived editorial taste;
4. if rejected editorially for scope/significance, record the reason;
5. revise framing/length for Tier-2 but preserve claims, outcomes, baselines and negative evidence;
6. if reviewers identify a scientific defect, reopen the relevant research gate before resubmission;
7. never use a fallback venue to evade an unresolved correctness or novelty problem.
