# Hostile Parent-Ownership Addendum — Wave 06 V1

**Status:** literature-ownership amendment for manuscript revision. It adds no paper identity, kernel coordinate, novelty claim, or scientific/publication authority.

This addendum records the changed-vocabulary contraction pass performed after the P-A–P-E and Computational Scientific Control V0 drafts. Its purpose is to make older and orthogonal parent traditions impossible to omit merely because the current programme uses AI-for-science vocabulary.

## 1. P-C — action selection, responsibility and escalation

### Rational metareasoning is a direct parent

Russell & Wefald's *Principles of metareasoning* (Artificial Intelligence 49, 1991, DOI `10.1016/0004-3702(91)90015-C`) explicitly studies how to select and justify computational actions under resource-bounded uncertainty using the value/utility of computation.

**Consequence:** P-C may not claim generic meta-level action selection, resource-bounded rational control, or value-of-computation theory. The residual P-C question is whether scientific hard obligations, semantic preservation, authority, `CANNOT_CHECK`, and witnessed action-space escalation require additional control semantics beyond a strongest metareasoning composition.

### Sequential model-based diagnosis is a direct parent

Rodler, *Sequential model-based diagnosis by systematic search* (Artificial Intelligence 323, 2023, DOI `10.1016/j.artint.2023.103988`) studies multiple diagnosis hypotheses and iterative discriminating queries/tests/probes.

**Consequence:** P-C may not claim generic plural fault diagnosis or discriminating-probe selection. Its residual must be scientific responsibility across evidence/search/representation/method/resource/authority/execution causes plus preservation and escalation consequences.

## 2. P-D / K3 — support maintenance and selective reopening

### Truth-maintenance systems are foundational parents

Doyle, *A truth maintenance system* (Artificial Intelligence 12, 1979, DOI `10.1016/0004-3702(79)90008-0`) records reasons for beliefs and revises them when assumptions conflict, including dependency-directed backtracking.

De Kleer, *An assumption-based TMS* (Artificial Intelligence 28, 1986, DOI `10.1016/0004-3702(86)90080-9`) represents assumption sets, supports inconsistent contexts, and explores multiple potential solutions without ordinary retraction/backtracking.

**Consequence:** ORION-V2 K3 is not a new truth-maintenance engine. It is an evidence/dependence/provenance/revalidation interface whose implementation should reuse or compare against TMS/ATMS-style support maintenance where appropriate. P-D's possible residual is scientific evidence dependence, support-family semantics, provenance/evaluator custody, and authority-bounded reopening—not generic dependency-directed belief revision.

### Belief revision is a direct theoretical parent

Darwiche & Pearl, *On the logic of iterated belief revision* (Artificial Intelligence 89, 1997, DOI `10.1016/S0004-3702(96)00038-0`) shows that sequential belief revision depends on epistemic state beyond a bare belief set and supplies postulates for iterated revision.

**Consequence:** P-D/CSC may not claim generic rational revision under sequences of observations. Scientific-control research must identify what extra evidence/provenance/dependence/authority constraints change protected decisions beyond established belief-revision theory.

## 3. P-B — transport and approximate relation

Pearl & Bareinboim's causal transportability programme supplies formal necessary/sufficient conditions and algorithms for transferring causal information between heterogeneous environments; see *Transportability of Causal and Statistical Relations: A Formal Approach* (AAAI 2011, DOI `10.1609/aaai.v25i1.7861`) and *Meta-Transportability of Causal Effects* (AISTATS 2013, PMLR 31:135–143).

Probabilistic-bisimulation metrics likewise provide mature formal machinery for approximate stochastic-process relations; see Abate, *Approximation Metrics Based on Probabilistic Bisimulations for General State-Space Markov Processes: A Survey* (ENTCS 297, 2013, DOI `10.1016/j.entcs.2013.12.002`).

**Consequence:** P-B cannot own causal transportability, stochastic approximate simulation, or generic relation composition. Its residual is a cross-parent coordination receipt that preserves native relation semantics, loss/error, context, authority and selective-revalidation consequences.

## 4. K6 / CSC — bounded stopping

Statistical/heuristic stopping for active-learning systematic review is a direct parent for search-route stopping. Callaghan & Müller-Hansen et al., *Statistical stopping criteria for automated screening in systematic reviews* (Systematic Reviews 2020, DOI `10.1186/s13643-020-01521-4`) gives confidence-based recall stopping. Boetje & van de Schoot, *The SAFE procedure* (Systematic Reviews 2024, DOI `10.1186/s13643-024-02502-7`) combines conservative stopping safeguards. A 2026 ASReview health-technology simulation further shows stopping reliability varies with prevalence/topic characteristics.

**Consequence:** CSC/ORION-V2 may not claim generic active-screening stopping. Its residual bounded-saturation question is broader: heterogeneous route coverage, censored routes, formulation/representation changes, omission challenge, active obligations and global scientific-stop semantics.

## 5. K3 provenance

W3C PROV-DM / PROV-O already define domain-agnostic entities, activities, agents, derivations, responsibility and provenance interchange (W3C Recommendations, 2013).

**Consequence:** ORION-V2 does not own generic provenance. Its claim, if any, concerns how provenance identity interacts with scientific support, dependence, criterion/evaluator custody, authority and selective revalidation. The V1 corroboration regression remains important precisely because valid provenance does not imply scientific correctness.

## 6. Evaluation-custody refinement

Recent LLM-evaluation work further strengthens the need for protected custody rather than weakening it. Benchmark-contamination research and causal/unbiased evaluation protocols show that public benchmarks, generated evaluators and model-training exposure can distort measured capability. These findings reinforce, but do not add a new V2 coordinate beyond, the already-frozen case/evaluator identity and contamination/custody controls.
