## Editorial judgment

The portfolio now has a substantially stronger *research architecture*, but architecture is not evidence. The conceptual-development, transfer-discovery, formal-mechanics, formalism-genesis and recursive science-of-scientific-development additions should remain in the manuscripts only as falsifiable mechanisms, formal Methods, registered predictions and contraction rules until the corresponding FM/FG/SD studies execute.

The strongest improvement is not additional terminology. It is that the programme now distinguishes several scientifically different questions:

1. Can existing native/parent methods solve the episode directly?
2. Can source-bound structure be discovered and transferred without false analogy?
3. Does the current concept system need revision?
4. Is the representational/formal language itself too coarse?
5. Is more observation/measurement needed instead of a new formalism?
6. Across many research trajectories, are there reusable development operators with explicit contraindications?
7. Can a meta-policy select among these modes prospectively and cheaply?
8. Does another abstraction level add held-out/prospective residual value, or should recursion stop at a bounded lower level?

The new `development_controller.py` makes **minimum-sufficient escalation** the architectural default. This is editorially important because E20 already warns against universal activation: SIMPLE/reflection observed 2/3 successes, versus 1/3 for F0/F2, although the pilot is underpowered and does not establish inferiority. A credible top-tier story must therefore reward *knowing when not to activate complexity*.

## Flagship

The flagship should be revised after decisive computation, not before, around a narrower hierarchy:

`scientific-state transition -> knowledge transformation -> transfer/concept/formal development -> learned development policy -> recursive higher-order test`.

The paper must not imply that every scientific episode traverses this hierarchy or that recursive abstraction converges to an ultimate law. The valid positive endpoint is a bounded higher-level residual with explicit scope and reopen conditions. The valid negative endpoint is equally strong: mature parent/local methods plus selective bridges may be sufficient.

New candidate claim `FL-C4` is frozen in `PORTFOLIO_CLAIM_EVIDENCE_LEDGER_V3_RECURSIVE_DEVELOPMENT_DELTA.json`. It remains `REFERENCE_ONLY` until SD held-out and prospective evidence exists.
