## Framework hierarchy now operationalized

1. scientific episodes / scientific-state transitions;
2. knowledge metabolism;
3. transfer discovery;
4. conceptual development;
5. formal transfer mechanics;
6. formalism genesis;
7. population-scale scientific-development episodes;
8. discovered development operators and matched failure controls;
9. recursive higher-order generalization;
10. minimum-sufficient cross-layer mode selection.

The cross-layer controller is required to prefer the least powerful justified mode. A failure in one mode does not authorize escalation automatically.

In particular:

- formalism genesis cannot activate until strongest parent, missing-observation, local-patch and representation-change alternatives have been tested or validly ruled out;
- recursive meta-learning cannot activate merely because one scientific episode failed;
- population regularities cannot become causal laws without prospective intervention;
