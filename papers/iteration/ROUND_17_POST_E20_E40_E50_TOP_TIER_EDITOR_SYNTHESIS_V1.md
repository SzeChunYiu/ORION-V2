## Top-tier completion standard after execution

A manuscript can reach R3 only when:

- its frozen central claim has a protected result or an honest negative/contraction terminal;
- strongest-parent/simple controls are actually executed;
- failure and missingness are complete;
- registered uncertainty/multiplicity analysis is complete;
- any post-pilot successor mechanism is separated from the predecessor claim;
- critical outcomes are not hidden by aggregate averages.

A manuscript reaches R4 only after R3 plus:

- reproducibility package;
- atomic-claim/evidence audit;
- source/citation entailment audit;
- independent scientific/adjudication review where required;
- data/code/model/compute disclosure;
- author contributions, conflicts and AI-use disclosure;
- target-specific format, figure and reference compliance;
- hostile-editor re-review.
