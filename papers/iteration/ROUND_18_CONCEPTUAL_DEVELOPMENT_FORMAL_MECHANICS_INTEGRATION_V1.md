## What changed

The programme now distinguishes five formal roles:

1. **relational transfer:** typed partial homomorphism with explicit violation classes;
2. **abstraction induction:** structure mapping plus anti-unification/MDL parents rather than fixed lesson tables;
3. **concept formation:** FCA/Galois closure as a parent baseline when applicable;
4. **representation/invariant tests:** invariance/equivariance and categorical functoriality only in domains where their native assumptions hold;
5. **negative transfer:** obstruction and counterexample search as a mandatory fail-closed stage.

Conceptual development is represented as a versioned transition `C_t -> C_t+1` with predecessor-valid-case retention plus either prospective hidden decision gain or checked formal necessity.

- calling a partial homomorphism an ORION invention;
- calling category theory a universal language of science;
- using an MDL improvement as scientific truth;
