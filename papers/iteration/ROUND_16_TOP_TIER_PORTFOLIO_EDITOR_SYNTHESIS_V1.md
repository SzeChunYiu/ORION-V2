## 2. Recursive academic-paper workflow applied

Every manuscript was processed through the same sequence:

1. **atomic thesis extraction** — one result-scale thesis and one non-claim;
2. **parent ownership reconstruction** — strongest parent federation and exact kill condition;
3. **claim–evidence binding** — each central claim receives evidence units and a falsifier;
4. **prospective Results architecture** — result slots and reporting grammar frozen before outcomes;
5. **Methods completion** — arms, resources, outcomes, failures, adjudication and analysis specified;
6. **figure–argument alignment** — four-item evidence spine per paper;
7. **negative-result retention** — parent wins, ties, drag and `CANNOT_CHECK` remain legitimate;
8. **target contraction** — one top-tier thesis rather than a programme catalogue;
9. **surface audit** — abstract, title, terminology, availability/disclosure slots and honest terminal;
10. **reopen rule** — material scientific defects reopen research rather than receive prose patches.

## 14. Result-writing rule

After protected execution, populate the frozen reporting sentences without changing the thesis to fit the result. Then:

- contract claims to the observed scope;
- report absolute counts and uncertainty;
- identify parent wins and critical failures;
- separate constructed, naturalistic and prospective evidence;
- distinguish scientific quality from efficiency;
- report component mechanisms only when interventions support causality;
- leave unavailable evidence as `CANNOT_CHECK`.
