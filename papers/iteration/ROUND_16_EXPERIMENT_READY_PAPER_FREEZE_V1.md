## 11. Result insertion discipline

Generated exports under `WORKDIR/paper_exports/` are reviewer aids, not manuscript text. A paper result may be inserted only after:

- the claim ID is identified;
- source analysis and raw artifacts are content-bound;
- outcome and resource identities are frozen;
- statistical and semantic review are complete;
- negative and parent-win cases are included;
- the result remains within the paper-scale thesis;
- publication readiness is re-evaluated under R4.

## 12. Top-tier and second-tier route

Venue selection follows evidence rather than aspiration.

- A broad, externally reviewed field synthesis with mature specialist evidence may target a top-tier Perspective.
- A decisive cross-domain method/result with strongest-parent comparisons may target a top-tier Article.
- A rigorous bounded negative, integration or reliability result may be stronger and more publishable in an appropriate specialist or second-tier journal than an inflated top-tier claim.
- A paper that loses to F0 or a simple control is contracted, merged or reframed; it is not rewritten until it appears positive.
