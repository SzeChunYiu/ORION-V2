---

# 1. Shared claim classes

Every central sentence in the paper programme should reduce to one of four statuses:

1. `SUPPORTED_PARENT_FACT` — established by literature/data already available;
2. `FRAMEWORK_DEFINITION_OR_PROPOSAL` — a declared object/contract, not evidence that it works;
3. `TESTABLE_HYPOTHESIS_REQUIRES_PROTECTED_RESULT` — must not be narrated as a result before the experiment exists;
4. `NONCLAIM_BOUNDARY` — what the paper explicitly does not own/establish.

The dominant current risk is category 3 being written with the rhetorical force of category 1.

# 6. P-C — Obligation-Driven Scientific Solver

## Archetype

Nature Machine Intelligence **Article** / agentic AI control architecture.

## Central testable claim

Obligation-driven coordination with responsibility diagnosis and witnessed minimum escalation may improve justified terminal states and reduce false completion/unnecessary escalation versus strongest parent-composed adaptive solvers while preserving protected V1 capabilities.

## Human-epistemics pressure

This paper receives the largest substantive revision:

- calibrated inquiry self-model rather than generic reflection;
- failure lesson formation and transfer scope;
- surprise/anomaly capture;
- problem framing as revisable state with strict comparison identity;
- representation restructuring/insight as an explicit high-level action;
- exploration preservation and method pluralism;
- external criticism/ask-for-help triggers.

## New protected benchmark families

1. `SELF_MODEL_CALIBRATION` — identical task accuracy, different ability to choose review/effort/stop.
2. `SELF_CRITIQUE_DEPENDENCE` — repeated same-model reflection versus independent external check.
3. `FRAMING_LOCK_IN` — local actions cannot solve a problem because the problem representation omits a needed variable.
4. `CRITERION_GAMING_CONTROL` — apparent reframing only changes what counts as success.
5. `FAILURE_LESSON_TRANSFER` — structured causal lesson versus raw error replay.
6. `SURPRISE_CAPTURE` — useful off-path anomaly versus irrelevant salient event.
7. `NOISE_CURIOSITY_TRAP` — random unpredictable novelty must not absorb exploration.
8. `INSTRUCTION_COMPETENCE` — correct procedure text but unsafe/ineffective execution under perturbed context.
9. `DEGENERATING_REPAIR_LOOP` — repeated post-hoc fixes versus representation/method change.
10. `METHOD_MONOCULTURE` — strongest parent route lies outside the solver's preferred representation.

## Parent threats expanded

- metacognition/confidence calibration;
- rational metareasoning/expected value of control;
- cognitive-control/task-representation models;
- CEGAR/CEGIS and representation refinement;
- intrinsic motivation/curiosity/open-ended learning;
- creativity/problem-finding/insight;
- error-driven continual learning;
- situated/distributed problem solving.

## Atomic evidence gaps

All new benchmark outcomes remain prospective. The paper cannot claim that self-models, typed surprise or failure lessons improve performance before those arms are implemented and evaluated.

Each paper should be re-read through at least four independent roles:

1. **Native-parent/novelty reviewer:** Can mature parents reproduce the decision under matched information?
2. **Methods/formal reviewer:** Are objects, protocols, statistics and falsifiers sufficient?
3. **Domain/practice reviewer:** Did abstraction erase native semantics, skill, context or authority?

# 11. Recursive completion rule

A manuscript passes another writing/review iteration only when the revision changes one of:

- unsupported/overstated claim;
- missing parent ownership;
- missing discriminating evidence;
- insufficient figure/evidence chain;
- unclear non-claim/boundary;
- target-journal reader comprehension.

Do not revise indefinitely for stylistic variation.

A specialist Article can reach `PUBLICATION_READY` only after its decisive empirical/formal claim ledger is green. A Perspective can reach `PUBLICATION_READY` only after every factual parent claim is sourced, the field residual is either supported by published/frozen specialist evidence or explicitly framed as hypothesis, and hostile external ownership review is complete.
