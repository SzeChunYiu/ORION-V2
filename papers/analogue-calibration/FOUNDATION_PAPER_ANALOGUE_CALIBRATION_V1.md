# Foundation-Paper Analogue Calibration V1

**Purpose:** calibrate the Machine Epistemics flagship and specialist-paper programme against genuinely comparable field-defining/conceptual papers rather than prestige alone.

**Method:** follows the `academic-paper-skills` analogue and unknown-paper protocols: broad quick profiles, then close reading of the most comparable cases, plus hostile counterexamples. Published patterns are treated as design evidence, not causal explanations of acceptance and never as prose templates.

## Target article type

Primary target: **Nature Machine Intelligence — Perspective**.

Current NMI guidance characterizes Perspectives as scholarly, peer-reviewed treatments of emerging/fast-moving topics that present a balanced but forward-looking viewpoint. They are more speculative than Reviews, are typically about **3,000–4,000 words**, permit roughly **100 references**, and should not become vehicles for substantial unpublished primary data.

Therefore the flagship's epistemic job is:

`field tension -> parent landscape -> compact synthesis -> falsifiable research programme -> boundaries/falsifiers`.

Original protected experimental results belong in P-A–P-D/P-E rather than being smuggled into the Perspective.

# A. Broad analogue set

## A1 — Krenn et al., “Sustainability Robotics: A Manifesto for a New Discipline”, Nature Machine Intelligence, 2026

### Epistemic job

Proposes a discipline where a fragmented area lacks a unifying framework.

### Architecture observed

- names a practical/scientific fragmentation problem;
- gives **three principles** and **two dimensions** rather than a large ontology;
- integrates engineering/science with economics, ethics and policy;
- frames the discipline through reusable organizing constraints.

### Transfer

Adopt the compression discipline: a new field proposal becomes readable when internal complexity is mapped into a few memorable public questions/principles.

### Reject

Do not infer that naming a discipline establishes its independence. Machine Epistemics needs stronger parent-composition falsifiers because its parent landscape is exceptionally dense.

---

## A2 — Rahwan et al., “Machine behaviour”, Nature, 2019

### Epistemic job

Proposes treating intelligent machines as a new class of behavioral actors, with a cross-disciplinary research programme.

### Architecture observed

- starts with a change in the object of study;
- imports organizing questions/dimensions from established behavioral science;
- expands to technical, institutional and societal implications;
- makes a broad agenda legible across disciplines.

### Hostile counterexample

Moss et al., “Machine behaviour is old wine in new bottles”, Nature, 2019, argues that the proposal under-credits cybernetics and risks rediscovering an older field.

### Transfer

This pair is the strongest hostile analogue for Machine Epistemics. Parent ownership must be visible in the flagship itself, not buried in references. The paper should define a residual by **protected decisions that the strongest parent composition cannot reproduce**.

### Reject

Do not write “AI changes everything, therefore a new field.” The field label is a hypothesis with a contraction terminal.

---

## A3 — Barabási, Gulbahce & Loscalzo, “Network medicine”, Nature Reviews Genetics, 2011

### Epistemic job

Organizes disease around network principles and shows how the abstraction yields concrete research/clinical utilities.

### Architecture observed

- imports a mature formal object (networks);
- shows why conventional object boundaries are insufficient;
- provides organizing principles, concrete examples and translational implications;
- links conceptual synthesis to questions readers can actually investigate.

### Transfer

A field proposal is stronger when every abstract principle corresponds to an executable/testable research programme.

---

## A4 — Lazer et al., “Computational social science”, Science, 2009, plus later field retrospectives

### Epistemic job

Identifies a technological/data transition that makes old scientific questions newly tractable, then defines a cross-disciplinary opportunity.

### Architecture observed

- establishes the new observational/interventional substrate;
- names scientific opportunities and barriers;
- does not claim that old social science becomes obsolete;
- later retrospectives identify institutional, privacy, access and methodological obstacles.

### Transfer

Machine Epistemics should show what becomes newly measurable/control-relevant when research processes themselves become executable, while explicitly preserving native scientific methods.

---

## A5 — Fortunato et al., “Science of science”, Science, 2018

### Epistemic job

Synthesizes quantitative study of science across disciplines and scales.

### Architecture observed

- defines an object of study (science as a system);
- organizes empirical regularities and mechanisms;
- links explanation to prediction/policy opportunities;
- surfaces limitations of available data and causal interpretation.

### Transfer

A proposed science needs measurable objects and laws, not only normative recommendations.

---

## A6 — Ilievski et al., “Aligning generalization between humans and machines”, Nature Machine Intelligence, 2025

### Epistemic job

Bridges AI and cognitive-science notions of generalization.

### Architecture observed

The synthesis is organized across **notions, methods and evaluation**, letting two mature literatures remain distinct while exposing where alignment is useful.

### Transfer

This is a close analogue for cross-parent coordination without erasure. Machine Epistemics should preserve parent-native semantics and identify composition interfaces.

---

## A7 — “A unifying framework from neural superposition to sparse interpretable codes”, Nature Machine Intelligence, 2026

### Epistemic job

Builds a conceptual bridge across several mature theoretical areas.

### Architecture observed

A compact **three-step framework** connects identifiability theory, compressed sensing and interpretability rather than presenting an exhaustive taxonomy.

### Transfer

A strong foundation paper can be rigorous by showing a small number of precise relations among mature parents, not by claiming ownership of every ingredient.

---

## A8 — Ito et al., “Quantifying artificial intelligence through algorithmic generalization”, Nature Machine Intelligence, 2025

### Epistemic job

Imports algebraic circuit complexity as a conceptual foundation for a science of algorithmic generalization.

### Architecture observed

- identifies a measurement/conceptual inadequacy;
- brings in a formal parent;
- derives a research programme and conceptual figures;
- keeps conceptual/mathematical evidence distinct from benchmark-style evidence.

### Transfer

If Machine Epistemics presents “laws,” each should connect to formal or empirical tests and to an explicit parent relation.

---

## A9 — Liu et al., “Rethinking machine unlearning for large language models”, Nature Machine Intelligence, 2025

### Epistemic job

Re-scopes a fast-moving area by clarifying what different methods/metrics/applications actually ask.

### Transfer

A Perspective can make a major contribution by correcting category errors and evaluation targets without pretending to introduce a wholly new algorithm.

## A10 — AI/science Perspectives and Reviews on scientific understanding, agentic science and autonomous laboratories

Relevant analogues include:

- Krenn et al., “On scientific understanding with artificial intelligence”, Nature Reviews Physics, 2022;
- Xin, Kitchin & Kulik, “Towards agentic science for advancing scientific discovery”, Nature Machine Intelligence, 2025;
- current autonomous-science primary work in Nature and related journals.

### Transfer

The opening must distinguish **capability to perform scientific tasks** from **custody of scientific-state transitions**. Otherwise the flagship reads as another agentic-science overview.

# B. Deep close-reading set

The five most useful structural analogues are:

1. Sustainability Robotics — disciplined field compression;
2. Machine behaviour + its cybernetics critique — field proposal under hostile parent ownership;
3. Aligning generalization between humans and machines — preserving two mature parent vocabularies while building a bridge;
4. Unifying framework from neural superposition to sparse codes — small precise synthesis across formal parents;
5. Quantifying AI through algorithmic generalization — formal parent import plus a testable conceptual programme.

## Cross-paper writing observations

### Opening move

Successful conceptual papers open with a **changed scientific situation or unresolved contradiction**, not with a glossary. The reader first learns why existing partitions make an important question hard.

**Machine Epistemics decision:** open with research processes becoming executable and with examples where task success does not settle whether a scientific commitment may change.

### Contribution placement

The organizing synthesis appears early and is small enough to remember.

**Decision:** expose four control questions in the first third of the flagship. K0–K6 remain implementation detail/technical mapping.

### Parent handling

The best cross-field papers preserve the identities of the parent disciplines rather than average them into one vocabulary.

**Decision:** use an explicit parent-ownership figure and repeated non-claim language. Include the “parent composition may be sufficient” branch in the main narrative.

### Evidence rhythm

Perspectives use examples, conceptual contrasts, established literature and occasionally compact formal objects. They do not pretend that conceptual diagrams are empirical proof.

**Decision:** keep the Machine-Epistemic Episode and foundation laws, but label every field-level residual as hypothesis unless specialist results are frozen.

### Failure/limitations

A credible new-field paper states failure conditions.

**Decision:** make the field contraction terminal a visible box/figure rather than a final-paragraph caveat.

# C. Figure calibration

## Figure 1 — The unit changed: from task output to scientific-state transition

Reader question: **What is the object of study?**

Show a bounded research episode with search/computation/experiment/review actions. Contrast:

- execution succeeded but evidence invalid;
- several reviewers agree but share one source/model;
- representation changes and an old claim may or may not survive;
- an unexpected event opens a new question without proving an explanation.

Function: orientation + problem necessity.

## Figure 2 — Parent ownership and the residual test

Reader question: **Why is this not just renamed epistemology/control/metareasoning/cognitive science?**

Columns:

- native parent problem;
- mature owner;
- composition interface;
- candidate residual protected decision;
- contraction terminal if parent composition suffices.

Include formal/control, philosophy/inquiry, cognition/practice, social/plural epistemology and AI-for-science parents.

Function: hostile novelty control.

## Figure 3 — Four control questions and three learning loops

Reader question: **What research programme follows?**

Top layer: four reader-facing questions:

1. what is known/in what form;
2. how may state/frame change;
3. how does the process detect its own failure;
4. when may it stop/escalate/explore/open a problem.

Bottom layer: world learning / self learning / search-space learning.

Function: synthesis + ML consequence.

## Figure 4 — Falsifiable field programme

Reader question: **What evidence would make us believe or reject the field hypothesis?**

Flow:

`native parent reconstruction -> strongest information-matched parent composition -> protected cross-domain cases -> independent evaluation -> residual / tie / parent win / domain-specific / cannot check`.

Function: field falsifier + empirical roadmap.

### Main-text discipline

Do not make separate figures for every K family, philosophy school or source mode. Those belong in supplementary/research artifacts. Four figures are enough if each answers a decision-relevant reader question.

# D. Proposed flagship argument architecture

1. **Executable research changes the unit of evaluation.**
2. **Task success is not enough to warrant a scientific-state transition.**
3. **Four control questions organize the problem.**
4. **Most component mechanisms already have mature parents.**
5. **Human inquiry widens the object beyond explicit claims: framing, self-monitoring, competence, criticism, surprise and distributed practice.**
6. **Machine Epistemics survives only if cross-parent control changes protected scientific decisions.**
7. **Three coupled learning loops show how epistemics could influence ML itself.**
8. **A protected benchmark programme can falsify the field hypothesis.**
9. **Boundaries: no consciousness requirement, no universal method, no all-human-knowledge saturation, no self-granted authority.**
10. **Outlook: field or integration discipline, depending on evidence.**

# E. Reviewer expectations inferred from analogues

## Editor lens

- broad scientific importance beyond ORION itself;
- one memorable thesis;
- intelligible without repository vocabulary;
- clear distinction from existing AI-for-science/agent papers;
- not a manifesto sustained only by aspiration.

## Novelty/ownership reviewer

- hostile reconstruction of computational epistemology, cybernetics/control, metareasoning, belief revision, philosophy of inquiry, cognitive science and social epistemology;
- strongest composed-parent alternative stated before field claim;
- `OLD_WINE_IN_NEW_BOTTLES` treated as a serious possible verdict.

## Methods/formal reviewer

- foundation laws have defined objects and counterexamples;
- no universal metric where parent semantics are heterogeneous;
- empirical field claims use protected known-answer or blinded independent evaluation.

## Cognitive/practice reviewer

- no slogans from philosophers;
- no ungrounded claims that human cognition is optimal;
- tacit/situated knowledge operationalized through transfer/performance failure rather than mystified;
- serendipity does not equal random novelty.

## AI/ML reviewer

- shows how state/control semantics change training/evaluation;
- compares self-modeling, curiosity, meta-learning and open-ended exploration parents;
- no “new algorithm” claim without experiments.

# F. What to adopt, adapt and reject

## Adopt

- compact organizing dimensions/questions;
- a visible object of study;
- conceptual figures that prove the synthesis rather than decorate it;
- mature-parent import with native semantics;
- explicit future research programme;
- field falsifiers.

## Adapt

- manifesto language -> hypothesis/falsifier language;
- broad interdisciplinarity -> explicit parent ownership and matched-composition baseline;
- forward vision -> bounded, testable ML programmes.

## Reject

- prestige-shaped prose imitation;
- exhaustive taxonomy in the main text;
- “AI is transformative” as sufficient novelty;
- new-field naming without hostile historical genealogy;
- unpublished ORION results inside the Perspective before specialist evidence is frozen;
- anthropomorphic claims about self/consciousness/creativity not needed by protected decisions.
