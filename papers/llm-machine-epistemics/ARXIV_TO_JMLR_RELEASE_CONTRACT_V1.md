**Paper:** Prospective Revision Audit for Autoregressive Representations  
**Issue:** #51  
**Primary public route:** arXiv first, then JMLR.

## 1. Scientific identity

The arXiv and JMLR versions share one claim ledger, proof set, protocol identity, citation boundary and reproducibility package. Posting the preprint is not a new experiment and does not reopen scientific degrees of freedom.

Current JMLR policy explicitly allows manuscripts already available as preprints, including arXiv. Nature Portfolio likewise allows/encourages primary-research preprints, but this paper's primary journal route is JMLR.

## 6. JMLR primary route

JMLR is the primary journal because its current scope explicitly includes:

- theoretical studies yielding insight into learning-system design/behavior;
- formalization of new learning tasks and methods for assessing those tasks;
- analytical frameworks advancing theoretical studies of practical learning methods.

This paper routes only through the **new assessment task / analytical framework** interpretation. The abandoned route is `new generic state theory`.

## 7. JMLR decision brief

The editor should be able to recover the following in under one page:

**Question.** Can current prediction and current decision adequacy certify later evidence-triggered revision adequacy?

**Answer.** No in general: the finite registered-channel construction matches the current linguistic target and unique current action while requiring one additional retained bit for correct later revision.

**Evidence.** Human-readable proof + deterministic finite exhaustive/mechanical audit + a practical Prospective Revision Audit protocol.

**Why JMLR.** The manuscript formalizes a representation-assessment task for intelligent systems and derives a testable evaluation prescription relevant to compression, memory and long-lived language-model/agent representations.

**Boundary.** It does not claim new causal-state/information-state theory and does not claim current LLMs empirically exhibit the constructed failure.
