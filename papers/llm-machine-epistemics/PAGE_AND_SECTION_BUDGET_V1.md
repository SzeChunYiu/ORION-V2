# Page and Section Budget V1

**Issue:** #51  
**Purpose:** keep a JMLR attempt concise enough that the assessment contribution is visible and the parent-owned state machinery does not dominate the paper.

Current JMLR guidance warns that papers above 35 pages (appendices included) may be harder/slower to review; papers above 50 pages require justification and may be desk rejected. The package target is therefore **<=35 JMLR pages including appendices**.

## Target allocation

| Title + abstract | 0.5 | 157-word abstract; no citations required in abstract |
| 1. Introduction + contributions | 2.0–2.5 | lead with assessment blind spot and no-certification question |
| 2. Strongest-parent claim ceiling | 2.5–3.0 | concise ownership table + only load-bearing theory history |
| 3. Responsibility / present state formal setup | 1.5–2.0 | minimum notation needed for theorem |

| Appendix proof detail | <=7–8 | complete enough for theory review while staying under cap |

Approximate total target: **27–33 pages**.

## Main-body priorities

The main paper must make sense even if the appendix is not read.

Keep in main:

- no-certification theorem statement;
- proof intuition and one-bit construction;
- strongest parent contraction;
- present-equivalence requirement;
- Prospective Revision Audit flow;
- update + maintain/selective-reopening metrics;
- alternate-channel retention gate;
- direct-neighbor distinctions;
- empirical nonclaim.

Move out first under pressure:

- exhaustive mutation details;
- universality boundary proof;
- full horizon proof;
- extended finite fixture table;
- full claim/receipt map;
- secondary rate-distortion calibration.

## Notation budget

Prefer only these load-bearing symbols in the main body:

```text
H, Y^+, S_P
r=(Q,A,ell,sigma)
C_stat^*
C_dyn^*
Omega_dyn
RAP_k
```

Use prose for additional bookkeeping where possible. Do not import the wider ORION episode/kernel notation into this standalone paper.

## Terminology budget

Preferred terms:

- linguistic predictive state/reference;
- current responsibility decision;
- prospective revision adequacy;
- representation intervention;
- alternate-channel retention;
- update / maintain / selective reopening;
- collision certificate.

Avoid unless needed for historical context:

- epistemic kernel;
- scientific-state transition system;
- knowledge metabolism;
- Machine Epistemics field claims;
- ORION module names.

The standalone paper should be understandable to an ML reviewer who has never heard of ORION.

## Title positioning

Current full title remains acceptable:

> **Beyond Predictive Sufficiency: A Prospective Revision Audit for Autoregressive Representations**

If an external editor finds “Beyond Predictive Sufficiency” too broad or theory-claiming, the only pre-authorized contraction is:

> **A Prospective Revision Audit for Autoregressive Representations**

Changing to a stronger title such as “A New Theory of Epistemic State” is prohibited.

## Display budget

Main body target:

- 2–3 figures;
- 2–3 compact tables.

Priority order is frozen in `FIGURE_AND_DISPLAY_SPEC_V1.md`.

## Editorial terminal

A mechanical LaTeX build that exceeds 35 JMLR pages must first compress/move parent-owned proofs and receipt detail. It may not solve page pressure by deleting claim ceilings, alternate-channel controls, or direct-parent discussion.
