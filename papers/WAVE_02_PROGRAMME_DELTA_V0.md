# ORION-V2 Paper Programme Delta — Wave 02

## New provisional candidate

**V2-C11 — Conservative Generalization and Adaptation of Domain Theories for Machine Scientific Reasoning**

The candidate studies how an industry- or discipline-specific theory can be transformed into a reusable machine object without erasing native decisions, assumptions, dynamics, resource semantics, authority, counterexamples, or epoch restrictions.

## Why the candidate exists

Wave-01 treated domain knowledge mainly as donors and structural neighbours. Wave-02 finds that retrieval and comparison are insufficient: ORION also needs a formal transport layer distinguishing exact interpretation, conservative generalization, decision-relative adaptation, and sound abstraction.

## Parent-subsumption risk

Very high. Institution theory, theory morphisms, conservative extension, abstract interpretation, model-driven engineering, ontology alignment, and domain-specific transport theories own most of the component structure.

C11 is not admitted as an ORION novelty claim.

## Relationship to existing candidates

### C01/C03 — donor hunting and machine reduction

C11 supplies the preservation and adaptation discipline after a donor is found. Default disposition: merge C11’s schemas and transport engine into C01/C03 unless a standalone theorem or benchmark result survives.

### C02 — context-relative structural space

C02 asks which relation holds between structures. C11 asks whether a native theory can be transported into a generalized envelope without scientific drift. C11 may become C02’s formal admission protocol.

### C04 — end-to-end solver

C04 consumes admitted methods and representations. C11 constrains when a donor-derived method may enter the solver and when target-native revalidation is required.

### C06 — cross-generation comparability

C06 specializes transport across epochs and framework versions. The Calibrated Correspondence Chain is shared theory and must have one final owner.

### C07/C08/C09

Dependence, reticulate provenance, and performative response become additional coordinates of a transport receipt. They should not be duplicated in C11.

## Priority change

Wave-02 does not displace the current highest-priority candidates C02, C04, C06, and C07. C11 is a high-value methodological candidate with a default **merge unless separated** disposition.

## Standalone admission gate

C11 survives as a paper only if, against the strongest parent union, it improves at least one protected coordinate without harming native fidelity:

- valid remote-method transfer;
- false-analogy rejection;
- native-judgment preservation;
- assumption/resource/authority violation detection;
- counterexample reflection;
- correct selective reopening;
- expert reduction reproducibility;
- cost of target-native adaptation.
