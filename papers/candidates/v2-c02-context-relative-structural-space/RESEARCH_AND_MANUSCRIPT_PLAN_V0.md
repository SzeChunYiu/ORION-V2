# V2-C02 Research and Manuscript Plan V0

**Working title:** *A Context-Relative Structural Space for Scientific Knowledge*  
**Status:** candidate paper; all claims `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## 1. Scientific object

Knowledge structures from remote domains may be equivalent under one observer, probe, intervention, decision or reachability class while distinguishable under another. The paper asks whether a machine-instantiable family of typed relations is scientifically preferable to one unconditional embedding distance.

## 2. Candidate contributions

### C02.1 — Context object

Define a context:

`C = (Q, I, D, B, kappa, target, tolerance, epoch)`

for admissible queries, interventions, decisions, resource/information bound, protected constitution, preserved consequences, approximation tolerance and semantic epoch.

### C02.2 — Relation family

Define parent-faithful interfaces for:

- isomorphism;
- structural/regular role equivalence;
- bisimulation/behavioural equivalence;
- interface/contextual equivalence;
- observational/Markov equivalence;
- predictive equivalence;
- decision dominance/equivalence and deficiency;
- rough indiscernibility;
- safe quotient/lumpability;
- viability/reachability equivalence;
- local/global compatibility and obstruction;
- approximation and incomparability.

### C02.3 — Relation receipt

Every judgment returns relation type, context, mapping, witness, loss/bound, donor-native judgment preservation, minimal counter-probe and expiration conditions.

### C02.4 — Cross-parent benchmark

Construct a protected benchmark combining exact native cases and cross-domain controls. The candidate integrated interface must outperform generic embeddings and any single parent interface on typed relation classification and counter-probe recovery without changing native verdicts.

## 3. Non-claims

The paper does not claim invention of any individual equivalence theory, sheaf method, causal Markov equivalence, rough sets, experiment comparison, lumpability or viability theory. It does not claim that all scientific meaning reduces to structure.

## 4. Formal programme

### F1 — Context monotonicity/anti-monotonicity audit

Study how relation judgments change when queries, interventions, targets or budgets expand. Observational equivalence may disappear with more interventions; safe quotient may expire under a new target.

### F2 — Witness and counter-probe soundness

For exact parent families, prove that returned witnesses certify the relation and counter-probes distinguish the structures under the declared semantics.

### F3 — Composition conditions

Determine which relations compose transitively and under which context/epoch constraints. Refuse transitive closure when parent conditions do not support it.

### F4 — Redundancy versus underdetermination

Give formal conditions separating multiple descriptions of one state from several live latent states with equal observations.

### F5 — Partial order/incomparability

Show why decision-relative informativeness need not form a total order and why a scalar nearest-neighbour ranking can be invalid.

## 5. Benchmark families

1. finite graph isomorphism and non-isomorphism;
2. regularly equivalent social roles;
3. bisimilar/non-bisimilar labelled transition systems;
4. terminal/interface-equivalent circuits/systems;
5. Markov-equivalent causal DAGs with interventional separators;
6. predictive causal-state examples;
7. Blackwell-style finite experiments and directional garblings;
8. rough-set lower/upper approximation examples;
9. safe/unsafe aggregation and lumpability;
10. viability/learning-space reachability examples;
11. local compatibility/global parity obstruction;
12. same words/different structure and different words/same role controls;
13. context/epoch changes expiring old receipts.

## 6. Baselines

- lexical/topic similarity;
- generic text and graph embeddings;
- graph edit distance/matching;
- one relation family at a time;
- rule-based typed signatures;
- learned relation classifier without witnesses;
- strongest union of parent solvers;
- candidate orchestration/interface.

The strongest parent union is mandatory. If it returns all required judgments through a thin interface, the paper contracts to a benchmark/resource or orchestration result.

## 7. Metrics

- exact relation accuracy per family;
- false equivalence and missed equivalence;
- context-scoping accuracy;
- native-verdict preservation;
- counter-probe validity and minimum cost;
- loss/deficiency-bound calibration;
- correct incomparability;
- certificate expiration/reopening accuracy;
- remote-neighbour retrieval at fixed topic distance;
- compute and annotation cost.

## 8. Data split and custody

- public training/development native cases;
- hidden held-out exact cases generated from frozen seeds;
- expert-adjudicated semantic cases from withheld domains;
- protected context-change and false-analogy cases;
- evaluators and gold held outside candidate custody.

## 9. Key hostile cases

- observational equivalence incorrectly promoted to causal identity;
- role equivalence incorrectly promoted to semantic identity;
- current-target-safe quotient used for an unseen future target;
- approximate mapping treated as exact;
- missing/censored probe treated as non-identifiability;
- generic embedding retrieves topical rather than structural neighbour;
- integrated interface changes a parent-native verdict;
- context omitted from a relation certificate.
