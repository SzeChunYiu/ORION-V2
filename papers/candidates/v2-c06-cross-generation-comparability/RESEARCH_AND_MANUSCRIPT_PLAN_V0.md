# V2-C06 Research and Manuscript Plan V0

**Working title:** *Cross-Generation Epistemic Comparability under Changing Representations and Measurements*  
**Status:** candidate paper; all claims `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## 1. Scientific problem

A scientific claim, score, latent coordinate, evidence item, model state or certificate may retain an identifier across versions while changing meaning, scale, measurement, target obligation or decision context. Ordinary provenance shows lineage but does not by itself establish comparability.

## 2. Parent convergence

- political ideal-point linking and dynamic scaling;
- psychometric equating, DIF and measurement invariance;
- metrological calibration, traceability and uncertainty;
- diachronic semantics;
- causal/population transport;
- ontology/schema evolution;
- V1 certificate lifting, regime transport and safe reuse.

The paper survives only if their strongest compatible product leaves a distinct certificate/decision problem.

## 3. Candidate thesis

A source-bound comparability certificate can distinguish exact, partial, non-comparable and unresolved cross-generation objects; preserve content while reopening obsolete closure; and improve safe scientific reuse over persistent identity, provenance-only and parent-specific linking baselines.

## 4. Candidate certificate

## 5. Formal questions

### Q1 — Composition

When do comparability maps compose across multiple generations? How do uncertainty, missing anchors and context changes accumulate?

### Q2 — Asymmetry

Can a richer target representation refine an old object while reverse transport is lossy or impossible?

### Q3 — Closure versus content

Under what conditions does evidence content transport while the old satisfaction/closure judgment must reopen?

### Q4 — Target-relative safety

Can a map be safe for current decisions but invalid for future responsibilities? How is the exposure represented prospectively?

### Q5 — Expiration

Which changes in construct, population, instrument, objective, authority or scale invalidate a prior certificate?

## 9. Hostile cases

- same ID but different construct;
- high semantic similarity with incompatible measurand;
- complete provenance but no common scale;
- valid anchor items that themselves drift;
- partial map silently promoted to exact;
- current target preserved, future query destroyed;
- content valid, authority epoch revoked;
- uncertainty exceeds a protected threshold;
- donor-specific link sufficient, making V2 wrapper redundant.
