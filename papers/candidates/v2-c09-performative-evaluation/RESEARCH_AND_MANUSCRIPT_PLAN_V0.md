# V2-C09 Research and Manuscript Plan V0

**Working title:** *Performative Scientific Evaluation under Strategic and Institutional Response*  
**Status:** candidate specialization with mature parent threats; all claims `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## 1. Scientific problem

Scientific benchmarks, policies, incentives, publications and autonomous-system deployment can change the agents, institutions and data-generating processes they evaluate. A relationship measured before deployment may therefore cease to hold after the evaluator becomes a target.

## 2. Parent threats

- Lucas critique and policy-regime dependence;
- Goodhart’s law and specification gaming;
- performative prediction;
- strategic classification and mechanism design;
- causal policy evaluation with behavioural response;
- organizational incentives, audit gaming and management override;
- benchmark contamination and publication effects;
- V1 protected goals, authority and evaluation integrity.

The paper must not claim that optimization changes behaviour or that benchmarks can be gamed. The candidate residual is a scientific-evaluation interface integrating response, hidden intent, evidence/authority and reopening.

## 3. Candidate thesis

A response-aware scientific evaluation model can identify when static validation ceases to transport after deployment/publication, prevent false progress or authority promotion, and select stable/incentive-compatible evaluation policies better than static protected-evaluator baselines while preserving feasible scientific assessment.

## 4. Candidate objects

### `PerformativeEvaluationModel`

- actor/system population and response channels;
- policy/evaluator identity and observability;
- pre-deployment and induced distributions;
- strategic/action response model;
- protected scientific intent and non-compensatory constraints;
- static versus performative risk/value;
- evaluator manipulation surface;
- response uncertainty and identification status;
- stability/tolerance conditions;
- epoch and reopen rules.

### `EvaluationTransportReceipt`

Declares whether static evidence transports under a bounded response model, with distribution/behaviour shift, decision loss, confidence/tolerance, counterexample and expiration.

### `IncentiveResponseAudit`

Records incentive channels, excluded/manipulated cases, denominator changes, gaming strategies, adaptive data generation, governance and independent custody.

## 5. Formal questions

- When is a static evaluator transportable after deployment?
- Which response maps yield stable fixed points or convergence?
- How should unidentifiable strategic response change the terminal?
- Can an incentive-compatible evaluator retain construct validity?
- How do evaluator publication and benchmark visibility alter research portfolios?
- When should prior results reopen after the environment adapts?
- How do multiple strategic actors and institutions interact?

## 9. Hostile cases

- ordinary covariate shift mistaken for strategic response;
- static evaluator remains valid but method overcomplicates it;
- hidden intent scorer itself changes or leaks;
- response model assumes perfect rationality;
- incentive-compatible formal rule misunderstood by bounded actors;
- strong protection makes evaluation useless;
- evaluator update and candidate update occur simultaneously;
- response not identified but point estimate promoted;
- policy changes the construct, not merely the distribution;
- current metric gain causes long-run scientific harm.
