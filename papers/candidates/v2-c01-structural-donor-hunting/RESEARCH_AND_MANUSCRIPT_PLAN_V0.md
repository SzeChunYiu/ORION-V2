# V2-C01 Research and Manuscript Plan V0

**Working title:** *Cross-Domain Structural Donor Hunting for Scientific Claim Reduction*  
**Status:** candidate empirical-method paper; all claims `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## 1. Research origin

The programme began by asking whether politics, linguistics, management, engineering, humanities and other fields contain models ORION had missed. Repository audit showed that some apparent omissions were already known, while changed-vocabulary search exposed remote parents and new coordinates. The process then found parents of itself—analogy, TRIZ, design-by-analogy, morphological analysis, C-K/FBS and literature-based discovery—contracting the broad novelty story.

## 2. Candidate thesis

A donor-faithful search process using native reconstruction, typed structural signatures, context-relative relations, strongest donor-product construction and counter-probes may discover decisive remote parents and contract false novelty more efficiently than lexical, citation, embedding, expert-local and mature analogy/design-search baselines.

## 3. Process under test

1. traverse an independent field taxonomy;
2. formulate each field’s canonical problems in native vocabulary;
3. reconstruct objects, operations, assumptions, invariants, probes, failures and decisions;
4. produce source-bound structural signatures;
5. retrieve remote candidate neighbours under multiple relation families;
6. reproduce donor-native known-answer judgments;
7. construct strongest compatible donor products;
8. generate counter-probes/falsifiers;
9. assign absorption/contraction/residual disposition;
10. evaluate on fresh domains under independent adjudication.

## 4. Non-claims

The paper does not claim invention of analogy, cross-domain transfer, LBD, TRIZ, morphological analysis, design-by-analogy, graph matching or embedding retrieval. A successful result may be only improved scientific governance/novelty contraction.

## 5. Dataset design

### Hidden known-parent corpus

Candidate scientific claims/mechanisms paired with strong native parents at varying topic/citation distance.

### Structural-neighbour corpus

Positive and negative pairs across role, behaviour, observation, prediction, decision, abstraction, reachability, gluing, dependence, provenance and strategic-response relations.

### Claim-contraction corpus

Claims whose correct outcomes include:

- fully parent-owned;
- parent special case;
- strongest donor-product equivalence;
- conservative envelope without strict separation;
- surviving residual;
- unresolved/`CANNOT_CHECK`.

### Fresh-domain split

Withhold complete disciplines and practitioner traditions from derivation.

## 6. Baselines

- keyword/native lexical search;
- generic web/scholar search;
- citation-neighbour search;
- topic/semantic embeddings;
- expert local-field search;
- literature-based discovery;
- structure-mapping/design-by-analogy;
- TRIZ/morphological abstraction where operationalizable;
- typed structural retrieval;
- strongest union/product under matched budget.

## 7. Required controls

- same words/different structure;
- different words/same structure;
- identical diagram but incompatible native semantics;
- apparent relation disappearing when a hidden assumption is restored;
- parent pair that cannot be validly composed;
- candidate found only after changing context/probe;
- relevant parent already present in ORION repo;
- censored search route;
- strongest donor product tying the proposed ORION residual.

## 8. Outcomes

- decisive-parent recall@budget;
- remote-parent recall stratified by topic distance;
- time/cost to strongest parent;
- native-verdict preservation;
- false-analogy and missed-analogy rates;
- correct relation/context classification;
- counter-probe validity and cost;
- candidate-claim contraction accuracy;
- ideal donor-product tie detection;
- number/quality of surviving residuals;
- `CANNOT_CHECK`/censoring calibration;
- fresh-domain transfer;
- independent adjudicator agreement.

## 10. Protocol custody

- hidden parent identities held outside candidate search;
- fixed source/search budgets;
- search traces and discarded candidates archived;
- structural schema frozen before final test;
- expert adjudication blinded to method identity;
- no post-outcome relation-type or threshold changes;
- ambiguous native cases remain unresolved.
