## Submission strategy

See:

- `PUBLICATION_TARGETS_AND_FALLBACKS_V1.md`

Every surviving paper is developed first to a Tier-1 journal standard and has a predeclared Tier-2 fallback. “Tier-1/Tier-2” are internal submission priorities, not universal venue rankings.

The current preferred ladder is:

- P-A: Nature Machine Intelligence → Artificial Intelligence (AIJ);
- P-B: Nature Computational Science → Artificial Intelligence (AIJ);
- P-C: Nature Machine Intelligence → npj Artificial Intelligence;
- P-D: Nature Machine Intelligence → npj Artificial Intelligence;
- P-E if it survives: Nature Computational Science → npj Artificial Intelligence;
- Machine Epistemics flagship: Nature Machine Intelligence Perspective → npj Artificial Intelligence Perspective/Review.

## Scientific rules

- candidate numbering and final titles remain provisional until protected evidence closes;
- no paper may claim novelty before strongest-parent reduction and protected evaluation;
- each primary paper requires a quantitative/formal discriminator, hostile controls, matched baselines, evidence package, falsifiers and honest negative terminals;
- a venue fallback may change framing or format but may not change frozen evidence, criteria, negative results or baseline outcomes to evade criticism;
- useful research engineering without incremental scientific value remains a methods/resource contribution only when independently justified;
- shared theory across papers must have one explicit owner and non-duplicative claim boundaries;
- P-E is dropped/merged if prospective scientific opportunity value cannot be measured independently of retrospective success;
- the Machine Epistemics field claim contracts if existing parent fields reproduce the same protected composition-level decisions.
