# ORION-V2 Candidate Paper Programme Ledger V0

**Status:** prospective candidates only; all terminals `CANNOT_CHECK`.

**Identity rule:** existing ORION papers, including P1–P15 and their successors/companions, remain owned by the ORION repository. The candidates below must not duplicate or silently renumber those papers. Final numbering, titles and paper identity are not frozen.

## Programme-level selection rule

A candidate survives only if it has:

- a distinct scientific object not already owned by V1;
- faithful reconstruction of strongest parent fields;
- an explicit strongest donor-composed baseline;
- a prospective quantitative/formal discriminator;
- hostile controls and honest negative terminals;
- fresh evaluation outside derivation domains;
- independent scientific and publication authority.

Deleting or merging a candidate after donor reduction is a successful programme outcome.

## V2-C01 — Cross-Domain Structural Donor Hunting as a Scientific Discovery Method

### Candidate thesis

A reproducible process that traverses independent knowledge taxonomies, reconstructs native problems, retrieves remote structural neighbours, contracts false novelty and generates discriminating scientific hypotheses may outperform lexical, citation, embedding and mature analogy/design-search parents under matched budget.

### Strong parent threats

Structure mapping, TRIZ, design-by-analogy, General Morphological Analysis, C-K/FBS design theory, literature-based discovery, network role equivalence and science-of-science combinatorial novelty.

### Protected discriminator

Typed structural retrieval must improve decisive remote-donor discovery or novelty contraction beyond the strongest parent union while preserving native donor judgments and acceptable false-analogy calibration.

### Minimum study

- hidden remote-parent corpus spanning many disciplines;
- same-words/different-structure and different-words/same-structure controls;
- matched lexical, citation, embedding, expert, analogy/TRIZ and combined baselines;
- donor recall@budget, time/cost to decisive parent, false analogy, claim contraction and fresh-domain transfer.

### Honest terminals

`USE_PARENT_SEARCH_METHOD`, `DONOR_CONTRACTION_VALUE_ONLY`, `REMOTE_RECALL_GAIN_FALSE_ANALOGY_TOO_HIGH`, `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## V2-C02 — A Context-Relative Structural Knowledge Space

### Candidate thesis

Scientific structures require a multi-relational space parameterized by probes, interventions, decisions, resources, authority and epoch; no single semantic metric correctly represents isomorphism, role equivalence, behavioural equivalence, predictive equivalence, decision dominance, safe quotienting, underdetermination and incomparability.

### Strong parent threats

Structural/regular equivalence, bisimulation, contextual equivalence, Markov equivalence, Blackwell/Le Cam experiment comparison, rough sets, sufficient statistics, abstract interpretation, lumpability/model reduction, computational mechanics, viability theory and sheaf/local-global methods.

### Protected discriminator

The integrated relation family must correctly classify cross-parent known-answer cases and context changes that no one parent or generic embedding handles under the same interface.

### Minimum study

Formal relation definitions, decision procedures/witnesses, minimal distinguishing probes, loss/deficiency bounds where defined, context-expiration rules and a protected cross-domain benchmark.

### Honest terminals

`PARENT_FAMILY_SUFFICIENT_NO_UNIFIED_THEORY`, `USE_ORCHESTRATION_ONLY`, `RELATION_FAMILY_NOT_FIDELITY_PRESERVING`, `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## V2-C07 — Dependence-Aware Scientific Evidence and Validator Diversity

### Candidate thesis

Scientific assurance should model common-cause, lineage, model, data and social dependence among evidence and validators; counting sources, agents or replications is not a valid independence measure.

### Strong parent threats

Meta-analysis with dependent effects, survey sampling/design effects, epidemiological interference, graphical models, reliability/common-cause failures, Byzantine systems, ensemble diversity, sociology of diffusion and V1 authority/provenance.

### Protected discriminator

A dependence-aware evidence model must improve calibration and false-authority prevention over source-count, majority-vote and naive ensemble baselines while retaining useful evidence.

### Minimum study

Known independent/correlated evidence worlds, shared-model validators, copied-source chains, multi-instrument data, strategic collusion controls and fresh scientific tasks.

### Honest terminals

`EXISTING_DEPENDENCE_MODELS_SUFFICIENT`, `DEPENDENCE_UNIDENTIFIABLE`, `OVERCONSERVATIVE_EVIDENCE_REJECTION`, `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## V2-C08 — Reticulate Scientific Provenance and Multi-Parent Inheritance

`GENERAL_PROVENANCE_GRAPH_SUFFICIENT`, `NO_INCREMENTAL_DECISION_VALUE`, `LINEAGE_UNRESOLVABLE`, `CANNOT_CHECK`.
