# ORION-V2 Paper Priority and Merge Review — Wave 01

**Status:** editorial-scientific triage only; no paper identity or novelty is authorized.  
**Input:** all-domain structural neighbourhoods Wave 01, solver/Jump studies, V1 ownership risks and parent-field pressure.

## Review panel

- programme architect: non-overlap and shared-theory ownership;
- hostile novelty reviewer: strongest parent and donor-product collision;
- empirical-method reviewer: executable protocol and protected evaluation;
- formal-theory reviewer: theorem object and countermodel;
- venue/editorial reviewer: coherent audience and paper-sized contribution;
- replication reviewer: artifacts, data, replay and external adjudication.

## Scoring dimensions

Each candidate is judged qualitatively on:

- distinctness from ORION V1;
- parent-subsumption risk;
- formal/quantitative tractability;
- protected benchmark feasibility;
- scientific importance;
- ability to stand alone;
- cross-paper dependency burden;
- likelihood of a useful negative result.

## Tier A — develop full protocols first

### A1 — V2-C02 Context-Relative Structural Knowledge Space

**Why first:** supplies the common mathematical language for donor search, solver state comparison, safe abstraction, comparability and Jump transport.

**Strongest value:** separates role, behaviour, observation, prediction, decision, quotient safety, obstruction and incomparability instead of forcing one embedding.

**Main threat:** may collapse into orchestration of established relation theories with no new theorem.

**Required paper-sized discriminator:** a formally specified relation family plus cross-parent known-answer benchmark where no single parent or generic embedding can return the correct typed relation, witnesses, counter-probes and context-expiration decisions.

**Editorial recommendation:** retain as a standalone theory/benchmark candidate; begin full related-work and theorem ownership audit.

### A2 — V2-C04 Obligation-Driven End-to-End Scientific Problem Solving

**Why first:** directly defines the scientific-system contribution and enables V1 parity evaluation.

**Strongest value:** integrates problem contracts, plural models, heterogeneous action selection, honest terminals, protected authority and witnessed escalation.

**Main threat:** strongest composition of systems engineering, POMDP/metareasoning, blackboard architectures, diagnosis, formal synthesis and scientific workflows may tie it.

**Required discriminator:** protected mixed-domain study showing improved justified-terminal rate or resource-adjusted justified reach without V1 parity, authority, semantic-fidelity or false-completion regressions.

**Editorial recommendation:** flagship systems paper only after a strict donor-product baseline is executable.

### A3 — V2-C06 Cross-Generation Epistemic Comparability

**Why first:** strong multi-field convergence and a concrete gap between persistent identity/provenance and justified comparability.

**Strongest value:** combines political/psychometric linking, metrological traceability, semantic drift and V1 transport/reopening.

**Main threat:** V1 P6/P7/P13 plus standard linking/invariance methods may already form the ideal product.

**Required discriminator:** comparability certificates prevent silent reuse errors or unnecessary reopening on cross-version scientific objects better than provenance-only and parent-linking baselines.

**Editorial recommendation:** standalone paper likely, but ownership boundary with C02 must be explicit: C02 owns relation semantics; C06 owns temporal/cross-generation measurement and certificate consequences.

### A4 — V2-C07 Dependence-Aware Scientific Evidence

**Why first:** direct safety/scientific-validity value and clear hostile cases involving shared models, copied sources and common calibration.

**Strongest value:** prevents source/agent/reviewer count from being mistaken for independent assurance.

**Main threat:** existing graphical/meta-analytic/common-cause methods may suffice when supplied provenance.

**Required discriminator:** improved calibration/false-authority prevention while retaining useful independent evidence, with dependence-identification uncertainty explicit.

**Editorial recommendation:** standalone methods/evaluation paper candidate.

## Tier B — develop in parallel, likely merge or specialize

### B1 — V2-C01 Cross-Domain Structural Donor Hunting

**Value:** the research procedure is reproducible and could improve remote-parent discovery and novelty contraction.

**Threat:** analogy, TRIZ, design-by-analogy, morphological analysis and literature-based discovery own much of the operator.

**Recommendation:** keep as a prospective empirical method paper; merge C03 machine engine into its artifact/method section unless automation itself shows a distinct fidelity result.

### B2 — V2-C05 Witnessed Scientific Jump

**Value:** minimum-responsible-level escalation is central to frontier science and gives a falsifiable alternative to vague creativity.

**Threat:** V1 negative Jump result plus strong parents across model expansion, representation change, design, synthesis and open-ended search.

**Recommendation:** retain independent paper only if the J0–J8 controller beats the strongest donor composition on genuine/false-impasse classification and preservation. Otherwise make it a major section/benchmark of C04.

### B3 — V2-C10 Frontier Opportunity Discovery and Problem Finding

**Value:** gives the solver a frontier-scanning entry mode and separates agenda proposal from authority.

**Threat:** problem-finding, LBD, anomaly discovery, portfolio optimization and open-ended goal search.

**Recommendation:** high-risk/high-value empirical paper; derive expert-owned opportunity benchmark before architecture claims.

### B4 — V2-C09 Performative Scientific Evaluation

**Value:** evaluation can change agents, institutions and data-generating processes.

**Threat:** performative prediction, mechanism design, Lucas critique and strategic classification are mature.

**Recommendation:** likely a scientific-governance specialization rather than general theory. Merge into C04/C07 unless a distinct scientific-evaluation theorem/benchmark survives.

## Tier C — resource or component papers unless separation emerges

### C1 — V2-C03 Machine-Executable Donor Reduction

**Likely role:** research infrastructure/resource paper jointly supporting C01/C02.

**Standalone condition:** machine system reproduces donor-native decisions, remote-parent discovery, counter-probe generation and expert claim contraction at strong fidelity and cost.

**Default recommendation:** merge into C01 plus release schemas/benchmark separately.

### C2 — V2-C08 Reticulate Scientific Provenance

**Likely role:** component of comparability, dependence and reopening.

**Standalone condition:** component-level multi-parent provenance changes certificate transport/reopening decisions beyond a general provenance graph and ordinary dependency analysis.

**Default recommendation:** keep as a focused study; defer paper identity.
