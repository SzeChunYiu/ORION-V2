## Proposed object

AI and hybrid research systems increasingly participate in multi-step research processes: literature search, hypothesis/model proposal, experiment or computation selection, tool/instrument execution, analysis, revision and scientific output.

The proposed object of study is **not the AI model or agent alone**. It is the controlled transition from one **scientific commitment state** to another inside a bounded research episode.

A transition may depend on:

- problem/criterion identity;
- current claims, alternatives and uncertainty;
- unresolved scientific obligations;
- admissible actions and resources;
- evidence identity, provenance and dependence;
- relation/transport assumptions across representations or contexts;
- verification/validation/evaluator identity;
- prior transition history;
- externally supplied authority or governance constraints.

The working name for the proposed research programme is:

**Machine Epistemics — the control science of AI-driven discovery.**

## Four proposed control problems

1. **Observe the epistemic state** — Which claims, alternatives, failure causes, evidence dependencies, coverage gaps and non-identifiabilities are distinguishable under available probes?
2. **Control and transport scientific transitions** — Which commitments may change or survive model, representation, population, measurement or context changes, under which assumptions and uncertainty?
3. **Assure evidence and authority** — Which support is independent/valid, which evaluators remain stable, and what externally supplied authority boundary constrains adoption?
4. **Govern escalation and closure** — When is local repair sufficient, when must the action/model/representation space change, and what bounded evidence licenses stopping?

## Examples of candidate composition gaps

These examples are hypotheses, not established field laws.

### Provenance → scientific validity

An execution/data lineage may be complete while the scientific inference remains invalid. Provenance is necessary for traceability/replay but is not itself a correctness criterion.

### Relation → scientific reuse

An abstraction, causal transport relation or measurement linking rule may preserve one decision but not another. Reuse therefore depends on the scientific question/context and may require selective revalidation.

### Evidence → authority-bounded adoption

A computation, experiment or consensus may support a claim without granting a machine or institution permission to adopt/deploy/publish it. Authority is externally supplied, not inferred from evidential confidence.

### Local route stop → scientific closure

An information-retrieval/systematic-review route may stop efficiently while the wider scientific search remains incomplete because other routes, representations or experiments were censored or unavailable.

### Local repair → search-space escalation

A failure may require another action inside the current model, or a new model/measurement/representation/problem formulation. A field-level hypothesis is that scientific constraints support measurable minimum-sufficient escalation rather than arbitrary “be more creative” jumps.

## Strongest field falsifier

The field claim should be rejected or contracted if an information-matched composition of mature parent methods makes the same protected scientific-transition decisions across materially different domains without a nontrivial residual.

Valid negative terminals include:

- existing parent field sufficient;
- integration engineering only;
- domain-specific methods with no general field;
- working label misleading/redundant even if some scientific results survive;
- insufficient evidence to demarcate.
