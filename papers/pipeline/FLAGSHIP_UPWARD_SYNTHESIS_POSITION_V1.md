# Flagship Position — From Parent Density to Absorptive Higher-Order Theory V1

**Status:** editorial/theoretical position for the next post-saturation flagship rewrite. It does not authorize another manuscript version while the synthesis gate remains blocked.

## 1. Revised field posture

Machine Epistemics should not defend itself by trying to find an intellectual territory with no nearby work. A scientifically important new field may have many close parents because it arises where those parents meet.

The correct flagship posture is:

> Machine Epistemics aims to absorb the valid theories and methods governing machine-mediated inquiry, preserve their native strengths, formalize their relations and build a higher-order science of scientific-state change that generates new consequences and improves decisions beyond the strongest parent federation.

This is a stronger and more ambitious claim than “there is an unowned integration gap”. It also carries a stronger burden of evidence.

## 2. Parent literature in the paper

The parent section should perform four functions:

1. **credit** — identify who owns each native mechanism;
2. **recover** — state the parent's object, assumptions and strongest results;
3. **locate the unresolved relation** — explain which cross-field question remains;
4. **define the lower bound** — construct the strongest federation the higher theory must exceed.

The paper should not treat citations as defensive concessions. They are the building blocks of the upward theory.

## 3. Proposed public argument

### Move 1 — Scientific processes have become executable systems

This changes the unit of evaluation from isolated model outputs to sequences of state-changing scientific actions.

### Move 2 — The relevant knowledge is already distributed across mature sciences

Formal epistemology, control, metareasoning, causal inference, measurement, statistics, assurance, cognition, social epistemology and scientific practice each govern different parts of the problem.

### Move 3 — Their relations are scientifically unresolved

Examples:

- execution identity versus evidential support;
- relation/transport certificate versus validity of reuse;
- dependence versus robustness and assurance;
- self-monitoring versus independent calibration;
- surprise versus scientific opportunity;
- machine-native representation versus human-facing evidence;
- scientific support versus institutional authority;
- local route stopping versus global scientific closure.

### Move 4 — Machine Epistemics proposes an absorptive theory

Native parents remain as recoverable views. Typed bridges and candidate transition laws explain how scientific commitments may move among them.

### Move 5 — The theory must generate

The flagship should state prospective consequences or research programmes that distinguish the higher theory from the parent federation.

### Move 6 — The theory must dominate, not merely encompass

Parent fidelity, protected decision quality, generativity, cross-domain scope, integration, robustness, efficiency, auditability and extensibility are evaluated under non-compensatory gates.

### Move 7 — The field can fail constructively

Possible terminals include absorptive supertheory, interfield theory, parent federation, domain federation, deceptive unification and `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## 6. Candidate abstract seed — not manuscript text

Artificial intelligence is turning scientific inquiry into an executable, state-changing process. The knowledge required to govern that process already exists in fragments across formal epistemology, control, statistics, causal inference, measurement, assurance, cognitive science, social epistemology and scientific practice. The central problem is therefore not the absence of parent science, but the absence of a tested higher-order theory explaining how these forms of evidence, action, representation, validation and authority interact when machines participate across an entire research loop. We propose Machine Epistemics as an absorptive field hypothesis: native parent theories remain recoverable within a typed federation, while bridge and transition laws aim to generate new cross-field predictions, failure boundaries and decision improvements beyond the strongest parent composition. We define the evidence required for that upward claim and the conditions under which it must contract to an interfield integration, parent federation or domain-specific science.

This paragraph is only an argument seed. It cannot be promoted until the foundation synthesis and evidence gates are satisfied.

## 7. Nearest-work rhetoric to avoid

Do not write:

- “Unlike all prior work...” when the contribution is integration;
- “No existing field considers...” without a bounded search;
- “Parents only address pieces...” unless their strongest composition has been reconstructed;
- “The unified framework is superior...” without the dominance protocol;
- “Human reasoning is limited...” as a warrant for machine-native claims;
- “A new field is necessary...” before prospective evidence.

Prefer:

- “The parent sciences supply...”;
- “The unresolved relation is...”;
- “The higher theory must recover...”;
- “The distinctive prediction is...”;
- “The strongest lower bound is...”;
- “The field hypothesis contracts if...”.

## 8. Paper portfolio effect

No new standalone paper is admitted by this reframing.

- P-A/P-B construct the absorption and bridge layer.
- P-C/P-D test cross-parent laws and decision value.
- P-E tests prospective generativity.
- P-F tests machine-native implementation freedom and value.
- The flagship explains the higher-order theory only after the specialist evidence and synthesis are sufficient.

If F2 fails, these papers can still survive locally under their native scientific contributions.
