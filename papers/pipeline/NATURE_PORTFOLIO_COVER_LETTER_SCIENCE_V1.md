# FLAGSHIP — Nature Machine Intelligence Perspective

> Dear Editors,
>
> We submit **“Machine Epistemics: Toward a Science of AI-Driven Inquiry and Scientific Change”** as a Perspective. AI research systems increasingly connect literature search, modelling, experimentation, tool use and revision, while the scientific validity of those transitions is distributed across mature fields including statistics, causal inference, metareasoning, measurement, formal methods, assurance, security and scientific practice. The manuscript asks whether the relationships among these local theories form a falsifiable higher-order research object rather than declaring a new field by definition.
>
> The Perspective makes three bounded contributions. First, it reconstructs four competing foundations: strongest parent federation, selective interfield bridge theories, an absorptive transition theory and plural domain federation. Second, it organizes the open problem as an ecology of knowledge, transfer, representation, method, action, evidence and agenda transitions, with candidate cross-transition validity constraints explicitly labeled hypotheses. Third, it gives a prospective programme—parent recovery, protected cross-domain decisions, generativity, resource adjustment and external demarcation—that can support or contract the field hypothesis.
>
> We believe this is suitable for Nature Machine Intelligence because it addresses the scientific methodology of emerging AI-driven inquiry while treating autonomous-science systems and contrary parent theories directly. The manuscript contains no claim that Machine Epistemics is already a founded field and no unpublished efficacy result is used to manufacture such a claim.
