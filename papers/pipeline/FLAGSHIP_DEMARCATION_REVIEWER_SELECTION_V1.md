## 2. Selection criteria (all must hold)

1. **Native ownership of a parent.** The reviewer has published primary work in at least one parent discipline that V21 concedes (§4 of the packet). The strongest reviewers are those the programme would have to *lose to*.
2. **No prior involvement.** No contribution to the framework, manuscript, any ORION repository, or any study design listed in packet §10; no prior correspondence about the programme's content.
3. **No access to internal outcomes.** Has not seen AH20 outcomes, ME-X4 development results, H-EXT-1 results, simulated reviews or the internal clean-room read. Outcome-blindness is the packet's core property.
4. **No shared dependence across the panel.** The three selected reviewers must not be current co-authors, supervisor/student, same research group, or co-chairs of the same standard; must not share a model, dataset, tool or adjudication process material to their judgement. Clusters are marked in §4 so that at most one member of each cluster is selected.
5. **Able to answer in writing in 2–3 hours** and willing to end with one of the seven terminals, including the negative ones.
6. **Hostile is fine; advocacy is disclosed.** Being cited in V21 is expected for parent owners and is a disclosure, not a disqualification. Strong public advocacy for a competing programme (for example, a named autonomous-science platform or a competing "epistemic control" architecture) is disclosed and, if present, balanced by lens rather than excluded.
7. **Diversity of institution and geography across the panel** where the above allow it.

## 3. Independence and conflict declaration (requested from every reviewer, packet §14)

Each reviewer records, before reading the manuscript: lens; institution/role (optional); prior involvement (none expected); exposure to internal outcomes (none expected); shared dependence with other panel members; citation in the V21 bibliography; conflicts or prior advocacy/opposition relevant to the label or to a competing programme.

The operator records, on the panel sheet: that no reviewer received anything beyond the manuscript, its figures and the packet; the date each reviewer received the packet; and that no outcome was disclosed to any reviewer before their written answers to D1–D18 were received. Post-outcome addenda, if ever supplied, are sent only after the pre-outcome answers are on file.

Excluded by construction: anyone who has committed to `SzeChunYiu/ORION-V2` or `SzeChunYiu/ORION-paper`; anyone who has read any AH20/ME-X/H-EXT result file; any collaborator of the operator on this programme.
