**Status:** `RESOLVED_AS_FALSIFIABLE_FIELD_HYPOTHESIS`.

**Concern:** identity binding, evaluator sensitivity and local deference may be sensible engineering principles rather than field laws.

**Resolution:** manuscript labels them hypotheses and requires protected cross-transition consequences beyond parent composition. Generic good-practice restatement does not found the field.

**Status:** `RESOLVED_BY_CLAIM_CEILING`.

## F-R3 — machine-native cognition/external witness boundary too normative

**Resolution:** framed as a programme boundary rather than established theorem; machines may use non-human internal processes, while adoption of scientific transitions requires externally checkable consequences proportional to the claim.

**Status:** `RESOLVED_BY_SCOPE`.
