# Flagship and Paper Programme — Foundation Saturation Gate V2 Delta

**Status:** cumulative delta to `FLAGSHIP_FOUNDATION_SATURATION_GATE_V1.md`. It records the parent pressure from Foundation Saturation Pass 02 and keeps all final manuscript promotion blocked.

## 1. Why the gate tightens

Pass 02 recovered four mature remote parent lanes:

- distributed consensus and replicated state machines;
- abstract/structured defeasible argumentation;
- decision making under deep uncertainty;
- evidence grading and evidence-to-decision frameworks.

The existence of these lanes is itself evidence that a polished conceptual manuscript can still omit decisive ownership. Therefore no new flagship version should be produced merely to incorporate their citations. Their native mechanisms and strongest products must first enter the atlas, benchmark and contradiction surfaces.

## 2. Flagship obligations added

Before the final foundation rewrite, the flagship must answer:

1. Why is scientific-state agreement different from distributed consensus, replicated consistency and protocol validity?
2. What does the field add beyond assurance cases and formal argumentation semantics?
3. Which control claims assume ordinary probabilistic uncertainty, and which survive deep/model uncertainty?
4. How are evidence certainty, recommendation, contextual values and legitimate authority kept separate?
5. Which of these distinctions are universal propositions, domain interfaces or parent-owned implementation choices?

The manuscript may state these as open parent challenges. It may not yet promote answers to foundation laws.

## 3. Specialist-paper pressure

### P-A

Compare remote donor/minimal-envelope claims against distributed-state representations, formal argument structures and MDL/sufficiency parents. Agreement among search agents is not novelty evidence.

### P-B

Relation/transport receipts must preserve fault assumptions, argument semantics, deep-uncertainty scope and decision context where these are material. A relation certified under one pathway or value context may not authorize another.

### P-C

The strongest solver baseline now includes DMDU/adaptive-pathway policies and fault-tolerant distributed control where relevant. The paper must measure safe nontermination, graceful degradation, trigger timing and common-mode agent faults.

### P-D

The strongest assurance baseline now includes SACM/GSN/CAE plus defeasible argumentation and distributed-system dependence/fault models. Reviewer consensus and argument acceptance must remain distinct from scientific correctness.

### P-E

Opportunity/adoption recommendations must separate evidence certainty from values, resources, feasibility and agenda authority. Robust option preservation competes with one-shot expected-value ranking.

### P-F

Machine-native multi-agent strategies must disclose consensus/fault assumptions and compare against distributed-systems, automated-reasoning and DMDU parents. Nonhuman difference remains secondary to protected scientific value.

## 4. Atomic claim restrictions

No paper claim can move to `FOUNDATION_LAW` or `RESULT` while:

- its relevant strongest-parent product is only `SPECIFIED_NOT_BOUND`;
- its fault, argument, uncertainty or decision-context assumptions are unstated;
- a parent win/tie has not been allowed;
- naturalistic domain restoration is absent;
- the post-material no-new-coordinate counter is below three.
