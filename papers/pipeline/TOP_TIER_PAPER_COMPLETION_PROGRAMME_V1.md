# ORION-V2 Top-Tier Paper Completion Programme V1

**Canonical foundation issue:** #41  
**Review surface:** #36  
**Status:** binding completion contract for the flagship and P-A–P-F. It does not grant novelty, scientific correctness, field status, journal acceptance or submission readiness.

## 1. Objective

The portfolio is not complete when every draft has fluent prose. A paper is complete only when its central scientific claim has a frozen identity, the strongest parent federation has been given a fair implementation, decisive evidence has been produced under outcome-blind criteria, failures and costs are visible, and the manuscript has been contracted to one result-scale thesis suitable for its target venue.

The programme therefore separates:

`MANUSCRIPT_COMPLETE` — all non-outcome-dependent sections and result slots are finished;

`SCIENTIFICALLY_COMPLETE` — the frozen evidence package decides the thesis honestly, including parent wins or `CANNOT_CHECK`;

`SUBMISSION_READY_R4` — scientific completion plus journal-format, source, figure, reproducibility, disclosure and hostile-editor gates;

`PUBLISHED` — external editorial and peer-review outcome, never granted internally.

## 2. Completion cell

Every paper is reviewed by a small independent cell.

1. **Native-parent custodian** — reconstructs the strongest parent methods and prevents false novelty.
2. **Domain scientist** — restores native scientific semantics and identifies unrealistic fixtures.
3. **Formal/statistical methodologist** — freezes estimands, uncertainty, stopping, negative controls and falsifiers.
4. **Systems and efficiency reviewer** — measures compute, latency, memory, expert labour, implementation burden and new failure surface.
5. **Machine-native intelligence reviewer** — asks whether human-style process assumptions constrain the system unnecessarily and whether gains arise from genuine machine affordances.
6. **Epistemic-integrity and governance reviewer** — audits source identity, dependence, numerical/oracle validity, privacy, custody and authority.
7. **Hostile journal editor** — demands one broad-interest thesis, rejects portfolio inflation and attempts to contract the paper to a parent, resource or domain result.

No originating manuscript team is the sole semantic evaluator of its own decisive cases.

## 3. Shared paper-completion object

Each paper binds:

`PaperCompletionRecord = (`
`paper_id, thesis, explicit_nonclaim, target_archetype, parent_federation, protected_question, cases, primary_estimands, hard_failures, minimum_important_difference, resource_contract, independent_evaluator, result_slots, figure_spine, source_ledger, reproducibility_package, kill_or_merge_condition, current_level)`.

### Mandatory evidence states

- `OPEN` — not executed;
- `REFERENCE_ONLY` — constructed/known-answer semantics only;
- `PROTECTED_RESULT` — outcome-blind experiment executed;
- `INDEPENDENTLY_ADJUDICATED` — required native/semantic review complete;
- `REPLICATED_OR_TRANSFERRED` — second domain, epoch, site or implementation as required;
- `CANNOT_CHECK` — blocked evidence remains explicit.

No manuscript language may imply a stronger state.

## 4. Shared top-tier scientific gate

Every Article candidate requires:

1. one result-scale scientific thesis;
2. one explicit non-claim boundary;
3. strongest information- and resource-matched parent federation, not isolated convenience baselines;
4. prospective case, criterion, budget and analysis identity;
5. hard integrity gates that averages cannot compensate for;
6. simple and negative controls where ORION complexity should lose;
7. FULL/MINUS/PARENT/MERGED and interaction interventions where causal attribution is claimed;
8. compute, wall time, memory, human labour and implementation/failure-surface curves;
9. at least one remote or naturalistic domain beyond authored fixtures;
10. independent or blinded adjudication when exact scoring is impossible;
11. negative, tie, parent-win and `CANNOT_CHECK` outcomes retained;
12. complete source/code/data/configuration/receipt package where legally and ethically possible;
13. numerical, software-oracle, uncertainty-form, semantic-context and governance checks whenever material;
14. title, abstract and figures intelligible without ORION vocabulary;
15. hostile-editor review against significance, ownership and scope inflation.

## 5. Flagship completion contract

### Scientific role

Field-defining Perspective comparing four foundations:

- F0 strongest parent federation;
- F1 selective interfield theories;
- F2 absorptive supertheory;
- F3 domain federation without universal transition laws.

### Central thesis

Machine-mediated inquiry creates a scientifically important class of cross-parent state-transition problems; Machine Epistemics is justified as a higher-order field only if F2 preserves native parents, generates prospective consequences and improves protected decisions beyond F0.

### Explicit non-claim

The Perspective does not prove F2, claim civilizational knowledge saturation, prescribe human-like cognition, or derive legitimate authority from evidence.

### Decisive evidence

- frozen F0–F3 discriminator study;
- parent-recovery results;
- at least two materially different naturalistic domains;
- at least one prospectively frozen generative consequence;
- component and resource attribution;
- independent external demarcation review.

### Four-figure spine

1. executable scientific episode and cross-parent failures;
2. native parent federation with embeddings/recovery and typed bridges;
3. F0–F3 prospective discriminator and generativity design;
4. theory-dominance/contraction outcomes.

### Kill/contraction

If F0, F1 or F3 is sufficient, publish an integration Perspective with the corresponding title and field boundary; do not preserve F2 through rhetoric.

## 6. P-A completion contract — Structural Donor Discovery

### Thesis

Source-bound structural donor discovery plus conservative, decision-preserving reduction improves remote-parent recovery and false-novelty contraction beyond strongest retrieval, analogy, literature-based-discovery, expert and MDL/sufficiency parents.

### Non-claim

Structural similarity is not scientific equivalence, a practical donor does not automatically own a scientific inference, and failure to retrieve a parent is not proof of novelty.

### Decisive evidence

- hidden known-parent benchmark with lexical/semantic distance strata;
- false-analogy and native-verdict hard gates;
- at least two remote scientific domains withheld from signature construction;
- strongest-union/F0 search baseline under matched budget;
- expert donor-card reproduction;
- source/custody correctness and search-censorship terminals;
- resource and component attribution.

### Figure spine

1. native donor card and source/ownership graph;
2. hidden-parent benchmark and baseline arms;
3. recall–false-analogy–native-fidelity–cost frontier;
4. contraction outcomes and failure anatomy.

### Kill/merge

If gain is search budget, hidden expert labour or parent methods alone, contract to a benchmark/resource or parent-specific methods paper.

## 7. P-B completion contract — Context-Relative Relations and Transport

### Thesis

Typed relation selection, conservative composition and selective reopening preserve native scientific judgments across representations and epochs better than universal similarity metrics or strongest isolated relation parents.

### Non-claim

No universal metric, equivalence relation or ontology is asserted; scientific and authority transport remain different.

### Decisive evidence

- known-answer suites for causal, measurement, abstraction, behavioural, procedural, numerical/uncertainty and authority relations;
- exact/approximate composition theorems or counterexamples;
- downstream decision-stability tests;
- calibration/evaluator iteration and boundary-interface cases;
- competence transport and tacit-interface controls;
- two naturalistic representation changes in materially different sciences;
- strongest parent product and native expert adjudication.

### Figure spine

1. typed relation family and context registration;
2. composition/obstruction and selective reopening;
3. known-answer plus naturalistic cross-domain results;
4. approximation, uncertainty, cost and native-fidelity failures.

### Kill/merge

If the interface wraps mature relation theories without changing a protected decision, publish as an interoperability resource or return claims to parents.

## 8. P-C completion contract — Obligation-Driven Scientific Control

### Thesis

A scientific controller using explicit state/obligations, calibrated self-models, plural alternatives, minimum sufficient escalation and typed exploratory/constructive actions improves justified terminals and resilience beyond strongest adaptive parent solvers without harming simple cases.

### Non-claim

The architecture is not a universal thinking method and does not require human-like internal reasoning.

### Decisive evidence

- exact V1 non-regression;
- strongest F0 adaptive controller and simple direct controls;
- hidden multiple-fault/mechanistic cases;
- exploratory/constructive/calibrative/replicative action cases;
- numerical/oracle/semantic/data-cascade diagnosis;
- self-model calibration and independent reflection controls;
- minimum escalation, false/missed escalation, stopping and resilience;
- fresh expert-adjudicated tasks in at least two scientific families;
- FULL/MINUS/PARENT/MERGED, interaction and resource curves.

### Figure spine

1. scientific state, obligations and action-mode control loop;
2. strongest-parent and intervention design;
3. justified-terminal/noncompensatory-failure/resource frontier;
4. causal component and failure attribution.

### Kill/merge

If F0 or a simpler controller matches the scientific frontier, report parent sufficiency or contextual activation rather than a general architecture advance.

## 9. P-D completion contract — Dependence-Aware Evidence and Dynamic Evaluation

### Thesis

Support-family, dependence, argument, test/oracle adequacy and evaluator-response modelling prevents false corroboration and false authority while preserving genuine independent support better than provenance, vote count and strongest assurance/evidence parents.

### Non-claim

Agreement, provenance, argument acceptability or high confidence does not establish scientific truth or legitimate authority.

### Decisive evidence

- duplicate/shared-source and genuine-independent positive controls;
- assumption-level dependence and discordance;
- criticism uptake versus review theatre;
- assurance plus defeasible-argumentation parent product;
- computational/oracle/test sensitivity failures;
- performative and adaptive-adversary evaluator cases;
- governed-data and participation/authority boundaries;
- support-family selective reopening;
- blinded semantic adjudication and calibration;
- cost and over-conservatism controls.

### Figure spine

1. evidence/support/argument/dependence hypergraph;
2. naive versus parent versus P-D assurance designs;
3. false corroboration, calibration, reopening and authority outcomes;
4. dynamic evaluator and failure/overhead anatomy.

### Kill/merge

If mature dependence, assurance, argumentation and governance products reproduce the decisions, contract to a traceability/evaluation resource or merge with the flagship.

## 10. P-E completion contract — Opportunity Discovery

### Thesis

Source-bound problem finding and typed encounter recognition can generate prospectively useful, testable research opportunities beyond experts and strongest anomaly/LBD/active-design/open-ended parents.

### Non-claim

Novelty, surprise and interestingness are not scientific value or agenda authority.

### Decisive evidence

- historical time-sliced and genuinely prospective information cutoffs;
- expert, LBD, anomaly, active-design, curiosity/open-ended and portfolio baselines;
- known-problem, noise, unfalsifiable and hindsight-story controls;
- frozen discriminators and later useful-action/result follow-up;
- analysis/artifact alternative explanations;
- independent agenda/adjudication and resource accounting.

### Figure spine

1. opportunity record and encounter-to-discriminator pipeline;
2. prospective information cutoff and arms;
3. value/duplicate/noise/authority outcomes;
4. deferred option value and failure analysis.

### Kill/merge

Merge into P-C unless independently measured prospective value survives strongest parents.

## 11. P-F completion contract — Machine-Native Scientific Intelligence

### Thesis

Machine-native representations, memories, search topologies or control policies can improve protected scientific transitions beyond human-mimetic, strongest-parent and hybrid systems, with gains causally attributable to substrate-relative mechanisms rather than compute, opacity or leakage.

### Non-claim

Difference from humans is not superiority, human thinking is not the constitutional target, and opacity is not scientific authority.

### Decisive evidence

- human-positive, human-neutral and human-trap strata;
- matched human-mimetic, machine-native, hybrid, F0 and simple arms;
- inductive-bias declarations and resource curves;
- non-linguistic internal representations with claim-sufficient external witnesses;
- numerical/oracle/semantic/security/governance hostile controls;
- mechanism ablations and second-domain transfer;
- cases where humans or simpler parents correctly win.

### Figure spine

1. substrate-relative design space and external witness boundary;
2. matched arms and problem strata;
3. quality–resource–auditability frontier;
4. mechanism attribution, transfer and human/parent win cases.

### Kill/merge

Merge into P-C/flagship unless a distinct causal machine-native gain survives resource matching and cross-domain restoration.

## 12. Candidate P-G admission rule

A new paper may enter only when a distinct formal or protected empirical residual cannot be carried cleanly by P-A–P-F or the flagship.

The leading candidate is **Scientific Warrant Factorization**: a formal and empirical theory of how source, semantic interpretation, model adequacy, computational validity, test/oracle adequacy, dependence, transport and authority jointly bound a scientific transition. It remains a contingent prospectus until it produces either:

- a nontrivial bridge/composition/impossibility theorem plus countermodels; or
- a protected cross-domain result that changes decisions beyond F0 assurance products.

Without this evidence it remains a flagship/P-D proposition, not a standalone paper.

## 13. Recursive writing protocol

Apply the academic-paper pipeline in cycles:

1. **claim atomization** — one proposition per ledger row;
2. **parent/source binding** — native ownership and exact evidence source;
3. **evidence-unit assignment** — theorem, table, figure, experiment or explicit open result;
4. **main-text contraction** — retain only claims necessary for the thesis;
5. **hostile reviewer pass** — novelty, correctness, significance, causality, cost and scope;
6. **surface audit** — abstract, title, terminology, figures, references and target format;
7. **reopen on material defect** — no stylistic patch can close a scientific issue.

A new manuscript version is justified only by changed evidence, parent ownership, theory identity, reviewer finding, figure/evidence chain or valid claim contraction.

## 14. Portfolio sequencing

Scientific sequencing—not paper prestige—controls effort:

1. finish F0 binding and parent-recovery suites;
2. execute constructed and protected F0–F3 discriminators;
3. advance P-B/P-D formal and known-answer results;
4. execute P-C/P-A cross-domain studies;
5. decide P-E/P-F survival;
6. freeze specialist evidence;
7. rewrite the flagship from surviving results;
8. run R4 source, figure, reproducibility and hostile-editor gates;
9. prepare Tier-1 and frozen-science Tier-2 packages.
