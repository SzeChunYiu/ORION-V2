# Recursive Scientific Development — Standalone Paper Admission Gate V1

**Programme:** ORION-V2 / SD10–SD80  
**Current status:** not admitted as a standalone paper.  
**Purpose:** distinguish successful corpus/infrastructure construction from a publishable meta-scientific thesis.

## 1. Current evidence

SD10 has executed a bounded, multi-mode source/corpus pipeline with explicit bias/missingness auditing.

This supports statements such as:

- a reproducible corpus of scientific-development episodes can be built from heterogeneous lawful source modes;
- source modes have different observability and selection biases;
- negative/failed/partial trajectories are systematically harder to observe than published successful science;
- later operator/meta-policy claims must account for these biases.

It does **not** support:

- a discovered universal scientific-development operator;
- a general meta-policy for doing science;
- a recursive stability law;
- superiority over science-of-science/metascience parents;
- a standalone top-tier paper by itself.

## 2. Candidate future thesis classes

A separate article can be admitted only if one of the following result classes survives strongest-parent reduction.

### Thesis A — development operator residual

Population-mined operator(s) improve held-out reconstruction, prediction or prospective scientific decisions across fields/epochs beyond strongest science-of-science/process-mining/knowledge-discovery controls.

### Thesis B — meta-policy residual

A policy selecting/sequencing development operators improves protected prospective scientific decisions under matched resources beyond parent heuristics and domain-local baselines.

### Thesis C — negative/failure learning residual

Including failed, abandoned or partial trajectories materially changes operator/policy validity and prevents errors that success-only learning does not.

### Thesis D — recursive abstraction residual

A higher-order principle predicts/intervenes on held-out scientific development beyond lower-level operators, with field/epoch holdout and no critical semantic loss.

### Thesis E — recursive stability candidate

After repeated abstraction under prospectively frozen tests, additional levels cease adding material protected residual across new domains/epochs. This can support a **stability candidate**, never an ultimate law of science.

## 3. Mandatory parent product

Before admission, reconstruct and compare against the strongest applicable composition of:

- science of science;
- metascience;
- process mining / workflow mining;
- innovation/search/exploration-exploitation literature;
- history/philosophy/sociology of scientific change;
- case-based reasoning/analogy;
- program/strategy induction;
- meta-learning / metareasoning;
- causal/process discovery where applicable;
- bibliometrics and team-science models.

A result that is predicted by citation/field/institution signals alone is not a Machine-Epistemics development-operator residual.

## 4. Mandatory anti-bias controls

- field holdout;
- epoch holdout;
- venue/institution holdout where feasible;
- success-only versus success+failure corpus;
- missing negative trajectory sensitivity;
- publication/citation prestige ablation;
- source-mode ablation;
- label leakage audit;
- naturalistic/prospective decision test;
- resource cost.

## 5. Outcome authority

Candidate operators/principles do not receive normative authority because they describe historical science.

A historically common strategy can be:

- scientifically wrong;
- socially biased;
- obsolete under new tools;
- effective only in one field;
- selected by publication incentives rather than epistemic value.

The paper must distinguish descriptive prevalence, predictive value and normative scientific recommendation.
