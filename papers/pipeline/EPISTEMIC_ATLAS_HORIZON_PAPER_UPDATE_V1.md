# Epistemic Atlas / Horizon Minimum-Change Paper Update V1

**Status:** manuscript-scope update receipt under the bound `academic-paper-skills` v1.9.0 workflow. This update changes conceptual synthesis and demarcation, not any frozen primary endpoint, comparator regime, Results order or empirical result.

## Trigger

A local-to-global omission test asked how Machine Epistemics can discuss a broader space of possible epistemic mechanisms when every observed human, non-human, collective, evolutionary or machine intelligence supplies only local evidence.

The initial phrase `global epistemic space` was judged too strong if read as an observable/enumerable totality. The hardened public object is an **expanding epistemic atlas with an explicit epistemic horizon**.

## Academic-paper-skill decisions

### Paper archetype

Flagship remains:

```text
PRIMARY = Perspective / emerging-field research agenda
SECONDARY = theory synthesis / demarcation
```

The new material is conceptual/formal synthesis, not an empirical study result.

### Reader workflow

The update serves two reader questions that were previously under-explained:

1. **Field identity:** how does Machine Epistemics differ from epistemology, formal epistemology, computational/formal learning epistemology, social epistemology and AI epistemology?
2. **Meaning/boundary:** what can `global` mean when all observed intelligence is local?

### Explanatory sufficiency

The flagship now supplies:

- identity of the parent epistemology families;
- why the Machine-Epistemics residual is system/control rather than replacement epistemology;
- identity of the atlas and horizon objects;
- local-to-global/gluing logic;
- explicit non-claim of total epistemic-space recovery;
- failure/obstruction and strongest-parent boundaries.

### Display decision

No new main figure is required for this revision. The atlas/horizon idea is currently best served by prose plus the existing taxonomy structure because there is no empirical visual evidence. A later conceptual diagram may be useful only if it encodes real distinctions among:

```text
local chart -> overlap/transport -> matching family -> global witness/obstruction
probe family -> observational partition -> horizon refinement
```

A decorative “map of intelligence” figure is rejected.
