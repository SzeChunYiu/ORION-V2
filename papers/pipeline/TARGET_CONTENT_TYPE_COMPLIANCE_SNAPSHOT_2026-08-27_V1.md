# ORION-V2 Paper Targets — Exact Content-Type Planning Snapshot

**Checked:** 27 August 2026

**Purpose:** submission-planning constraints only. Live target guidance must be re-checked immediately before submission; a local snapshot never outranks the current official journal page.

## Nature Machine Intelligence — Article

Official current content-type page checked: `https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/content`.

Current planning constraints:

- substantial novel research study with a complex story, often involving several techniques/approaches;
- main text up to **3,500 words**, excluding abstract, Methods, references and figure legends;
- unreferenced abstract up to **150 words**;
- up to **6 display items** (figures and/or tables);
- article flow: unheaded Introduction, Results, Discussion, Methods;
- Results and Methods use topical subheadings; Discussion does not use subheadings;
- roughly **50 references** as a guideline;
- peer reviewed; supplementary information allowed.

Applies to current Tier-1 targets P-A, P-C and P-D if they survive as Articles.

### Manuscript-development consequence

A protocol-like document cannot be made NMI-Article-ready by compression. The decisive protected results, strongest baselines, main figures and Methods/reproducibility package must exist first.

## Nature Machine Intelligence — Perspective

Official current content-type page checked: `https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/content`.

Current planning constraints:

- scholarly discussion of a fast-moving or emerging topic;
- original, forward-looking vision;
- more thought-provoking/speculative and potentially narrower than a Review, while remaining balanced;
- **3,000–4,000 words** main text;
- up to roughly **100 references** (exceptions possible);
- peer reviewed;
- non-primary content type: should not include original previously unpublished research findings and may include only minimal new supporting data.

Applies to the Machine Epistemics flagship.

### Manuscript-development consequence

The flagship must stand on published literature, conceptual synthesis and a falsifiable research agenda. Unpublished ORION-V2 parity, benchmark or solver outcomes cannot be used as field evidence in this Perspective. If later primary evidence is scientifically central, cite the separately published/accepted primary work or reconsider article type/timing rather than leaking unpublished results into the Perspective.

## Nature Computational Science — Article / Perspective resolution

Official current content-type page checked: `https://www.nature.com/natcomputsci/content`.

Current target candidates:

- P-B — planned as Article if formal/computational results survive;
- P-E — planned as Article only if prospective results survive.

The exact Article rules must be re-checked at the submission stage. The current official page also distinguishes Review and Perspective formats; a Perspective is scholarly primary-literature discussion appropriate to narrower/speculative or viewpoint-driven topics rather than a replacement for missing Article results.

### Manuscript-development consequence

P-B or P-E may not switch from Article to Perspective merely because protected primary results fail. A format switch is allowed only if the surviving scientific contribution is genuinely a literature-grounded Perspective with its own bounded thesis and no hidden unpublished-primary-result dependency.

## npj Artificial Intelligence — Article / Perspective / Review

Official current content-type page checked: `https://www.nature.com/npjai/for-authors-and-referees/about/content-types`.

Current fallback use:

- P-C / P-D / P-E — Article fallback where the primary study survives but is not accepted by the Tier-1 target;
- Machine Epistemics flagship — Perspective or Review fallback depending final balance/thesis.

Current Article page states:

- Introduction followed by Results, Discussion, Methods and Data Availability;
- topical subheadings in Results and Methods;
- up to **10 display items**;
- around **60 references** as a guideline;
- peer reviewed.

The exact Perspective/Review rules must be checked again immediately before any fallback transfer.

### Fallback integrity rule

Venue rejection may justify structural/length/style adaptation but **not**:

- deleting negative or parent-tie results;
- relaxing protected criteria;
- replacing the strongest baseline with an easier one;
- upgrading an unresolved claim;
- altering the data split, endpoint or analysis after seeing outcomes without a disclosed new study identity.
