# P-A — Discovering Remote Scientific Structure Without Losing Native Meaning

## Question

Can a machine find a scientifically relevant donor whose vocabulary/topic is remote from the target while preserving the donor's native judgments and rejecting false transfer?

## Bounded answer sought

The paper tests a donor-discovery process that separates candidate retrieval, native reconstruction, structural alignment, hostile challenge and final scientific disposition. It earns an independent result only if protected cases show value beyond the strongest retrieval/analogy/formal/expert parent composition without critical false analogy or native-fidelity loss.

## Decisive evidence required

- hidden remote-donor recovery on case-level protected tasks;
- native known-answer fidelity including negative cases;
- target-consequence fidelity;
- false-analogy/counter-probe outcomes;
- correct parent-sufficiency/novelty contraction;
- matched-resource comparison and heterogeneity.

## Strongest alternative

Current scientific analogy/LBD/retrieval systems plus formal/native parents already solve the task; the integrated workflow is only packaging.

## Boundary

No claim that relational analogy, LBD, anti-unification, FCA, MDL or remote scientific analogy is new.

## Nature Machine Intelligence triage condition

A protected result must show a decision-relevant residual across materially different task families, not merely improved retrieval recall.

# P-B — Scientific Relations Are Context-Relative

## Question

Can one typed coordination interface safely manage heterogeneous notions of equivalence/transport and improve protected reuse or selective-reopening decisions beyond calling the strongest parent theories separately?

## Bounded answer sought

The interface exposes relation family, context, direction, witness, loss, obstruction and revalidation consequence while deferring local mathematics to the relevant parent. The four manuscript propositions are supporting semantics, not the novelty route.

## Decisive evidence required

- native relation judgment preservation;
- false-exact/missed-valid reuse outcomes;
- approximate/incomparable/CANNOT_CHECK handling;
- selective reopening decisions;
- comparison with strongest native parent or parent federation;
- interface/resource cost.

## Strongest alternative

The interface is an ontology/schema and every protected decision can be made by routing directly to the correct parent at equal or lower cost.

## Boundary

No universal relation calculus; no generic category-theory, causal-transport, measurement-invariance or equivalence novelty.

## Nature Computational Science triage condition

Standalone Article status requires protected decision value or a nontrivial cross-parent formal consequence. Otherwise route to methods/resource/merge.

# P-C — Scientific Control by Minimum Sufficient Intervention

## Question

Can a scientific agent choose the smallest sufficient intervention for the current blocker, preserve prior capability, and avoid both false completion and unnecessary escalation better than strong simple/adaptive parent controllers?

## Bounded answer sought

The paper studies scientific control over a partial preorder of admissible interventions rather than a mandatory pipeline. A contextual regime policy may choose SIMPLE/F0/F1/F2/ABSTAIN. No F2 superiority is assumed.

## Decisive evidence required

- independent semantic V1 parity;
- justified terminal correctness across protected task families;
- critical false-completion and CANNOT_CHECK calibration;
- minimum-sufficient intervention correctness;
- under/over-escalation;
- resource curves;
- component/drag study;
- strongest direct/simple/F0/adaptive comparisons.

## Strongest alternative

Modern adaptive agents or metareasoning/POMDP-style control already solve the routing problem; full F2 adds overhead without protected benefit.

## Boundary

No universal J0–J8 ladder, no claim that all AI-scientist systems are rigid pipelines, and no credit for additional tool use or compute itself.

## Nature Machine Intelligence triage condition

Article survives only if protected decision quality and/or minimum-sufficient control is materially better than the strongest fair comparator while parity and critical-error gates pass.

# P-D — Dependence-Aware Evidence and Dynamic Evaluation

## Question

Does representing evidence dependence together with evaluator sensitivity and environment response improve validity/reopening decisions beyond mature dependence/provenance and performative-evaluation methods applied separately?

## Bounded answer sought

The paper tests two established parent lanes and a joint state/interface only when their interaction changes a protected decision.

## Decisive evidence required

- false-corroboration/calibration under known dependence;
- unknown/misspecified dependence controls;
- preservation of genuinely independent support;
- stable-environment negative controls;
- response/performative evaluator cases;
- joint versus separate-parent decision comparison;
- resource/overconservatism cost.

## Strongest alternative

The dependence and performativity problems are separable. Their Cartesian combination adds no scientific value and should not be one paper.

## Boundary

No blanket distrust of correlated evidence, no new generic performative-prediction theory, and no claim that consensus equals independence or truth.

## Nature Machine Intelligence triage condition

The unified Article requires a protected interaction/joint decision residual. If separate parents match it, split/contract according to the frozen terminal.

# Shared editorial rule

No paper receives a top-tier target solely because the manuscript is polished. If the protected result closes as parent sufficiency, null, adverse, or CANNOT_CHECK, the paper follows its predeclared contraction/merger/alternate-content-type branch.
