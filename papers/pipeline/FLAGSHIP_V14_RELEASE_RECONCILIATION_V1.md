## Current official target check

Nature Machine Intelligence Perspective guidance was rechecked on 2026-08-30. The current public format specifies approximately 3,000--4,000 main-text words and up to 100 references; Perspectives are peer reviewed and are non-primary content, so substantial unpublished original research should not be used to carry the article.

V14 was therefore written as a selective synthesis. EL10/EL20 evidence is used to constrain programme claims, not as a new primary-results story.
