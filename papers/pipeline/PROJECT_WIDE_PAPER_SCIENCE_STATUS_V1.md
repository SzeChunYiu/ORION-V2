# Current 2026 venue-policy state

Nature Portfolio current policy treats substantial interpretive/methodological AI use as requiring transparent human-led verification/accountability and prohibits opaque substitution of AI for scholarly judgment.

Nature Machine Intelligence and Nature Computational Science currently do **not** accept presubmission enquiries.

Current Nature Article formats for NMI/NCS use:

```text
main text <= 3,500 words
abstract <= 150 words
<= 6 main display items
~50 references guideline
Methods outside main-text word count
```

Current npj Artificial Intelligence Article guideline:

```text
main text typically <= 4,000–4,500 words
abstract ~150 words
<=10 display items
~60 references guideline
```

Policies must be refreshed immediately before actual filing.
