# P-A — Finding the Same Scientific Problem in Different Worlds

## Editorial triage

`CURRENT_CLAIMS_NOT_ESTABLISHED`

The manuscript is a strong study protocol/method hypothesis, but Nature Machine Intelligence Article review would require the protected comparative evidence that the draft currently describes only prospectively.

## Must-address concerns

### PA-R1-01 — hidden human work can manufacture the apparent machine contribution

- class: `technical_blocker`
- challenged claim: machine-assisted structural donor discovery/reduction adds value beyond strongest parent search/reduction methods
- concern: native theory reconstruction, donor mapping, counter-probe validation and target adaptation may depend on unmeasured expert labour. A system can appear to outperform retrieval baselines if experts silently perform the hard semantic work.
- resolution test: freeze the human-intervention taxonomy and time/decision budget; report machine-only, expert-only, parent-composed and integrated arms; ensure every expert action is logged and budgeted.
- cheapest repair now: protocol hardening; no new scientific result can be written yet.

### PA-R1-02 — protected benchmark unit and leakage boundary are underspecified

- class: `technical_blocker`
- challenged claim: remote-parent recall and false-analogy performance can be compared fairly
- concern: paper/domain/problem/canonical-solution units, source date, corpus access, donor visibility and annotation leakage must be fixed prospectively.
- resolution test: define unit of observation/inference, hidden-parent construction, training/development/held-out split, source cutoff and adjudication protocol.

### PA-R2-01 — integrated novelty is vulnerable to strongest-union subsumption

- class: `publication_criteria_blocker`
- challenged claim: the integrated donor-faithful pipeline is a distinct scientific method contribution
- concern: literature-based discovery + structure mapping/TRIZ + formal abstraction + human domain reconstruction may already yield the same protected decisions.
- resolution test: build and allow the strongest parent-composed baseline the same source access and expert budget; paper survives only on a protected residual.

### PA-R2-02 — novelty contraction is valuable but may be a resource contribution rather than an Article

- class: `claim_recalibration`
- concern: if remote recall does not improve but the system reliably prevents false novelty/analogy, the result may support a methods/resource paper but not the current broad method claim.
- resolution test: predefine this negative/contracted terminal and target implications.

### PA-R3-01 — current manuscript exposes project-process vocabulary instead of reader-facing science

- class: `clarity_explanation_reporting`
- concern: sections named “survival gate”, internal candidate labels and relationship-to-programme language read like research governance rather than an Article.
- resolution test: next manuscript-facing version uses standard scientific section logic; internal gates remain in pipeline state/Methods/SI where scientifically relevant.

## Round-01 editor decision

`BLOCKED_ON_PROTECTED_RESULTS_AFTER_PROTOCOL_REPAIR`

No prose revision can close the central evidence blocker. Continue protocol/citation/figure work but do not manufacture a Results section.

# P-B — Same Position, Different Worlds

## Editorial triage

`CURRENT_CLAIMS_NOT_ESTABLISHED`

The object is potentially suitable for Nature Computational Science, but the current paper mixes a proposed interchange/interface, multiple theorem directions and a future benchmark. It needs at least one established formal or empirical result before Article review.

## Must-address concerns

### PB-R1-01 — theorem, conjecture and engineering-interface claims are not separated

- class: `technical_blocker`
- concern: relation refinement, conservative composition, minimal reopening and counter-probe completeness are listed as “formal directions”; none should appear later as established theory without proof.
- resolution test: create a formal-claim ledger marking theorem/proposition/conjecture/design rule; prove or narrow every manuscript-facing formal claim; include degenerate/counterexample checks.

### PB-R1-02 — shared schema may add bureaucracy without scientific decision value

- class: `technical_blocker`
- challenged claim: typed coordination reduces unsafe reuse/unnecessary reopening
- resolution test: protected cross-parent cases must show a decision change versus strongest parent composition; schema completeness alone is insufficient.

### PB-R2-01 — parent relation families own most mathematical semantics

- class: `publication_criteria_blocker`
- concern: bisimulation, Blackwell/Le Cam comparison, causal equivalence/transportability, metrology, abstract interpretation, lumpability and provenance each have mature theory. A generic “relation receipt” is not inherently novel.
- resolution test: prove/measure a cross-parent coordination residual unavailable to an information-matched parent composition while preserving native verdicts.

### PB-R2-02 — relation selection can hide expert judgment

- class: `major_repairable`
- concern: choosing the correct relation family may be the entire hard scientific problem.
- resolution test: report when relation family is supplied, inferred or `CANNOT_CHECK`; quantify expert selection burden and misclassification.

### PB-R3-01 — audience overload

- class: `clarity_explanation_reporting`
- concern: a broad computational-science reader cannot carry a dozen mature relation theories at once.
- resolution test: introduce the reader problem first, organize parent families by the scientific question they answer, and move detailed genealogy to a table/supporting material.

## Must-address concerns

### PC-R1-01 — “fixed pipelines” is presently too broad a foil

- class: `claim_recalibration`

- class: `technical_blocker`
- concern: J0–J8 labels may reflect ORION history rather than a universally meaningful intervention order.

### PC-R2-01 — strongest comparator must be a composition, not one agent framework

# P-D — When Agreement Is Not Independence

## Editorial triage

`CURRENT_CLAIMS_NOT_ESTABLISHED`

The paper has a compelling failure mode, but dependence-aware evidence and performative evaluation risk reading as two papers joined by governance vocabulary. The unifying object needs sharpening before results are generated.

## Must-address concerns

### PD-R1-01 — dependence and performativity require one causal validity object

- class: `major_repairable`
- concern: static dependence concerns correlated support; performativity concerns distribution/environment response. The paper should explain their common scientific question rather than concatenate literatures.
- resolution test: define evaluator/support validity as conditional on a generating/dependence environment and show both failures as invalid transport of assurance across that environment.

### PD-R1-02 — dependence may be unidentifiable

- class: `technical_blocker`
- concern: shared prompts, corpora, hidden assumptions or model ancestry may not be observable.
- resolution test: separate known, estimated and unknown dependence; include misspecification/sensitivity and honest `CANNOT_CHECK` terminals.

### PD-R2-01 — authority is partly normative, not an empirical score

- class: `claim_recalibration`
- concern: “authority violation rate” is meaningful only under a frozen authority contract; the paper should not imply that statistics create institutional authority.
- resolution test: clearly separate empirical/calibration results from externally supplied authority rules.

### PD-R2-02 — strongest parent composition is formidable

- class: `publication_criteria_blocker`
- concern: correlated/dependent evidence models + provenance + performative prediction/strategic response + preregistered evaluator custody may already solve most cases.
- resolution test: protected residual required; otherwise contract to governance resource/methods synthesis.

### PD-R3-01 — negative control is publication-critical

- class: `major_repairable`
- concern: a system that declares dependence/performativity everywhere will be safe but useless.
- resolution test: main evaluation includes genuinely independent support and stable-environment cases measuring unnecessary refusal/false alarms.

## Round-01 editor decision

`BLOCKED_ON_PROTECTED_DEPENDENCE_AND_DYNAMIC_EVALUATION_RESULTS`

# Machine Epistemics flagship — The Control Science of AI-Driven Discovery

## Editorial triage

`REPAIR_BEFORE_REVIEW`

Unlike P-A–P-E, this is a literature-based Perspective and can be advanced substantially now. The concept is promising, but V1 is too project-centred and too enumerative for a top-tier foundation paper.

## Must-address concerns

### ME-ED-01 — the paper must prove a field object before naming a field

- class: `publication_criteria_blocker`
- concern: V1 introduces Machine Epistemics quickly, then enumerates parent fields, a tuple, ten laws and ORION papers. Foundation precedents instead first establish a new empirical/process object or fragmentation problem.
- resolution test: opening establishes the historical shift to executable scientific processes and the specific object `machine-mediated scientific-state transition`; the field name follows the object.

### ME-R1-01 — ten equal-weight laws are framework inflation

- class: `major_repairable`
- concern: ten laws read like a private ontology and are hard to retain.
- resolution test: organize the field around four memorable control problems: epistemic-state observability; admissible transition/transport; evidence/authority assurance; escalation/closure. Ten laws move to a compact secondary box/table if retained.

### ME-R1-02 — testable versus normative propositions are mixed

- class: `major_repairable`
- resolution test: distinguish formal/empirical research questions from externally supplied governance/authority constraints. Do not imply that the field scientifically derives who should have authority.

### ME-R2-01 — anti-columbusing burden is unusually high

- class: `publication_criteria_blocker`
- concern: `Machine behaviour` was directly criticized in Nature for rediscovering cybernetics. Machine Epistemics spans cybernetics, metareasoning, truth maintenance, diagnosis, provenance, formal methods, causal transport, evidence synthesis and philosophy; a generic “intersection” is not enough.
- resolution test: conspicuous parent-ownership/demarcation table and explicit field falsifier: if information-matched parent composition makes the same protected cross-domain decisions, contract to integration engineering.

### ME-R2-02 — current manuscript contains ORION self-reference that weakens field credibility

- class: `publication_criteria_blocker`
- concern: ORION K0–K6, P-A–P-E and publication architecture make the proposed field look like branding for one framework.
- resolution test: remove project modules/paper programme from manuscript-facing V2; ORION may appear only later as one possible experimental instantiation after external primary evidence exists.

### ME-R2-03 — the timeliness claim needs a published evidence substrate

- class: `major_repairable`
- concern: “AI is becoming part of the scientific method” must be supported by representative published agentic/autonomous science, self-driving laboratory, automated theorem/computation/review and AI-for-science work, not asserted from project experience.
- resolution test: citation-verified examples across at least several research modes and domains.

### ME-R3-01 — reader baseline is too specialist

- class: `clarity_explanation_reporting`
- concern: terms such as transport, selective reopening, non-identifiability, authority ceiling and saturation require local intuitive explanation for NMI's broad AI readership.
- resolution test: every central concept receives minimum identity/purpose/logic/boundary explanation; tuple notation follows prose rather than leads it.

### ME-R3-02 — figures should carry field logic, not ORION architecture

- class: `major_repairable`
- resolution test: three central conceptual evidence units: (1) new object/transition and failure examples; (2) parent fields + composition gap; (3) four control problems + measurable questions. Optional fourth figure shows field falsification/benchmark path.

### ME-R4-01 — field status must remain conditional

- class: `claim_recalibration`
- concern: a Perspective may advocate a discipline, but ORION-V2 cannot self-grant its establishment.
- resolution test: use `we propose`, `we argue`, `field hypothesis`; state negative field terminal explicitly.

### ME-R4-02 — compare with successful non-field syntheses

- class: `major_repairable`
- concern: broad AI-for-science and roadmap papers show that coordination can be valuable without a new discipline.
- resolution test: explain why a distinct object/method programme is needed beyond a review, roadmap or benchmark framework; otherwise use an integration Perspective target.
