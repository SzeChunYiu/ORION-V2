Therefore the project must not use an AI-policy question as a disguised paper-fit presubmission enquiry.

## 2. Why a policy question may still be warranted

Nature Portfolio's current AI framework permits assistive/evaluative AI with transparency, verification and demonstrable human accountability, but prohibits opaque substitution of AI for scholarly judgment or presenting AI-generated core reasoning as human-derived.

ORION-V2 used LLMs materially for:

- literature discovery/synthesis;
- candidate formalizations and methodology;
- adversarial critique;
- software/test generation;
- drafting/editing.

Because this is substantive interpretive assistance, the author group should not assume that a generic writing disclosure is enough.

We intend to disclose these uses transparently.
