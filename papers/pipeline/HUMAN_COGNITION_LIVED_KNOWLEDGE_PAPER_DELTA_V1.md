## P-A — structural donor discovery

- `SourceMode` and `KnowledgeForm` to donor-search coverage;

### Do not claim

## P-B — relation, comparability and transport

### Add

- procedure/skill transport versus propositional/textual transport;
- tacit-dependency marker;
- affordance-relative action equivalence;
- distributed-cognitive-system equivalence versus agent identity;
- exemplar/skill recovery after representation transport.

### New hostile case

Two textual procedures are semantically near-identical, but changed material/tool conditions require different skilled actions. Text equivalence must not authorize competence transfer.

### Do not claim

A universal formalization of tacit knowledge.

## P-C — obligation-driven solver

### Add

- metacognitive self-model and calibration;
- object-level vs meta-level failure;
- error-learning/failure-lesson state;
- online reflection versus independent review;
- insight/representation restructuring as a specific escalation hypothesis;
- curiosity/active-sampling action class;
- observer coupling and second-order process diagnosis.

### New benchmark families

1. same task accuracy, different self-calibration;
2. high-confidence errors followed by corrective evidence;
3. self-reflection correlated with the original model versus independent review;
4. current representation cannot express the decisive discriminator;
5. noisy novelty versus useful surprise;
6. ordinary local repair where metacognitive escalation would be overreach.

### Do not claim

Generic metacognition, generic self-reflection, generic curiosity or generic insight mechanisms as ORION inventions.

## P-D — dependence-aware evidence

### Add

- cognition distributed across agents, artifacts, instruments and practices;
- tacit/common-practice dependence not visible in document provenance;
- social/apprenticeship source dependence;
- observer/evaluator coupling;
- independent-looking reviewers sharing a learned practice/model source.

### New benchmark family

Same nominal number of validators under different hidden cognitive/artifact dependencies.

### Do not claim

That all social or practice similarity implies dependence; the relation must be decision-relevant.

## P-E — frontier opportunity discovery

### Add

- Yaqub-style serendipity mechanisms: theory-led, observer-led, error-borne, network-emergent;
- encounter generation separated from value recognition;
- surprise vector rather than scalar novelty;
- curiosity budget and cross-problem relevance;
- accidents/failures as source-bound opportunity triggers;
- ordinary-life/practice observations as admissible opportunity sources.

### New prospective benchmark

Inject unexpected events containing:

- useless stochastic surprise;
- known/duplicate curiosity lure;
- useful side effect relevant to another problem;
- theory-contradicting anomaly;
- practical observation with no scholarly vocabulary match.

Measure whether the system recognizes useful cross-problem value without granting novelty or agenda authority.

### Survival implication

If this serendipity/opportunity machinery adds value only as the P-C exploration policy, P-E still merges into P-C.

## Machine Epistemics flagship

The Perspective needs a bounded new section, not a fifth control problem.

### Proposed insertion

**Human inquiry is not only explicit reasoning.**

Use the section to establish three points:

1. reflexive inquiry: humans monitor confidence, errors and reasoning effort;
2. lived knowing: skill can be tacit, embodied, situated and distributed across artifacts/people;
3. open-ended inquiry: surprise, error and accidental encounter can create new problems rather than merely solve the current one.

Then state the machine-science consequence:

> A Machine-Epistemic Episode must therefore be capable of representing not only explicit claims and planned actions, but also calibrated self-models, competence/tacit dependencies, unexpected encounters and the possibility that the current search objective or representation is itself the problem.

Keep direct parent ownership visible: Dewey, Peirce, Ryle, Polanyi, Kuhn, metacognition/meta-reasoning, distributed/situated cognition, serendipity and curiosity research.

### Flagship field test extension

A field-level residual is stronger if the same scientific-control law applies across:

- formal scholarly reasoning;
- experimental/scientific workflows;
- practical/tacit skill episodes;
- surprise-driven opportunity formation.

If the proposed episode abstraction only works for formal research workflows, narrow the field accordingly.
