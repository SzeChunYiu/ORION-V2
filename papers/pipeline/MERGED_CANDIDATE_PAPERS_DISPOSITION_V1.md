# Merged Candidate Papers Disposition V1

**Programme:** ORION-V2 / Machine Epistemics  
**Purpose:** prevent speculative paper proliferation and duplicate claims while preserving resurrection gates for genuinely new evidence.

## P-E — Frontier Opportunity Discovery

### Current disposition

`MERGE_INTO_P-C`.

Opportunity/problem finding remains scientifically important, but a standalone top-tier Article requires prospective evidence that cannot be manufactured retrospectively.

### Material retained in P-C

- source-bound problem/opportunity proposal as an action family;
- separation of novelty/surprise/diversity from scientific value;
- pre-outcome source cutoff and criterion freeze;
- authority boundary: proposing a research direction does not create institutional agenda authority.

### Resurrection gate

All must be met:

1. real candidate portfolio frozen before outcomes;
2. prospectively defined follow-up horizon;
3. independent expert adjudication;
4. observable future scientific actions/evidence/outcomes;
5. strongest expert/problem-finding parent comparator;
6. missing/censored follow-up retained;
7. outcome criterion not rewritten after future success/failure.

Without this, a standalone opportunity paper is hindsight science.

# P-F — Machine-Native Scientific Intelligence

### Current disposition

`MERGE_INTO_FLAGSHIP_AND_P-C`.

The proposition that machine cognition need not mimic human thought is important but not a standalone empirical paper without a mechanism.

### Material retained

**Flagship:** machine-native internal freedom + external scientific witness.  
**P-C:** machine-native proposal/search/operator arms as optional control policies under the same scientific terminal/evaluator gates.

### Resurrection gate

A standalone P-F requires:

1. one frozen machine-native mechanism, not a broad philosophy;
2. matched-compute human-mimetic/direct/F0 controls;
3. mechanism ablation;
4. second-domain transfer;
5. external witness/validation adequate to the adopted scientific transition;
6. gain not explained solely by extra search/compute.

# P-G-KM — Scientific Knowledge Metabolism

### Current disposition

`MERGE_INTO_FLAGSHIP + P-A + P-C`.

Knowledge transformation, memory, provenance, negative history and selective update are infrastructure themes shared with many parent theories.

### Material retained

- flagship transition ecology: knowledge/source transitions;
- P-A donor-native reconstruction and learned transfer material;
- P-C evidence/memory state and scientific-control actions.

### Resurrection gate

Execution-linked evidence must show a specific component or substrate changes protected scientific outcomes beyond mature retrieval/provenance/revision/continual-memory parents under matched resources.

A conceptual “knowledge metabolism” architecture is not sufficient.

# P-G-WF — Scientific Warrant Factorisation

### Current disposition

`MERGE_INTO_FLAGSHIP + P-B + P-D`.

Mature assurance, argumentation, verification, provenance, evidence and measurement theories already separate many validity layers.

### Material retained

- flagship candidate non-amplification/weak-link constraint;
- P-B typed relation/loss/authority boundaries;
- P-D evaluator adequacy, dependence and validity context.

### Resurrection gate

One of:

1. nontrivial theorem with assumptions that is not an assurance/argumentation corollary; or
2. protected decision improvement beyond strongest assurance/provenance/argument parents.

Otherwise it remains a useful explanatory decomposition, not a standalone paper.
