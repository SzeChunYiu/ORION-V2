# Flagship and Paper Programme — Foundation Saturation Gate V1

**Status:** binding internal editorial gate under issue #41. It does not grant publication readiness. It prevents the paper programme from outrunning the parent-space and foundation synthesis.

## 1. Editorial principle

The Machine Epistemics flagship must not be written as though a field foundation has already been established. The specialist papers must not use the size of the literature programme as evidence that their mechanisms are novel or useful.

The paper programme therefore follows:

`parent saturation -> foundation synthesis -> protected residual evidence -> manuscript promotion`.

Writing, literature coverage and formal reference tests can proceed recursively, but claims cannot move to a stronger epistemic category merely because the prose becomes polished.

## 2. Flagship gate

The flagship may currently claim:

- AI-mediated research creates a scientifically important transition-control problem;
- Machine Epistemics is a provisional and falsifiable field hypothesis;
- mature parent disciplines own most component mechanisms;
- four public control questions and the bounded episode are candidate organizing devices;
- human inquiry is a donor, hostile control and collaboration boundary, not a normative optimum;
- machine-native internal cognition is compatible with external scientific accountability;
- parent wins, field contraction and `CANNOT_CHECK` are legitimate outcomes.

The flagship may not currently claim:

- a saturated foundation;
- a complete genealogy;
- stable universal foundation laws;
- a distinct composition-level residual supported by protected naturalistic evidence;
- that K0–K6 are the final ontology;
- that the three learning loops have demonstrated value;
- that machine-native inquiry outperforms human-mimetic or strongest-parent systems;
- publication readiness.

### Flagship entry to final rewrite

A post-saturation flagship rewrite requires:

1. issue #41 synthesis entry criteria satisfied;
2. parent-space atlas frozen for the manuscript cutoff;
3. minimal foundation candidate selected against parent-composition and domain-federation alternatives;
4. every main-text proposition represented in the foundation proposition ledger;
5. strongest counterevidence and field falsifiers retained;
6. external hostile demarcation review completed;
7. manuscript length and figure allocation re-contracted after—not before—the foundation is known.

## 3. Main-text architecture after synthesis

The public article should not reproduce the atlas. Its likely architecture remains:

1. changed scientific object: executable research episodes;
2. bounded object and central transition question;
3. dense parent ownership and “old wine” challenge;
4. three to five organizing questions/propositions from the final synthesis;
5. protected formal/empirical programme;
6. barriers, non-claims and field contraction.

The number of laws and figures is not fixed until synthesis. Citation breadth belongs in source ledgers and supplementary maps unless it is necessary for the main argument.

## 4. P-A gate — structural donor discovery

Before result or novelty promotion, P-A must bind:

- literature, practice and remote-domain donor lanes in the atlas;
- strongest retrieval, analogy, formal abstraction, sufficient-statistic and MDL/compression baselines;
- source-mode and scientific-parent distinctions;
- analysis-workflow provenance where donor conclusions depend on model construction;
- counter-probes for false analogy and decision-relative reduction;
- protected resource-matched results.

Possible contraction:

`PARENT_SEARCH_OR_MDL_SUFFICIENT`, `PRACTICE_DONOR_ONLY`, `CONTRACTION_RESOURCE`, or `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## 5. P-B gate — relations, competence and transport

P-B must bind:

- native relation families and exact parent verdicts;
- causal, measurement, abstraction, procedural and authority transport lanes;
- test sensitivity for relation claims;
- robustness across plausible assumptions;
- discordance and unresolved anchors;
- selective reopening and cost;
- naturalistic cases from at least two sciences.

Possible contraction:

`PARENT_RELATION_PRODUCT_SUFFICIENT`, `INTERCHANGE_SCHEMA_ONLY`, `DOMAIN_SPECIFIC`, or `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## 6. P-C gate — obligation-driven control

P-C must bind:

- metareasoning, experimental design, diagnosis, workflow, statistical analysis workflow, resilience and structured-method efficacy parents;
- simple/direct controls;
- self-model calibration independent of same-model reflection;
- false/missed escalation and criterion-gaming cases;
- recovery and graceful degradation under resource/tool/evaluator disturbances;
- component-value arms for every added control mechanism;
- V1 parity and protected mixed-task results.

Possible contraction:

`USE_PARENT_COMPOSED_SOLVER`, `CONTEXTUAL_CONTROL_ONLY`, `REDUNDANT_DRAG`, `V2_OVERREACH`, or `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## 7. P-D gate — dependence, assurance and dynamic evaluation

P-D must bind:

- dependent evidence/meta-analysis, provenance, robustness/triangulation, assurance case, social epistemology and performativity parents;
- assumption-level and source-level dependence;
- genuine independent-support positive controls;
- criticism uptake versus review theatre;
- assurance argument completeness and circularity cases;
- discordance preservation;
- evaluator response and observer coupling;
- blinded independent evaluation.

Possible contraction:

`PARENT_ASSURANCE_AND_DEPENDENCE_SUFFICIENT`, `TRACEABILITY_RESOURCE_ONLY`, `DOMAIN_SPECIFIC`, or `CANNOT_CHECK`.

## 8. P-E gate — opportunity discovery

P-E remains contingent and must bind:

- problem finding, abduction, serendipity, anomaly detection, statistical workflow and active design parents;
- severe/probative discriminators;
- analysis artifacts versus real anomalies;
- prospective information cutoff and outcome-blind evaluation;
- noise curiosity and famous-accident hindsight controls;
- agenda authority;
- future value beyond experts and parent compositions.

Default contraction remains `MERGE_INTO_P_C`.

## 9. P-F gate — machine-native inquiry

P-F remains a prospectus, not a portfolio paper. It must bind:

- automated reasoning, self-play, world-model planning, MDL/representation selection, meta-learning, open-endedness, distributed AI and human–AI complementarity parents;
- human-positive, human-neutral and human-trap strata;
- causal mechanism ablations;
- compute/resource curves;
- non-linguistic internal representations with sufficient external witnesses;
- resilience and cross-domain transfer;
- leakage audit;
- evidence that difference—not merely scale or opacity—causes protected value.

Default contraction remains `MERGE_P_C_AND_FLAGSHIP`.

## 10. Atomic claim binding

Every central manuscript claim receives:

- `claim_id`;
- `paper_id`;
- `claim_type` — parent fact / definition / hypothesis / result / normative boundary / non-claim;
- atlas lane(s);
- native source(s);
- strongest parent composition;
- foundation proposition, if any;
- required figure/evidence unit;
- protected result identity;
- strongest counterevidence;
- current terminal.

Claims with an open atlas lane or missing parent product cannot be promoted to `RESULT` or `FOUNDATION_LAW`.

## 11. Recursive writing rule

Run another manuscript iteration only when at least one of these changes:

- parent ownership;
- new material evidence/counterevidence;
- protected result;
- foundation proposition;
- journal/archetype requirement;
- independent reviewer concern;
- claim contraction;
- figure evidence chain.

Do not produce V11, V12 and so on merely by stylistic rewriting while the foundation gate is blocked.
