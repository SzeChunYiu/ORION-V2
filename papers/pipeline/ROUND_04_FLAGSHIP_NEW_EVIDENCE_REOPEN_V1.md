## New direct threats

### R4-N1 — Epistemic control in ML-based science

Emanuele Ratti, **Epistemic Control and the Normativity of Machine Learning-Based Science** (2026), explicitly formulates ML-based science through *epistemic control* and develops tracking/tracing conditions for human epistemic control.

**Effect on flagship:**

- generic 'epistemic control' is parent-owned language;
- Machine Epistemics must not claim that it first recognized control as the epistemic problem of ML-based science;
- the subtitle/tagline `control science of AI-driven discovery` is now a higher-collision formulation and should be reconsidered;
- the residual must be the richer executable **scientific commitment transition** object and its cross-parent operational/falsifiable programme.

### R4-N2 — Spec-driven architecture of epistemic control

Grzegorz Wojarnik, **Spec-Driven AI for Empirical Research: A Scoping Review and an Architecture of Epistemic Control** (2026 preprint), independently combines preregistration, registered reports, multiverse/specification analysis, reproducible research, provenance standards and AI-for-science workflows into an epistemic-control architecture for empirical research.

**Effect on flagship:**

- an integrated AI-research epistemic-control architecture is no longer merely hypothetical prior art;
- Machine Epistemics cannot claim integration of provenance + study specification + claim discipline as its standalone field residual;
- strongest-parent comparison must include this convergent architecture despite its preprint status, with publication-status caveat.

### R4-N3 — Provenance as trust foundation in autonomous science

MacKnight et al., **Provenance grounds trust in autonomous science**, *Nature Computational Science* (20 August 2026), argues that trust in autonomous science should be grounded in a complete, reopenable record of reasoning, action and measurement that can be audited and corrected.

**Effect on flagship:**

- 'reopenable record' and correction/audit are now direct contemporary autonomous-science parents;
- provenance-to-trust is an active scientific programme, not a hypothetical composition gap;
- the manuscript must sharpen the distinction `provenance/reopenability is necessary infrastructure != scientific-validity decision`, while acknowledging how much this recent programme already closes.

### R4-N4 — Epistemic control as a research-evaluation concern

Kim & Park, **Evaluating LLM-assisted research: stage-sensitive asymmetries in productivity and epistemic control**, *Research Evaluation* (2026), explicitly uses epistemic control in analysing AI-assisted research.

**Effect on flagship:**

- the term is converging across philosophy, research evaluation and AI-science system design;
- novelty cannot be based on naming the control problem;
- the field proposal may be stronger as a **synthesis/formalization of converging strands** than as a newly discovered territory.

### R4-N5 — Automated scientific discovery is already an established historical field

Kramer et al., **Automated Scientific Discovery: From Equation Discovery to Autonomous Discovery Systems**, *Machine Learning* (2026), traces automated scientific discovery back to the 1970s and surveys closed-loop/autonomous systems.

**Effect on flagship:**

- Machine Epistemics cannot imply that executable scientific discovery itself is new as an intellectual programme;
- the historical novelty is the present scale/heterogeneity/persistence of executable research loops and the need to coordinate assurance/transition semantics, not automated discovery as such.

## Revised editorial hypothesis

The strongest defensible foundation-paper story is now:

> AI-driven science is producing a **convergence problem**. Computational and machine epistemology, epistemic control, provenance/reopenability, verification-first autonomy, metareasoning, diagnosis, formal transport, measurement science and evidence assurance are independently attacking fragments of the same machine-mediated scientific-transition problem. **Machine Epistemics** is proposed as a compact umbrella and formal research programme for testing whether those fragments share a stable object and cross-domain laws.

This is weaker as a priority claim and stronger as a scientific Perspective.
