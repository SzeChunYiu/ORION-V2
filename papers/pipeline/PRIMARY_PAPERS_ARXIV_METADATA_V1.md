# Shared human-only arXiv decisions

For every paper, the human submitter decides after inspecting current arXiv rules:

- author list/order;
- corresponding contact;
- primary category and cross-list(s);
- endorsement/category eligibility;
- license/copyright option;
- submission timing.

The manuscript-development AI may not infer those from repository metadata.
