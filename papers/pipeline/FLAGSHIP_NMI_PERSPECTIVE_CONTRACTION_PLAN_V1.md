## 5. Balance requirement

The final Perspective must give serious space to reasons the field may **not** be distinct:

- F0 composition may suffice;
- F1 selective bridge theories may be enough;
- F3 may preserve domain semantics better;
- current epistemic-control/provenance/spec-driven programmes may already cover much of the territory;
- recursive development may recover publication/citation biases instead of scientific principles.
