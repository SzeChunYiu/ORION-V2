# Flagship Evidence Spine V1
## Machine Epistemics as an Absorptive Science of Machine-Mediated Inquiry

**Target archetype:** Nature Machine Intelligence Perspective  
**Target length:** 3,000–4,000 words; up to 100 selective references  
**Status:** pre-manuscript evidence architecture. It does not authorize the final flagship rewrite or field-foundation claim.

## 1. Editorial thesis

The paper should make one bounded claim:

> As scientific research becomes an executable human–machine process, a higher-order science may be needed to govern how scientific commitments, representations, evidence, tests and agendas change across the mature parent sciences. Machine Epistemics is the falsifiable hypothesis that these parent sciences can be absorbed into a typed federation with a small set of cross-parent transition constraints that generate better scientific decisions than their strongest existing federation.

This claim is ambitious enough for a Perspective but remains conditional. The paper must not state that F2 has already won.

## 2. Main non-claim

Machine Epistemics does not own the native mechanisms of:

- epistemology and learning theory;
- control and metareasoning;
- statistical inference and experimental design;
- causal transport and measurement;
- numerical analysis and software validation;
- assurance and formal argumentation;
- cognitive or social epistemology;
- data governance;
- autonomous agents and laboratory systems.

Its candidate contribution lies in the theory and evidence of their cross-boundary scientific transitions.

## 4. Claim classes

### C-F1 — Changed scientific object

**Claim:** agentic AI changes the unit of evaluation from isolated outputs to sequences of machine-mediated scientific-state transitions.

**Evidence required:** current autonomous-science systems plus concrete cases where execution success does not determine scientific validity.

**Status:** supported parent/application observation; wording should remain bounded.

### C-F2 — Parent-density claim

**Claim:** the controlling knowledge is distributed across mature sciences whose relations are not fully captured by one local method.

**Evidence required:** selective parent map; explicit interfield questions; no “no existing field considers” language.

**Status:** supported synthesis claim, not novelty by absence.

### C-F3 — Absorptive-theory hypothesis

**Claim:** a typed parent federation with cross-parent transition constraints may outperform the strongest parent federation on protected scientific decisions.

**Evidence required:** F0–F3 blueprint, theory-dominance protocol and at least one frozen specialist result before submission.

**Status:** field hypothesis until evidence exists.

### C-F4 — Machine-native intelligence boundary

**Claim:** machine cognition need not imitate human internal reasoning, but scientific adoption requires claim-sufficient external witnesses.

**Evidence required:** P-F/P-C machine-native comparison and formal/empirical examples of non-human internal strategies with external validation.

**Status:** candidate interface proposition.

### C-F5 — Authority boundary

**Claim:** scientific support, recommendation, consent, custody and institutional permission are separable.

**Evidence required:** social/governance parents and protected cases; no normative authority generated by ORION.

**Status:** strong non-amplification boundary, not an empirical law of nature.

### C-F6 — Field falsifier

**Claim:** the field-level hypothesis contracts when F0, F1 or F3 reproduces all protected decisions/generative consequences with equal or lower cost.

**Evidence required:** theory-dominance protocol and explicit negative terminals.

**Status:** programme constitution.

## 5. Candidate main-text propositions

Do not pre-commit to nine laws. After saturation and synthesis, select approximately four or five propositions that jointly satisfy parent recovery, cross-domain materiality and explanatory economy.

The leading candidates are currently:

1. **Bound transition identity** — silent changes to subject, criterion, source, representation or authority cannot inherit the same scientific decision automatically.
2. **Warrant factorization and non-amplification** — source, semantics, model, computation, test, argument, transport and authority remain separable failure points; validity cannot be borrowed from another layer.
3. **Typed conservation through composition** — relation, approximation, uncertainty and dependence cannot silently strengthen or disappear.
4. **Selective invalidation and reopening** — failures reopen only commitments whose complete sufficient support is lost.
5. **Exploration/confirmation separation** — exploratory and constructive actions may expand the inquiry space without directly confirming an explanation.
6. **Parent recovery and local deference** — a higher theory must preserve native parent decisions and remain inactive when a local method is sufficient.
7. **Bounded closure** — stopping is relative to declared routes, censorship and reopening conditions.

Machine-native external-witness and error-detection-adequacy propositions may enter the main set or become consequences of warrant factorization depending on final contraction.

## 6. Figure evidence plan

### Figure 1 — From agent output to scientific transition

**Reader question:** What is the object of the proposed field?

Show a bounded episode in which search, computation, experiment, interpretation, review and adoption can fail independently. Use four concise examples:

- consensus on a false model;
- correct computation of an inadequate model;
- exploratory observation mistaken for confirmation;
- strong evidence without authority to act.

**Evidence function:** orientation and necessity.

### Figure 2 — Parent sciences as native views

**Reader question:** Is this merely renamed prior work?

Show native parent views around a shared transition problem. For each cluster, identify:

- native owner;
- strongest output/certificate;
- unresolved cross-parent relation;
- local-deference condition.

**Evidence function:** ownership and absorptive architecture.

### Figure 3 — F0–F3 and prospective generativity

**Reader question:** What exactly is being compared?

Show the four candidate foundations and one prospective case flowing through each. F2 must generate a discriminator or transition consequence not inserted after the outcome.

**Evidence function:** falsifiable theory comparison.

### Figure 4 — Theory dominance and contraction

**Reader question:** What would found or refute the field?

Show hard validity gates, parent recovery, quality/generativity, cross-domain transfer and resource fronts. Terminals:

- F2 absorptive-supertheory candidate;
- F1 interfield advance;
- F0 parent federation sufficient;
- F3 domain federation;
- deceptive unification;
- `CANNOT_CHECK`.

**Evidence function:** field demarcation and intellectual honesty.
